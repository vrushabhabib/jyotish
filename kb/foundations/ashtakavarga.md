## 2. Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)

`SAV[sign] = Σ over the seven planets' BAV[sign]`; total always 337; mean per sign 28.08. Lagna's own BAV is excluded from the 337 (some software shows an 8-row total of 386 — label it).

### 2.1 Per-sign thresholds
- [PVR] ch. 12: **≥30 strong** (house matters flourish, transits there give good results); **25–30 average**; **<25 weak**. This confirms the "<25 / 25–30 / >30" rule in the brief.
- Common alternative: 28 as the dividing line (337/12), "more than 28 progressively strong" [IAS].
- Finer bands seen on popular sites (40+ exceptional, 30–39 strong, 25–29 average, 20–24 weak, <20 very weak) have no classical source; treat as presentation only.

### 2.2 Per-planet BAV thresholds
In a planet's own BAV a sign holds 0–8 bindus: 0–3 weak/unfavourable, 4 neutral, 5–8 favourable. A natal planet sitting in a sign where it has ≥5 of its own bindus acts well even with poor dignity; with ≤3 it under-performs even if exalted (Raman's standard teaching, [MEM]).

### 2.3 House-comparison rules [MEM — Raman / C.S. Patel style rules, not re-read]
- 11th > 10th: gains exceed effort; 10th > 11th: more work than reward.
- 11th > 12th: income exceeds expenditure, savings; 12th > 11th: spending/loss exceeds gain.
- Lagna > 12th and > 6th/8th: self prevails over losses, enemies, illness.
- 2nd > 12th: wealth is retained.
- 1st, 9th, 10th, 11th all ≥30: strong prosperity indication; 6th, 8th, 12th low is good.
- 12th < Lagna < 2nd < 11th is the "ideal" financial ordering.
- Group sums sometimes used: 1+5+9 (dharma), 2+6+10 (artha), 3+7+11 (kama), 4+8+12 (moksha); the largest group shows the life emphasis.
These are comparisons of raw (unreduced) SAV.

## 3. Transit use

1. A planet transiting a sign gives results in proportion to its own BAV bindus in that sign: 0–3 difficult, 4 mixed, 5+ helpful. This overrides the generic "good/bad house from Moon" transit rule and is the main reason two people feel the same transit differently.
2. Also read the SAV of the transited sign: a slow planet crossing a sign with SAV ≥30 is productive, <25 troublesome [PVR].
3. Sade Sati and Jupiter/Saturn transits should be qualified by Saturn's/Jupiter's BAV in the three signs.

### 3.1 Kakshya (eighth-part) subdivision [MEM — standard; not found on a page read this session]
Each sign = 8 kakshyas of 3°45'. Lords in fixed order (slowest to fastest, Lagna last):

| # | Degrees in sign | Lord |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0°00'–3°45' | Saturn |
| 2 | 3°45'–7°30' | Jupiter |
| 3 | 7°30'–11°15' | Mars |
| 4 | 11°15'–15°00' | Sun |
| 5 | 15°00'–18°45' | Venus |
| 6 | 18°45'–22°30' | Mercury |
| 7 | 22°30'–26°15' | Moon |
| 8 | 26°15'–30°00' | Lagna |

Rule: while transiting planet X is in the kakshya of lord L, the transit is favourable if L contributed a bindu to that sign in X's Prastharashtakavarga, otherwise unfavourable/neutral. This gives timing inside a sign (Saturn ≈ 3.75 months per kakshya, Jupiter ≈ 1.5 months, Sun ≈ 3.75 days).

## 4. Trikona shodhana (first reduction) [PVR, Parashara's version]

Apply separately to each planet's BAV, on each trine group of signs (Aries-Leo-Sagittarius, Taurus-Virgo-Capricorn, Gemini-Libra-Aquarius, Cancer-Scorpio-Pisces):
1. If at least one of the three signs has 0 → no reduction.
2. If all three are equal → all three become 0.
3. Otherwise subtract the smallest of the three from all three.

Variant noted by [PVR]: some authors apply rule 1 only when exactly one sign is zero. [TEM] states only rules 1 and 3 and groups the triplets by house from lagna (same sets of signs).

## 5. Ekadhipatya shodhana (second reduction) [PVR]

Apply after trikona shodhana, to each pair of signs owned by one planet: Aries/Scorpio, Taurus/Libra, Gemini/Virgo, Sagittarius/Pisces, Capricorn/Aquarius. Cancer and Leo are never reduced. "Occupied" = a planet is in the sign in the natal chart.
1. If at least one of the two has 0 → no reduction.
2. If both signs are occupied → no reduction.
3. One occupied, one empty:
   a. empty sign has the lower (or equal) value → set the empty sign to 0;
   b. empty sign has the higher value → set the empty sign equal to the occupied sign's value.
4. Both empty:
   a. equal values → both become 0;
   b. different values → the higher is reduced to the lower.

([PVR] words 3a as "lower"; Raman treats the equal case as reduce-to-zero as well — verify.) A different, cruder rule set appears on [TEM] (one occupied → the empty one becomes 0 regardless; both empty unequal → the lower becomes 0). That does not match PVR/Raman; do not use it unless a source text is produced.

## 6. Shodhya pinda [PVR]

After both reductions, for each planet's reduced BAV:
- `Rasi pinda = Σ_signs reduced_value[sign] × rasi_multiplier[sign]`
- `Graha pinda = Σ_planets reduced_value[sign occupied by that planet] × graha_multiplier[planet]`
- `Shodhya pinda = Rasi pinda + Graha pinda`

| Sign | Ar | Ta | Ge | Cn | Le | Vi | Li | Sc | Sg | Cp | Aq | Pi |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rasi multiplier | 7 | 10 | 8 | 4 | 10 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 5 | 11 | 12 |

| Planet | Sun | Moon | Mars | Mercury | Jupiter | Venus | Saturn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graha multiplier | 5 | 5 | 8 | 5 | 10 | 7 | 5 |

## 7. Use in timing [MEM — Raman; summarised, verify formulas before coding]

- Transit timing via BAV + kakshya (section 3) is the mainstream, defensible use.
- Shodhya-pinda formulas (BPHS ch. 70–71 style): multiply a planet's shodhya pinda by the bindus in a relevant house of its BAV, divide by 27 → remainder = nakshatra (from Ashwini); divide by 12 → remainder = sign. When Saturn (for adverse events) or Jupiter (for favourable) transits that nakshatra/sign or its trines, the event tied to that house is timed. Classic example: Sun's shodhya pinda × bindus in the 9th from the Sun in the Sun's BAV → danger period for the father. Treat these as low-confidence experimental outputs.
- Ashtakavarga ayurdaya (longevity) and Ashtakavarga dasha exist (BPHS ch. 71–72); not recommended for the engine's first version.
- Dasha use: a dasha lord with ≥5 bindus in its own BAV in the sign it occupies, and whose sign has SAV ≥30, delivers more easily; during the dasha, weight transits of the dasha lord by BAV.
- Muhurta: prefer lagna/Moon/Sun in signs with natal SAV ≥30 [PVR].

## Implementation notes

- Count houses inclusively from the reference's sign: house h → sign index `(ref_sign + h − 1) mod 12`.
- Unit tests: each BAV sums to its fixed total for any chart; SAV sums to 337.
- Keep three layers: prastara (8×12 booleans per planet), BAV (12 ints), SAV (12 ints). Keep reduced tables separately; never overwrite raw values.
- SAV for house reading is by sign; with whole-sign houses, house n = sign (lagna_sign + n − 1).
- Variant switch `parashara | varahamihira` affects only Moon's and Venus's BAV and therefore SAV distribution (not total). Store which was used with every output, since SAV per sign can differ by ±1 and cross a threshold.
- Kakshya of the Lagna reference (8th kakshya) uses the Lagna row of the planet's prastara.
- Ekadhipatya "occupied" test: decide whether Rahu/Ketu count as occupants (most authors count only the seven planets; Raman — verify).
- Reductions for the SAV are done per BAV and then summed; do not reduce the SAV directly.
- Rahu and Ketu have no BAV in BPHS (some later authors add one); do not invent.

## Reliability notes

- BAV tables: read from [PVR] in this session and cross-checked row by row against recall of Raman and against [IAS] pages for Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus. The [IAS] Moon page has transcription errors (its Venus and Saturn rows do not sum to 49) and its Venus page omits house 5 from the Moon row; PVR's table is internally consistent (all totals match).
- The four Parashara/Varahamihira differences are real textual variants, confirmed by [PVR]'s own discussion.
- SAV thresholds 25/30: confirmed in [PVR].
- Kakshya table and house-comparison rules: from recall (standard and widely reproduced), not re-read.
- Ekadhipatya rule 3a equal-value case and the Virgo multiplier (5 vs 6): unresolved; check BPHS ch. 67–69.
- Predictive validity: the BAV-in-transit rule is the part with the broadest agreement among practitioners; shodhya-pinda timing is rarely used in practice and should be labelled experimental.
