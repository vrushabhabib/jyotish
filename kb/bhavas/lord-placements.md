# Bhava lords in the 12 houses (BPHS core, condensed)

Read with dignity: well-placed lord (own/exalted/friend, not combust) gives the positive reading; debilitated/combust/with malefics gives the negative. "Lord of X in Y" always means X's matters are tied to Y's matters.

## Lagna lord
1: strong body, self-made, long life. 2: earns by own effort, family-oriented, good speech. 3: courageous, siblings supportive, restless. 4: property, education, mother, happy home. 5: children, intelligence, honour; luck through past merit. 6: sickly early, enemies, debts; but competitive & wins litigation if strong. 7: spouse dominant in life, travels; marriage central to identity. 8: obstacles, health scares, occult, inheritance; life turns after mid-30s. 9: fortunate, dharmic, father supportive, travels abroad. 10: career-defining, status, self-employed potential. 11: gains, income, elder sibling support. 12: expenses, foreign residence, spiritual; weak body if afflicted.

## 2nd lord
1: self-earned wealth. 2: rich, good speech, family wealth. 3: earns through communication/courage. 4: property, vehicles, mother's wealth. 5: speculation, children bring wealth. 6: wealth through service/medicine/litigation; debts if afflicted. 7: spouse brings wealth, business partnerships. 8: inheritance, insurance, sudden wealth or losses. 9: fortune, father's wealth. 10: wealth through career. 11: gains, rich (2L–11L link = Dhana yoga). 12: expenses, foreign income.

## 3rd lord
1: brave, self-driven. 2: wealth via communication/siblings. 3: strong siblings. 4: property via effort. 5: creative, intellectual. 6: enmity with siblings. 7: spouse from communication field. 8: trouble to siblings. 9: fortune through effort. 10: media/communication career. 11: gains via siblings. 12: siblings abroad.

## 4th lord
1: property, comfort, education. 2: family property. 3: property via effort. 4: property, vehicles, mother's long life. 5: learned. 6: mother ill, property disputes. 7: property via spouse. 8: mother troubled, property loss. 9: foreign property, fortunate. 10: real estate/education career. 11: gains via property. 12: property abroad, mother's separation.

## 5th lord
1: intelligent, children, mantra siddhi. 2: wealth via speculation. 3: communication talent. 4: education. 5: intelligent, good children. 6: children troubled. 7: love marriage, children after marriage. 8: children's troubles, occult mind. 9: fortune via children. 10: career via intellect. 11: gains via speculation. 12: children abroad or delayed, loss via speculation.

## 6th lord
1: sickly, enemies, self-sabotage. 2: wealth via service, family disputes. 3: disputes with siblings. 4: mother's illness, property disputes. 5: children's troubles. 6: defeats enemies, healthy. 7: spouse disputes, lawsuits with partners. 8: Viparita — enemies destroyed. 9: father's troubles. 10: service/government job. 11: gains via disputes/medicine/law. 12: Viparita — enemies vanish; foreign service.

## 7th lord
1: spouse dominant, early marriage. 2: wealth via spouse. 3: spouse from siblings' circle. 4: spouse brings comfort. 5: love marriage. 6: spouse troubled, divorce risk if afflicted. 7: good marriage, business partner. 8: spouse's health, difficult marriage if afflicted. 9: fortune via spouse, foreign spouse. 10: career via partnerships. 11: gains via spouse. 12: spouse abroad, secret relationships.

## 8th lord
1: long life if strong. 2: inheritance/insurance wealth. 3: sibling trouble. 4: mother's illness. 5: children issues. 6: Viparita — disease destroyed. 7: spouse troubled. 8: long life, occult. 9: father's trouble. 10: career obstacles. 11: sudden gains. 12: Viparita — moksha.

## 9th lord
1: fortunate, dharmic. 2: father's wealth. 3: fortune via effort. 4: property, harmony. 5: children fortunate. 6: father's troubles. 7: fortune via spouse, foreign spouse. 8: father's illness. 9: fortunate, religious. 10: career fortune, honours. 11: gains via fortune. 12: fortune abroad.

## 10th lord
1: self-made career. 2: wealth via career. 3: communication career. 4: property/education/vehicle career. 5: career via intellect. 6: service career. 7: career via partnerships. 8: career obstacles, occult career. 9: fortune via career. 10: strong career, authority. 11: gains via career. 12: career abroad.

## 11th lord
1: gains via self-effort. 2: wealthy family. 3: gains via siblings. 4: gains via property. 5: gains via children/speculation. 6: gains via disputes/service. 7: gains via spouse. 8: sudden gains. 9: gains via fortune. 10: gains via career. 11: rich. 12: gains abroad, losses.

## 12th lord
1: expenses, foreign residence. 2: loss of family wealth. 3: loss via siblings. 4: loss of property. 5: loss via children. 6: Viparita — enemies destroyed. 7: spouse abroad. 8: Viparita — long life. 9: father abroad. 10: career abroad. 11: income abroad. 12: expenses, spiritual.
