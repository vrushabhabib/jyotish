## Structural rules
- **Kendras** (1,4,7,10) = Vishnu sthana, pillars. Kendra lords lose their natural nature: benefics owning kendras acquire blemish (kendradhipatya dosha — Jupiter/Venus/Mercury/waxing Moon), malefics lose their malefice.
- **Trikonas** (1,5,9) = Lakshmi sthana. Lords always auspicious regardless of natural nature.
- **Kendra lord + Trikona lord** connected (conjunct, mutual aspect, exchange) = **Raja Yoga**. The 1st is both, so lagna lord is the pivot.
- **Dusthanas** (6,8,12): lords are functional malefics. A planet in a dusthana suffers, *except* a dusthana lord in another dusthana (Viparita Raja Yoga) and the 6th for competitive careers.
- **Upachaya** (3,6,10,11): malefics improve results here over time.
- **Marakas** (2,7): their lords and planets in them are marakas — relevant in dasha only when longevity is otherwise judged short.
- **Yogakaraka**: one planet owning both a kendra and a trikona. Mars for Cancer & Leo lagnas, Venus for Capricorn & Aquarius, Saturn for Taurus & Libra.
- **Bhavat bhavam**: 10th from 10th = 7th also shows career; 9th from 9th = 5th; 4th from 4th = 7th.
- **A house is judged by**: sign, lord (placement, dignity, aspects), occupants, aspects on it, karaka, its condition in the relevant varga. Karaka *in* its own bhava harms it (karako bhava nashaya): Jupiter in 5th, Venus in 7th, Saturn in 8th, Sun in 9th, Mars in 3rd — softened by dignity.
