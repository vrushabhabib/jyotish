# Planets in houses, house lords in houses, two-planet conjunctions

Tradition: Parashari, Lahiri, whole-sign houses from Lagna. Rule shape for code: `planet P in house H` → base result; then apply modifiers in this order: (1) dignity of P (exalted/own/friend vs enemy/debilitated), (2) which houses P rules for this lagna (functional nature), (3) aspects/conjunctions on P, (4) combustion/retrogression, (5) the same placement in navamsa, (6) dasha — results surface mainly in P's dasha/antardasha.

General classical rules (apply before any single line below):
- Natural malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) do well in upachaya houses 3, 6, 10, 11 and harm the living relatives of kendra/trikona houses they sit in. Natural benefics do well in kendras and trikonas and are wasted in 6, 8, 12 (except Jupiter/Venus in 12, Mercury in 8 per some texts).
- A planet in its own or exaltation sign protects the house it sits in even if malefic; debilitated or in an enemy sign it harms even if benefic.
- Karaka in its own house can hurt the relative (*karako bhava nashaya*): Jupiter in 5 (children), Venus in 7 (spouse), Sun in 9 (father), Moon in 4 (mother), Mars in 3 (younger sibling) — applied when the karaka is alone and afflicted; contested rule. **Source read in the Sanskrit (Phaladeepika 15.26, 12 Nov 2026): Mantreshvara lists Sun in 9, Moon in 4, Mars in 3, Jupiter in 5, Venus in 7, Saturn in 8 and closes "so say others" - he reports the rule as another school's opinion, not as his own. Treat it as a minority view: never state it by itself.**
- Project calibration: one dignified occupant does not outvote three malefic influences on the same house.

Source basis for Part A: paraphrased consensus of BPHS (bhava chapters 12-23), Phaladeepika ch. 8 and Saravali ch. 30, written from working knowledge — these chapters were **not** re-read verse by verse this session (see Reliability notes). Part B is paraphrased from the BPHS chapter "Effects of the Bhava Lords" (ch. 24 in the e-text read this session: https://sanskritdocuments.org/brihatparashara (Sanskrit root text; English reading not yet re-verified) ). Part C is consensus of the classical two-planet conjunction chapters (Saravali ch. 15, Brihat Jataka ch. 14, Phaladeepika ch. 18), also from working knowledge.

Where the old texts address a male native, "wife" = spouse; where texts differ for a female chart (Stri-jataka chapters) it is noted as **F:**.
