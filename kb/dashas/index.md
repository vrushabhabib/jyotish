# Dasha systems and interpretation

## Vimshottari (default, 120 years)
Order & years: Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17. Starts from the lord of the Moon's nakshatra, with the elapsed portion deducted. Sub-periods (antar) proportional: `maha_years × antar_years / 120`. Pratyantar likewise.

### How to judge a dasha lord
1. **Houses owned** — trikona lord = good period; dusthana lord = trouble in that house's domain; kendra lord = mixed by natural nature.
2. **House placed in** — results manifest in that house's affairs. Lord in 6/8/12 = dasha of struggle unless Viparita.
3. **Dignity** — exalted/own = full results; debilitated = frustration unless Neecha Bhanga.
4. **Yogas** it participates in — a Raja yoga fruits in its planets' periods.
5. **Relation to lagna lord and to maha lord** (for antar) — friend/enemy modifies.
6. **Nakshatra lord** of the dasha lord — the dispositor at nakshatra level (KP emphasis).
7. **Position from the maha lord** — antar lord in 6/8/12 from maha lord = friction within the period.

### Sandhi
The last ~year of a maha dasha and first year of the next (dasha chhidra) is turbulent, esp. Mars→Rahu, Rahu→Jupiter, Jupiter→Saturn, Saturn→Mercury transitions. Matchmakers avoid marriage timing at sandhi.

### General flavour per maha lord (modulated by the rules above)
- **Ketu (7)**: detachment, sudden changes, spiritual pull, losses that free. Good if Ketu in 3/6/9/12 with benefic dispositor.
- **Venus (20)**: comfort, marriage, arts, vehicles, wealth. Long — often the "settling" period. Venus in 6/8/12 or debilitated = relationship and expense troubles.
- **Sun (6)**: authority, father, government, health of heart/eyes. Short and sharp.
- **Moon (10)**: mind, mother, public, travel, emotional life. Waxing strong Moon = popularity.
- **Mars (7)**: property, courage, conflict, siblings, surgery, energy. Real estate often acquired.
- **Rahu (18)**: ambition, foreign, technology, obsession, illusions; rise if Rahu in 3/6/10/11 or with yoga planets; fall/scandal if in 8/12 afflicted. Reads through its dispositor.
- **Jupiter (16)**: children, wisdom, wealth, dharma, teachers, expansion. Usually the most benign long period unless Jupiter is 6/8/12 lord (Gemini/Virgo/Capricorn/Aquarius lagnas — check).
- **Saturn (19)**: discipline, karma settled, slow gains, labour, chronic issues. Rewards structure. Saturn as yogakaraka (Taurus/Libra lagnas) = major rise.
- **Mercury (17)**: commerce, communication, learning, nervous system. Excellent for business if Mercury strong.

## Ashtottari (108 years) — used when Rahu is in a kendra/trikona from lagna lord (regional: Bengal, some South). Order: Sun 6, Moon 15, Mars 8, Mercury 17, Saturn 10, Jupiter 19, Rahu 12, Venus 21.
## Yogini (36 years, 8 yoginis) — popular in North/Nepal for timing events; cross-check only.
## Chara dasha (Jaimini) — rashi-based; used with Karakamsa and Arudha for career/status. Advanced; see K.N. Rao.
## Kalachakra — rarely used in practice; skip.

## Timing method (what the agent does)
Event = (natal promise) × (dasha activation) × (transit trigger).
- Marriage: 7L/Venus/Jupiter(f) dasha-antar + Jupiter transit on 7th/1st/5th/9th/11th from Moon or lagna or aspecting 7L, or Saturn/Jupiter double transit on 7th.
- Children: 5L/Jupiter periods + Jupiter transit on 5th/9th/1st.
- Career rise: 10L/9L/lagna lord/yogakaraka periods + Jupiter/Saturn transit on 10th.
- Property: 4L/Mars periods.
- Foreign: 12L/9L/Rahu periods + Rahu transit on 12th/9th.
- Health crises: 6L/8L/maraka periods + Saturn transit on 1st/8th from Moon (Sade Sati peak).
