# Doshas — formation and cancellation (parihara)

## Mangal (Kuja) Dosha
Mars in 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12 from **Lagna, Moon, or Venus** (South India also counts from Moon primarily; some traditions omit 2nd). Marriage friction, spouse's health, delayed marriage.
**Strength**: from Lagna > Moon > Venus. In 7th or 8th = strongest; 12th and 2nd = mild.
**Cancellations** (any 2–3 = effectively nil):
- Mars in own sign (Aries, Scorpio) or exalted (Capricorn) or Moolatrikona.
- Mars in Aries in 1st, Scorpio in 4th, Capricorn in 7th, Cancer in 8th, Sagittarius in 12th (per Muhurta Chintamani).
- Mars conjunct/aspected by Jupiter or Moon.
- Mars in Leo or Aquarius (Sun's / Saturn's signs, milder).
- Saturn or Rahu/Ketu in the same house as Mars, or in 1/4/7/8/12 — "matched malefic" cancels.
- Mars combust.
- Native's age > 28 (dosha weakens) — practical tradition.
- Both partners Manglik — cancelled by mutual matching.

## Kaal Sarp Dosha
All seven planets hemmed between Rahu and Ketu (clockwise Rahu→Ketu). Twelve named types by Rahu's house: Anant (1), Kulik (2), Vasuki (3), Shankhpal (4), Padma (5), Mahapadma (6), Takshak (7), Karkotak (8), Shankhachur (9), Ghatak (10), Vishdhar (11), Sheshnag (12). Not in BPHS — a later/regional construct, but universally asked about.
**Partial** if one planet is outside the axis or a planet is conjunct Rahu/Ketu at the boundary. **Effects**: delays, struggle in first half of life, sudden reversals; often also great drive and fame (many public figures have it). Judge by whether Rahu is in a good house with dignity.
**Cancellation**: Raja yoga in the chart, strong lagna lord, Jupiter in kendra, Rahu in 3/6/10/11 (upachaya).

## Pitru Dosha
Sun (or 9th house / 9L) afflicted by Rahu/Ketu/Saturn, esp. Sun+Rahu in 9th or 5th; Ketu in 9th. Ancestral karma; father's troubles, delays in progeny. Remedy: tarpan, Pitru Paksha shraddha, feeding Brahmins/crows, Gaya pind daan.

## Grahan Dosha
Sun+Rahu/Ketu (solar) or Moon+Rahu/Ketu (lunar) within ~12°. Ego/mind clouded; father/mother affected. Stronger if birth near an actual eclipse.

## Kemadruma (see yogas) — loneliness/poverty, cancelled easily.

## Shani doshas
- **Sade Sati / Dhaiya**: transit, not natal — see `transits/`.
- Saturn in 1/4/7/8/10 afflicted; Saturn aspecting Moon; **Vish yoga** (Saturn+Moon).
- **Shrapit** (Saturn+Rahu), **Shani-Mangal** (Saturn+Mars: accidents, harsh labour).

## Guru Chandal
Jupiter + Rahu (within same sign). Beliefs unorthodox, teachers disappoint, sometimes brilliance in unconventional fields. Jupiter+Ketu = detachment from dharma / deep spirituality.

## Angarak — Mars + Rahu. Accidents, blood, fire, anger, surgery. Watch Mars/Rahu dashas.

## Daridra — 11L in dusthana. Gains blocked; check 2L/11L dignity.

## Nadi Dosha (matching) — same nadi. See `matching/`.
## Bhakoot Dosha (matching) — 6/8, 2/12, 5/9 Moon signs. See `matching/`.

## Gandanta
Birth with Moon/Lagna in the last 3°20' of Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces or first 3°20' of Leo/Sagittarius/Aries (water→fire junction). Karmic knot; classically inauspicious for the parent of the same gender; remedy by Ganda-mool shanti. **Mool nakshatra births** (Ashwini, Ashlesha, Magha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Revati) — regional shanti performed on the 27th day.
