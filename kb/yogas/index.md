# Yogas — formation, strength, cancellation

Rule: a yoga is only as strong as its planets. Check dignity, combustion, dusthana placement, and whether the yoga repeats in D9. Yoga fruits arrive in the dasha/antar of the yoga-forming planets.

## Raja Yogas (power, status)
- **Kendra–Trikona**: lord of a kendra + lord of a trikona conjunct / mutual aspect / exchange (parivartana). Strongest with lagna lord, 9L, 10L.
- **Dharma-Karmadhipati**: 9L + 10L together. The premier Raja yoga.
- **Viparita Raja**: 6L/8L/12L placed in 6/8/12 (other than own). Rise through others' fall or through crisis. Sudden.
- **Neecha Bhanga Raja**: debilitated planet cancelled (see below) — late but large rise.
- **Parivartana** (exchange): Maha (two good houses), Khala (one is 3rd), Dainya (one is 6/8/12). Dainya exchange = struggle.
- **Rahu/Ketu** in kendra/trikona with a kendra/trikona lord = Raja yoga (Rahu especially in 10th).

## Dhana Yogas (wealth)
- 2L, 11L, 5L, 9L, lagna lord interlinked. 2L+11L strongest.
- Lakshmi: 9L in kendra/trikona in own/exalted, lagna lord strong.
- Chandra-Mangala: Moon + Mars conjunct/aspecting — wealth via enterprise, also ties to mother/emotional volatility.
- Jupiter in 2nd/5th/9th/11th strong.

## Pancha Mahapurusha (one planet in own/exalted sign in a kendra from lagna)
- **Ruchaka** (Mars): commander, athlete, property, courage.
- **Bhadra** (Mercury): intellect, commerce, eloquence.
- **Hamsa** (Jupiter): wisdom, respect, spiritual authority.
- **Malavya** (Venus): luxury, beauty, vehicles, arts.
- **Shasha** (Saturn): authority over masses, land, discipline, long career.
Diminished if combust or in a Rahu-Ketu axis.

## Lunar / Solar yogas
- **Gaja Kesari**: Jupiter in kendra from Moon (best if both strong). Fame, wisdom, lasting reputation.
- **Sunapha / Anapha / Durudhara**: planet (not Sun) in 2nd / 12th / both from Moon. Support around the Moon = comfort, wealth.
- **Kemadruma**: no planet (excl. Sun, Rahu, Ketu) in 2nd or 12th from Moon, and Moon not with any planet. Loneliness, struggle. **Cancelled** if Moon in kendra from lagna, or aspected by/with Jupiter, or a planet in kendra from Moon.
- **Adhi**: benefics in 6/7/8 from Moon — minister/leader.
- **Budha-Aditya**: Sun + Mercury (nearly universal; only significant if Mercury not combust within 3° — then intellect, communication skill).
- **Vesi / Vosi / Ubhayachari**: planet in 2nd / 12th / both from Sun.

## Neecha Bhanga (cancellation of debilitation) — any one:
1. Lord of the debilitation sign is in a kendra from lagna or Moon.
2. Lord of the exaltation sign of that planet is in a kendra from lagna or Moon.
3. The debilitated planet is aspected by/with its exaltation-sign lord or its debilitation-sign lord.
4. The planet that gets exalted in that sign is in a kendra from lagna/Moon.
5. Debilitated planet is exalted in D9 (vargottama-type strength).
6. Debilitated planet is retrograde (partial).

## Arishta / affliction yogas
- **Kaal Sarp**: all planets between Rahu and Ketu (see `doshas/`).
- **Shakata**: Jupiter in 6/8/12 from Moon — fluctuating fortune.
- **Daridra**: 11L in 6/8/12.
- **Balarishta**: Moon in 6/8/12 afflicted by malefics, weak lagna — early childhood health risk (classical; read with caution).
- **Grahan** (eclipse) yoga: Sun or Moon with Rahu/Ketu.
- **Guru Chandal**: Jupiter with Rahu — ethical ambiguity, unconventional beliefs; Jupiter with Ketu — spiritual detachment.
- **Angarak**: Mars + Rahu — accidents, aggression, surgery.
- **Vish**: Saturn + Moon — depression, delayed emotional maturity, endurance.
- **Shrapit**: Saturn + Rahu — karmic debt, delays.

## Sanyasa / Pravrajya yogas
Four or more planets in one house (esp. with Saturn strong), Saturn aspecting lagna lord unaspected by others, Moon in Saturn's drekkana aspected by Saturn/Mars — renunciation or a life of disciplined service.

## Nabhasa yogas (pattern of all planets — 32 types)
Rajju (all in movable), Musala (fixed), Nala (dual), Yava, Vajra, Kamala, Vapi, Shakata, Gada, etc. Mentioned for completeness; low priority in modern practice except Kamala (all in kendras — great fame) and Vajra/Yava.

## Career-specific yogas
- **Amala**: benefic in 10th from lagna or Moon — clean reputation, lasting fame.
- **Parvata / Kahala / Chamara**: classical honour yogas; see BPHS ch. 36.
- Sun/Jupiter/Saturn/Mercury strong in 10th or 10L in kendra = leadership; Mars+Saturn in 10th = engineering/technical/military; Venus in 10th = arts, luxury goods, hospitality; Rahu in 10th = foreign / unconventional / tech / mass-market.
