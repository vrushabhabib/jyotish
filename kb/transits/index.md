# Gochar (transits)

All transits judged **from Moon sign** primarily (Chandra lagna), then from Lagna. Confirm with Ashtakavarga bindus (>4 in the transited sign = good, <3 = bad) and Vedha (obstruction points).

## Favourable transit houses from Moon (classical)
Sun: 3,6,10,11 · Moon: 1,3,6,7,10,11 · Mars: 3,6,11 · Mercury: 2,4,6,8,10,11 · Jupiter: 2,5,7,9,11 · Venus: 1,2,3,4,5,8,9,11,12 · Saturn: 3,6,11 · Rahu/Ketu: 3,6,11 (10 debated).

## Sade Sati (Saturn transiting 12th, 1st, 2nd from Moon) — ~7.5 years, once every ~30 years
- **Phase 1 (12th from Moon, rising)**: expenses, loss of sleep, plans unsettle, moving/travel. Financial drain.
- **Phase 2 (over Moon, peak)**: mental pressure, health, family, reputation tests, hardest; but also *karmic settlement* — many achieve their defining work here through sustained effort.
- **Phase 3 (2nd from Moon, setting)**: family and finance strain, then gradual recovery; consolidation.
- **Small panoti / Dhaiya**: Saturn in 4th (Ardhashtama) or 8th (Ashtama) from Moon — 2.5 years each; Ashtama is the harsher (accidents, obstacles, losses).
- **Modifiers**: Saturn is yogakaraka or well-placed natally (own/exalted, in 3/6/11 natally) → Sade Sati brings discipline and gains, not ruin. Moon strong (waxing, own/exalted, Gaja Kesari) → cushioned. Concurrent bad dasha → severe. Saturn transiting a sign with high Ashtakavarga bindus → mild.
- Rising & setting phases are usually milder than the peak; the peak's worst stretch is when Saturn is within ±3° of natal Moon.
- Remedies: Shani mantra (Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namah), Saturday oil/black sesame/black urad daan, Hanuman Chalisa, service to the elderly/labourers, iron ring (some), blue sapphire *only* if Saturn is a functional benefic (rare; must test).

## Jupiter transit (~1 year per sign)
Good in 2,5,7,9,11 from Moon: marriage (7), children (5), wealth (2,11), fortune (9). Jupiter on 1st = weight gain, new beginnings after expansion. Jupiter in 3,6,8,10,12 = its house of blessing is blocked; in 8th (Ashtama Guru) = expenses/hidden. Jupiter's aspect (5,7,9) on natal planets also fruits.

## Rahu–Ketu transit (~18 months per sign)
Rahu on natal Moon/lagna = confusion, obsession; on 10th = career upheaval/foreign work; on 12th = foreign travel; Ketu on 7th = detachment in relationships. The axis over 1/7 or 4/10 marks major turning points.

## Double-transit rule (K.N. Rao)
An event manifests when **both** Jupiter and Saturn (by transit, position or aspect) touch the relevant house and/or its lord, during a supportive dasha. Marriage: 7th & 7L. Child: 5th & 5L. Job: 10th & 10L. Property: 4th & 4L.
