# Full Reading Protocol — the default for every chart

Status: DEFAULT. Built 4 Oct 2026 from the Hoang Anh reading (13 parts, verified against her life, errors audited); updated same day with the business/investment specification, the language rule, and the lessons of Vrushab's own 13-part reading (verified 7/8 on an 8-item check). When Vrushab shares a horoscope, a PDF, or a date/time/place, produce the reading in THIS form unless he asks for something shorter. Read `kb/dashas/antar-event-rules.md` with this file.

## 0. Intake and verification (before any interpretation)
1. Extract name, sex, date, time (24h), place, lat/lon, tz, time confidence (record / memory / estimate). Ask one question: is the person employed, in business, or both? (It decides how Part 6 is written.) Ask the sibling count and birth order at intake — the 3rd/11th houses are easy to confirm and it is a free verification point.
2. Build the chart with the engine; cross-check lagna degree, Moon nakshatra-pada, dasha balance against any source document. >1° mismatch → stop and resolve. Take Shadbala, SAV/BAV, Bhava Bala, D7/D9/D10, Jaimini karakas, KP sub-lords from the source PDF if present. Save `data/charts/<name>_<date>.json`.
3. Compute and record: lagna distance to sign boundary (minutes of time it represents); D2 (hora) per planet; D30 (trimsamsa) per planet; D4 if property matters; waning/waxing and tithi; Arudha Lagna, Darapada (A7), Upapada (UL) with the 1st/7th exceptions; A10; 2nd and 8th from UL; 7th from Moon and Venus; 5th from Moon and Jupiter; 10th from lagna, Moon and Sun; 10th from Venus and Jupiter; derived parent houses (father's wealth = 10th, mother's wealth = 5th, maternal relatives = 6th, paternal siblings = 11th); Dhana/Lakshmi/Viparita/Chandra-Mangala/Mahapurusha yogas with strength. **Write out each planet's two house rulerships before writing a single line and check every "X lord" phrase against that list** (error caught: "Jupiter joins the 11th lord to the 2nd lord" in a Makara chart where Jupiter rules 3/12).
4. Compute transits for the next 25 years (Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu/Ketu ingress dates). pyswisseph is often unavailable: use the Keplerian-elements script (±2 weeks) and SAY the tolerance. Ingress dates are universal — reuse the dated table across charts and only re-place them in the new chart's houses. Derive Sade Sati from Saturn's ingress into 12th/1st/2nd from Moon, never from memory. Note exact-degree crossings of transiting Saturn/Rahu/Ketu over natal planets (they date the sharpest months).
5. Verify ALL antardasha dates by arithmetic (MD years × AD lord years / 120) before quoting any; compute pratyantars for the current AD. Keep one dated table and quote only from it.
6. BIRTH-TIME TEST when the lagna is near a boundary: send 4 questions where Lagna-A and Lagna-B give opposite answers, plus 4 dated life periods. When the time is confirmed and the lagna is mid-sign, still send an 8-item dated check (one per major sub-period) as Part 1 and record the score in the document. Ask the items ONE AT A TIME with yes/partly/no; a batch invites vague answers. Record hits and misses visibly. Only then write the future.

## 1. Delivery form
- **Language: English only by default.** Translate (Vietnamese or any other language) ONLY when Vrushab asks for it for that person. Never add a translation on your own. When bilingual: the full English document first, then the full translation, in one file.
- **Delivery: one PDF of all 13 parts by default** (markdown source → pandoc → wkhtmltopdf with DejaVu fonts for diacritics; A4, TOC). Keep the markdown source in outputs alongside the PDF so corrections are one edit and a rebuild. Chat-part delivery only if asked.
- 13 parts, each self-contained. Each part has: **The astrology** (every factor named and placed) → **In plain words** → **The years** (period by period with dates) → **rules for you**.
- Headings are PART n. TITLE. No sub-lettering (no 10A/10B); an add-on is "PART n, ADDITION. TITLE".
- Parts: 1 How this was made and checked (with the verification table and its recorded score) · 2 Chart at a glance · 3 Nature (full depth: lagna+lord+star, Sun, Moon and every planet with it, the two weakest planets, nodes, AL, D9 lagna, named strengths/weaknesses, what others see / what intimates see) · 4 Past timeline (dasha-dated, for verification) · 5 Now (current AD with its pratyantars month by month, every live transit incl. exact crossings) · 6 Career, business & investment (see §2) · 7 Money & property · 8 Health · 9 Family · 10 Marriage & love (+ addition: where the differences will be, and what holds) · 11 Children · 12 Long view (year by year with age) · 13 Uncertainties, spiritual note, remedies (remedies repeat each part's rules once).
- Corrections found later go into the source AND the PDF is rebuilt; a short standalone correction message goes to the person if they already have the old version.

## 2. Part 6 — Career, Business & Investment (the most detailed part for anyone in business)
For an employee: career blocks A–D. For a business owner or anyone who asks: ALL blocks A–L, each at full depth. Never summarise a business chart into "suits trade"; name the type, the product, the structure, the market, the money, the timing.

**A. Employment vs own business — decide it from the chart, state the verdict and the evidence.** Own business: strong lagna/lagna lord, strong Mars/Sun, 10L in 3/7/11 or with 7L/11L, Chandra-Mangala, Mercury strong and unafflicted, 3rd/7th/11th emphasis, Rahu in 10/11/7 (unconventional enterprise), D10 lagna lord in kendra/own sign, Sun/Mars strong in D10. Employment: 10L/Saturn in kendra in own sign without 7/11 links, 6th emphasis, D10 planets in the 6th, weak lagna lord, Moon/Mercury in 6th. Mixed charts: say which years favour which (own venture in ADs of 3L/7L/11L/lagna lord; employment in ADs of 6L/10L/Saturn).
**B. The line of work and the product/service.** From the 10th from lagna, Moon AND Sun (all three signs and their lords), the D10 lagna lord and strongest D10 planet, the Amatyakaraka, the 2nd/10th-house nakshatra deities. Map planets to trades: Sun = authority, government, gold, leadership, energy; Moon = liquids, FMCG, hospitality, the public, shipping, dairy, textiles; Mars = manufacturing, engineering, land, metals, defence, surgery, sport, real estate; Mercury = trade, commerce, writing, media, IT, accounts, agencies, brokerage, retail; Jupiter = finance, law, education, consulting, religion, publishing, banking; Venus = luxury, beauty, fashion, food & drink, arts, hospitality, vehicles, entertainment, women's products; Saturn = labour, oil, mining, construction, agriculture, old/antique, iron, infrastructure, long-term service; Rahu = foreign trade, import/export, technology, aviation, chemicals, intoxicants, unconventional, speculation; Ketu = spiritual, pharma, research, healing, hidden services. Signs by element: fire = energy, metal, leadership; earth = land, agriculture, manufacturing, finance; air = communication, tech, services, trade; water = liquids, coast, shipping, hospitality, chemicals. 8L strong in lagna = transformation/distillation/legacy; Chandra-Mangala in a water sign = trade in liquids; Jupiter own-sign with the stellium = protected origin / legitimacy; Ketu in 9th in Hasta = ancestral craft taken abroad (worked example: Vrushab). State the ONE best line and the two acceptable ones.
**C. Structure and partnerships.** 7th house and lord for partners (sole vs partnership; partner's nature from 7th sign/lord/occupants; A7); 11th for investors and networks; 3rd for co-founders/siblings in business; 6th for employees and disputes; 8th for other people's money, insurance, secret partners; Rahu/Ketu on the 1–7 or 2–8 axis = dilution, hidden terms. **7L conjunct Rahu within a few degrees = every partner's stated position exceeds the real one; verify by deeds, date every promise** (verified). When a partner's chart exists, run `engine/matching.py` business synastry and report points AND verdict. State in plain words whether to take a partner, an investor, or stay sole, and in which years.
**D. Money in the business.** Capital source: 2nd (own), 8th (loans, inheritance, partner's), 11th (investors), 6th (debt), 9th (father/backers), 12th (foreign capital). D2 hora count. Dhana links (2L–11L, 5L–9L, with lagna lord). Cash-flow nature by 11th condition. Profit vs turnover (11th vs 10th); **10th strong by SAV with 2nd/11th weakest = reputation strong, retention weak — prescribe a money-keeper who is not the owner.** Which planet's periods bring capital in, which take it out. Rule: never advise borrowing in an AD of a weak 6L/8L/12L; never advise capital expansion in Sade Sati peak unless 10th/11th are activated by benefic double transit. **A sub-period of a planet that rules BOTH 2nd and 11th, placed in an enemy's sign, coinciding with the Sade Sati onset = read as collapse of the gains house, not "money tight" (verified: Covid destroyed the business in Ven/Sat 2020–23; I understated it).**
**E. Investment and speculation.** 5th house and lord (speculation, stock market, bets), 9th (long-term fortune), 2nd/11th (accumulation), 4th (land/property), 8th (insurance, hidden assets, crypto/unconventional via Rahu), 12th (foreign assets). Verdict per asset class: property, equities, business reinvestment, gold, foreign assets, speculative. Give the years to invest (ADs of 2L/5L/9L/11L with Jupiter/Saturn transits on 2/5/9/11; Ashtakavarga bindus of the transit sign) and the years to hold cash (ADs of 6L/8L/12L, Ketu ADs, Rahu over 2nd/5th). Name the typical loss pattern of the chart (Ketu in 2nd = lumps; Rahu in 5th = speculation; 12L strong = foreign leakage; 6L in 2nd = contracts/disputes).
**F. Location and market.** Home vs abroad from 4th, 9th, 12th and their lords, Rahu's aspects, 10L placement; export/import if 12th/9th/Rahu touch the 10th/11th. Direction by sign of the 10th and of the lagna lord (Aries/Leo/Sagittarius east; Taurus/Virgo/Capricorn south; Gemini/Libra/Aquarius west; Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces north) and by planet (Sun east, Venus southeast, Mars south, Rahu southwest, Saturn west, Moon northwest, Mercury north, Jupiter northeast). Market type: mass vs premium (Moon/Saturn vs Venus/Jupiter), B2B vs B2C (Saturn/Mercury vs Moon/Venus), government vs private (Sun vs Mercury), online/tech vs physical (Rahu/Mercury vs Mars/Saturn). Say which of home market / foreign market / both, and which direction from the birthplace, and admit the chart cannot name a country. Mars debilitated in the D10 9th = far-country execution is the weak point → appoint a verified local operator.
**G. Branding, visibility and reputation.** Arudha Lagna and its lord (how the business is seen), A10 (arudha of the 10th), Sun and Venus in D10, 3rd house (marketing, communication), Mercury condition (contracts, messaging). Note litigation and reputation-risk periods (6th/8th/12th ADs, Rahu over 10th/AL).
**H. Team, labour, operations.** 6th house (staff, service providers, disputes), Saturn (labour, process, discipline), Mars (execution), 3rd (sales effort). Risk of employee theft/fraud (Rahu/Ketu with 6L, 12th afflicted). Three planets crowded in the D10 5th = planning argues with itself → one written annual plan.
**I. Regulation, government, licences.** Sun and the 10th/9th; Saturn (compliance, delay); 8th/12th for tax and penalties; 6L in 2nd = paperwork errors. Periods when licences come easily and when they are blocked.
**J. Risk profile and failure pattern.** Name the chart's specific way of failing in business (over-expansion, partner betrayal, speculation, legal, health, over-work) from the weakest house/planet, and the ADs when it is most likely. Give the guard-rail for each.
**K. The years.** Business timeline AD by AD for the current and next MD with transits: when to start, when to expand, when to take investment, when to partner, when to buy property for the business, when to export, when to hold, when to exit or sell; the career peak (double transit on the 10th); the Saturn return; nodal returns; Rahu over natal Mercury = sign nothing.
**L. Rules for the business.** Five to eight plain operating rules derived from the chart, repeated once in Part 13.

## 3. Completeness procedure (per part)
List and tick before writing: occupants; lord's placement, dignity, retrogression, combustion; EVERY aspect on the house and on the lord (Mars 4/7/8, Jupiter 5/7/9, Saturn 3/7/10, Rahu/Ketu 5/7/9); natural karaka; chara karaka; the same house from Moon and Sun (from Venus for marriage, from Jupiter for children/husband); the relevant varga; KP sub-lord; SAV/BAV; cross-links found in earlier parts; what the master doc / people file already says. **A feature omitted is the same class of error as a feature invented — and a feature softened out of caution is the same error too.** Never tilt toward anyone. Counter-indications that cut against the person's hopes (e.g. Ketu transiting the 7th during a Jupiter-in-7th window; the alcohol rule for a distiller with Rahu-Moon + Venus in 12th) must be stated.

## 5. Honesty and purity rules
- State the verification error rate and say it applies forward. Least reliable: sex of a child; another person's character from this chart; the exact country of a market; business specifics read with knowledge of the person's trade (say so).
- Purity: a reading sent to a third person describes THEIR chart only — never Vrushab's situation, current events between them, or his hopes. A reading of Vrushab's own chart shared with a third person likewise names no other person.
- Health lines are tendencies for a doctor; remedies are tradition; business timing is not a guarantee of returns and never replaces due diligence, accounts or a lawyer. Don't predict death.
- Spiritual beliefs the person states: map the placements that carry them respectfully; the chart confirms the person, not the story.
