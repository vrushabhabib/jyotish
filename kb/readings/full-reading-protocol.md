# Full Reading Protocol — the default for every chart

Status: DEFAULT. Built 4 Oct 2026 from the Hoang Anh reading (13 parts, verified against her life, errors audited); updated same day with the business/investment specification and the language rule. When Vrushab shares a horoscope, a PDF, or a date/time/place, produce the reading in THIS form unless he asks for something shorter. Read `kb/dashas/antar-event-rules.md` with this file.

## 0. Intake and verification (before any interpretation)
1. Extract name, sex, date, time (24h), place, lat/lon, tz, time confidence (record / memory / estimate). Ask one question: is the person employed, in business, or both? (It decides how Part 6 is written.)
2. Build the chart with the engine; cross-check lagna degree, Moon nakshatra-pada, dasha balance against any source document. >1° mismatch → stop and resolve. Take Shadbala, SAV/BAV, Bhava Bala, D7/D9/D10, Jaimini karakas, KP sub-lords from the source PDF if present. Save `data/charts/<name>_<date>.json`.
3. Compute and record: lagna distance to sign boundary (minutes of time it represents); D2 (hora) per planet; D30 (trimsamsa) per planet; D4 if property matters; waning/waxing and tithi; Arudha Lagna, Darapada (A7), Upapada (UL) with the 1st/7th exceptions; 2nd and 8th from UL; 7th from Moon and Venus; 5th from Moon and Jupiter; 10th from lagna, Moon and Sun; 10th from Venus and Jupiter; derived parent houses (father's wealth = 10th, mother's wealth = 5th, maternal relatives = 6th, paternal siblings = 11th); Dhana/Lakshmi/Viparita/Chandra-Mangala/Mahapurusha yogas with strength.
4. Compute transits for the next 25 years (Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu/Ketu ingress dates). pyswisseph is often unavailable: use the Keplerian-elements script (±2 weeks) and SAY the tolerance. Derive Sade Sati from Saturn's ingress into 12th/1st/2nd from Moon, never from memory.
5. Verify ALL antardasha dates by arithmetic (MD years × AD lord years / 120) before quoting any. Keep one dated table and quote only from it.
6. BIRTH-TIME TEST when the lagna is near a boundary: send 4 questions where Lagna-A and Lagna-B give opposite answers, plus 4 dated life periods. Let the person answer cold. Record hits and misses visibly. Only then write the future.

## 1. Delivery form
- **Language: English only by default.** Translate (Vietnamese or any other language) ONLY when Vrushab asks for it for that person. Never add a translation on your own.
- 13 parts, sent one at a time, each self-contained. Each part has: **The astrology** (every factor named and placed) → **In plain words** → **The years** (period by period with dates) → **rules for you** → "Next part: …".
- Headings are PART n. TITLE. No sub-lettering (no 10A/10B); an add-on is "PART n, ADDITION. TITLE".
- Parts: 1 How this was made and checked · 2 Chart at a glance · 3 Nature · 4 Past timeline (dasha-dated, for verification) · 5 Now · 6 Career, business & investment (see §2) · 7 Money & property · 8 Health · 9 Family · 10 Marriage & love (+ addition: where the differences will be, and what holds) · 11 Children · 12 Long view · 13 Uncertainties, spiritual note, remedies.
- After approval: fill the person's Claude Doc section by section, export PDF. Corrections found later go out as short standalone messages AND into the doc.

## 2. Part 6 — Career, Business & Investment (the most detailed part for anyone in business)
For an employee: career blocks A–D. For a business owner or anyone who asks: ALL blocks A–L, each at full depth. Never summarise a business chart into "suits trade"; name the type, the product, the structure, the market, the money, the timing.

**A. Employment vs own business — decide it from the chart, state the verdict and the evidence.** Own business: strong lagna/lagna lord, strong Mars/Sun, 10L in 3/7/11 or with 7L/11L, Chandra-Mangala, Mercury strong and unafflicted, 3rd/7th/11th emphasis, Rahu in 10/11/7 (unconventional enterprise), D10 lagna lord in kendra/own sign, Sun/Mars strong in D10. Employment: 10L/Saturn in kendra in own sign without 7/11 links, 6th emphasis, D10 planets in the 6th, weak lagna lord, Moon/Mercury in 6th. Mixed charts: say which years favour which (own venture in ADs of 3L/7L/11L/lagna lord; employment in ADs of 6L/10L/Saturn).
**B. The line of work and the product/service.** From the 10th from lagna, Moon AND Sun (all three signs and their lords), the D10 lagna lord and strongest D10 planet, the Amatyakaraka, the 2nd/10th-house nakshatra deities. Map planets to trades: Sun = authority, government, gold, leadership, energy; Moon = liquids, FMCG, hospitality, the public, shipping, dairy, textiles; Mars = manufacturing, engineering, land, metals, defence, surgery, sport, real estate; Mercury = trade, commerce, writing, media, IT, accounts, agencies, brokerage, retail; Jupiter = finance, law, education, consulting, religion, publishing, banking; Venus = luxury, beauty, fashion, food & drink, arts, hospitality, vehicles, entertainment, women's products; Saturn = labour, oil, mining, construction, agriculture, old/antique, iron, infrastructure, long-term service; Rahu = foreign trade, import/export, technology, aviation, chemicals, intoxicants, unconventional, speculation; Ketu = spiritual, pharma, research, healing, hidden services. Signs by element: fire = energy, metal, leadership; earth = land, agriculture, manufacturing, finance; air = communication, tech, services, trade; water = liquids, coast, shipping, hospitality, chemicals. State the ONE best line and the two acceptable ones.
**C. Structure and partnerships.** 7th house and lord for partners (sole vs partnership; partner's nature from 7th sign/lord/occupants; A7); 11th for investors and networks; 3rd for co-founders/siblings in business; 6th for employees and disputes; 8th for other people's money, insurance, secret partners; Rahu/Ketu on the 1–7 or 2–8 axis = dilution, hidden terms. When a partner's chart exists, run `engine/matching.py` business synastry and report points AND verdict. State in plain words whether to take a partner, an investor, or stay sole, and in which years.
**D. Money in the business.** Capital source: 2nd (own), 8th (loans, inheritance, partner's), 11th (investors), 6th (debt), 9th (father/backers), 12th (foreign capital). D2 hora count. Dhana links (2L–11L, 5L–9L, with lagna lord). Cash-flow nature by 11th condition. Profit vs turnover (11th vs 10th). Which planet's periods bring capital in, which take it out. Rule: never advise borrowing in an AD of a weak 6L/8L/12L; never advise capital expansion in Sade Sati peak unless 10th/11th are activated by benefic double transit.
**E. Investment and speculation.** 5th house and lord (speculation, stock market, bets), 9th (long-term fortune), 2nd/11th (accumulation), 4th (land/property), 8th (insurance, hidden assets, crypto/unconventional via Rahu), 12th (foreign assets). Verdict per asset class: property, equities, business reinvestment, gold, foreign assets, speculative. Give the years to invest (ADs of 2L/5L/9L/11L with Jupiter/Saturn transits on 2/5/9/11; Ashtakavarga bindus of the transit sign) and the years to hold cash (ADs of 6L/8L/12L, Ketu ADs, Rahu over 2nd/5th). Name the typical loss pattern of the chart (Ketu in 2nd = lumps; Rahu in 5th = speculation; 12L strong = foreign leakage).
**F. Location and market.** Home vs abroad from 4th, 9th, 12th and their lords, Rahu's aspects, 10L placement; export/import if 12th/9th/Rahu touch the 10th/11th. Direction by sign of the 10th and of the lagna lord (Aries/Leo/Sagittarius east; Taurus/Virgo/Capricorn south; Gemini/Libra/Aquarius west; Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces north) and by planet (Sun east, Venus southeast, Mars south, Rahu southwest, Saturn west, Moon northwest, Mercury north, Jupiter northeast). Market type: mass vs premium (Moon/Saturn vs Venus/Jupiter), B2B vs B2C (Saturn/Mercury vs Moon/Venus), government vs private (Sun vs Mercury), online/tech vs physical (Rahu/Mercury vs Mars/Saturn). Say which of home market / foreign market / both, and which direction from the birthplace, and admit the chart cannot name a country.
**G. Branding, visibility and reputation.** Arudha Lagna and its lord (how the business is seen), A10 (arudha of the 10th), Sun and Venus in D10, 3rd house (marketing, communication), Mercury condition (contracts, messaging). Note litigation and reputation-risk periods (6th/8th/12th ADs, Rahu over 10th/AL).
**H. Team, labour, operations.** 6th house (staff, service providers, disputes), Saturn (labour, process, discipline), Mars (execution), 3rd (sales effort). Risk of employee theft/fraud (Rahu/Ketu with 6L, 12th afflicted).
**I. Regulation, government, licences.** Sun and the 10th/9th; Saturn (compliance, delay); 8th/12th for tax and penalties. Periods when licences come easily and when they are blocked.
**J. Risk profile and failure pattern.** Name the chart's specific way of failing in business (over-expansion, partner betrayal, speculation, legal, health, over-work) from the weakest house/planet, and the ADs when it is most likely. Give the guard-rail for each.
**K. The years.** Business timeline AD by AD for the current and next MD with transits: when to start, when to expand, when to take investment, when to partner, when to buy property for the business, when to export, when to hold, when to exit or sell; the career peak; the Saturn return; nodal returns.
**L. Rules for the business.** Five to eight plain operating rules derived from the chart, repeated once in Part 13.

## 3. Completeness procedure (per part)
List and tick before writing: occupants; lord's placement, dignity, retrogression, combustion; EVERY aspect on the house and on the lord (Mars 4/7/8, Jupiter 5/7/9, Saturn 3/7/10, Rahu/Ketu 5/7/9); natural karaka; chara karaka; the same house from Moon and Sun (from Venus for marriage, from Jupiter for children/husband); the relevant varga; KP sub-lord; SAV/BAV; cross-links found in earlier parts; what the master doc / people file already says. **A feature omitted is the same class of error as a feature invented.** Never tilt toward anyone.

## 4. Interpretation rules learned (verified against life events)
- Sambandha test for sub-periods: an event type is read in an AD/PD ONLY if the sub-lord occupies/owns/aspects/disposes the relevant house/lord/karaka (Venus/7th for love; 10th/10L/AmK for career; 2/11 for money; 5/5L/Jupiter for children; 4/4L for property; 3/7/11/lagna lord for ventures). No filler.
- Female chart husband karaka = Jupiter. Darakaraka describes the spouse and times Chara dasha only. Jupiter retrograde as husband karaka = second-approach marriage / a man known before.
- Sun in the 10th is the native's own authority first. Attribute strength to the father only after checking 9L and whether Saturn/Sun are combust or weak.
- 6th house = contest first, trouble second (scholarships, competition, service excellence, immunity; also debt, illness, maternal relatives, employees).
- 12th with exalted Venus = foreign life + private happiness + spending, not poverty. 7L in 12th in D1 and D9 = foreign spouse / residence abroad.
- UL in 9th = formal, sanctioned marriage; UL lord in 6th = contest/paperwork; 2nd from UL strong = lasts; 8th from UL empty = no break. A7 on AL = partner and public image coincide.
- Mangalya sthana (8th) for women = marriage longevity.
- Marriage timing = double transit on the 7th inside a linked sub-period; engagement often one AD earlier when Jupiter aspects UL or lagna.
- Children timing = double transit on the 5th inside the AD of the planet in the 5th from Moon/Jupiter or ruling the 5th; Saturn with 5L = 35+; Rahu on D7 lagna/5th + Jupiter in 6th = medical route / foreign birth; Moon+Mars in D7 12th = supervise pregnancy, said plainly.
- Health: D30 portions; weakest planet names the soft point; Sade Sati peak = don't-push-through period. Regime, not diagnosis.
- Money: D2 hora; 2L/11L link; 5th for speculation; 8th Rahu = in-laws/inheritance; 4th for property timing.
- Family: childhood dasha boundaries often match confirmed events (fostered until the Mars→Rahu change at 4y10m).
- Dates: never shift birth time to rescue an interpretation. Keep consistency across parts. Quote Saturn/Jupiter/Rahu returns.

## 5. Honesty and purity rules
- State the verification error rate and say it applies forward. Least reliable: sex of a child; another person's character from this chart; the exact country of a market.
- Purity: a reading sent to a third person describes THEIR chart only — never Vrushab's situation, current events between them, or his hopes.
- Health lines are tendencies for a doctor; remedies are tradition; business timing is not a guarantee of returns and never replaces due diligence, accounts or a lawyer. Don't predict death.
- Spiritual beliefs the person states: map the placements that carry them respectfully; the chart confirms the person, not the story.

## 6. Remedies by lagna (never by Moon sign)
- Strengthen the weakest functional benefic first; at most one stone; no stones for maraka lords or combust Saturn; pacify Saturn by service; Rahu remedy = plainness; Ketu in 2nd = speak before writing; Jupiter = respect a teacher; weekday observances per planet. For business charts add the Mercury/Jupiter practices (Wednesday/Thursday) and the rule set from Part 6L.
