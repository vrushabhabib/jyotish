## 2. The method the two texts share
Read together, both texts teach one procedure, and it is narrower and more disciplined than popular practice.

1. **Three things are judged for every matter: the house, its lord, its significator** - and the same again counted from the Moon (BPHS 7.39-43, 66.13; PD 15.20-25, 16.12). Parashara adds the Sun as a third reference when Sun, Moon and lagna are in three different signs (BPHS 74.19-20).
2. **A house is built by** its own lord and benefics joining or aspecting it, benefics in its kendras and trines, malefics in its 3rd, 6th and 11th; **and broken by** its lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th, combust, debilitated or in an enemy's sign, and by malefics in its 6th, 8th, 12th, 4th and trines (PD 15.1-7; BPHS 11, 50.4-6).
3. **A planet gives by its dignity on a fixed scale**: exalted full, moolatrikona three quarters, own sign half, a friend's a quarter, a neutral's an eighth, nothing when debilitated, combust or in an enemy's sign (BPHS 3.59-60; PD 4.7, 9.14-19). A retrograde planet is treated as strong even in debilitation; an exalted but combust planet as weak (PD 4.4, 9.20).
4. **Timing is by dasha first.** What a period is about comes from the lord's houses (BPHS 48; PD 20); whether it goes well comes from the lord's strength and placement (PD 20.14-20); the sub-period is judged by the sub-lord's own placement **and by its house counted from the dasha lord** - kendra, trine, 3rd, 11th good; 6th, 8th, 12th bad (BPHS ch. 52-60, in nearly every one of the 81 pairs).
5. **Transit is second and is read through Ashtakavarga**: "transit is looked into only until the Ashtakavarga is to hand" (BPHS 72.30-31); "where the points are more the transit is good even in the 6th, 8th or 12th" (PD 26.41). The Moon's sign is the chief reference for transits (PD 26.1).
6. **Nothing is said on one indication.** "It becomes clear when two or three agree" (PD 15.6, 14.1). When the yearly wheel and Ashtakavarga agree the result is full; when they differ, the stronger decides (BPHS 74.27-28).
7. **The chart is checked against the life**: "for those whose birth is unknown, the running dasha is known from the results being experienced" (BPHS 76.18); planets and time are to be computed "so that calculation and observation agree" (BPHS 3.8; PD 1.3).
8. **The texts state their own limit**: certainty about the course and length of life "is beyond Vasishtha and Brihaspati - how much more men" (BPHS 66.10-12); no reckoning of life-span before the age of twenty-four (BPHS 9.2).

The project's working rules - verify first, count evidence, log predictions, never state one indication alone - are therefore the texts' own method, not a modern caution added on top.

## 3. Where the two texts differ
| Subject | Parashara | Mantreshvara | What the project does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dasha of a house lord | one result by lordship: 11th lord "obstacles in gains", 3rd "bad", 12th "much hardship" (48.2-7) | every lord's dasha good when strong, bad when weak - 6th, 8th, 11th, 12th included (20.2-20) | lordship = the subject; strength = the outcome; cite both |
| Children in a woman's chart | 5th house (80.3) | 9th house (11.1) | read both, name the text |
| Great-person yogas | from the lagna, planet strong (75.1-2) | from the lagna and from the Moon (6.4) | lagna count is primary; Moon count labelled as Mantreshvara's |
| Moolatrikona of Venus | Libra 0-15° (3.54) | Libra 0-5° (1.7) | Parashara |
| Planets strong by night | Moon, Mars, Saturn (3.36) | Mars, Moon, Venus (4.1) | Parashara (used in Shadbala) |
| Combined Ashtakavarga threshold | above 30 good, 25-30 middling (72.3) | 28 the dividing line (23.20); also 30 / 25 (24.37) | print the number with both readings |
| Rahu's favourable signs | three lists (55.1; 57.68; 58.47) | Virgo, Pisces, Scorpio (19.16) | only Virgo is in all five lists; not used as a rule |
| Shrinatha, Kahala, Parvata, Lakshmi yogas | one set of definitions (ch. 36) | different definitions under the same names (6.21, 28, 35) | engine follows Parashara; catalogue notes the variant |
| "Significator in its own house harms it" | not stated as a rule | reported as what "others say" (15.26) | minority view, never stated alone. Its only verses are in the 15th-century Jataka Parijata (2.52, 2.72), and those two lists disagree with each other |
| Required strength of the Sun (Shadbala) | 390 = 6.5 rupas (27.32-33); Jataka Parijata 2.40 the same | - | **texts' figure used for ratio and rank; Raman's and AstroSage's 5.0 shown beside it for cross-checking only** |
| Ashtakavarga tables | three cells differ from the usual table (66.16-60) | - | the usual table is **Varahamihira's** (Brihat Jataka 9.1-7, all seven tables checked cell by cell); default stays Varahamihira, Parashara's is `variant="bphs"` |
| States of a planet | nine states and six moods (45.7-17) | - | Jataka Parijata 2.16-18 has a different list of ten; the worksheet prints Parashara's |
| Trine-sign portions | Jupiter 10°, Venus 15°, Mercury 15-20° (3.51-54) | Venus 0-5° (1.7) | Jataka Parijata 1.26-28: 20° for Sun, Venus, Saturn, Jupiter; Mercury 15-25°. Three texts, three lists - a dignity that rests on this boundary alone is not firm |
| Gulika | the sign rising in Saturn's eighth of the day; start or end not said (3.66-70) | fixed ghatis that fall just before the end (25.2) | both printed; "uncertain" stated when they give different signs |
| Share of result in an enemy's sign | nothing (3.59-60) | - | Varahamihira 20.11: "little"; same scale, one step apart |
| Dasha system | Vimshottari "the chief" (46.3, 46.14) | Vimshottari (ch. 19-21) | Varahamihira does not teach it at all (ch. 8: life-span dasha and a natural dasha). Vimshottari rests on Parashara and Mantreshvara |
| Which aspects count | all special aspects with graded strength (ch. 26) | "the 7th aspect alone is the chief one" (4.9) | Parashara's special aspects are kept; Mantreshvara's caution is noted beside them |
| Friendship used for results | five-fold combined friendship (3.57-58) | "natural friendship alone is the proper measure" (4.10) | the engine's strength arithmetic uses the five-fold table; Mantreshvara's preference is noted |

## 4. Where Parashara's own text is uneven
- Rahu's good signs are given three ways (above).
- 54.30-32 gives bad results for a good position, and 57.13 good results under the bad-position heading.
- The Ashtakavarga count-verse for Venus reads "two" where its own name-list gives three (66.35-37).
- The sub-sub-period, sukshma and prana chapters (61-63) drop every chart condition and go by the planet's natural character only; many verses of 62 and 63 are identical.
- The e-text of the Venus dasha (ch. 60) breaks off after the opening verses of the last sub-period.
- Chapters differ in voice (ch. 83 is a dialogue of Shiva and Parvati; ch. 81-82 are physiognomy). The text as transmitted is a compilation; that is a reason to prefer rules that recur across chapters over a rule that appears once.

## 6. What the reading corrected in the engine and the knowledge base
Calculation and data
- Moolatrikona of the Moon (Taurus 3-30°) and Mercury (Virgo 15-20°); both texts agree and the data had neither.
- The nodes' friendships set to PD 2.35; their dignities to BPHS 47.35-36.
- Chara dasha: the sign-strength step for two-lord signs (46.163); the ±1 year for an exalted or debilitated lord available as an option (46.165).
- Ashtakavarga: the Sanskrit differs from the standard printed table in three cells (two in the Moon's table, one in Venus's); both versions are now available. Measured on 1,500 random charts the two versions give a different total in 39% of signs, almost always by one point - so a sign sitting exactly on a threshold must not be over-read.
- Combined-table thresholds set to the verse (above 30 / below 25).
- Shadbala: Parashara does not double the Moon's fortnight strength (27.10-11) - default restored earlier today.
- Chara karakas in Parashara's order (32.13-16); his own list of good, bad and yoga-giving planets for each lagna (ch. 34).

Rules
- Sub-lord in the 2nd from the dasha lord is supportive, not adverse; only the 6th, 8th and 12th are.
- Serpent's curse, father's curse, gandanta, eclipse birth, Saturn's renunciation, Shrinatha, Lagnadhi, Kalpadruma, Kalanidhi, Bheri, Parvata, poverty and wealth combinations - each rewritten to its verse (list in `bphs-verse-readings.md` and the chapter files).
- Shakata wording; one false attribution to Phaladeepika removed; "significator in its own house" demoted to a minority view; nodes in transit "like the Sun".
- Remedy table: Mars 10,000 recitations; Rahu's metal is lead.
- Uses of the sixteen divisions restated in Parashara's words.

New in the worksheet (each from a verse)
- Sub-lord's house from the dasha lord beside every sub-period (BPHS 52-60).
- Which third of its period a planet delivers in (BPHS 47.3-4).
- Rays (BPHS 73) and the running Sudarshana year (BPHS 74).
- Livelihood pointer from lagna, Moon and Sun (PD 5.1; Parashara's method).
- "Parashara says" column for functional nature; husband described by the 7th (BPHS 80.22-25).

## 7. What the logged cases can and cannot say
- Ledger on 12 Nov 2026: 39 questions about the past, 62% fully right. All eight statements that placed an event in a sub-period whose lord had **no connection** to the matter were wrong. That is the one pattern in our own data strong enough to lean on, and it is exactly what the texts require (the sub-lord must be connected: BPHS 48.9-20, PD 10.13, 12.25, 15.30).
- New test today on the six dated events held privately (counts only; the events are not in this repository):

| Rule tested at the date of the event | Hits | Chance level (same test on 1,800 random dates) |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-lord not in the 6th, 8th or 12th from the dasha lord (BPHS 52-60) | 6 of 6 | 69% |
| Jupiter in a trine of the sign or navamsa sign of the house lord (PD 16.32) | 4 of 6 | 42% |
| The same with the sign alone | 1 of 6 | 25% |
| Mantreshvara's four transit triggers, at least two together (PD 16.31-35) - counted from the lagna | 4 of 6 | 58% |
| The same, at least three together | 1 of 6 | 20% |
| The same counted from the Moon, at least two | 3 of 6 | 66% |
| Sun in an own or exaltation sign of the dasha or sub-period lord (PD 20.38) | 2 of 6 | 32% |
| Sun in a debilitation or enemy sign of those lords (the events were mostly welcome ones) | 1 of 6 | 44% |

  Six of six against a 69% chance level happens by luck about one time in nine; four of six against 42% is no evidence at all. The transit-trigger track and the month trigger sit exactly at chance. They are coded (`engine/classical.py`) and the month trigger is printed in the worksheet marked tentative; the four-trigger track is **not** printed as a prediction. **Neither rule is confirmed. Neither is refuted.** With six events nothing can be. The honest position is unchanged: the calculation side is verified to the arc-second; the predictive side has a consistent method taken from the source, a small past-question record, and no matured forward prediction.
- What would settle it: forward statements logged before the event. Every reading from now on logs its dated statements with the rule that produced them, so that in a year each rule in section 2 has its own hit rate.

## 8. How common the new quantities are (so they are not over-read)
- Rays: average 22 over 1,500 random charts; 11% exceed 30; 5% fall below 12.
- Combined Ashtakavarga: 29% of signs are above 30 and 24% below 25 - "strong" and "weak" signs are ordinary; only the comparison between houses of one chart carries meaning (BPHS 72.6-8).
- Sub-lord "supportive" from the dasha lord: about 69% of the time by chance.
- Jupiter in a trine of a given sign or its navamsa sign: about 42% of the time.
- Yoga base rates were re-run after today's rule changes (`data/base_rates.json`).
- Moods (BPHS 45.11-17) over 1,200 random charts: "glad" on 68% of planets and "hungry" on 47% - these two say almost nothing; "proud" 11%, "ashamed" 4%, "thirsty" 1%, "agitated" 1%. One planet in seven is hungry or agitated with nothing to offset it; one chart in three has no such planet.
- Argala (BPHS 31): four houses in five have some unobstructed argala; 45% of charts have it on the 1st, 5th and 9th together (the verse's "ruler" combination) - not a distinction. A house with none (21%) or with benefics alone (13%) is the informative case.
- New combinations: "loss of money in a planet's own period" (42.13) fires in 68% of charts and the arudha pair of 39.23 in 56% - both near-useless alone; the lord of the Moon's navamsa with a maraka (42.10) in 32%; a raja yoga through the three lagnas (39.12) in 11%; the single-planet raja yogas of ch. 24 in 8-9% each; the rest under 6%.
- Gulika: Parashara's start-of-part and end-of-part readings name the same sign in only 24% of charts - which is why the worksheet prints both, with Mantreshvara's Mandi as the tie-breaker.
- The Sun's Shadbala rank: with the texts' 6.5 rupas in place of 5, the Sun's ratio falls by 23% in every chart. On the ten charts on file the printed figure made the Sun the strongest planet in nine; by the texts' figure it is the strongest in one. A rank that puts the same planet first in nine charts out of ten was never information. Any earlier statement that "the Sun is the strongest planet" taken from the printed rank must be re-checked against the new column.

What cannot be closed from a desk
1. **Jaimini's sutras and Saravali**: no clean free Sanskrit text found. If one is supplied, the same reading is owed to them.
2. **Jataka Parijata chapters 5-18** beyond the cited verses: unread. It is a late compilation, so nothing in the method depends on it, but the reading is not complete.
3. **Evidence.** Six dated events cannot separate any rule from chance. Only forward statements logged before the event, and more dated events given with consent, can turn "consistent with the sources" into "tested". The predictive side of this project is still unproven; the calculation side is verified.
