# Laghu Parashari (Ududaya Pradipa) - all 42 verses, read from the Sanskrit on 13 Nov 2026

Source: Sanskrit Wikisource, page "लघुपाराशरी" (https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/लघुपाराशरी), free text, author unknown. Read through the built-in browser; the English below is my own reading of the Sanskrit, not a copy of the Hindi gloss printed on the same page. Where the gloss and the Sanskrit differ this is said. Places I am unsure of are marked [?]. Not a critical edition.

This replaces every [MEM] line about Laghu Parashari in `kb/dashas/dasha-systems.md` section 1.

## Ch. 1 - Terms (v.1-5)
- 1 Invocation. 2 The work follows Parashara's hora and calls itself Ududaya Pradipa ("lamp on the star periods").
- 3 Results are given by the star-dasha method; **Vimshottari is to be used, Ashtottari is not accepted here.**
- 4 Houses and the rest are to be learnt from the general texts; only the special terms of this method are stated.
- 5 All planets aspect the 7th; Saturn, Jupiter and Mars also aspect the 3rd and 10th, the trines (5th, 9th), and the 4th and 8th respectively.

## Ch. 2 - Deciding good and bad (v.6-13)
- 6 **Lords of the trines all give good results; lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th give bad results.**
- 7 **Natural benefics owning kendras do not give good; natural malefics owning kendras do not give bad.** Each set is stronger in rising order (1st < 4th < 7th < 10th; 5th < 9th; 3rd < 6th < 11th).
- 8 The lords of the 12th and the 2nd give results according to the planets they keep company with and according to their other house.
- 9 The 8th lord does not give good, the 8th being the house of loss from the 9th; he does give good if he is himself the lagna lord.
- 10 The kendra-lordship blemish is strongest for Jupiter and Venus; so is their power as marakas when they stand in a maraka house.
- 11 Mercury comes after them, then the Moon. The 8th-lordship blemish does not attach to the Sun and the Moon.
- 12 Mars as 10th lord is good only when he also owns a trine, not from the 10th lordship alone.
- 13 **Rahu and Ketu give, strongly, the results of the house they occupy and of the house lord they are joined with.**

## Ch. 3 - Yogas (v.14-22)
- 14 Kendra and trine lords in mutual relation give special results when not tied to other [bad] houses.
- 15 Even if blemished themselves, a kendra lord and a trine lord become strong yoga-makers by relation alone.
- 16 The 9th and 10th lords in each other's houses, or together in one of them, or either one in the other's house: yoga-makers.
- 17 Any relation between a strong kendra lord and the two trine lords makes a good yoga.
- 18 The yoga mostly comes in the dashas of the two yoga-makers [second half of the verse, about a benefic's dasha lying between the two, is obscure - not used] [?].
- 19 Even planets malefic in themselves, when related to a yoga-maker, give the yoga's fruit according to the sub-period.
- 20 One planet owning a kendra and a trine is a yoga-maker; if also related to the other trine lord, nothing is better.
- 21 Rahu or Ketu in a kendra or trine, related to the lord of the other kind, become yoga-makers.
- 22 If the 9th and 10th lords are also the 8th and 11th lords, their relation alone does not give the yoga.

## Ch. 4 - Marakas (v.23-28)
Defines the 2nd and 7th as maraka houses and ranks the planets that end life. **Excluded from use by the project's rule (no statement about death or length of life); recorded only so the count is complete.**

## Ch. 5 - Dasha results (v.29-42)
- 29 **No planet gives the good or bad that belongs to its own nature in its own sub-period of its own dasha.** (The Sanskrit has the negative "na disheyuh"; the Hindi gloss on the page drops it.)
- 30 **A dasha lord gives its own result in the sub-periods of planets related to it and of planets of the same nature as itself.**
- 31 In the sub-periods of the others - unrelated and giving results opposed to the dasha lord - the result is to be reasoned from both.
- 32 In a kendra lord's dasha the sub-period of a trine lord gives good, and the reverse likewise - when the two are related; without relation it turns to bad [word order of the verse is loose] [?].
- 33 A raja yoga begins in the sub-periods of marakas and spreads step by step in the sub-periods of malefics.
- 34 [It grows] in the sub-periods of benefics related to the yoga-maker; in those of unrelated benefics things stay level.
- 35 In the dasha of a benefic related to them, the yoga-makers sometimes give the yoga's fruit in their own sub-periods.
- 36 Rahu and Ketu in good houses, related to no one, act as yoga-makers according to the sub-period.
- 37-38 **If the dasha lord is malefic: sub-periods of benefics not related to it give bad results; of benefics related to it, mixed results; of yoga-makers not related to it, very bad results.**
- 39 About marakas - excluded as ch. 4.
- 40 **Saturn and Venus exchange: in Saturn's dasha Venus's sub-period gives Saturn's results, and in Venus's dasha Saturn's sub-period gives Venus's, good or bad.**
- 41 Lagna lord and 10th lord in each other's houses: raja yoga, fame and victory. 42 The same for the 9th and 10th lords.

## What this text is and is not
It is a complete, short doctrine of DIRECTION: which planets are good or bad for a lagna (v.6-13) and how a sub-period turns out from the natures of the two lords and whether they are related (v.29-38). It has nothing on what a period is about, on strength, dignity, or transits.

## The rule taken from it for testing (written down BEFORE the pooled test was run)
Functional nature of a planet for the lagna: +1 for each trine it owns (1st, 5th, 9th), -1 for each of the 3rd, 6th, 11th, -1 for the 8th unless the planet also owns the lagna or is the Sun or Moon, 0 for the 4th, 7th, 10th, 2nd and 12th. Sum above zero = benefic, below zero = malefic, zero = neutral. Rahu and Ketu take the nature of the lord of the sign they occupy (v.13). "Related" = conjunction, mutual aspect or exchange.
