- v.25-27 Rahu: 1st short span [as worded], money and strength, illness of the upper body; 2nd guarded speech, mouth trouble, money through a ruler, comfortable; **3rd proud, opposed to brothers, firm mind, long life, wealthy**; 4th foolish, gives trouble, friends, sometimes happy; 5th nasal speech, hard-hearted, stomach trouble; **6th prosperous, long-lived, though troubled by rivals and harsh planets**; 7th loss of money through women, independent, small mind; 8th [hardship]; 9th contrary speech, head of a group or town; **10th famous, few children, busy with others' work, fearless; 11th prosperous, few children, long life, ear trouble**; 12th secret wrongdoing, much spending.
- v.28-33 Ketu: 1st ungrateful, unhappy, displaced; 2nd without learning and money, poor speech, eats at others' cost; **3rd long life, strength, money, fame, spouse, good food - loss regarding a brother**; 4th loss of land, vehicle, mother's comfort and birthplace, lives in another's house; 5th stomach trouble, poor judgment; **6th generous, excellent qualities, firm, famous, authority, crushes rivals, wishes fulfilled**; 7th humiliation, bowel trouble, separation from spouse; 8th [hardship], quarrel, wounds; 9th wrong inclination, the father's fortune lacking, want; 10th obstacles to good works, yet brilliant, brave and very well known; **11th stored wealth, many qualities, enjoyment, every aim achieved**; 12th secret wrongdoing, spending on low ends, eye trouble.
- **v.34 "A planet at the same degree as the rising degree gives its full result. Rahu gives like Saturn and Ketu like Mars."**
- **v.35 Planets at the same degree as the house give the house's result in full; more or fewer degrees, the result grows or shrinks in proportion.**

## Ch. 16 — Results of the twelve houses, 35 verses
- v.1 The body is like the lord of the lagna's navamsa, or the strongest planet; the complexion like the lord of the Moon's navamsa.
- **v.2 Lagna lord in a kendra or trine, bright, in exaltation or own sign; the 8th lord outside the kendras; benefics in the lagna: long-lived, wealthy, of honoured qualities, praised by rulers, good-looking, firm body, fearless, righteous, good family.**
- **v.3 Lagna lord linked with benefics: lives in a good place, in good company; with a strong planet: backing of a well-known ruler; in exaltation: a ruler himself; in own sign: stays in the birthplace. In a movable sign: travels; fixed: stays; dual: both.**
- v.4 Lagna lord bright and well placed: famous, happy, growing; badly placed: unhappy, in a poor place. A strong lagna: healthy and vigorous; a weak one: struck by troubles again and again.
- v.5-6 2nd lord in a chief house with benefics in the 2nd: family wealth, far-sighted. Learning by the planet linked with the 2nd lord: Sun - knowledge and money used for others; Saturn - small learning; Jupiter - sacred law; Mercury - economics; Venus - the arts of love and wit; Moon - some arts; Mars - harsh arts; Rahu - a stammer; Ketu - untruth; malefics - dull and poor.
- v.7-9 Lagna lord and 3rd lord strong and in each other's signs: courage, harmony with brothers and sisters, daring work. 3rd lord strong with benefics and the significator in a good house: brothers and sisters prosper.
- v.10-12 4th lord and Moon strong, with benefics, benefics in the 4th from each: the mother's well-being [the contrary is a loss wording - not used]. Lagna lord in the 4th, or 4th lord in the lagna, or the two linked, and likewise with the Moon: looks after the mother to the end; in mutual enemy or debilitation signs or 6th/8th: does not. "The same way for father, brothers and children - by the house, its significator, its lord, the lagna and the lagna lord."
- v.13-14 4th lord and Venus well placed and linked with the lagna or 4th: vehicles, authority, jewels, comforts, cattle. 4th lord badly placed with Mars or the Sun: the birth-house damaged by fire; with Rahu or Saturn: an old house; with the 6th lord: taken by others.
- v.15 5th in a benefic's sign and navamsa with a benefic, its lord in good divisions and a good house: intelligent and guileless.
- v.16-19 Rivals: malefics in the 6th, the 6th lord strong and linked with the lagna lord, the lagna lord in the 6th, or the 6th lord in a kendra with malefics - much trouble from rivals. **Lagna lord much stronger than the 6th lord, in a benefic's navamsa with benefic aspect, and the 4th lord strong in a kendra or trine: health, good fortune, a firm body.** The houses whose lords are joined with the 6th lord, or hold the 6th lord, or whose lords are in the 6th "become hostile".
- **v.20 7th linked with benefics and its lord strong: a devoted spouse with every good quality, with children.**
- v.21 8th lord outside the kendras and weaker than the lagna lord: no anxiety, no obstacle, no distress, long life.
- v.22-26 The father [loss and adoption wordings - not used]. **v.23 The Sun for a day birth or Saturn for a night birth well placed with benefic aspect, and the 9th lord strong: the father lives long.**
- **v.27-28 A benefic in the 10th, or the 10th lord strong in a kendra or trine in own sign or exaltation, or the lagna lord in the 10th: wide and honoured fame, engaged in right works, fortune like a ruler's. The Sun or Mars in the 10th: vigorous, popular, accomplishes great and forceful work - if the 10th lord is well placed. Benefics there: good and praised work. Rahu, Saturn or Ketu there: [wrong work].**
- **v.29 The house whose lord is joined with the 11th lord, or in which the 11th lord sits: gain of the things of that house.**
- **v.30 The house whose lord is in the 12th, or in which the 12th lord sits: loss of the things of that house.**
- **v.31 Timing. When the lagna lord in transit reaches a trine of the sign or navamsa occupied by the lord of a house, or that house itself; or the house lord reaches a trine of the sign or navamsa of the lagna lord, or the lagna; or the two join or aspect each other: that house's matter succeeds. The same with the significator's meeting with the lagna lord. Reckon from the lagna and from the Moon.**
- **v.32 When Jupiter in transit is in a trine of the sign or navamsa occupied by the lord of a house: that house bears fruit.**
- v.33-34 Whether a rival submits (lagna lord stronger than the 6th lord) and when rivalry or friendship with the person of a house arises (temporary enmity or friendship of the two lords in transit).
- **v.35 "When the lagna lord and the lord of a house come together - that is the time of that house's result": good if the house lord is strong, otherwise the reverse. From the lagna and from the Moon.**
