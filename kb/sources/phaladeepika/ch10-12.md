# Phaladeepika chapters 10–12 — marriage, women's charts, children (read from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

Guardrail: verses that state the death of a spouse, widowhood, childlessness, a child's sex or the number of children are not carried over; their existence is noted.

## Ch. 10 — The 7th house, 15 verses
- **v.1 The 5th and the 7th, counted from the lagna and also from the Moon, joined or aspected by their own lords or by benefics: good for both (children and spouse); otherwise neither succeeds.** [The second half - Venus hemmed by malefics, malefics in the 4th, 8th, 12th from Venus - is a loss wording, not used.]
- v.2-4 [Loss and separation combinations - not used as worded. Kept as: the 7th or its lord with malefics, hemmed by malefics, in a debilitation or enemy's sign, or combust, strains the marriage; Venus in a division of Mars or Saturn and aspected by them shows wandering attention.]
- v.5 Two planets in the 11th: two spouses. The 7th lord and Venus in a dual sign or navamsa: two marriages. The count goes by the planets joined with those two.
- **v.6 The number of spouses is judged from the planets in the 7th. A strong benefic 7th lord: a good spouse and good children. Even a malefic in its own sign in the 7th does good. Benefics in the 7th give happiness - unless they are the lords of the 6th, 12th or 8th.**
- v.7 The 2nd and 7th joined or aspected by malefics harm the marriage - "the harsh aspect especially"; the same from the wife's side for the 7th and 8th. Aspected and joined by benefics: both partners fortunate.
- v.8 [Remarriage and loss wordings - not used.]
- **v.9 The 7th house and its lord in even signs, Venus likewise, the lords of the 5th and 7th strong and not combust: spouse and children.**
- **v.10 The lords of the 2nd, 7th and 12th aspected by Jupiter and in kendras or trines; benefics in the 2nd, 7th and 11th from the 7th lord: a happy spouse with children.**
- v.11 The spouse's birth sign: a trine of the sign or navamsa of the lagna lord or the 7th lord, or its exaltation or debilitation sign; or the sign with the most points in the Moon's ashtakavarga. The same for a woman's husband.
- **v.12 The direction the spouse comes from: the sign and direction of the planet in the 7th, of the 7th lord, and of Venus. Timing: when Venus or the 7th lord in transit reaches a trine of the sign or navamsa of the lagna lord, marriage.**
- **v.13 In the dasha of a planet in the 7th, aspecting the 7th, or the 7th lord; and when the lagna lord in transit comes to the 7th sign - a spouse is gained.**
- **v.14 In the dasha of the stronger of: the lords of the sign and navamsa holding the 7th lord, Venus, and the Moon; with Jupiter in transit in a trine of the sign or navamsa holding the 7th lord - marriage.**
- v.15 7th lord in an enemy's or debilitation sign, combust or aspected by malefics; 7th with or aspected by malefics: "loss regarding the spouse, say the wise".
- Citation check: KB marriage rules cited to "Phaladeepika ch. 10" (timing by the dashas of the 7th lord, Venus and the Moon with Jupiter's transit; direction of the spouse) - **confirmed** at v.12-14. New from the reading and now in `kb/life-areas/marriage-spouse.md`: the transit of Venus or the 7th lord over the trines of the lagna lord's sign (v.12) and of the lagna lord over the 7th (v.13).

## Ch. 11 — Women's charts, 11 verses
- **v.1 "Whatever is said of a man's chart applies in full to a woman's." Particular to her: [the husband's life from the 8th]; children from the 9th; her looks from the lagna; the husband and her good fortune from the 7th; her associations and conduct from the 4th. Benefics there are good; malefics are bad unless they own the house.**
- v.2 Lagna and Moon in even signs aspected by benefics: good children, husband, ornaments, wealth, excellent conduct. In odd signs with malefics: mannish, [character wording].
- v.3 The 7th in a benefic's sign and navamsa: the husband has lustre, fame, learning and means; the reverse: [the opposite]. [The second half states widowhood and loss - not used.]
- v.4-7 [Few children with the Moon in Scorpio, Virgo, Taurus or Leo in the 5th; then the thirtieth-part character table as in Parashara 80.9-16 - **not used**.]
- v.8 Read the thirtieth-part from the stronger of the Moon and the lagna.
- v.9 [Birth-star statements that a woman harms a named in-law, as in Parashara 94.10-12 - **not used**.]
- v.10 The lords of the Moon's sign, the 7th, the lagna and the 9th with benefics in good houses and bright: honoured among her people, skilled in good works, beautiful, fortunate, dear to her husband, good children; [and a statement tied to the 8th].
- v.11 Timing of conception from the Moon's transit [not used].
- Citation check: KB "Phaladeepika gives children to the 9th in a female chart" - **confirmed** (11.1). Parashara gives the 5th (80.3). The two texts differ; a reading of a woman's chart checks both houses and says which text each comes from.

## Ch. 12 — Children, 34 verses
- **v.1 The 5th lord and Jupiter well placed from the lagna and from the Moon; the 5th joined or aspected by lords of good houses and by benefics; the lords of the lagna and the 5th together, in mutual aspect, or in each other's signs: children are assured.**
- v.2 [The contrary - not used as a statement.]
- **v.3 A malefic in its own sign in the 5th gives children; with another malefic, many.** [Harm wording for a benefic's sign with a harsh planet not used.] Scorpio, Taurus, Virgo and Leo are "signs of few children": with one of them on the 5th, few children, and later.
- v.4-9 [Combinations for few, late, adopted or no children - not used as statements. Kept as: a late child "with effort" is the text's own wording in v.4.]
- v.10-11, 13 [Sex and number of children - **not used**.]
- v.12 Conception is possible when, for a man, the Sun and Venus are strong in their own signs or navamsas in upachaya houses; for a woman, Mars and the Moon.
- **v.14 Fertility points: the sum of the longitudes of Jupiter, the Moon and Mars falling in an even sign and an even navamsa shows the woman's strength; the sum of the Sun, Venus and Jupiter falling in an odd sign and navamsa shows the man's; mixed gives mixed, "with effort".**
- v.15-18 A lunar-day method (five times the Moon's longitude less five times the Sun's) with the rite for each lunar day.
- v.19-24 The cause, by the afflicting planet, as a fault from a former life, with the remedy: recitation, gifts, good works; bathing at Setu, hearing sacred stories, worship of Shiva and Vishnu, vows, charity, ancestral rites, installing a serpent image.
- **v.25 Timing: in the dasha and sub-period of the lagna lord, the 7th lord, the 5th lord, Jupiter, or a planet aspecting or occupying the 5th; and when Jupiter in transit is in the sign of the 5th lord [or of Yamakantaka], or the sign of their navamsa.**
- **v.26 When the lagna lord in transit joins the 5th lord, or reaches its own or exaltation sign, or comes to the 5th sign or the sign holding the 5th lord.**
- v.27-28 In the sub-periods of planets in the 5th, aspecting it, or its lord; in the dasha or sub-period of the stronger of the 5th lord, Jupiter, and the lords of the signs and navamsas they occupy.
- **v.29 With Jupiter in transit in a trine of the sign or navamsa of Jupiter or of the 5th lord.**
- v.30-34 Further timing from the birth star and from the conception chart [not used].
