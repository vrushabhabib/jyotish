# Phaladeepika chapters 9, 14, 18, 19 — signs and dignities, illness, two planets together, results of the dashas (read from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

## Ch. 9 — Results of the signs and of dignity, 20 verses
- v.1-12 One verse for the lagna in each sign (build, habits, fortunes). v.13 "What is said for the lagna is to be said for the Moon's sign as well", fitted to the sign's nature.
- **v.14-19 A planet by dignity. In exaltation: owner of treasures, praised by rulers, a home of great wealth, noble qualities. In own sign: an established and steady position through a patron, new land and house in its period, regard from people, recovery of what was lost. In a friend's sign: success of work through friends, new friendships, good spouse, children, money and fortune, everyone's goodwill. In an enemy's sign: a lowered state, living on others and in another's house, want, trouble from rivals - "even a friend turns hostile". In debilitation: a fall in one's work, dejection, debt, low company, living in a poor place, service, pointless travel and effort. Combust: [loss], pointless quarrel, blame, humiliation. In a neutral's sign: no special result.**
- **v.20 "A retrograde planet gives the result of exaltation even in an enemy's or debilitation sign. A planet in a vargottama navamsa gives as if in its own sign."**

## Ch. 14 — Illness, 29 verses
- **v.1 Illness is judged from the planets in the 6th, those in the 12th and 8th, the 6th lord and the planets joined with it - "say it when two, three or more agree".**
- v.2-9 A list of ailments and fears for each planet, Sun to Ketu and Gulika. [Recorded as existing; the project does not diagnose. General headings only are kept in `kb/life-areas/health-longevity.md`: Sun heat, fever, heart, eyes, bones; Moon sleep, phlegm, cold fevers, digestion, blood, the mind; Mars thirst, blood and bile, burns, wounds, the eyes; Mercury confusion, speech, throat and nose, skin; Jupiter the abdomen, ears, sorrow; Venus the urinary and reproductive system, eyes, wasting; Saturn wind and phlegm, the feet and legs, fatigue, the side of the body; Rahu the heart, skin, feet; Ketu and Gulika likewise.]
- v.10-11 Combinations for the eyes, ears, stomach and other parts.
- v.12-21 The cause and manner of death [**not used**].
- v.22-29 The next world and the former life [**not used**].
- Citation check: KB "Phaladeepika 14.2" (the Sun's ailments) - **confirmed** as to content; the rule kept from this chapter is v.1, the agreement of two or three indications.

## Ch. 18 — Two planets together, 17 verses
- v.1 The Sun with: the Moon - a maker of machines or stone-work; Mars - [given to wrong]; **Mercury - skilled, intelligent, famous and happy**; Jupiter - harsh, busy with others' work; Venus - earns by the stage or by arms; Saturn - skilled with metals or vessels.
- v.2 The Moon with: Mars - deals in [liquor and pots], hard on the mother; Mercury - courteous speech, clever with money, fortune and fame; **Jupiter - valiant, first in the family, an unsteady mind, lord of wealth**; Venus - skilled in the cloth trade; Saturn - [a birth wording].
- v.3 Mars with: Mercury - a trader in roots and oils, a wrestler; **Jupiter - head of a town, a ruler, or a learned man with wealth**; Venus - a herdsman, wrestler, skilled, a gambler; Saturn - unhappy, untruthful, censured.
- v.4 Mercury with: Jupiter - a performer, fond of song and dance; Venus - eloquent, lord of land and of a group; Saturn - clever at deception. Jupiter with Venus - good learning, wealth, spouse, many qualities; with Saturn - a barber, potter or cook.
- v.5 Venus with Saturn - weak sight, wealth that grows through a woman, knows writing, books and painting.
- v.6-11 The Moon in each sign aspected by Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn and the Sun - six short results a sign. v.12-15 The Moon in the navamsas of each planet, so aspected. v.16-17 How to weigh sign and navamsa results (a strong navamsa lord overrides the sign result).
- Citation check: KB "Budha-Aditya" (Sun with Mercury: skill, intelligence, fame) - the classical basis is **v.1, confirmed**; it is present in about half of all charts, so it is never offered as a distinction.

## Ch. 19 — Results of the dashas, 26 verses
- **v.1 "The dasha taught by Parashara, gathered from the science and found most fruitful after much testing" - Vimshottari.**
- v.2-3 Lords from Krittika: Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17, Ketu 7, Venus 20; the balance from the part of the star still to run.
- v.4 Years are counted by the Sun's return to its birth position.
- v.5-6 Sun: quarrel, anger of authority, sudden illness of one's people, wandering, sharp anger; well placed: money through bold acts, travel and rulers, wandering in forest and hill, great fame, victory, energy.
- v.7 Moon: peace of mind, success in everything, good food, spouse and children, ornaments, clothes, cattle and land.
- **v.8 The Moon's results go by its strength: middling in the first ten days of the lunar month, full in the middle ten, small in the last ten.**
- v.9 Mars: money through fire, authority, conflict, medicine, [deceit] and harsh acts; fevers; enmity with children, spouse, relatives and elders.
- v.10 Mercury: friends arrive, well-being, a name praised by the learned, the teacher's favour, eloquence, help to others, the family's welfare.
- v.11 Jupiter: right works, children, honour from the ruler, standing among the eminent, vehicles, company of spouse, children and friends, wishes fulfilled.
- v.12 Venus: means of enjoyment, vehicles, cattle, gems, ornaments, joy with the spouse, learning, travel by water, auspicious events, honour from the ruler.
- v.13 Saturn: wind ailments to spouse and children, loss in farming, bad talk, separation from staff, living away, sudden loss of relatives, land, comfort and money.
- v.14-16 Rahu: fear from authority, thieves, poison, fire and weapons; trouble to children; confusion; slander; loss of position. **With a benefic in a good house: a good dasha - wealth like a ruler's, success, comfort at home, steady savings, fame. Rahu in Virgo, Pisces or Scorpio: comfort, headship of a region, vehicles - "and at the end of the dasha the loss of everything".**
- v.17 Ketu: trouble from rivals, thieves and authority, wounds, heat ailments, false accusation, disgrace to the family, fire, leaving one's country.
- v.18-26 A second verse for each planet in dasha order, mixing good and bad (Sun: money through boldness and rulers, yet eye and stomach trouble, separation from elders; Moon: prosperity through mantra, the learned and rulers, land and spouse, yet wind ailments; Mars: prosperity through land and rulers, cattle and fields, yet enmity with brothers and children; Rahu: a hard temper or deep illness, trouble from rivals, the ruler's dislike; Jupiter: clothes and wealth, a mature household, great honour, children and friends, yet separation from an elder; Saturn: prosperity that comes through pressure on the country, old servants gained, buffalo and bulls, yet pain in the limbs; Mercury: steady success with teachers, money through the learned, land, cattle and horses, savings; Ketu: grief and confusion through women, trouble from superiors, loss of money, leaving one's country; Venus: spouse, gems, clothes, lustre, treasure, vehicles, bed and seat, money through trade, farming and travel by water, yet separation from an elder).
- Check: dasha lords and years identical to Parashara and the engine. Rahu's favourable signs here (Virgo, Pisces, Scorpio) differ again from Parashara's three lists (BPHS 55.1, 57.68, 58.47); Virgo is the one sign in all five lists.
