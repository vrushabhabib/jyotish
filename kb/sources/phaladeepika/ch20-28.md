# Phaladeepika chapters 20–28 — dashas of the house lords, ashtakavarga, renunciation, conclusion (12 Nov 2026)

What was read (second pass, 12 Nov 2026, afternoon): **ch. 20, 21, 23, 24, 25, 27 and 28 in full.** Ch. 22 (Kalachakra) gives its sequences in letter-number phrases that were not decoded - Parashara ch. 46 gives the same sequences in plain number-words and was decoded there. Ch. 13 (length of life) and ch. 17 (death) are excluded by rule; only their opening verses were looked at (13.3: "until the twelfth year the length of life cannot be settled").

Correction of my own earlier note: I had assumed the rest of ch. 20 was a list of the 81 sub-periods. It is not. Verses 22-63 are the general rules for judging any dasha and sub-period - among the most useful in the book - and ch. 21 is the list of 81.

### Ch. 20 vv.22-63 - general rules for dashas and sub-periods
- v.22 A vargottama planet's dasha is good; mixed if it is combust or debilitated. In the dashas and sub-periods of the lords of the 8th, 12th and 6th even a well-placed planet there gives bad.
- v.23 Hard sub-periods: of a harsh planet; of the lords of the 3rd, 5th and 7th stars counted from the birth star; of the lord of the 8th from the birth sign.
- v.24 Hard dashas by their order in life: Saturn's when it is the 4th, Jupiter's when the 6th, Mars's or Rahu's when the 5th; also that of a planet at the very end of a sign, and of a lord of a bad house.
- v.25-26 Mars exalted in an upward-facing sign in the 10th or 11th: authority, victory, a following. Venus exalted in the 12th, 10th or 11th, not combust and without malefics: jewels, wide means, good name.
- v.27 Benefics in debilitation, an enemy's sign, the 6th or 12th give bad; malefics there give unfailing hardship.
- v.28 In the sub-period of an enemy of the dasha lord, of a planet in an enemy's sign, or of an enemy of the lagna lord: trouble from rivals, loss of place - "even a friend turns hostile".
- **v.29 "The sub-period lord gives the results of the house it occupies counted from the dasha lord; in the 6th, 12th or 8th from it, hardship; elsewhere, comfort."** [The same rule as Parashara ch. 52-60, here in one line, and again with the 2nd not among the bad places.]
- **v.30 Good results: full in exaltation, three quarters in moolatrikona, half in own sign, a quarter in a friend's, little in an enemy's, none in debilitation; bad results in the reverse order; a combust planet is like a debilitated one.**
- v.31-32, 40, 55, 58 [The end of life - not used.]
- **v.33 Jupiter, the lord of the birth sign and the lagna lord in kendras give happiness in the middle of life. Planets in back-rising, both-ways and head-rising signs give at the end, the middle and the start of their period.** [= Parashara 50.88-89.]
- **v.34-35 The house through which the dasha lord is passing in transit prospers at that time if the dasha lord is there in its own, exaltation or a friend's sign and was strong at birth; if it was weak at birth and is combust, debilitated or in an enemy's sign in transit, the house it passes through is harmed.**
- **v.36 When the Moon in transit is in the dasha lord's exaltation or friend's sign, or in the 3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th, a trine or the 7th from the dasha lord: good; otherwise trouble.**
- **v.37 When the dasha lord in transit is debilitated, combust or in an enemy's sign: hardship; in its own or exaltation sign, or retrograde: comfort.**
- **v.38 When the Sun in transit enters the own or exaltation sign of a dasha lord that promises good, that good arrives; the same with Jupiter. When the Sun enters the debilitation or enemy sign of a dasha lord that promises hardship, the hardship arrives.** [A month-level trigger.]
- v.39 Rahu gives the good and bad of the planet it is with; that planet, even a benefic, gives trouble, especially at the end of its dasha.
- **v.41 "Parashara's view": a benefic owning a kendra gives bad and a malefic owning one gives good; all trine lords give good; the lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th give trouble; the 8th lord too - unless it is also the lagna lord, or is the Sun or the Moon.**
- **v.42 A trine lord in a kendra, or a kendra lord in a trine: both give yoga; in each other's sub-periods they help each other.**
- **v.43-44 "Planets do not give their own results in their own sub-period of their own dasha; they give them in the sub-periods of planets connected with them and of planets of like nature."**
- v.45-49 Kendra and trine lords connected in any way become yoga-givers even if otherwise blemished; malefics connected with a yoga-giver give the yoga's result in their sub-periods.
- **v.50-51 The blemish of owning a kendra is strongest for Jupiter and Venus, then Mercury, then the Moon; malefics owning kendras are progressively better.**
- v.52-53 Rahu and Ketu in a kendra or trine and connected with the lord of the other kind become yoga-givers; in a good house and unconnected they give according to the sub-period.
- v.54 A raja yoga begins in the sub-periods of the "death-inflicting" lords and spreads in the sub-periods of malefics.
- v.56-57 "Descending" and "ascending" dashas as in Parashara 50.81-83. A planet in a good sign and navamsa that is debilitated, in an enemy's sign or combust: mixed, and it gives in the second half of its dasha.
- v.59 The dasha is good when its lord is [in transit] in the lagna or the 3rd, 6th, 10th or 11th from it, or a friend or benefic is in the lagna.
- v.60-62 Through the years of a dasha, follow where its lord moves; the Moon in the dasha lord's friendly or exaltation sign, or in the 7th, a trine or an upachaya from it, gives good, and the house that sign is in the birth chart is the one that prospers.
- v.63 "Consult the star dasha of the Saravali and Varahamihira's Hora as well."
- These verses carry the same doctrine that circulates as "Laghu Parashari" (v.41-54). The knowledge base had those rules tagged as from memory; they are now sourced to Phaladeepika 20.41-54.


- **A real difference between the two texts:** Parashara gives each house lord one result by lordship alone (BPHS 48.2-7: the 11th lord "obstacles in gains", the 3rd "bad", the 12th "much hardship"). Mantreshvara makes every lord's dasha good when strong and bad when weak - including the lords of the 6th, 8th, 11th and 12th. The project's rule: **lordship says what the period is about; the lord's strength and placement say whether it goes well** - and both verses are cited.

## Ch. 21 - The 81 sub-periods, 84 verses
- v.1-2 The share of a sub-period: dasha years x sub-lord's years / 120; the first sub-period is the dasha lord's own.
- v.3-83 One verse a pair, **with no chart condition** - the result is given by the two planets alone, as in Parashara's finer levels (ch. 61-63) and unlike his conditional treatment of sub-periods (ch. 52-60). - Read against Parashara: both texts make Ketu's sub-period hard in nearly every dasha, the Sun's hard in Venus's dasha, Rahu's hard in Saturn's, and Jupiter's good in almost every dasha. They differ on Jupiter-Saturn (hard here; good when well placed in Parashara), Venus-Moon (hard here) and Mercury-Sun against Sun-Mercury. **Because this chapter ignores placement, Parashara's conditional rules (and Mantreshvara's own 20.29) take precedence; the grid is a tie-breaker only.**

## Ch. 25 - Gulika and the shadow points, 30 verses
- v.1-4 Mandi (Gulika), Yamakantaka, Ardhaprahara and Kala: their times by weekday in letter-number code, scaled to the length of day or night; the sign rising then is their place. v.5 Dhuma, Vyatipata, Parivesha, Indrachapa and Ketu from the Sun - the same arithmetic as Parashara 3.61-64.
- v.8-14 Gulika in the twelve houses: hardship in most; **in the 3rd - proud, busy earning, free of grief and fear, without brothers; in the 6th - destroys many rivals, a brave and excellent child; in the 11th - comfort, children, intelligence, lustre.**
- v.15-17 Gulika with each planet [harm wordings - not used].
- **v.18-21 "With Gulika say the faults everywhere; with Yamakantaka say good everywhere." Gulika is to be read like Saturn, Yamakantaka like Jupiter, Ardhaprahara like Mercury, Kala like Rahu.**
- v.22-29 The five smoke-points with a house harm it, "says Parashara"; they are also described as things seen in the sky (a comet, a meteor, a halo, a rainbow).
- v.30 The lord of Gulika's sign strong in a kendra or trine, in own, exaltation or a friend's sign: vehicles, wide fame, authority.

## Ch. 23 — Ashtakavarga, 24 verses
- **v.1 "For finding the good and bad results that come to people through the planets in transit, the great method called Ashtakavarga."**
- v.3-9 The favourable places of each planet from each of the eight, written in letter-number code (katapayadi). [Not decoded in this pass; the engine's tables were checked today against Parashara ch. 66 instead.]
- v.10 The good is fuller when the planet is in an upachaya house, a friend's sign or exaltation; poorer in the other houses, debilitation or an enemy's sign.
- v.11 By the number of points where a planet stands: none - the worst; one - loss; two - spending; three and four - fear; five - money; six - a spouse or companionship; seven - prosperity; eight - authority.
- v.12-15 Take the sign of each planet as a lagna and read the houses from it; a planet in transit passing a sign that has a point counted from the significator strengthens that house; several such planets together strengthen it fully.
- **v.16-19 Timing inside a sign: divide it into eight parts ruled in order by Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, the Sun, Venus, Mercury, the Moon and the lagna; the planet gives its good while in the part of a giver of a point.**
- **v.20 In the combined table a sign with more than 28 points is good; with fewer, trouble.**
- v.21-22 Do auspicious work when the Moon and the other planets are in signs with many points.
- **v.23-24 Even a malefic in its own sign builds the house; in debilitation or an enemy's sign it surely harms it. Even an exalted benefic harms a house if it is the lord of a bad house; an exalted malefic that is lord of a good house helps.**

## Ch. 24 — Results of Ashtakavarga, 44 verses
- v.1-15 Timing of hardship to father, mother and self by the method of Parashara ch. 70, and counts of brothers and children [**not used**].
- v.16-23 The trine reduction and the one-lord reduction - the same rules as Parashara ch. 67-68.
- v.24-26 Multipliers: Taurus and Leo 10; Gemini and Scorpio 8; Libra and Aries 7; Virgo and Capricorn 5; Cancer, Sagittarius, Aquarius, Pisces their own number. Jupiter 10, Mars 8, Venus 7, Mercury 5, the others 5 - identical to Parashara 69.2-4.
- v.27-35 A dasha system built on the reduced figures [length-of-life material - not used].
- **v.36 "Ashtakavarga carries the results of all undertakings; without it strength and weakness are hard to know."**
- **v.37-40 Signs with more than 30 points give good; above 25, middling; below, hardship. More in the 11th than the 10th: gain; less in the 12th than the 11th, and more in the lagna than the 12th: comforts and means. House by house: more is good, less is a fault. Use the best signs for all good undertakings, leaving out the 6th, 8th and 12th.**
- v.41-43 Totals from the lagna to Saturn and the like as ages of trouble [not used].
- Check: identical in substance to Parashara ch. 67-72; the engine's house checks follow v.37-39.

## Ch. 27 — Renunciation, 8 verses
- v.1-2 Four planets with the 10th lord in kendras or trines; four or more in one sign - renunciation of the kind shown by the strongest, "or, some say, by the 10th lord".
- v.3 The Moon in a drekkana of Saturn aspected by Mars and Saturn; or in a navamsa of Mars aspected by Saturn.
- v.4 The lord of the birth sign aspected by Saturn alone.
- v.5 The kind by planet. v.6 A very strong Moon in the bright half aspecting a weak lagna lord: an ascetic in hardship.
- **v.7 If a raja yoga is present with a renunciation yoga, the hardship is uprooted: a ruler who is initiated and of good conduct.**
- v.8 Four planets with the 10th lord in a kendra or trine, or three strong in a good house: renunciation succeeds - honoured if benefics predominate.
- Check: agrees with Parashara ch. 79; the engine follows Parashara's stricter wording for the Moon-Saturn case.

## Ch. 28 — Conclusion, 6 verses
- v.1-4 The text's own list of its 27 chapters: definitions; significators; divisions; strength; livelihood; yogas; raja yogas; planets in houses; the lagna signs; the spouse; women's charts; children; early dangers and length of life; illness; judgement of houses; results of the twelve houses; the end of life; two planets together; results of the dashas; dashas of the house lords and sub-periods; sub-sub-periods; Kalachakra; Ashtakavarga and its results; Mandi and the shadow points; transits; renunciation.
- v.5-6 Written by Mantreshvara, an astrologer born at Shalivati, by the grace of the goddess Sukuntalamba, "for the pleasure of good astrologers".
