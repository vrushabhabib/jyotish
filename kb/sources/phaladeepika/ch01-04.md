# Phaladeepika chapters 1–4 — signs and houses, planets, divisions, strength (read from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

## Ch. 1 — Signs and houses, 18 verses
- v.2 Mantreshvara says he is condensing what Atri, Parashara and others taught.
- **v.3 "First the time of birth is to be known exactly, to the fraction, with instruments; the planets' positions by calculations that agree with observation; then the houses, the strengths, and the results."**
- v.4 Limbs of Time from the lagna: head, face, chest, heart, belly, hip, lower abdomen, private parts, thighs, knees, shanks, feet. The ends of Scorpio, Pisces and Cancer are the junction points.
- v.5 Haunts of the signs from Aries (forest, field, bedroom, pond-side, hill, cropland, market, hole, ruler's place, watery woodland, potter's place, water).
- **v.6 Lords of the signs; exaltation: Sun Aries 10°, Moon Taurus 3°, Mars Capricorn 28°, Mercury Virgo 15°, Jupiter Cancer 5°, Venus Pisces 27°, Saturn Libra 20°; debilitation opposite.**
- **v.7 Moolatrikona: Sun Leo 20°; Moon Taurus, 27° after the exaltation part; Mars Aries 12°; Mercury Virgo, 5° after the exaltation part; Jupiter Sagittarius 10°; Venus Libra 5°; Saturn Aquarius 20°.** Human signs: the first half of Sagittarius, Virgo, Gemini, Aquarius, Libra; Scorpio is the "insect"; watery: Cancer, the second half of Capricorn, Pisces; the rest four-footed.
- v.8 Back-rising: Taurus, Cancer, Sagittarius, Aries, Capricorn; Gemini and Pisces [Pisces both ways]; the rest head-rising. The back-rising ones and Gemini are night signs, the rest day signs.
- v.9 Movable, fixed, dual; mineral, plant, living; harsh and gentle; directions east, south, west, north by the trines from Aries; odd male, even female.
- v.10-16 What each house stands for: **1** body, form, head, the present, birth. **2** wealth, learning, food and drink, right eye, face, documents, speech, family. **3** right ear, [chest], courage, valour, brothers and sisters. **4** house, land, maternal uncle, sister's child, relatives, friends, vehicles, mother, cattle, perfumes, clothes, ornaments, water, bridges, rivers. **5** marks of authority, counsel, intelligence, foresight, children, belly, learning heard and remembered. **6** debts, weapons, thieves, wounds, illness, rivals, disputes, fear, contempt. **7** desire, travel, the road, spouse. **8** [the married state], weak points, defeat, length of life, distress, blame, impurity, obstacles. **9** teacher, deity, **father**, former good fortune, worship, austerity, merit, grandchildren, good lineage. **10** trade, position, honour, work, victory, good name, sacrifice, livelihood, conduct, command. **11** gain, income, attainment, elder brother or sister, left ear, satisfaction, good news. **12** sorrow, feet, left eye, loss, want, wrongdoing, the bed, spending, confinement.
- v.16-18 "Hidden" houses: 3rd, 6th, 12th, 8th. Bad houses: 8th, 6th, 12th; the rest are good. Kendras 1, 10, 7, 4; then panapharas and apoklimas; chaturasra 8 and 4; upachayas 10, 3, 6, 11; trines 9 and 5.

Moolatrikona: **Moon (3-30° of Taurus) and Mercury (15-20° of Virgo) agree with Parashara and with the correction made today; Venus differs - 5° here, 15° in Parashara (the engine follows Parashara).** The father is in the 9th here too (v.14).

## Ch. 2 — The planets, 37 verses
- v.1-7 What each planet stands for. Sun: father, self, well-being, vigour, courage, victory, service of a ruler, light, travel in forest and hill, fire rites, temple, sharpness, energy; copper, gold. Moon: the mother's welfare, peace of mind, sea-bathing, white things, fruit, flowers, crops, good name, pearls, silver, sweet things, milk, cloth, water, cattle, good food, bodily ease, beauty. Mars: strength, produce of land, the character of brothers and sisters, harshness, battle, daring, enmity, the kitchen fire, gold, kin, weapons, thieves, rivals, energy, [untruth], valour, high spirit, command, wounds. Mercury: scholarship, good speech, skill in arts, praise from the learned, maternal uncle, eloquence, skill in worship, reasoning, intelligence, truthful speech, places of recreation, craft, relatives, the heir, friends, sister's children. Jupiter: knowledge, good qualities, children, a counsellor, one's own conduct, teaching, greatness, sacred learning, all-round rise, devotion, sacrifice, faith, the treasury, mastery of the senses, a woman's happiness from her husband, honour, compassion. Venus: wealth, vehicles, clothes, ornaments, music-dance-song, happiness from the spouse, perfumes, flowers, love, the bed, the house, prosperity, poetry, pleasure, charm, a minister's post, witty speech, weddings. Saturn: length of life, fear, a fall, sorrow, humiliation, illness, want, servants, blame, impurity, misfortune, steadiness, dependence on low people, sloth, debt, iron, servitude, farm tools, confinement.
- v.8-14 Bodies and temperaments of the seven. v.15-16 Places and directions: Sun east, Moon north-west, Mars south, Mercury north, Jupiter north-east, Venus south-east, Saturn west, the nodes south-west. v.17-20 People, animals and birds of each.
- **v.21-22 Natural friendships** - identical to Parashara's table and the engine's.
- **v.23 Temporary friends: planets in the 3rd, 4th, 2nd, 10th, 12th and 11th from each other. Aspects: Saturn on the 3rd and 10th, Jupiter on the 5th and 9th, Mars on the 4th and 8th see fully; all see the 7th fully; the other places by quarters.**
- v.24 Time spans, classes, qualities and seasons of the planets.
- **v.25 Father and mother: the Sun and Venus for a day birth, Saturn and the Moon for a night birth; the other pair then stands for the father's brother and the mother's sister.** Left eye the Moon, right eye the Sun; Mars the younger brother, Jupiter the elder, Mercury an adopted child.
- **v.27 Malefics: the waning Moon, Sun, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, Saturn - and Mercury when with a malefic.** Neuter: Ketu, Mercury, Saturn; female: Moon, Rahu, Venus; the rest male. Deities and elements (Sun and Mars fire, Moon and Venus water, Mercury earth, Jupiter ether, Saturn air).
- v.28-31 Grains, regions, gems (ruby, pearl, coral, emerald, topaz, diamond, blue sapphire; hessonite and cat's-eye for the nodes), metals, cloths, tastes.
- v.32-35 Marks and ages; Rahu and Ketu described; lead and old cloth for Rahu, an earthen pot and patterned cloth for Ketu. **Friends of both nodes: Mercury, Saturn, Venus; Mars neutral; the rest enemies.**
- **v.36 A planet is "badly placed" when combust, debilitated, in an enemy's sign, or in the 8th, 6th or 12th; otherwise "well placed".** The Moon in the 12th, 11th, 1st, 6th, 5th or 7th favours works of growth.
- v.37 Trees of the planets.

## Ch. 3 — The divisions, 20 verses
- v.1-2 Ten divisions: sign, drekkana, navamsa, tenth, hora, thirtieth, seventh, sixtieth, sixteenth, twelfth. Vargottama = the navamsa of a sign that is the same sign. Seven divisions = the ten without the tenth, sixtieth and sixteenth; six = also without the seventh. **"Some hold that the navamsa gives results equal to the sign chart."**
- **v.3 Share of results: full in the sign chart, half in the other divisions, a quarter in the sixteenth, tenth and sixtieth.** Five ages of a planet by 6° steps in an odd sign - infant, child, youth, mature, dead - reversed in an even sign.
- v.4-6 Rules for hora, drekkana (lords of the 1st, 5th and 9th), twelfth (from the sign itself), thirtieth (odd: Mars 5, Saturn 5, Jupiter 8, Mercury 7, Venus 5; even reversed), navamsa (from Aries, Capricorn, Libra, Cancer by element), seventh (odd from itself, even from the 7th), tenth (odd from itself, even from the 9th), sixteenth; the names of the sixtieths.
- v.7-10 Names by the number of good divisions a planet holds (Parijata, Uttama, Gopura, Simhasana, Paravata, Devaloka, Suraloka, Airavata) and the results, rising with the number.
- v.11-12 A lagna with benefics in more of the six divisions: prosperous and long-lived; with the lords of those divisions strong: a ruler's standing. Hora results by odd and even sign.
- v.13-14 Kinds of drekkana (armed, noose, fetter, bird, vulture-faced, boar-faced, serpent, four-footed) and what they show.
- v.16 Checking the lagna: it falls in a trine of the sign of Mandi's sign-lord, or of that lord's navamsa sign, or of Gulika's navamsa sign; when the Moon is strong, judged likewise from the Moon. [A rectification aid; uncertain reading, not used.]
- **v.18-20 States of a planet: blazing (exalted), happy (moolatrikona), at ease (own sign), pleased (friend's sign), calm (in benefics' divisions), able (with bright rays); distressed (defeated in planetary war), base (in malefics' divisions), very unhappy (enemy's sign), very afraid (debilitated), crippled (combust). The blazing one gives in full, the crippled one nothing, the rest in proportion.**

## Ch. 4 — Strength, 24 verses
- v.1 Six strengths: of time, of motion, of exaltation, of direction, of the half-year, of position. **By night Mars, the Moon and Venus are strong; the others by day; Mercury always.** Benefics in the bright half, the others in the dark half.
- v.2 Motion-strength: the full Moon, the Sun in the northern course, retrograde planets, and the bright northern winner of a planetary war. Direction: Sun and Mars in the 10th, Moon and Venus in the 4th, Mercury and Jupiter in the lagna, Saturn in the 7th. Half-year: Mercury, Saturn and the Moon in the southern course, the rest in the northern.
- v.3 Position: in exaltation, own and friends' signs and the six own divisions; full, half and quarter in kendras, the next houses and the last. Natural strength rising from Saturn through Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and the Moon to the Sun.
- **v.4 "A retrograde planet with full bright rays is strong even in a debilitation or enemy's sign or navamsa - like the Moon; and a planet in exaltation, a friend's or its own sign is weak if its light is lost."**
- v.5 Rahu is strong in Cancer, Taurus, Aries, Aquarius and Scorpio; Ketu in Pisces, Virgo, Taurus and the later half of Sagittarius [?].
- **v.7 Full strength in exaltation, three quarters in moolatrikona, half in own sign, a quarter in a friend's, little in an enemy's, none in debilitation or combustion.**
- **v.9 "The aspect on the 7th alone is the chief one and is to be stated everywhere; the other aspects are not so - in yogas and the like they give less."**
- **v.10 "Natural friendship and enmity alone are the proper measure for results; the temporary kind is stated for a purpose and does not last."**
- v.11 For removing faults and increasing good, Jupiter's strength is above all the planets'; Mercury and Venus have a quarter and a half of it; the Moon's strength is the seed of every planet's strength.
- v.12-20 Three fine divisions of the Moon's star (60, 12 and 36 parts) with a phrase for each; "to be used in birth, electional and question charts".
- v.21 For the Moon the strength of the fortnight is the special one; for the others, that of position.
- **v.22-23 Strength needed (in rupas): Sun 6½, Moon 6, Mars 5, Mercury 7, Jupiter 6½, Venus 5½, Saturn 5.**
- v.24 Strength of a house: that of its lord, plus direction, plus aspects.
