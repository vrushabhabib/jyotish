# Phaladeepika chapters 5–7 — livelihood, yogas, raja yogas and cancellation of debilitation (read from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

## Ch. 5 — Livelihood, 9 verses
- v.1 (a) Money comes through the father, mother, an adversary, a friend, a brother, the wife or a servant when the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn is in the 10th from the stronger of the lagna and the Moon. (b) The kind of work: take the strongest of the Sun, the lagna and the Moon; from it find the 10th lord; **the lord of the navamsa that 10th lord occupies shows the livelihood.**
- v.2 Sun's navamsa: fruit and trees, recitation of mantras, [deceit, gambling], wool, medicines, metal-work, service of a ruler.
- v.3 Moon's: trade in things from water, farming, cattle, pilgrimage, support from women, trade in cloth.
- v.4 Mars's: metals, fighting, cooking, land, gold, weapons, daring, service of foreigners, [informing, theft].
- v.5 Mercury's: poetry, the sacred texts, writing and clerical work, astrology and mathematics, reciting for others, priestly work.
- v.6 Jupiter's: support of the learned and of temples, favour of a ruler, the old texts, the sciences, policy, teaching of right conduct, lending money.
- v.7 Venus's: through women, cattle and horses, music-dance-song, silver, perfumes, milk products, ornaments, cloth, a minister's work, poetry.
- v.8 Saturn's: roots and fruits, hard labour, service, [low dealings], coarse grain, carrying loads, crafts, wood-work.
- v.9 The navamsa lord strong: money without effort; weak: little. Place: the direction of the 10th sign from the lagna; in one's own country if that sign is joined or aspected by its lord, or the navamsa is fixed; abroad if by other planets.

## Ch. 6 — Yogas, 70 verses
- v.1 Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Shasha: Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn in own sign or exaltation in a kendra.
- v.2-4 Their results (Ruchaka: long face, daring, wealth, brave, a commander; Bhadra: long-lived, sharp mind, praised by the learned, very wealthy; Hamsa: praised by the good, marks on hands and feet, fine body, eats well, righteous; Malavya: sturdy, steady, wealthy, fortune through spouse and children, vehicles, fame, learned; Shasha: praised by all, good servants, head of a village or a ruler, [character wording]). **v.4 "These five, from the lagna and also from the Moon, give sovereignty": with one, fortunate; two, like a ruler; three, a ruler; four, a great ruler; five, more.**
- v.5 Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhura: planets other than the Sun in the 2nd, the 12th, or both from the Moon; otherwise Kemadruma. **"Kemadruma is not, when the Moon is in a kendra or joined by a planet - or when planets are in kendras from the Moon."**
- v.6-7 Results (Sunapha: self-earned wealth, a ruler or like one, intelligent, known; Anapha: a master, healthy, good conduct, fame, comforts, well dressed; Durudhura: comforts, wealth, vehicles, generous, servants; Kemadruma: dirty, unhappy, low, poor, a servant - even if born in a ruler's line).
- v.8-13 Vesi, Vasi, Ubhayachari: planets other than the Moon in the 2nd, 12th, or both from the Sun; with benefics good, with malefics "papa". Shubha-kartari: benefics on both sides of the lagna; Papa-kartari: malefics. Sushubha: benefics in the 2nd from the lagna, unaspected by malefics. Results of each.
- **v.14 Mahabhagya: a man born by day with Sun, Moon and lagna in odd signs; a woman born by night with them in even signs. Kesari: Jupiter in a kendra from the Moon. Shakata: the Moon in the 12th, 8th or 6th from Jupiter - "not when the Moon is in a kendra from the lagna". Adhama, Sama, Varishtha: the Moon in a kendra, the next houses, or the last houses from the Sun.**
- v.15-18 Results (Mahabhagya: delight of all eyes, generous, famous; Kesari: destroys rivals like a lion, mature speech in assembly, long-lived, very famous, quick mind; Shakata: fortune lost now and then and regained again and again, not well known, an inner thorn that cannot be removed).
- v.19-20 Vasumat: all benefics in upachaya houses from the Moon or lagna. Amala: a benefic in the 10th from the Moon or lagna. Pushkala: the lord of the Moon's sign with the lagna lord in a kendra in a great friend's sign, and a strong planet aspecting the lagna.
- v.21-25 Shubha-mala: benefics in the 5th, 6th and 7th in a row; Ashubha-mala: in the 8th, 6th and 12th. **Lakshmi: the 9th lord and Venus both in own sign or exaltation, in a trine or kendra. Gauri: the Moon so placed and aspected by Jupiter.**
- **v.26-27 Sarasvati: Venus, Jupiter and Mercury in kendras, trines or the 2nd, with Jupiter strong in own, exalted or a friend's sign.** Result: skilled in drama, prose, verse, mathematics and poetics; famous; wealthy; honoured by rulers.
- v.28-31 Shrikantha: lagna lord, Sun and Moon in kendras or trines in exalted, own or friends' signs. Shrinatha: Venus, the 9th lord and Mercury so placed. Virinchi: Jupiter, the 5th lord [?] and Saturn so placed. [Different from Parashara's Shrinatha, which the engine follows.]
- **v.32-34 Exchange of signs between two house lords: 66 combinations - 30 "Dainya" (involving the lords of the 12th, 6th or 8th), 8 "Khala" (involving the 3rd lord), the remaining 28 "Maha".** Results: Dainya - foolish, speaks ill of others, harassed by rivals, efforts broken; Khala - wayward, sometimes gets all fortune and sometimes loses it; Maha - favoured by fortune, a master, wealthy, honoured by rulers, vehicles, children.
- v.35-36 Kahala: the lord of the sign holding the lord of the sign in which the lagna lord sits, in own sign or exaltation in a trine or kendra. Parvata: the lord of the sign holding the lagna lord, so placed. [Different from Parashara's definitions, which the engine follows.]
- v.37-38 Raja yoga: the lords of the 9th and 10th together in a good house. Shankha: [a kendra and a trine lord joined - the line is unclear in this copy [?]].
- v.39-41 Number yogas (not counting those named earlier): seven planets in seven signs Vallaki, six Dama, five Pasha, four Kedara, three Shula, two Yuga, one Gola; results.
- v.42-43 Adhi yoga: benefics in the 7th, 6th and 8th from the Moon - and likewise from the lagna: a leader, minister or ruler; lasting wealth, supports many relatives, defeats rivals.
- **v.44-56 Twelve good house-yogas, one per house from the lagna, when the house is joined or aspected by benefics and its lord is in a good house, bright, in own sign or exaltation: Chamara (1), Dhenu (2), Shaurya (3), Jaladhi (4), Chhatra (5), Astra (6), Kama (7), Asura (8), Bhagya (9), Khyati (10), Suparijata (11), Musala (12).** Results verse by verse (Chamara: grows daily like the waxing Moon, famous, a leader, long-lived; Dhenu: food, wealth, all learning, large family; Shaurya: praised by famous younger brothers, valour, engaged in public work; Jaladhi: cattle, grain, a house full of relatives, a fine position, vehicles; Chhatra: happy family life, children, wealth, wise, a minister; Astra: subdues strong rivals, harsh in action, proud, scarred, fond of dispute; Kama: turns from others' spouses, good spouse and children, surpasses the father; Asura: spoils others' work, selfish, poor, obstinate; Bhagya: lasting wealth, stands in right conduct, honours ancestors and the learned; Khyati: good deeds approved by all, protects the good, famous; Suparijata: always festive, stored wealth, good family; Musala: money got with difficulty, humiliated, unsteady wealth, spends fittingly).
- **v.57-69 Twelve house-yogas when the lord of each house is badly placed (in the 6th, 8th or 12th) and the house is joined or aspected by malefics: Avayoga (1), Nihsva (2), Mriti (3), Kuhu (4), Pamara (5), Harsha (6), Dushkriti (7), Sarala (8), Nirbhagya (9), Duryoga (10), Daridra (11), Vimala (12).** Nine give hardship. **Three are good: Harsha (6th lord) - comfort, enjoyment, fortune, strong body, rivals destroyed, fears wrongdoing, dear to leading people, wealth, friends, fame, children (v.63); Sarala (8th lord) - long-lived, firm mind, fearless, prosperous, learning, children and money, succeeds in undertakings, defeats rivals, widely known (v.65); Vimala (12th lord) - spends little and saves much, agreeable to all, happy, independent, respectable work, known for good qualities (v.69).**
- v.70 Duryoga in general: the lords of the 8th, 6th and 12th strong in kendras and trines while the lords of the 1st, 10th, 4th and 9th are in the 6th, 8th and 12th, weak and dim. The reverse makes a fortunate, wealthy, happy and righteous person.

## Ch. 7 — Raja yogas, 30 verses
- v.1-2 Three or more planets in exaltation or own sign in kendras: a famous ruler; five or more: a ruler even if not born to rule. Those of ruling families become rulers unless born in a bad yoga or with the planets combust; others become equal to rulers.
- v.3 Even one bright planet in a good house, retrograde, though in debilitation, makes one like a ruler; two or three more so.
- v.4 Two or three planets with directional strength; five other than Saturn, or four: a ruler even from another line.
- v.5-7 Lagna or Moon in a vargottama navamsa aspected by four planets; lagna lord in a kendra and the 9th lord vargottama or in exaltation or own sign; the Moon aspected by planets in exaltation or own sign; a full Moon in a kendra other than the lagna.
- v.8-10 Venus rising in Ashwini aspected by the planets; lagna lord strong with Venus in the 2nd, not in an enemy's or debilitation sign; Mars in an Aries, Leo or Sagittarius lagna aspected by a friend; **10th lord in the 9th and 9th lord in the 10th**.
- v.11-19 Further combinations built on a bright Moon (in the Sun's navamsa with benefics in kendras; with three planets strong in their own navamsas; in the best navamsa aspected by a strong planet; vargottama and full; in a watery navamsa in the lagna with no malefic in a kendra), on benefics bright in the 9th aspected by friends (v.14), and on **Jupiter and the Moon in kendras aspected by Venus with no planet in debilitation (v.18)**.
- v.20-23 Venus aspected by Jupiter; Jupiter in a lagna other than Capricorn; the lord of the birth sign strong in a kendra; Mercury aspected by Jupiter; one exalted planet aspected by a friend; the full Moon in Pisces aspected by a friend or in exaltation; the Moon in a great friend's navamsa aspected by Venus or Jupiter.
- v.24 Four combinations: malefics in the 3rd, 6th and 11th from the lord of the birth sign; Mars and Mercury in the 2nd from the lagna; Sun and Venus in the 4th; Mars, Saturn and Jupiter in the 10th, 11th and lagna.
- v.25 One of the lords of the 11th, 9th and 2nd in a kendra from the Moon, with Jupiter as lord of the 2nd, 5th or 11th.

### Cancellation of debilitation (v.26-30)
- **v.26 A planet in debilitation at birth: if the lord of that sign, or the lord of its exaltation sign, is in a kendra from the Moon.**
- **v.27 If the lord of that sign and the lord of its exaltation sign are in a kendra [from the lagna].**
- **v.28 If the planet is aspected by the lord of the sign it is in - "how much more if it is in a good house".**
- **v.29 If the lord of the sign of the debilitated planet is in a kendra from the lagna or from the Moon, or the lord of that planet's exaltation sign is in a kendra.**
- **v.30 If the lords of its debilitation and exaltation signs - both or even one - are in a kendra.**
- Each is said to make "a ruler honoured by rulers, righteous".
