# Phaladeepika chapters 15 and 26 — how to judge a house; transits (read from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

## Ch. 15 — Judgement of the houses, 30 verses
- **v.1 A house joined or aspected by its own lord, by lords of good houses or by benefics, and free of malefic aspect and company, gives its full result. Malefics count as good for the house they own. Every planet gives good when it is not debilitated, combust or in an enemy's sign.**
- **v.2 Counted from the house itself: benefics in its trines, 2nd, 4th, 7th and 10th, no malefic aspect, its lord with it, no malefics in it - the house thrives. The contrary destroys it. Mixed gives mixed. So for all twelve.**
- **v.3 The lord of a house in the 8th, combust, in a debilitation or enemy's sign, without the company or aspect of benefics, destroys that house.**
- v.4 Malefics in the 6th, 8th and 12th counted from a house destroy it; benefics there do not give much.
- **v.5 A house whose lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th, or which is occupied by the lord of one of those: the house is harmed; aspected by a benefic, it does well.**
- **v.6 House lord, house and significator all weak; hemmed between malefics; aspected by malefics and enemies and not by others; malefics in its 4th, 8th, 12th or trines - the house is lost. "It becomes clear here when two or three of these agree."**
- v.7 When the harm comes: in the dashas of the lord of the 8th counted from that house, the lord of its 22nd drekkana, weak planets in its 6th, 8th and 7th. What supports a house: malefics in its 3rd, 6th and 11th, benefics in its kendras and trines, strong friends of its lord.
- v.8 Planets that harm in their own dashas: the lord of the 8th from the Moon sign and from the lagna, a planet in or aspecting the 8th, Saturn, the lord of the 22nd drekkana, the lord of Gulika's sign, the lords of the signs and navamsas of these, and Rahu - when weak and badly placed.
- **v.9 The house whose lord is joined with the lagna lord prospers, and so does the house the lagna lord sits in; in a bad house the reverse. With the house lord weak the harm is great; strong, small.**
- v.10 Even a malefic lagna lord increases the house it occupies.
- **v.11 A planet owning two houses: the result of its moolatrikona house is the chief one and that of the other house half. Some say: in an odd sign it gives the result of its odd-sign house in the first half of its dasha and of the even-sign house in the second half; in an even sign the other way.**
- v.12 In the dasha of a planet that is a great enemy of the house lord, or that sits in a sign without points: harm to that house.
- **v.13-14 Even an exalted planet of full strength gives nothing at the border of a house; at the exact middle of the house it gives the full result; between, in proportion.**
- v.15-16 What each planet stands for: Sun - self, father, influence, health, energy, fortune; Moon - mind, understanding, favour of the ruler, mother, wealth; Mars - courage, illness, qualities, younger brothers and sisters, land, rivals, kin; Mercury - learning, relatives, discernment, maternal uncle, friends, speech, work; Jupiter - wisdom, wealth, bodily well-being, children, knowledge; Venus - spouse, vehicles, ornaments, love, comforts; Saturn - length of life, livelihood, adversity, servants; Rahu - paternal grandfather; Ketu - maternal grandfather.
- **v.17 Significators of the twelve houses: 1 Sun; 2 Jupiter; 3 Mars; 4 Moon and Mercury; 5 Jupiter; 6 Saturn and Mars; 7 Venus; 8 Saturn; 9 Sun and Jupiter; 10 Jupiter, Sun, Mercury and Saturn; 11 Jupiter; 12 Saturn.**
- v.18-19 Benefics increase and malefics damage a house - the reverse in the 12th, 6th and 8th, where malefics increase the house's own (unwelcome) matters and benefics destroy them.
- v.20-25 Take any house as a lagna and read the twelve from it; do the same from the significator's sign - the father from the Sun's sign, and so on for mother, brother, maternal uncle, child, husband, servant. From the Sun: 1st the father's form, 2nd his means, 3rd his brothers, 4th his mother and comfort, 5th his mind, 6th his troubles, 7th his desire, 8th his span, 9th his merit and his own father, 10th his work, 11th his gain, 12th his loss. House, significator and house lord all strong: that house does well.
- **v.26 "Others say": the Sun in the 9th, the Moon in the 4th, Mars in the 3rd, Jupiter in the 5th, Venus in the 7th and Saturn in the 8th trouble that house.** [The text reports this as others' opinion, not its own rule.]
- v.27-28 The lagna lord gives the results of the house it is in and of the house whose lord it is joined with - good when that lord is strong, the opposite when weak.
- **v.29 The lord of a bad house sitting in its other, good, own sign gives only the result of that own house: "Saturn in Capricorn in the 5th gives children; the 6th-lord fault does not apply."**
- **v.30 The five kinds of connection between planets: being in each other's signs; conjunction; aspect; being in kendras [from each other]; being in trines [from each other].**
- Citation check: KB "Phaladeepika 15.26" (significator in its own house harms it) - **confirmed, with a correction**: Mantreshvara gives it as what "others say"; the KB now says so. KB significator table - **confirmed** (v.17). New from the reading: the agreement rule of v.6 ("two or three agree") is the classical basis of the project's evidence count; the five connections of v.30 are wider than the worksheet's "strong link" (which uses occupation, ownership, conjunction and significator) - mutual kendra and trine are noted as weak links.

## Ch. 26 — Transits, 48 verses
- **v.1 "Of all lagnas, the Moon's is the chief one for transits."**
- **v.2 Good places from the Moon: Sun 6, 3, 10; Moon 3, 10, 6, 7, 1; Jupiter 7, 9, 2, 5; Mars and Saturn 6, 3; Mercury 6, 2, 4, 10, 8; every planet in the 11th; Venus everywhere except 10, 7, 6; Rahu and Ketu like the Sun.**
- v.3-8 Obstruction (vedha): a planet in a good place gives its good only if no other planet is in the paired place.

| Planet | Good place → obstructing place | Not obstructed by |
|---|---|---|
| Sun (v.3) | 11→5, 3→9, 10→4, 6→12 | Saturn |
| Moon (v.4) | 7→2, 1→5, 6→12, 11→8, 10→4, 3→9 | Mercury |
| Mars (v.5) | 3→12, 11→5, 6→9 | - |
| Saturn (v.5) | as Mars | the Sun |
| Mercury (v.6) | 2→5, 4→3, 6→9, 8→1, 10→8, 11→12 [last word unclear in this copy] | the Moon |
| Jupiter (v.7) | 2→12, 11→8, 9→10, 5→4, 7→3 | - |
| Venus (v.8) | 1→8, 2→7, 3→1, 4→10, 5→9, 8→5, 9→11, 12→6, 11→3 | - |

- v.9-24 What each planet gives in each place from the Moon (Sun 9-11; Moon 12; Mars 13-16; Mercury 17; Jupiter 18-20; Venus 21; Saturn 22-23; Rahu 24). Examples used in the KB: Sun in the 10th success in work, in the 11th position and honour; Jupiter in the 2nd money and family happiness, 5th a child and favour, 7th travel for a good cause and happiness with the spouse, 9th "every good fortune", 11th position and honour; Saturn in the 3rd position, staff and money, 6th all comforts, 11th many kinds of gain and good name; Saturn in the 1st, 8th, 12th and Jupiter in the 3rd, 6th, 8th, 12th the hard ones.
- **v.25 When inside the sign a transiting planet acts: Mars and the Sun in the first third; Jupiter and Venus in the middle third; the Moon and Saturn in the last third; Mercury and Rahu throughout.**
- v.26-29 Transit by star: a wheel of seven lines; the birth star, the 10th star and the 19th star are the sensitive ones; a malefic there harms, and so do an ingress, an eclipse, a planetary war or a meteor on them. [Danger wordings not used.]
- **v.30 A planet giving bad results that is aspected by a benefic, and one giving good that is aspected by a malefic: both become fruitless; so does a planet aspected by its enemy.**
- **v.31 A planet in an unfavourable place does no harm if it is in its own or exaltation sign; in a favourable place it then gives in full.**
- **v.32 A planet in a favourable place that is debilitated, in an enemy's sign or combust is fruitless; in an unfavourable place it gives much hardship.**
- v.33-34 The hard combinations (Saturn, Sun, Mars, Jupiter in the 12th, 8th and 1st; and one place each for the other planets). [Danger wordings not used.]
- v.35-40 A body-figure counted in stars from the birth star for each planet.
- **v.41 "Where the ashtakavarga has more points, a planet passing there gives good - even in the 12th, 6th or 8th."**
- v.42-47 Latta (the "kick"): counted from the star a planet is in - Sun the 12th ahead, Mars the 3rd, Jupiter the 6th, Saturn the 8th; behind: Venus the 5th, Mercury the 7th, Rahu the 9th, the Moon the 22nd; trouble when it falls on the birth star.
- v.48 The Sarvatobhadra wheel is to be considered as well.
