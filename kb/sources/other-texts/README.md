## Laghu Jataka - what matters for this project
- 2.7, 2.11 A planet is strong in its own or exalted sign or division, a friend's sign, aspected by benefics.
- **10.1-6 The dasha it teaches is the life-span dasha (each planet's share of the computed life-span, the strongest of lagna, Sun and Moon first), not Vimshottari.** Same as Brihat Jataka ch. 8. The engine does not compute this dasha at all.
- 10.3 The dashas of planets in a friend's, exalted or own sign or navamsa are all good, also of planets moving toward exaltation; not of planets in the opposite conditions.
- 10.5 Sub-periods belong only to planets placed with the dasha lord (half share), in its trines (a third), its 7th (a seventh), its 4th and 8th (a quarter).
- 11.15 After the Ashtakavarga tables: "planets in transit give the particular good and bad results" - direction is assigned to TRANSIT through Ashtakavarga.
- 12 (signs' nature) v.4 Benefics build the houses they sit in, malefics damage them; benefics in the 6th destroy enemies; all planets are bad in the 12th and 8th. v.7 The good a planet gives grows by quarters in a friend's, own, trine and exalted sign; little in an enemy's sign; **a combust planet gives bad, never good.**
- Ch. 7-9 and 15 (early dangers, length of life, manner of death): excluded by the project's rule.

## Chamatkara Chintamani
One verse per planet per house for the nine planets. Description only; no dasha, no transit, no strength rule. The e-text has many typing errors. Use: wording for Part 3 (nature) only, never for timing.

## Bhrigu Sutram
Planets in houses as short sutras. Two features: (a) most results are conditioned - "with a benefic's aspect no fault", "if the house lord is strong ..."; (b) many sutras attach an AGE ("in the 25th year ...", "at 32 ..."). The ages are fixed numbers for everyone with that placement. They are testable in principle (age of an event against the age stated), but a fixed age for a twelfth of all people is a weak claim; not adopted. Many lines fall under the project's exclusions and are not recorded.

## Shatpanchashika and Daivajna Vallabha - question charts
A different branch from everything this project does: the chart is cast for the moment a question is asked, and the answer (will it happen, will the traveller return, will the lost thing be found, which side wins) is read from the rising sign, the Moon and benefics or malefics in set houses. Shatpanchashika 1.3-4: a house grows when its lord or benefics join or aspect it and is harmed by malefics; a benefic or benefic division rising in a head-rising sign = the matter succeeds. Daivajna Vallabha 1.3-6 lists ten states of a planet used for questions (blazing, wretched, at ease, glad, asleep, afflicted, robbed, losing strength, gaining strength, very strong). **The project has no question-chart module.** Its attraction for testing: an answer is due in days or weeks, so forward tests mature fast.

## Vriddha Yavana Jataka ch. 6-7 - dashas and sub-periods (read in full 13 Nov 2026; the e-text has gaps marked by dots)
**Ch. 6, 43 verses.**
- 1 A planet's dasha is the share of the life-span it gave - the life-span dasha, as in Varahamihira. Not Vimshottari.
- 2 **The dasha of a planet in its own navamsa, a friend's sign or its own sign is good by nature; otherwise it gives bad results.**
- 3-16 For each of the seven planets, one verse for its good dasha and one for its bad dasha (Sun: standing and gold when good, fear, quarrels and loss of judgement when bad; Moon: ornaments, food, favour / travel, quarrels with one's own, illness; Mars: gain by courage and land / fevers, anger; Mercury: praise, gems / confusion, bad company; Jupiter: honour, learning / worry, separation; Venus: comforts of family / conflict with authority; Saturn: land through the ruler, coarse goods / family enmity, loss of cattle).
- 17-21 The lagna has a dasha of its own, graded by which third of the sign rises and whether the sign is movable, fixed or dual; good, middling and bad versions.
- 22-23 Order: the strongest of Moon, Sun and lagna first, then planets in its kendras, then those in the following houses.
- 24-25 **Sub-period shares: a planet with the dasha lord takes a half, in its trines a third, in its squares a quarter, in its seventh a seventh.** (Same as Laghu Jataka 10.5.)
- 26-33 How the shares are worked out; exalted double, debilitated or combust half.
- 34-41 Early death - excluded by the project's rule.
- 42-43 **Planets heading to debilitation, inside the Sun's disc, beaten in planetary war or in bad divisions do not give good "like snakes struck by a spell"; they destroy good results and increase bad ones; mixed planets give mixed results.**
**Ch. 7, 70 verses.** The result of each sub-period inside each dasha, pair by pair, for the seven planets and the lagna. The verses go by the natures of the two planets only - no house, no strength. Pattern: sub-periods of Jupiter, Venus, Mercury and the Moon read well in most dashas; those of the Sun, Mars and Saturn read badly; the lagna's sub-period is good or bad as the rising sign is benefic or malefic. Like Parashara ch. 61-63 and Phaladeepika 21, these are fixed grids.
**What this adds:** a third witness (with Varahamihira and Laghu Jataka) for the life-span dasha and its share rule; a direction rule by natural benefic or malefic; and the same warning as Jataka Parijata 18.28 about combust and debilitated planets. No new method of timing.

## Jataka Parijata 18.77-172 (read 13 Nov 2026 - chapter 18 is now read in full)
- 77-166 One verse for each Vimshottari sub-period inside each dasha, and a verse or two for each dasha as a whole. Fixed results by the pair of planets, no chart condition - a grid like Parashara ch. 61-63 and Phaladeepika 21. Pattern: sub-periods of Jupiter and Mercury read well almost everywhere, Venus mostly well, the Moon mixed, the Sun mixed, Mars, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu badly (with exceptions: Jupiter in Ketu's dasha and Mercury in Rahu's and Saturn's read well).
- 82 What part of a period gives what: the house result first, the sign result in the middle, the result of aspects at the end. (Same as 18.57-58.)
- 93, 108, 156 Mars: loss at the start, fear in the middle and end. Rahu: sorrow at the start, great comfort in the middle, loss of place at the end. Ketu: trouble at the start, gain in the middle, comfort at the end.
- 94, 119, 131, 142-143, 167-168 **A planet exalted by sign but in a debilitated navamsa gives bad results in its dasha; debilitated by sign but in an exalted navamsa gives good.** Stated for Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury and Venus. The engine prints D9 dignity but no rule uses this reversal - to be coded and tested.
- 105-107 Rahu's dasha is good when Rahu is in Cancer, Taurus or Aries; Virgo, Pisces or Sagittarius; Leo, Taurus, Virgo or Cancer [three lists that overlap - a fourth version beside Parashara's three].
- 154-155 Ketu with a benefic gives comfort in its dasha; with a malefic, fear and loss.
- 169 A strong planet in its own exalted portion gives a full dasha; a weak one an empty one; one in a debilitated or enemy's navamsa a harmful one.
- 170 In the dashas and sub-periods of a house's lord joined with its significators the matters of that house suffer [reading uncertain] [?].
- 171 Strong benefics in a trine, the 2nd or the 11th give much comfort in their dashas and sub-periods.
The full grid (9 x 9 pairs) can be coded as one more pre-stated direction rule once the Sun's and Moon's rows (v.60-76) are transcribed from the file.

## Jataka Parijata ch. 10 - Ashtakavarga, 72 verses (read in full 13 Nov 2026)
- 3-8 In a planet's own table: 8 points strengthens the house; 5, 6, 7 good; 4 mixed; 1, 2, 3 not good; 0 illness and fear. One verse of results for each count from 1 to 8.
- 9 A planet passing through a sign where it has 5 or more points is good; otherwise it gives nothing or harm.
- **10-11 Planets in exaltation, a friend's division or strong in a kendra all give bad results if they have few points; planets in bad houses or in debilitated or enemy divisions all give good if they have many.** Points override dignity. No rule in the engine uses this for a planet's own period.
- 12 Saturn passing a zero-point sign of a planet's table harms that planet's relatives and matters.
- 13-32 Results planet by planet for given counts (statements about length of life left out).
- 33-35 The eight-part division of a sign: the part whose lord gave a point delivers; a planet in an enemy's or debilitated sign or combust cancels it even with a point.
- 36-51 The two reductions and the life-span derived from them - excluded by the project's rule.
- 52-53 Combined table: up to 25 fruitless, up to 30 middling, above 30 good. (Same thresholds as Parashara.)
- 54-55 The same override for the combined table: well-placed planets in low-point signs harm.
- 56 11th above the 10th, 12th below the 11th, lagna above the 12th = happy and well-off. (The worksheet's checks.)
- 57-59 Three parts of life from the combined points; the text counts the parts from Pisces, Cancer and Scorpio and notes that "some" count from the 12th house - a variant of Parashara 72.9-10 as the worksheet prints it.
- 60 The number of points in the lagna is the age at which fortune arrives [a fixed-age statement; noted, not adopted]. 61-66 Royal combinations by points. 67 Direction of gain by groups of signs.
- 68-69 A timing device: points from the lagna to Saturn, times 7, divided by 27, gives a star; a malefic passing that star brings trouble, and the mirror count a star where a benefic brings good. Not in the engine.
- 70-71 Life-span - excluded.

## Jataka Parijata ch. 17 - Kalachakra dasha, 111 verses (read in full 13 Nov 2026)
Source: sanskritdocuments.org e-text. My reading; not reviewed by a Sanskrit scholar.
- **What it is.** A dasha of SIGNS, not planets, set by the quarter of the Moon's star at birth. Years belong to the sign's lord (v.6, repeated v.106): Sun 5, Moon 21, Mars 7, Mercury 9, Jupiter 10, Venus 16, Saturn 4.
- **Two directions** (v.7-11): star groups counted forward from Aries (Ashvini, Punarvasu, Hasta, Mula, Purva Bhadrapada groups) and backward from Scorpio (Rohini, Magha, Vishakha, Shravana groups). Each star quarter has a "body" sign and a "life" sign (v.13-14, 26-27).
- **Sequences** are given as eight coded word-strings each way (v.102-105 and the two lists after). The e-text is damaged here (a verse number repeated, one marked with a star), so the sequences CANNOT be taken from this file alone. Use the sequences already decoded from BPHS 46 (kb/sources/bphs/ch46-50.md); this chapter serves only as a cross-check where it is legible.
- **Results by house** (v.47-75): the dasha of the sign in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th, 11th house is good; of the 6th, 8th, 12th bad. Modified by whether the sign is a benefic's or a malefic's and by who sits there (v.48-50).
- **Lord's strength** (v.77-81): a strong sign lord doubles the good; a weak, combust, debilitated lord in 6/8/12 doubles the bad.
- **Three jumps** (v.89-101): frog jump (Virgo-Cancer, Leo-Gemini), back-going (Cancer-Leo), lion's glance (Pisces-Scorpio, Sagittarius-Aries). The text ties these change-overs to trouble. Much of the wording is about death and illness of the person and relatives; under our rules none of that is passed on.
- **Sub-periods** (v.107-109): by proportion of the lords' years within the total of the cycle.
- **Gap this exposes:** the engine has Vimshottari, Yogini, Ashtottari and Chara, but NOT Kalachakra. Parashara keeps Vimshottari as the chief dasha (BPHS 46.3) but gives Kalachakra over a hundred verses; the sequences are already decoded in kb/sources/bphs/ch46-50.md. It is a second, independent clock that we have never built or tested. It depends on the Moon's star-quarter, so it is sensitive to birth time.
- **Candidate rule (NOT tested - needs the dasha built first):** event is good when the running Kalachakra sign is house 1,2,3,4,5,7,9,10,11 from the lagna, bad when 6, 8 or 12 (v.47-75).

## Vriddha Yavana Jataka ch. 8 - Ashtakavarga, 129 verses (read in full 13 Nov 2026)
Source: sanskritdocuments.org e-text. Gaps marked by dots: the Moon's table (v.3-4) and Mars's (v.5-6) are incomplete, and the first half of the method verse (v.16) is missing.
- **v.1-15: the point tables** for the seven planets. NOT compared value by value with the engine's tables (from BPHS / Brihat Jataka): the verses use number-words that I have not decoded one by one, and two tables are damaged. No claim of agreement is made.
- **Naming is reversed from the southern books:** here a "line" (rekha) is the GOOD mark and a "dot" (bindu) the BAD one - the same usage as Varahamihira. Anyone comparing with Jataka Parijata, where the dot is good, must keep this in mind.
- **v.16-17: method (uncertain reading - half the verse is missing).** The planet gives the result "named after its own eighth part". A stronger planet beside it overrides the result; if equal, the natural result stands.
- **v.18-129: results.** For each planet, eight verses for sitting on a LINE in the 1st to 8th "place" (all good) and eight for a DOT (all bad). v.58 says in so many words that these are results the planet gives **in its dasha**. So the chapter is a dasha-quality rule: a planet standing in an eighth-part whose owner gave it a point gives good in its period; otherwise bad.
- **What the eight "places" are:** most likely the eight parts of a sign (3 deg 45 min each), each owned by one of the seven planets or the lagna. The ORDER of owners is not given in this chapter; the order the engine uses (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Lagna) comes from later practice and is tagged UNSOURCED until found in a text.
- **Candidate rule (NOT tested, by decision):** sub-lord good if the owner of its natal eighth-part gave it a point, bad if not. The engine can already compute this. Held back because (a) the reading of v.16 is uncertain, (b) the owner order is unsourced, (c) it would be the tenth direction rule on the same 25 events. It goes to the design stage with the others.

## Daivajna Vallabha - all 15 chapters, 248 verses (read in full 13 Nov 2026)
Source: sanskritdocuments.org e-text (transliteration by Manish Shrivastaw). Attributed in its last verse to Varahamihira. NOTE ON USE: the e-text carries the line "only for personal reading and use, should not be exploited commercially". The Sanskrit itself is ancient and free; this typed copy is for private study. Our notes below are our own reading, not a copy. If the project is ever sold as a service, re-read from a scanned print edition.
A book on QUESTION CHARTS only (the chart of the moment a question is asked). Nothing in it applies to a birth chart.
- **Ch. 1 (13 v.) Ten states of a planet** by position: exalted "blazing", debilitated "wretched", own sign "at ease", friend's sign "glad", enemy's sign "asleep", beaten in planetary war "pressed", combust "robbed", moving toward debilitation "losing strength", moving toward exaltation "gaining strength", in benefic divisions "extra strong" (v.3-6); result of each (v.7-8). How and where the question should be put (v.9-13).
- **Ch. 2 (26 v.) Good or bad in general.** Count: five good signs = gain, five bad = loss (v.1). A house looked at or joined by its own lord, or by benefics, grows; with malefics it is harmed (v.2). What each of the twelve houses stands for in a question (v.4-16). **The house of the thing asked about is treated as the lagna** (v.18-19). Core test (v.20-21): a head-rising sign, benefics in the lagna, benefics in 1/4/7/10/5/9 and malefics in 3/6/11 = success; the reverse = failure.
- **Ch. 3 (5 v.) Gain or no gain.** Benefics in 11, 7, 5, 3 give gain; malefics there do not (v.1). Moon in 7/10/2/6/3/11 seen by a benefic = gain (v.2). Benefics in angles and trines plus malefics in 3/6/11 = quick gain (v.3).
- **Ch. 4-6 (33 v.) Travel, an enemy's approach, the absent person.** Fixed sign rising = no movement, nothing changes; movable sign = it happens; dual sign = half and half (4.1; repeated 14.12-13 as a general rule for every kind of question). **Timing devices:** the house number of the strongest planet from the lagna = months until return (4.8), doubled for a fixed navamsa, tripled for a dual one (4.9); the number of signs between lagna and Moon = days (5.12); house number times twelve = days (6.11); the 7th lord turning retrograde = the time of return (4.10).
- **Ch. 7 (29 v.) Victory and defeat** - war questions. Not used.
- **Ch. 8 (8 v.) Illness.** States outcomes including death. NOT passed on under our rules. The usable part: fixed sign = no change, movable = change (v.8).
- **Ch. 9 (17 v.) Lost or stolen things.** Fixed sign or vargottama lagna = the thing is still in the place, taken by one's own people (v.1); dual = by a neighbour (v.2). Recovery: full Moon or a benefic in the lagna, benefics in 2, 3, 4, 7, 10, 11 (v.11-15); malefic lagna seen by a malefic = gone for good (v.16). Direction from planets in angles, distance from navamsas passed (v.9).
- **Ch. 10 (13 v.) Reading the unspoken thought:** which person (v.1-4), size, colour and shape of an object (v.6-8), whether it is mineral, plant or living (v.9-13).
- **Ch. 11 (4 v.) Rain.**
- **Ch. 12 (13 v.) Marriage questions.** Moon in 3/5/6/7/11 from the lagna seen by Sun, Jupiter, Mercury = marriage follows (v.1, 11); Venus looking at Mercury or Moon in the lagna = soon (v.12); Saturn in an even sign = the match is obtained, odd = not (v.3). v.5-10 state death of a spouse, widowhood and the character of the woman - NOT passed on.
- **Ch. 13 (12 v.) Pregnancy and sex of the child** - NOT used (our rule: never state a child's sex).
- **Ch. 14 (32 v.) Mixed.** General fixed/movable/dual rule (v.12-13). A shadow method: length of a gnomon's shadow times the lagna's minutes, divided by seven, remainder names a planet; benefic = success (v.18-20); the remaining planet gives the UNIT of time - Mars or Sun days, Venus or Moon fortnights, Jupiter months, Mercury seasons, Saturn years (v.22). Dream questions (v.24-27). First letter of the question to find the lagna when the time is unknown (v.29-32).
- **Ch. 15 (43 v.) Lagna chapter - setting out on a campaign.** Twelve houses as body, treasury, soldiers, vehicles, counsel, enemy, road, life, mind, action, gain, minister (v.1). Benefics strengthen every house except the 6th; malefics harm all except 3 and 11 (and Sun and Mars in the 10th are good) (v.2-9). Thirty-six decanates each given a subject (v.11-17). Mostly war. Not used.

**What this gives the project**
- A second, complete source for question charts beside Shatpanchashika, by the same author. The two agree on the core test (benefics in angles and trines, malefics in 3/6/11, fixed/movable/dual sign).
- These rules give a YES or NO and sometimes a count of days or months - exactly the kind of statement that can be scored quickly. That is why a question-chart module is the fastest honest test of whether any of this predicts.
- Usable subjects: gain or no gain, will a journey or return happen, lost article, will a marriage proposal go through, general success of an undertaking. Excluded: illness outcome, death, widowhood, child's sex, war.
- Limit: the texts assume the question is asked sincerely, once, at a moment not chosen for the chart. A test must record the moment automatically.

## Jataka Parijata ch. 6 - "What breaks a chart", 102 verses (read in full 13 Nov 2026)
Source: sanskritdocuments.org e-text, many typing errors; my reading.
Four things break a chart's promise (v.1): combinations that cancel raja yogas, "Reka" combinations, poverty combinations, servitude combinations. Then bodily defects (v.46-59) and illness (v.60-98).
- **Raja-yoga breakers, v.2-20.** Mostly single fixed pictures (Sun in Aries but Libra navamsa seen by a malefic; Venus in Virgo and Virgo navamsa; Sun at its deepest fall). General statements worth keeping:
  - v.10: Moon at the END of a movable sign, the START of a fixed sign or the MIDDLE of a dual sign, weak, with the lagna unseen by any planet - a raja yoga is destroyed.
  - v.11: lords of the lagna, Moon sign and 9th, with Sun, Moon and Jupiter, in enemy or debilitation divisions - dependent on others.
  - v.18: all malefics in angles in debilitation or enemy signs, benefics in 6/8/12 looking on, cruel sixtieth-parts - the raja yoga is killed.
- **Reka, v.21-27.** Built from: lagna lord weak and seen by the 8th lord; Jupiter combust; 4th lord combust and seen by the 12th lord; 4th lord seen by the 6th lord or with the 8th lord; 9th lord combust with lagna lord and 2nd lord debilitated; three planets debilitated or combust with the lagna lord weak or in a bad house. v.25 is a TIMING line: such planets act in the first, middle or last third of life according to their place.
- **Poverty, v.28-38.** Structural tests: the 8th lord stronger than the 9th lord (v.28); the 11th lord combust, weak and outside the angles (v.28); the 12th lord stronger than the lagna lord (v.30); fixed lagna with all malefics in angles and trines and benefics outside the angles (v.33).
- **v.36 - the navamsa reversal, stated outright:** a planet in its exaltation sign but a debilitated navamsa quickly gives LOW results; a debilitated planet in an exalted navamsa gives GOOD results. This is the sixth place in this book that says so. No rule in our engine uses it. It belongs on the build list near the top.
- **Servitude, v.39-45.** Fixed pictures; several turn on planets at sign junctions (v.41-43) and on night birth in the dark fortnight.
- **Bodily defects v.46-59 and illness v.60-98.** Read. NOT used: our rules forbid medical statements, and these are single-picture verses with no general principle.
- **v.101 the counter-case:** Jupiter strong in good divisions in the lagna or the 4th, planets in 2, 11 and the upachaya houses, lagna lord strong - a happy life.
- **Use for the project:** the engine's yoga catalogue lists raja yogas but applies only the cancellations from ch. 7. The structural tests above (8th lord vs 9th lord strength, 12th lord vs lagna lord strength, 11th lord combust, Moon at the weak part of its sign, v.36 reversal) are measurable from quantities the engine already computes and are candidates for the "promise" side of a reading. None is a timing rule.
