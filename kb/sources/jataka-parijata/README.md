# Jataka Parijata of Vaidyanatha — what was read in the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026

Source: sanskritdocuments.org `jAtakapArijAtaH.itx` (transliterated by Radu Canahai; last update 6 Mar 2022). Eighteen chapters, about 300,000 characters. **The file itself is headed "Needs proofreading" and has three lost pages in chapter 2**; where a verse is plainly corrupt this is said. Same terms of use as the other files: my translation only, no Sanskrit reproduced.

Vaidyanatha (15th century) says in his opening that he compiled "from the Saravali and other chief texts" (1.3) and from "Garga, Parashara and others" (1.1). He quotes Varahamihira verbatim in many places (marked "horAyAm"). So this is a late compilation: useful as a witness to what the tradition held, not as an independent source.

## What ch. 1-2 settle
- **1.26-28 Trine-sign portions:** Sun, Venus, Saturn and Jupiter - the first 20 degrees of the trine-sign, then own sign. Moon - first 3 degrees of Taurus exaltation, the rest trine-sign. Mars - first 12 degrees of Aries. Mercury - first half of Virgo exaltation, the next 10 degrees trine-sign, the last 5 own sign. Rahu: trine-sign Aquarius, exaltation Gemini, own sign Virgo. **Differs from Parashara 3.51-54** for Jupiter (10 degrees), Venus (15) and Mercury (15-20 only). The engine follows Parashara.
- 1.30-36 Ten divisions and their lords; 1.44-46 the names of the grades (two good divisions Parijata, three Uttama, four Gopura, five Simhasana, six Paravata, seven Devaloka, nine Airavata).
- **1.57 "The Moon's degrees that signal danger, in elections and in births"**, in letter-number code. Decoded: Aries 8, Taurus 25, Gemini 22, Cancer 22, Leo 21, Virgo 1, Libra 4, Scorpio 23, Sagittarius 18, Capricorn 20, Aquarius 20, Pisces 10 (the words for Aries and Libra are corrupt in this e-text and are restored from the list as usually quoted). **This is the only such list in the chapter, it is for the Moon alone, and it is not the Moon column of the eleven-column "fatal degrees" table in `kb/foundations/strength-systems.md`.** That table's attribution to this text is therefore not supported; it stays "source unidentified".
- **1.58 Pushkara degrees**, in letter-number code. Decoded: 21, 14, 18, 8, 19, 9, 24, 11, 23, 14, 19, 9 for Aries to Pisces - **exactly the column the knowledge base attributes to this verse. Confirmed.**
- 2.8-10 Benefics: Moon, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus. Malefics: waning Moon, Saturn, Sun, Rahu, Ketu, Mars, and Mercury joined with a malefic. The Moon: middling in the first ten days of the bright half, very good in the second ten, weak in the last ten.
- **2.16-18 Ten states:** blazing (exalted or trine-sign), at ease (own sign), glad (friend's sign), calm (in a benefic's divisions), able (bright rays), oppressed (defeated in planetary war), poor (enemy's sign or navamsa), wicked (in malefics' divisions), afraid (debilitated), crippled (combust). **A different list from Parashara 45.7-9** (nine states; "crippled" there is "with a malefic", "angry" is "with the Sun"). The worksheet prints Parashara's.
- **2.21 The nine gems:** ruby for the Sun, pearl for the Moon, coral for Mars, emerald for Mercury, topaz for Jupiter, [Venus's word is corrupt in this e-text - diamond in every printed edition], blue sapphire for Saturn, hessonite and cat's-eye for the other two. The classical verse the remedies file refers to. Confirmed, with that one corrupt word.
- 2.30-31 Aspects: quarter on the 3rd and 10th, half on the trines, three quarters on the 4th and 8th, full on the 7th; Saturn, Jupiter and Mars strong in the first three (as Varahamihira 2.13).
- **2.40 Required strength in rupas: Sun 6.5, Jupiter 6.5, Venus 5.5, Mercury 7, Moon 6, Saturn and Mars 5.** With Parashara 27.32-33 this makes two texts for 6.5 for the Sun; Raman and AstroSage print 5. The worksheet now ranks by the texts' figure and shows the printed one beside it.
- 2.41-44 Temporary and natural friendships - as in the data file.
- 2.49-50 Significations of the planets; Rahu the paternal grandfather, Ketu the maternal grandfather. **2.51 House significators:** Sun; Jupiter; Mars; Moon and Mercury; Jupiter; Saturn and Mars; Venus; Saturn; Sun and Jupiter; Jupiter, Sun, Mercury and Saturn; Jupiter; Saturn - for houses 1 to 12.
- **2.52 "Venus in the 7th, Mercury in the 4th and Jupiter in the 5th cause trouble in every chart; Saturn in the 8th always gives good."** **2.72 "Fruitless: the Moon with the Sun, Mercury in the 4th, Jupiter in the 5th, Mars in the 2nd, Venus in the 6th, Saturn in the 7th."** These two verses are the textual base of the saying that a significator in its own house harms it. Parashara and Mantreshvara do not say it as a rule (Mantreshvara: "others say"). So it is a 15th-century compiler's rule, with two lists that do not agree with each other (Venus in the 7th in one, in the 6th in the other). Low weight.
- 2.60 Each planet is strengthened by another: Saturn by the Sun, Mars by Saturn, Jupiter by Mars, the Moon by Jupiter, Venus by the Moon, Mercury by Venus, the Moon by Mercury.
- 2.61-68 When each planet is strong (sign, division, day, hour, half of the year, part of the sign). 2.69 "Results stated for houses, yogas and dashas do not come out properly from planets that lack these strengths."
- 2.73-74 Each planet in turn removes the faults of those before it: Mercury Rahu's; Saturn both; Mars the three; Venus the four; Jupiter the five; the Moon the six; the Sun all seven, most of all in the northern course.
- 2.75-81 Troubles caused by each planet [illness wordings - not used].
- **2.82 Sun and Mars give at the start of a sign, Saturn and the Moon at its end, Jupiter and Venus in the middle, Mercury throughout** (as Varahamihira 22.6). **2.86 A planet in a head-rising sign gives at the start of its period, in a back-rising sign at the end, in a both-ways sign always** (as Varahamihira 22.5, Parashara 50.88).
- 2.84-85 Age states by six-degree steps; awake in exaltation or own navamsa, dreaming in a friend's, asleep in debilitation or an enemy's.

## Verses read from later chapters
- **5.52-53 Seven "gap" planets:** the 8th lord, a planet in the 8th, one aspecting the 8th, the lord of the 22nd drekkana, a planet with the 8th lord, **the lord of the 64th navamsa**, and the 8th lord's great enemy. So the 64th navamsa is in this text, in the chapter on length of life (which the project does not use). The verse does not say from where the 64 are counted.
- **7.23 Cancellation of debilitation:** the lord of the sign where a planet is debilitated, and the lord of that planet's exaltation sign, in a kendra from the Moon - "a righteous ruler". (Same as Phaladeepika 7.26.)
- **7.24** The lord of the navamsa occupied by the debilitated planet in a trine or kendra, with a movable sign rising, or the lagna lord in a movable sign or navamsa. (New to the catalogue: `neecha_bhanga_12`.)
- **7.30** Two, three or four debilitated planets in good sixtieth-parts or in their own exaltation navamsa. (The base of "debilitated but exalted in navamsa"; the verse speaks of several planets.)
- **Not found in ch. 7:** "the planet exalted in that sign in a kendra", "exchange with the dispositor", "aspected by another debilitated planet", "dispositor and exaltation lord in mutual kendras". Those four catalogue entries are now marked as modern list items with no verse.
- **7.71-83 Kemadruma.** 7.71 The Moon in the lagna or the 7th without Jupiter's aspect; or all planets weak with very few ashtakavarga points. 7.72-73 Absence of Sunapha, Anapha and Durudhara. 7.74-79 Further poverty combinations built on a weak, afflicted Moon. **7.80-81 Exceptions: the Moon in a kendra, or Venus so, aspected by Jupiter; the Moon with a benefic, or even between malefics, aspected by Jupiter; the Moon in her own, exaltation or great friend's navamsa aspected by Jupiter.** 7.82 "Where Kemadruma applies, raja yogas come to nothing, like elephants at the sight of a lion." 7.83 quotes Varahamihira 13.3.
- **7.108-109 Shakata:** Jupiter in the 6th or 8th from the Moon and not in a kendra from the lagna. (No 12th; the kendra test is on Jupiter. Phaladeepika 6.14 has the Moon in the 6th, 8th or 12th from Jupiter, cancelled by the Moon in a kendra.)
- 7.110 Results of the graded divisions.

## Ch. 14 — 7th, 8th and 9th houses, 103 verses (read in full, second sitting, 12 Nov 2026)
**7th house (v.1-47).** Most verses describe a spouse's character, body or number of spouses, or loss of a spouse; those are not used. What is usable:
- v.2 7th lord badly placed, in a malefic's sign, under malefic aspect: middling result for the 7th; otherwise all good.
- v.22, 24 7th lord in a kendra under benefic aspect, in a benefic's sign or navamsa: a devoted spouse of good family; in malefic divisions the reverse.
- **v.27 Marriage when Jupiter transits the sign given by adding the longitudes of the lagna lord and the 7th lord** (or of the lord of the Moon's star and the 7th lord). This confirms rule MAR-T10, which had no source.
- **v.28 In the period of the stronger of Venus, the Moon, and the lords of the sign and navamsa holding the 7th lord, when Jupiter transits a trine of that sign or navamsa** (the same rule as Phaladeepika 10.13).
- **v.29 Marriage in the period and sub-period of: the lord of the sign holding Venus and the 7th; the lord of the sign holding the 2nd lord; the 10th and 8th lords; a planet in the 7th joined with the 7th lord.**
- v.30 A benefic in a benefic's sign gives at the start of its period; a benefic in a malefic's sign gives marriage and the like in the middle; a malefic in a malefic's sign at the end; with a benefic in a benefic's sign, throughout.
- v.31 By transit: the Moon and Jupiter in the sign of the lord of the navamsa of the lagna lord; or Jupiter in the sign of the 7th lord or Venus.
- v.32 The spouse born with the lagna or navamsa of the 7th lord or the lagna lord "pleases the partner's mind". v.33 The spouse's Moon sign is among the signs occupying or aspecting the 7th; direction and region from Venus.
- **v.34 "Mars in the 2nd, 12th, 7th, 4th or 8th at birth harms the wife; likewise in a girl's chart it harms the husband." v.36 "Malefics in the 7th and 2nd bring the sorrow of separation - but joined to a partner with the same combination, he lives with children and wealth."** **Correction of my earlier note:** the keyword search found no "kuja dosha" because the verse does not use that name. The list that counts the 2nd house and leaves out the lagna is therefore in this 15th-century text; Parashara's (BPHS 80.47) is 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, 12th. Both carry the same cancellation - both partners having it.
- v.35, 38-46 not used (widowhood, body description, timing of a spouse's death).

**8th house (v.48-64).** Length of life and timing of death - not used. Recorded only: v.57 the 8th lord's period brings illness when it sits in the 8th; the lagna lord's period brings bodily trouble first and recovery after.

**9th house (v.65-103).**
- v.65 Fortune, influence, teacher, duty, austerity and good works are judged from the 9th and from Jupiter. 9th lord and Jupiter in good divisions with a benefic in the 9th: fortune comes.
- v.66 Planets in the 9th that are malefic, in enemy or debilitation signs or combust: without fame, money or merit. **But even a malefic in its exaltation or a friend's sign in the 9th gives fortune.**
- **v.67 The 9th under the influence of benefics and its own lord gives fortune; the lord of the sign holding the 9th lord is "the maker of fortune", the 9th lord "the one who ripens it" and the 5th lord "the one who wakes it".**
- v.68 Four or five strong planets in the 9th in good divisions: great fortune - at home if in own or exalted divisions, abroad if in others'.
- v.69-72 Jupiter in the 9th under each planet's aspect. v.73-87 Two and three planets together in the 9th (occupational sketches). v.88 Planets in the 9th without Jupiter or Mercury: troubled.
- v.89-90 9th lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th under debilitated or enemy planets in harsh divisions: without fortune; with benefics and benefic aspect: fame, money, fortune.
- v.93-98 Devotion to a teacher, dutifulness, austerity - from Jupiter, the 9th lord and their divisions. v.99-100 not used (father's death). v.101-103 A strong 9th lord with Jupiter and Venus: given to recitation and meditation.

## Ch. 16 — Women's charts, 54 verses (read in full)
- **v.4-6 The method.** Results that fit a man are read for her husband. Her own body and wellbeing from the Moon or the lagna, whichever is stronger; from that same point: means, looks and strength; **children from the 9th from it; the husband's standing from the 7th**; married good fortune from the 8th. Then "some say": **children from the 5th**, the husband's happiness from the 7th, renunciation from planets in the 9th. "Everything else is the same for women and men." So this text records both views on a woman's children - the 9th (Mantreshvara 11.1) and the 5th (Parashara 80.3) - and prefers the 9th.
- v.7-11 = Varahamihira 24.2 and 24.15 (even and odd signs; the learned woman). v.12-18 Character by thirtieth-part - not used. v.19-24, 27, 29-35, 43-45 Widowhood, conduct, death - not used.
- v.23 Sun in the 7th under an enemy planet's aspect: left by the husband; mixed planets in the 7th: marries again; Saturn in the 7th under an enemy's aspect: grows old unmarried (as Varahamihira 24.8-9).
- v.25 A benefic's aspect on the 7th sign and navamsa: loved by her husband. **v.28 Benefics in the 9th, even with a malefic in the 7th or 8th: a long life together with the husband's prosperity, children and comfort.**
- v.36-39 Moon and Venus rising; Jupiter and Mercury rising; Jupiter in the 9th, 5th or a kendra in exaltation or the like: good conduct, good children, "brings fame to both families".
- v.40-42 = Varahamihira 24.16: "to be considered at birth, at the wedding, at a query and at the choosing; what is said for women applies to their husbands too."
- v.46-47 Brothers, from the 3rd lord.
- **v.48-53 The husband.** v.48 = Varahamihira 24.8. v.49-50 By the planet in the 7th or its navamsa: Sun - gentle, playful; Moon - comfortable; Mars - [fond of other women]; Mercury - learned; Jupiter - self-controlled, excellent; Venus - handsome, happy; Saturn - old and dull. v.52-53 By a planet actually in the 7th: Sun - fair, passionate, sharp-eyed; Moon - good-looking, virtuous, lean, prone to illness; Mars - [harsh], lazy, a clever talker, ruddy; Mercury - learned, well-off, accomplished; Jupiter - long-lived, means like a ruler's; Venus - handsome, witty, a poet; Saturn - an aged, unsteady body; Rahu or Ketu - [low-minded].
- v.54 The husband's direction, country, standing, duty and work are read from her chart; the 7th lord in a kendra gives a large, auspicious wedding.
- Use: confirms the worksheet's husband-by-7th block (three texts now give the same list) and adds v.28 as a mitigating rule. Descriptions of other people remain the project's weakest category.

## Ch. 13 — 5th and 6th houses, about 81 verses (read in full)
**5th house.** v.1 Children, deity, a ruler's favour, the father, intelligence and merit are judged from the 5th; children also from the 5th from Jupiter (v.9).
- v.8 = Varahamihira 23.1 (the 5th and 7th prosper when held or aspected by benefics or their own lord, from lagna or Moon).
- **v.14, 16-19 Children are gained when:** the 5th, its lord or Jupiter is joined or aspected by a benefic; the lagna lord is in the 5th with a strong 5th lord and a fully strong Jupiter; a strong Jupiter in the 5th is aspected by the lagna lord; Jupiter and the 5th lord in good divisions are aspected by the 9th lord. The 5th lord in a kendra or trine in a benefic's sign with a benefic: a child early.
- v.20-28, 32-37 Combinations for want of children and the curses behind them - recorded as existing; **never stated** (v.24 is Parashara 83.7 word for word).
- v.29, 38-43 Adoption (the 5th in a sign of Mercury or Saturn with Saturn or Mandi; a strong planet in the 5th unaspected by the 5th lord).
- **v.30-31 Jupiter in the 5th by sign:** in Pisces few children, in Sagittarius with difficulty, in Cancer and Aquarius [none]. So this text's caution about Jupiter in the 5th is tied to four signs, not to the placement as such. Never stated to a person.
- v.44-45 A child after delay. v.46-48 Arithmetic for the number of children - **not used.**
- **v.48-50 Timing.** Jupiter transiting a trine of the sign given by adding the longitudes of the 5th lord and the lagna lord. The periods and sub-periods of the lords of the 5th counted from Jupiter, the Moon and the lagna, and of the 9th from there; of the planet ruling the star of (5th lord + 7th lord); of planets joining or aspecting those. Strong benefics give children in their periods; weak lords of bad houses linked to the 5th lord and its significator do the reverse.
- v.52-57 The father: his wellbeing from the 9th lord and the Sun in good divisions. A child whose lagna is the father's 10th sign takes after him; the 3rd star from the father's - obedient; his 6th or 8th - at odds. v.58-66 not used (parents' death).
- v.67-68 5th lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th or invisible: slow mind; in a kendra or trine with Mercury and Jupiter and strong: intelligent, reads intentions.

**6th house (v.71-80).** Illness, rivals, vices and wounds from the 6th and from Mars. **"Jupiter in the 6th: he overcomes his rivals"** (v.71 - the same reading the project learned from its own cases). Benefics on the 6th: no fear from rivals; malefics: wounds, fear, a mark. v.74 The 6th lord debilitated, retrograde or combust: many rivals. v.77-78 Where on the body a mark or wound falls, by house and planet. v.80 A weak 6th lord under malefics: troubled by rivals; the 6th lord badly placed with a strong lagna lord: rivals come to nothing.

## Ch. 15 — 10th, 11th and 12th houses, 83 verses (read in full)
**10th house.** v.1 Command, honour, dress, trade, sleep, farming, renunciation, learning, work, livelihood, fame and knowledge - judged from the 10th lord, Sun, Mercury, Jupiter and Saturn.
- v.2-14 Religious works and pilgrimage [period detail].
- **v.5 Lagna lord and 10th lord together, or one planet ruling both: he does his works with money he earned himself.** (This is the verse behind rule CAR-36, "self-made", cited before through a secondary page as "XI.18".)
- v.15-42 Renunciation: four or five strong planets together in a kendra or trine; the order by the strongest; long lists of four-planet groups; v.40-42 = Varahamihira 15.2-3.
- **v.43 = Varahamihira 10.1.** v.43½ "Take the stronger of the lagna and the Moon; the livelihood is by the lord of the navamsa sign of the 10th lord from it."
- **v.44-50 Work by that navamsa lord.** Sun: medicines, wool, grasses, grain, gold, pearls; go-between work. Moon: trade in things from water, farming, music, support of a woman of rank, cloth. Mars: metals, disputes, fighting, fire, daring. Mercury: crafts, writing, texts, astrology and calculation, recitation, priestly work. Jupiter: service of the learned, teaching, law and ethics, religious instruction. Venus: gold, gems, horses, cattle, sweets and dairy, through a woman. Saturn: hard or low work, woodcraft, carrying loads. (Fuller than Varahamihira's list; the worksheet's livelihood pointer uses this method.)
- v.51 Benefics in all four kendras: head of the family, or dear to rulers. v.52-58 Planets in the 10th from the Moon: Sun - success in what is begun; Mars - daring; Mercury - learned; Jupiter - like a ruler; Venus - enjoys life; Saturn - worn by grief; and pairs.
- v.59-65 Authority and name: the 10th lord a benefic or with benefics; Moon in Cancer aspected by Jupiter and Venus. Mars and the 4th lord strong in a kendra, trine or the 11th with the 10th lord under Venus and the Moon: wealth from land and cattle; Mercury linked to the 10th: trade.

**11th house (v.66-71).** "All agree that every kind of gain is from the 11th; a fully strong planet there gives money": Sun - through relatives; Moon - the mother's side; Mars - brothers; Mercury - friends, employers, the learned, a maternal uncle; Jupiter - learning, good conduct, children; Venus - women, poetry, drama, music; Saturn - the work of servants. A benefic in the 11th: clean money; a malefic: ill-gotten; mixed: mixed. v.71 The 11th lord in a kendra or trine: wealthy even with a malefic in the 11th; the money comes in that planet's period and from its direction.

**12th house (v.75-83).** A strong malefic in the 12th: loss of what was built up. A benefic there: generous. Sun or waning Moon: money lost to the authorities; Mars: lost in many ways; **Jupiter, Moon or Venus in the 12th protect money.** v.79-81 Comfort of the bed. v.82 The after-state [not used].
- **v.83 Closing rule: the lagna lord, strong and under benefic aspect, makes whatever house it sits in prosper greatly - the reverse when debilitated or in an enemy's sign; a weak 8th lord harms the house it sits in.**

## Ch. 18 — Results of dashas, v.1-76 read (general rules, the Sun's and part of the Moon's period)
- v.2 "As the yoga follows strength, so the dasha follows the yoga; the result of the dasha is the whole result."
- **v.3 Vimshottari, from Krittika: Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Ketu, Venus - years in letter-number code, decoded: 6, 10, 7, 18, 16, 19, 17, 7, 20** (the word for Saturn is misprinted in the e-text). So this text teaches Vimshottari, as Parashara and Mantreshvara do and Varahamihira does not.
- **v.4-5 A dasha lord gives good when** rising toward exaltation, strong, with many Ashtakavarga points, in the 10th, 11th or lagna, in exaltation or the like divisions; **bad when** with Mandi or in its sign, with few points, in an enemy's or debilitation sign, combust, with a malefic, or at the junction of two houses or two signs.
- **v.6-9, 14 The moment a dasha begins:** good when its lord is then in the lagna or the 3rd, 6th, 10th or 11th from it; and when the Moon is then in the dasha lord's friend's or exaltation sign, or in the 7th, a trine or an upachaya house from it (= Varahamihira 8.10). The natal house in which that Moon falls is the one that grows.
- v.10-11 A dasha gives what the birth chart already promised; more good than bad gives good, equal gives mixed. v.12-13 = Varahamihira 8.20, 8.22.
- **v.15 The dasha lord in a good house, in own, exaltation or a friend's sign, under a benefic or friend: good in its period and sub-period; in a kendra or trine, very good; malefics in the 3rd, 6th, 11th likewise.**
- **v.16 Dasha lord and sub-lord mutual friends and both strong: good. Mutual enemies and weak: harm.**
- v.17-18 A planet gives in its period the matters of the house it sits in - well if it is a benefic, badly if a malefic; joined to a benefic very well, joined to a malefic without fruit.
- v.19-22 The period of the planet that signifies or does a thing brings that thing: the 10th lord's - works; the 9th lord's - merit and austerity; the planet of rulership - favour of those in power.
- v.24 Head-rising sign: at the start of the period; back-rising: at the end; both-ways: throughout. (Fourth text for this rule.)
- **v.25 Sub-periods of the lords of the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th and 9th stars counted from the birth star (the "wealth", "wellbeing", "achieving", "friend" and "great friend" stars) bring prosperity.** The 6th lord's period brings complaint; a combust planet's, loss among relatives.
- v.27 A planet at a sign junction: grief and illness in its period.
- **v.28 A debilitated planet, and a planet joined to it, cannot give good; nor one joined to Rahu; nor the planet in its sign or the lord of its sign.**
- v.29 The great enemy of a house's lord, or a planet in a sign with no points: in its period, harm to that house.
- **v.30 The obstruction-lord and planets with it: grief and illness. Dasha lord and sub-lord in the 6th and 8th from each other: fear, leaving one's place, loss; when they are friends and benefics, mixed.** (The same rule as Parashara's sub-lord test, here stated for the mutual 6th-8th.) v.53 repeats it: loss of position.
- v.31-32 The period by the planet's state (the ten states of 2.16-18): blazing - rule, money, fame; at ease - good conduct, health, comfort; glad - favour, prosperity; calm - health, enthusiasm; able - learning, success; oppressed - fear from thieves, rivals, authority; poor - want; crippled - grief and illness; wicked - mental strain; afraid - fear.
- v.33-40 Other dashas (from the strongest of several stars; Utpanna, Adhana, Kshema; Gulika's; Shula) - recorded only; several are death-timing devices and are not used.
- v.41-52 Sub-periods of malefics inside the periods of each house lord: loss in that house's matters; of benefics, gain (for example 5th lord's period: benefic sub-period - children, money, favour; malefic - anxiety over children).
- v.54-56 not used (death).
- **v.57-58 Which part of a period gives what: a malefic gives the result of its dignity at the start, of its house in the middle, of the aspects on it at the end; a benefic gives the house at the start, the sign in the middle, the aspects at the end.**
- v.59-71 The Sun's period and its nine sub-periods. v.71 An exalted planet in a debilitation navamsa gives trouble; a debilitated one in an exaltation navamsa gives well at the end.
- v.72-76 The Moon's period begun.
- **Not read: v.77 to the end** - the rest of the list of sub-periods planet by planet. Like Phaladeepika ch. 21 it is an unconditional list, used only as a tie-breaker.
