# Brihat Jataka — chapter notes (my translation; Sanskrit not reproduced)

## Ch. 1 — Signs, 20 verses
- v.2 "For those whose effort broke in crossing the great ocean of horoscopy I begin a small boat of a treatise - short, varied in metre, full of meaning."
- v.3 **"Some derive *hora* from *ahoratra* (day-and-night). It makes plain the ripening of what was earned by acts in a former birth."**
- v.4 Limbs of the Time-person by sign from Aries: head, face, chest, heart, belly, waist, lower abdomen, private parts, thighs, knees, shanks, feet. (This is the verse the health file cites; confirmed.)
- v.6-7 Lords of signs and navamsas; navamsas start from Aries, Capricorn, Libra, Cancer for the four triplicities. Thirtieth-parts: odd signs Mars 5, Saturn 5, Jupiter 8, Mercury 7, Venus 5; reversed in even signs. Junction signs: end of Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces.
- v.9 A planet's own sign, hora, drekkana, navamsa, twelfth-part and thirtieth-part are its "varga".
- v.10 Night signs: Taurus, Aries, Sagittarius, Cancer, Gemini, Capricorn; these (except Gemini) rise back-first; the rest are day signs and rise head-first; Pisces rises both ways. (Matches the engine.)
- v.11 Signs alternately harsh and gentle, male and female, movable-fixed-dual; directions east, south, west, north from Aries with their trines. Hora: Sun then Moon in odd signs, reversed in even. Drekkanas: lords of the sign, its 5th and 9th. v.12 "Some" give other hora and drekkana lords.
- v.13 Exaltations: Sun Aries 10, Moon Taurus 3, Mars Capricorn 28, Mercury Virgo 15, Jupiter Cancer 5, Venus Pisces 27, Saturn Libra 20; debilitation opposite. (Identical to the data file.)
- v.14 Vargottama navamsas (first, middle, last in movable, fixed, dual signs) are good. Trine-signs: Leo, Taurus, Aries, Virgo, Sagittarius, Libra, Aquarius.
- v.15-19 Names of the houses; upachaya = 3, 6, 10, 11 ("not always, say some"); kendra, panaphara, apoklima; strength of sign-types in the kendras.
- v.20 Colours of the signs. The 2nd from the Sun is "Veshi".

## Ch. 2 — Planets, 21 verses
- v.1 Sun the soul of Time, Moon the mind, Mars strength, Mercury speech, Jupiter knowledge and happiness, Venus desire, Saturn sorrow. Sun and Moon kings, Mars commander, Mercury prince, Jupiter and Venus ministers, Saturn servant.
- v.5 Colours; deities; lords of the eight directions (east Sun, south-east Venus, south Mars, south-west Rahu, west Saturn, north-west Moon, north Mercury, north-east Jupiter). **Malefics: waning Moon, Sun, Mars, Saturn; Mercury when joined with them.**
- v.6-7 Sex, elements, classes and qualities of the planets. v.8-11 Appearance and humours; tissues.
- v.12 Places, cloth, metals, seasons.
- v.13 **Aspects: on the 3rd and 10th a quarter, the 5th and 9th half, the 4th and 8th three quarters, the 7th full; Saturn, Jupiter and Mars are strongest in the first three pairs respectively.**
- v.14 Time-spans: Sun half-year, Moon a moment, Mars a day, Mercury a season, Jupiter a month, Venus a fortnight, Saturn a year. Tastes.
- v.15 One school's friendships; then Satya's rule (the lords of the 2nd, 12th, 5th, 9th, 8th, 4th from a planet's trine-sign and its exaltation lord are friends), which he accepts.
- v.16-17 The resulting natural friends, neutrals and enemies of each planet. (Checked line by line against `data/rashis.json`: identical.)
- v.18 Temporary friends: planets in the 2nd, 12th, 11th, 3rd, 10th, 4th from each other; "some add the exaltation sign". Combine natural and temporary into five grades.
- v.19-21 The strengths: by position (exaltation, friend's, own, trine-sign, own navamsa); by direction (Mercury and Jupiter east, Sun and Mars south, Saturn west, Venus and Moon north); by motion (Sun and Moon in the northern course, the others retrograde or with the Moon, and winners in planetary war); by time (Moon, Mars, Saturn by night, Mercury always, the rest by day; malefics in the dark half, benefics in the bright; lords of year, day, hour, month). Natural strength rising in the order Saturn, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Sun. (The six heads of Shadbala.)

## Ch. 8 — Dashas, 23 verses
- v.1 The strongest of lagna, Sun and Moon comes first; then planets in kendras, panapharas, apoklimas give in the first, middle and last part of life.
- v.2 The dasha of each is the span of life it contributed (ch. 7); ties by strength, then by the larger span, then by earlier rising.
- v.3-4 Sub-periods: a planet with the dasha lord takes half, in its trine a third, in the 7th a seventh, in the 4th or 8th a quarter; reduce to a common denominator.
- v.5-7 **Grades of a dasha:** planet strong in its exaltation part - "complete"; without strength - "empty"; in a debilitation or enemy navamsa - "unwelcome"; falling from exaltation - "descending"; in a friend's or exaltation navamsa - "middling"; rising from debilitation - "ascending"; in a debilitation or enemy navamsa - "lowest"; in such a navamsa but a good sign - mixed. (Parallel to BPHS 50.73-83.)
- v.9 The natural dasha: Moon 1, Mars 2, Mercury 9, Venus 20, Jupiter 18, Sun 20, Saturn 50 years; "the Yavanas say the lagna's dasha at the end is good; some do not accept it".
- v.10 **A dasha that begins with its lord in the lagna, in a friend's or benefic's division, or in the 3rd, 10th, 6th or 11th, is good; and when the Moon at that moment is in the friend's sign, exaltation, an upachaya, the trine or the 7th of the dasha lord. Otherwise the reverse.**
- v.11 Result by the sign the Moon is in when a dasha begins (own sign honour and money; Mars's sign trouble through the spouse; Mercury's learning, friends, money; Leo work in forts, forests, farming; Venus's food; Saturn's a bad partner; Jupiter's honour, money, comfort).
- v.12-18 Results of each planet's dasha: gains (from what and whom), and troubles. Sun: earnings by sharp things, travel, rulers, battle; steady effort, fame; trouble through spouse, children, money, enemies, fire, rulers. Moon: gains through advisers, the learned, sugarcane, milk, cloth, flowers, sesame, food; sleepiness; gentleness; rise and fall of name and money. Mars: money by crushing rivals, from rulers, brothers, land, wool; quarrels with children, friends, spouse, brothers; fever, bile, injury. Mercury: money through messages, friends, teachers, the learned; praise, fame, gold, land; skill in wit; harsh words, toil, confinement, mental grief. Jupiter: honour, rise of merit, intelligence, lustre, money through counsel, policy, rulers, study; gold, horses, children, elephants, cloth; ear trouble; enmity with the irreligious. Venus: pleasure in song, scents, food, drink, cloth, women, gems; friends; skill in buying and selling; farming, treasure; enmity with groups and rulers; grief through affection. Saturn: gain of donkeys, camels, birds, buffaloes, an elderly woman; headship of a guild, village or town; coarse grain; phlegm, envy, wind trouble, delusion; scolding of servants, children, spouse.
- v.19 Good dashas give good, "unwelcome" ones bad, mixed ones mixed; the lagna's dasha like its lord's.
- v.20 **Everything said of a planet anywhere - its substances, its livelihood, its house, aspect and yoga results - is to be applied in its dasha.**
- v.22-23 A good dasha shows as a good inner state; when one planet promises two opposite results, the stronger ripens; one planet does not cancel another's result - each gives its own in its own dasha.

## Ch. 9 — Ashtakavarga, 8 verses
- v.1-7 The favourable places of each of the seven planets from the eight reference points. Sun: from himself, Mars and Saturn 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11; from Venus 6, 7, 12; from Jupiter 5, 6, 9, 11; from the Moon 3, 6, 10, 11; from Mercury 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12; from the lagna 3, 4, 6, 10, 11, 12. Moon: from the lagna 3, 6, 10, 11; from Mars 2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11; from herself 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11; from the Sun 3, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11; from Saturn 3, 5, 6, 11; from Mercury 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11; from Jupiter 1, 4, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12; from Venus 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11. Venus: from the lagna 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11; from the Moon the same with 12; from herself the same as the lagna's with 10; from Saturn 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11; from the Sun 8, 11, 12; from Jupiter 5, 8, 9, 10, 11; from Mercury 3, 5, 6, 9, 11; from Mars 3, 5, 6, 9, 11, 12. (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter and Saturn are in v.3, 4, 5 and 7.)
- v.8 **"What is named is favourable, the rest unfavourable; the result ripens strongly counted from the natal sign. In upachaya houses, friend's signs, own sign and exaltation the good is full-grown; in the other houses, debilitation and enemy signs the good is poor."**

## Ch. 10 — Livelihood, 4 verses
- v.1 Money comes from the father, mother, an opponent, a friend, a brother, a woman or a servant according as the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn is in the 10th from the lagna or the Moon; and by the calling of the lord of the navamsa occupied by the lord of the 10th from the lagna, the Moon and the Sun.
- v.2-3 That navamsa lord - Sun: grasses [scents], gold, wool, medicines. Moon: farming, things from water, dependence on women. Mars: metals, fire, weapons, daring. Mercury: writing, calculation, poetry, crafts. Jupiter: the learned, the gods, mines, religious work. Venus: gems, silver, cows, buffaloes. Saturn: labour, executions [harsh work], carrying loads, low crafts.
- v.4 Money comes from the side of a friend, an enemy or one's own according to the sign that planet is in; by one's own strength when the Sun is exalted and strong; in many ways when strong benefics are in the lagna, 2nd and 11th.

## Ch. 11 — Raja yogas, 20 verses
- v.1 "The Yavanas say: with harsh planets exalted, a harsh-minded ruler. Jivasharma: not a ruler by harsh planets."
- v.2 Thirty-two combinations from Mars, Saturn, Sun, Jupiter - all or three exalted, with one of them rising; or two or one of them rising with the Moon in Cancer.
- v.3 The lagna or the Moon vargottama, aspected by four or more planets other than the Moon: twenty-two combinations.
- v.4-11, 14-18 Specific full-chart combinations (not coded: each fixes five or more planets by sign).
- v.12-13 **These make a ruler even of one low-born. Three or more strong planets in exaltation or trine-sign: rulers if of a ruling family; five or more: rulers from any family; fewer: wealthy, not rulers.** (Same as BPHS 39.44-46.)
- v.19 **The standing comes in the dasha of the planet in the 10th or the lagna, or of the strongest; it is lost in the dasha of a planet in an enemy's or debilitation sign.**
- v.20 Jupiter, Venus or Mercury rising, Saturn in the 7th, Sun in the 10th: a life of enjoyment. Benefics strong in kendras with malefics in harsh signs: head of rough men, yet wealthy.

## Ch. 12 — Nabhasa yogas, 19 verses
- v.1 Their count: 32 by one reckoning, 1,800 by the Yavanas'.
- v.2 Rajju, Musala, Nala (all planets in movable, fixed, dual signs) - "Satya's" yogas of position; Mala and Sarpa (benefics, malefics in kendras) "told by Parashara".
- v.3 "Others say the position yogas merge into Yava, Kamala, Vajra, Vihaga, Gola and the rest, and the two Dala yogas have no separate result."
- v.4 Gada: two adjacent kendras. Shakata: lagna and 7th. Vihaga: 10th and 4th. Shringataka: 9th, 5th and lagna. Hala: the other trines.
- v.5 Vajra: benefics in lagna and 7th, malefics in 4th and 10th; Yava the reverse; Kamala: mixed in the kendras; Vapi: all outside the kendras.
- v.6 **"I have given Vajra and the rest following earlier texts - but how can Mercury and Venus be in the 4th sign from the Sun?"** (Varahamihira flags an impossible combination in his own sources.)
- v.7-9 Yupa, Ishu, Shakti, Danda (four houses from each kendra); Nau, Kuta, Chatra, Chapa (seven houses from each kendra); Ardha-chandra (seven from the other houses); Samudra (six alternate houses from the 2nd); Chakra (from the lagna).
- v.10 By number of signs occupied: Vallaki 7, Damini 6, Pasha 5, Kedara 4, Shula 3, Yuga 2, Gola 1 - only when none of the earlier ones applies.
- v.11, 13-19 Results of each. v.12 The position yogas are fruitless when mixed with another.
- v.19 **"These yogas give their fixed results in all dashas."**

## Ch. 13 — Moon yogas, 9 verses
- v.1 Moon in a kendra, panaphara or apoklima from the Sun: modesty, means, knowledge, intelligence and skill are low, middling, high. Moon in her own or a great friend's navamsa, aspected by Jupiter (day birth) or Venus (night birth): wealthy and happy.
- v.2 **Adhi yoga:** benefics in the 7th, 6th and 8th from the Moon: a commander, minister or ruler; comfortable, long-lived, free of illness and fear.
- v.3 Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhara: planets other than the Sun in the 2nd, the 12th, both from the Moon. Otherwise Kemadruma "by many". **Not held when the Moon is in a kendra or joined by a planet** (or, on the other reading of the same words, when a kendra or the Moon is joined by a planet). "Some say [reckon] by kendra and navamsa - that is not established."
- v.4 31 kinds each of Sunapha and Anapha, 180 of Durudhara.
- v.5-6 Results of the four. v.7-8 Results by the planet forming it.
- v.9 **All benefics in upachaya houses from the lagna: very wealthy; from the Moon: wealthy; two: middling; fewer: little. "This prevails even over other, contrary results."**

## Ch. 14 — Two planets together, 5 verses
- Sun with Moon: maker of machines and stonework; with Mars: inclined to wrong; with Mercury: skilful, intelligent, famous, happy; with Jupiter: harsh, busy in others' work; with Venus: earns by the stage or weapons; with Saturn: skilled in metals or pottery.
- Moon with Mars: deals in tricks, women, liquor, pots; unkind to the mother; with Mercury: courteous speech, clever with money, fortunate, famous; with Jupiter: valiant, head of the family, unsteady mind, very rich; with Venus: skilled in cloth and trade; with Saturn: child of a remarried woman.
- Mars with Mercury: trades in roots, oils, forgeries; a wrestler; with Jupiter: head of a town, or a ruler, or a rich learned man; with Venus: a cowherd or wrestler, skilful, fond of others' spouses, a gambler; with Saturn: miserable, untruthful, blamed.
- Mercury with Jupiter: performer, fond of song and dance; with Venus: eloquent, lord of land and people; with Saturn: skilled in deceit, breaks his word.
- Jupiter with Venus: good learning, money, spouse, many virtues; with Saturn: a barber, potter or cook.
- Venus with Saturn: weak sight, wealth grown through a woman's support, knows writing, books and painting.
- Use: low. These are occupational sketches from the 6th century; the project uses them only as the classical basis of Part C of `kb/planets/planets-in-houses.md`.

## Ch. 15 — Renunciation, 4 verses
- v.1 Four or more strong planets in one sign: an ascetic of the order of the strongest (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Moon, Venus, Saturn, Sun name seven orders). If that planet is defeated in war, he falls from it.
- v.2 If combust: not initiated but devoted to it. If defeated and aspected by others: seeks initiation without getting it.
- v.3 The lord of the Moon's sign, unaspected by others, aspecting Saturn; or Saturn aspecting that lord when weak; or the Moon in Saturn's drekkana and a navamsa of Mars or Saturn, aspected by Saturn.
- v.4 Jupiter, the Moon and the lagna aspected by Saturn with Jupiter in the 9th, in a raja-yoga chart: a founder of a teaching. Saturn in the 9th unaspected, in a raja-yoga chart: a ruler who takes vows.

## Ch. 20 — Planets in the houses, 11 verses
- v.1-3 Sun - 1st: bold, stiff, weak sight, [hard] (in Aries: wealthy with eye trouble; in Leo: night-blind; in Libra: poor sight and poor; in Cancer: bleary-eyed); 2nd: much money, taken by the ruler, trouble in the face; 3rd: intelligent and valiant; 4th: unhappy, troubled mind; 5th: few children, little money; 6th: strong, overcomes rivals; 7th: slighted by women; 8th: few children, weak sight; 9th: children, money, comfort; 10th: comfort and valour; 11th: much money; 12th: fallen from position.
- v.4-5 Moon - 1st: [defects of speech, mind, hearing; a servant] - but in Cancer, Aries or Taurus: wealthy, many children; 2nd: wealthy, a large family; 3rd: harsh; 4th and 5th: the good of those houses; 6th: many rivals, soft body, weak digestion and desire, sharp, lazy; 7th: jealous, strong desire; 8th: restless mind, troubled by illness; 9th: fortunate, with children, friends, relatives, money; 10th: succeeds, with duty, money, intelligence, valour; 11th: famous, gains; 12th: mean, a defective limb.
- v.6 Mars - 1st: wounds on the body; 2nd: poor food; 9th: wrongdoing; elsewhere like the Sun. Mercury - 1st to 8th: learned; wealthy; hard; learned; a counsellor; without rivals; knows duty; famous for qualities; beyond that like the Sun.
- v.7 Jupiter, 1st to 12th: learned; good speech; tight with money; happy; intelligent; without rivals; surpasses the father; low; an ascetic; wealthy; gains; hard.
- v.8 Venus - 1st: skilled in love, happy; 7th: fond of quarrels, desirous; 5th: happy; elsewhere like Jupiter; 12th: wealthy.
- v.9 Saturn - 1st: poor, sickly, [unclean], troubled in childhood, slow speech - but in his own sign, exaltation or a sign of Jupiter: like a ruler, head of a village or town, learned, good-looking; elsewhere like the Sun.
- v.10 **Weigh every house result by whether the planet is in a friend's, enemy's, other's, own or exaltation sign. "Satya says benefics make a house grow and malefics harm it - the reverse in the 12th, 6th and 8th."**
- v.11 Share of the good result: exaltation full, trine-sign three quarters, own sign half, friend's a quarter, enemy's little, debilitation or combustion none.
- Rahu and Ketu are not mentioned.

## Ch. 21 — Yogas of placement, 10 verses
- v.1 Planets in their own signs, one to seven: equal to his family, head of it, honoured by relatives, wealthy, comfortable, enjoys life, a ruler. In friends' signs, one to seven: lives on others, supported by friends, by relatives, head of a group, of a force, a ruler.
- v.2 **Even one exalted planet aspected by a friend makes a ruler; joined by a friend, wealth.** Planets in enemy and debilitation signs: poor, unhappy, dull, ill, confined.
- v.3 "Satya says an Aquarius lagna is not good; the Yavanas say only the Aquarius twelfth-part. Vishnugupta: which sign lacks that part? - an over-reach."
- v.4-5 Malefics in odd signs in the Sun's hora: famous, energetic, strong, wealthy, vigorous. Benefics in even signs in the Moon's hora: gentle, lovely, comfortable, fortunate, sweet-spoken. Mixed: middling; reversed: without these.
- v.6-10 Moon by drekkana, vargottama navamsa, twelfth-part; planets in their own thirtieth-part.

## Ch. 22 — Miscellaneous, 6 verses
- v.1-3 **Planets in own, exaltation or trine-signs in kendras from one another are "mutual significators" (karakas) - the one in the 10th most of all.** Example: Cancer rising with the Moon, and Mars, Saturn, Sun, Jupiter exalted.
- v.4 Good: birth in a vargottama, a benefic in the Veshi place, kendras not empty, such karaka planets.
- v.5 **Jupiter, the lord of the Moon's sign and the lagna lord in kendras give comfort in the middle of life. Planets in back-rising, both-ways and head-rising signs ripen at the end, the middle and the beginning.**
- v.6 **Sun and Mars give on entering a sign, Jupiter and Venus in its middle, Saturn and the Moon on leaving it, Mercury at all times.**

## Ch. 24 — Women's charts, 16 verses
- v.1 Whatever result suits a woman is said of her; the rest is said of her husband. [Widowhood from the 8th - not used.] Her body from the lagna and the Moon; husband and good fortune from the 7th.
- v.2 Lagna and Moon in even signs: womanly in nature, of good conduct and adorned when aspected by benefics. In odd signs: manly in form and manner; with malefic influence, fewer virtues.
- v.3-6 Character by the thirtieth-part of the lagna or Moon - **not used.** v.7 not used.
- v.8 7th empty and weak with no benefic aspect: a poor sort of husband. Mercury and Saturn in the 7th: [an impotent husband]; a movable sign there: always travelling. Sun in the 7th: left by the husband. Mars there under malefic aspect: [widowed young - not used]. Saturn there under malefic aspect: grows old unmarried.
- v.9 [Malefics in the 7th - widowhood; not used.] Mixed planets there: marries again. A weak malefic in the 7th aspected by a benefic: put aside by the husband. v.10 not used.
- v.11-12 **The husband by the sign or navamsa on the 7th.** Saturn's: old, dull. Mars's: fond of women, quick-tempered. Venus's: very handsome and fortunate. Mercury's: learned, skilful. Moon's: led by desire, gentle. Jupiter's: virtuous, self-controlled. Sun's: very gentle, hard-working. (Identical to BPHS 80.22-25 as coded in `husband_by_seventh`.)
- v.13 Moon and Venus rising: jealous, fond of comfort. Mercury and Moon: skilled in arts, happy, virtuous. Venus and Mercury: lovely, fortunate, skilled in arts. All three benefics: much wealth, comfort and virtue.
- v.14 [Widowhood timing - not used.] Moon in Virgo, Scorpio, Taurus or Leo: few children.
- v.15 Saturn of middling strength, Moon, Venus and Mercury weak, the rest strong, an odd sign rising: manly in manner. Jupiter, Mars, Venus and Mercury strong with an even sign rising: famous, versed in many sciences, a teacher of sacred knowledge.
- v.16 A malefic in the 7th with a planet in the 9th: she takes the vows of that planet's order. **"All of this is to be applied at the wedding, at the choosing, at the giving, and at a query."**
