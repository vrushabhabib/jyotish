# Brihat Jataka of Varahamihira — read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026

Source: sanskritdocuments.org `brihajjAtakam.itx` (encoded by Mizue Sugita, 1994; ITRANS conversion by Radu Canahai, 2002). 28 chapters, 407 verses. The file's own terms: "prepared by volunteers ... for personal study and research ... not to be copied or reposted". So this folder holds **my own translation and summary only; the Sanskrit is not reproduced.** Square brackets mark my glosses or wording the project does not use.

Why it matters: Varahamihira (6th century) is the oldest dated author the tradition quotes, and he names his own sources (Maya, Yavana, Manittha, Shakti/Parashara, Satya, Vishnugupta, Jivasharma, Devasvami, Siddhasena). Where he and the Parashara text agree, a rule is old; where they differ, the difference is real and is recorded.

## What the reading settled
1. **The "standard" Ashtakavarga table is Varahamihira's (ch. 9).** All seven of his tables were decoded from the verses and checked giver by giver against `engine/ashtakavarga.py`: every cell agrees with the default table. The three cells in which the Parashara recension differs (Moon from Jupiter 2nd/12th, Moon from Moon/Mars 9th, Venus from Mars 4th/5th) are therefore a difference between two old texts, not an error in either. The engine default (Varahamihira = Raman = AstroSage) stays; `variant="bphs"` gives Parashara's.
2. **Varahamihira does not teach Vimshottari.** His dasha (ch. 8) is the life-span dasha: the strongest of lagna, Sun and Moon first, then planets by kendra, panaphara, apoklima; and a "natural" dasha of 1, 2, 9, 20, 18, 20, 50 years for Moon, Mars, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, Sun, Saturn. Vimshottari rests on Parashara alone among the texts read. His rules for **grading** a period by the planet's state (8.5-7) match BPHS 50.73-83.
3. **Timing inside a period (22.5):** planets in back-rising signs give at the end, both-ways in the middle, head-rising at the start - the third text to state it (BPHS 50.88-89, PD). The worksheet's "delivery" line now has three sources.
4. **Transit timing inside a sign (22.6):** Sun and Mars give their result on entering a sign, Jupiter and Venus in its middle, Saturn and the Moon on leaving, Mercury throughout. Added to `kb/transits/transits.md`.
5. **Kemadruma (13.3):** "when no planet other than the Sun is in the 2nd or 12th from the Moon, many call it Kemadruma; it is not held when the Moon is in a kendra, or joined by a planet" - the words can equally be read "when a kendra, or the Moon, is joined by a planet", and commentators take it both ways. Either way the cancellation is in the root verse itself. He adds that counting it by kendra *and navamsa* "is said by some, but is not established".
6. **Vasumati (13.9):** all benefics in upachaya houses from the **lagna** - "very wealthy"; from the **Moon** - "wealthy"; two - middling; fewer - little. The catalogue called the lagna form a "Raman variant"; it is Varahamihira's own and the stronger of the two. Corrected.
7. **Share of result by dignity (20.11):** exaltation full, trine-sign three quarters, own sign half, friend's a quarter, enemy's "little", debilitated or combust nothing. Parashara (3.59-60) inserts "neutral: an eighth" and gives the enemy's sign nothing. Same scale, one step different.
8. **Women's chapter (24):** verses 8-12 are the same rules as BPHS 80 - the husband described by the sign and navamsa on the 7th (list identical; the Sun's is "very gentle, hard-working", which corrects the word "harsh" in rule SP-10), Sun in the **7th** = left by the husband (so the alternative reading in PL-18 is Varahamihira's), Saturn in the 7th under malefic aspect = late or no marriage. 24.1 gives the method: whatever suits a woman is read for her, the rest for her husband; her body from lagna and Moon, husband and good fortune from the 7th. 24.16: "apply all of this at the time of marriage, of choosing, of giving and of a query." The thirtieth-part character labels (24.3-6) are **not used**, as with BPHS 80.8-16.
9. **Livelihood (10.1-3):** source of income by the planet in the 10th from lagna or Moon (father, mother, an opponent, a friend, a brother, a woman, a servant for Sun to Saturn) and by the lord of the navamsa occupied by the lord of the 10th from lagna, Moon and Sun - exactly the method the worksheet's livelihood pointer uses. Now cited to the verse, not to a secondary page.
10. **Things that are not in this text either:** Kaal Sarp, Manglik, Sade Sati by name, Pitru dosha, Guru-Chandala, Vish yoga, any use of Rahu or Ketu in houses, Vimshottari, divisional charts beyond hora, drekkana, navamsa, dvadashamsha and trimshamsha.

## Not used from this text
Ch. 3 (non-human births), ch. 4-5 (conception, circumstances of delivery), ch. 6 (early death), ch. 7 (length of life), ch. 23 (deformity, disease and loss wording), ch. 24.3-7 and 24.14 (character labels on women, widowhood), ch. 25 (manner of death), ch. 26 (rebuilding a lost birth time from a query), ch. 27 (images of the 36 decanates). They are summarised in `ch01-28.md` for completeness and never enter a reading.
