# BPHS chapters 11–20 — the houses, lagna to 9th (read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

## Ch. 11 — what is judged from each house (bhāva-viveka), 16 verses
- v.2 Lagna: body, appearance, knowledge, complexion, strength and weakness, happiness and sorrow, own nature.
- v.3 2nd: wealth, grain, family, death, enemies, metals and gems.
- v.4 3rd: courage, servants, brothers and sisters, instruction, journeys, death of the parents.
- v.5 4th: vehicles, relatives, mother's happiness, treasure, land, house.
- v.6 5th: yantra and mantra, learning, intelligence, literary composition, children, fall from position.
- v.7 6th: maternal uncle, fear of death, enemies, wounds, stepmother.
- v.8 7th: wife, travel, trade, recovery of what was lost, death of one's own body.
- v.9 8th: length of life, battle, enemies, forts, wealth of the dead, what has gone and what is to come.
- v.10 9th: fortune, wife's brother, dharma, brother's wife, pilgrimage.
- v.11 10th: authority, livelihood, honour, **the father**, living away from home, debt.
- v.12 11th: gain of all kinds, the son's wife, income, growth, cattle.
- v.13 12th: expenditure, dealings of enemies, the end.
- v.14-15 A house joined or aspected by benefics or by its own lord, with its lord in a youthful, adult, or royal state, gives its happiness.
- v.15-16 When the lord of a house is lost, joined with the lords of 6, 8 or 12, does not aspect its house, or is in a sleeping, old, dead or afflicted state, the results of that house are destroyed.
- Note for the project: the father is named under the 10th (v.11), yet all the father combinations sit in the 9th-house chapter (ch. 20) and "lord of the father's house" there is used of the 9th. Both houses are Parashara's; the 9th carries the detail.

## Ch. 12 — the lagna, 15 verses
- v.1 L1 with a malefic in 8, 6 or 12 removes bodily ease; L1 in a kendra or trine always gives it.
- v.2 L1 combust, debilitated or in an enemy's sign causes illness; benefics in kendras and trines remove all illness.
- v.3 Lagna or Moon joined or aspected by cruel planets, without benefic aspect: no bodily ease.
- v.4 Benefic in the lagna: good-looking; cruel planet: not; lagna joined or aspected by benefics: bodily comfort.
- v.5 L1, or Mercury, Jupiter or Venus, in a kendra or trine: long life, wealth, intelligence, favour of rulers.
- v.6 L1 in a movable sign aspected by benefics: fame, wealth, enjoyment, bodily comfort.
- v.7 Mercury, Jupiter or Venus in the lagna with the Moon, or in a kendra from the lagna: marks of royalty.
- v.8 Birth with the cord around the body: lagna Aries, Taurus or Leo holding Saturn or Mars; the limb by the sign and navamsa.
- v.9 Twins: Sun in a quadruped sign, the others strong in dual signs.
- v.10 Sun and Moon in one house and one navamsa: raised by three mothers for three months, then by father and brother [?].
- v.11 The same is judged from the Moon.
- v.12-15 Marks and scars: the three drekkanas of the lagna map the houses onto head-to-neck, neck-to-navel and pelvis-to-feet; a malefic in a limb gives a wound there, with Mercury certainly; benefics give a mark.

## Ch. 13 — the 2nd house, 13 verses
- v.1-2 L2 in the 2nd or in a kendra or trine increases wealth; in 6, 8 or 12 it loses wealth. A benefic in the 2nd gives wealth, a malefic destroys it.
- v.3 Jupiter as L2 in the 2nd, or with Mars: wealthy.
- v.4 L2 in the 11th or L11 in the 2nd, or both in kendras or trines: wealthy.
- v.5 L2 in a kendra, L11 in a trine from it, joined or aspected by Jupiter and Venus: gain of wealth.
- v.6 L2 in the 6th with L11 also there, or the 2nd and 11th joined or aspected by malefics: without wealth.
- v.7 L2 and L11 combust and with malefics: poor from birth, living on alms.
- v.8 L2 and L11 in 6, 8 or 12, Mars in the 11th, Rahu in the 2nd: loss of wealth through a ruler's penalty.
- v.9 Jupiter in the 11th, Venus in the 2nd, L2 with a benefic, a benefic in the 12th: money spent on good works.
- v.10 L2 in own sign or exalted: supports many people, helpful, well known.
- v.11 L2 with a benefic in a Paravata or higher division: all wealth comes to the house without effort.
- v.12 The lord of the eyes (L2) strong: good eyes; in 6, 8 or 12: defect of sight.
- v.13 L2 with a malefic and a malefic in the 2nd: a tale-bearer, untruthful, troubled by wind disorders.

## Ch. 14 — the 3rd house and siblings, 15 verses
- v.1 3rd joined or aspected by benefics: has brothers, courageous.
- v.2 L3 with Mars aspecting the 3rd, or in the 3rd: brothers indicated.
- v.3 Those two with a malefic or in a malefic's sign destroy the siblings [literal].
- v.4 L3 a female planet, or a female planet in the 3rd: a sister; male planet in a male sign: a brother.
- v.5 Mixed: mixed, by strength. Mars and L3 in the 8th [mṛtau]: loss of siblings.
- v.6 Mars or L3 in a kendra or trine, or in own, exalted or friendly divisions: happiness from brothers.
- v.7-8 3rd with Mercury, L3 with the Moon, Mars with Saturn: an elder sister, a younger brother, the third dies [literal].
- v.8-9 Mars with Rahu and L3 debilitated: no younger siblings, three elder ones.
- v.9-11 L3 in a kendra, Mars in a trine from it, exalted and with Jupiter: twelve siblings, six of them long-lived [the count is not usable].
- v.12 Mars with L12 or with Jupiter, Moon in the 3rd: seven siblings.
- v.13 Moon in the 3rd aspected only by male planets: brothers; by Venus: sisters.
- v.14 Sun in the 3rd harms the elder born, Saturn the younger, Mars both [literal].
- v.15 All these are weighed by strength.
- Project note: verses 8-9 and 14 are the classical basis for our own Rule 9 (malefics on the 3rd and no younger siblings). The text ties "no younger siblings" to Mars with Rahu plus a debilitated 3rd lord; our rule used Ketu in the 3rd with Saturn and Mars aspects. Related, not identical.

## Ch. 15 — the 4th house, 14 verses
- v.2 L4 in the 4th, or L1 there, aspected by a benefic: full happiness of home.
- v.3 L4 in own sign, own navamsa or exalted: land, vehicles, houses, music.
- v.4 L4 with L10 in a kendra or trine: a house with fine walls and mansions.
- v.5 L4 a benefic joined or aspected by benefics, Mercury in the lagna: honoured by relatives.
- v.6 4th with a benefic, L4 exalted, the significator (Moon) strong: long life for the mother.
- v.7 L4 in a kendra, Venus in a kendra, Mercury exalted: full happiness from the mother.
- v.8 Sun in the 4th, Saturn [with it], Moon in the 9th, Mars in the 11th: gain of cattle.
- v.9 4th and its lord in movable signs, Mars in the 6th or 12th: dumbness [literal].
- v.10-14 Vehicle at stated ages (12, 32, 42, 12) from combinations of L4, L1, L10, L11, Sun and Venus; ages not usable. v.13: L11 in the 4th and L4 in the 11th.
- v.14 Good when the house is good, otherwise the reverse.

## Ch. 16 — the 5th house and children, 32 verses
- v.1-2 L1 in the 5th and L5 in the 5th, or in a kendra or trine: full happiness from children. L5 in 6, 8 or 12: no children [literal].
- v.3 L5 combust, or overrun by malefics and weak: no child is born, or it does not live [literal].
- v.4 L5 in the 6th and L1 with Mars: the first child is lost and the wife bears only once [literal].
- v.5 L5 debilitated in 12, 6 or 8, Ketu and Mercury in the 5th: bears only once.
- v.6 L5 debilitated and not aspecting the 5th, Saturn and Mercury there: bears only once.
- v.7 L9 in the lagna, L5 debilitated, Ketu and Mercury in the 5th: a child after difficulty.
- v.8 L5 in 6, 8 or 12, or debilitated or in an enemy's sign, [or such a one] in the 5th: a child after difficulty.
- v.9 5th house in a sign of Mercury or Saturn, with Saturn, joined or aspected by Mandi: adopted children.
- v.10 Sun and Moon in one sign and one navamsa: raised by three mothers or two fathers.
- v.11 Six planets in the 5th, L5 in the 12th, L1 and Moon strong: an adopted child.
- v.12 5th aspected by strong Mercury, Jupiter and Venus, L5 strong: many children.
- v.13 L5 with the Moon or in the Moon's drekkana: daughters.
- v.14-15 Two combinations for a child fathered by another [recorded, never used].
- v.16 L5 exalted, or in the 2nd, 5th or 9th, joined or aspected by Jupiter: fortune through children.
- v.17 Three or four malefics in the 5th, no benefic, L5 debilitated: a child of low conduct.
- v.18-20 Child at stated ages (32-33, 30 or 36, 40): Jupiter in the 5th with L5 joined to Venus; L5 in a kendra with Jupiter; Jupiter in the 9th and Venus in the 9th from Jupiter, or L1 with Venus. Ages not usable; the pattern is "Jupiter, Venus and the 5th lord linked".
- v.21-23 Loss of a child at stated ages: Rahu in the 5th, L5 with a malefic, Jupiter debilitated; a malefic in the 5th from Jupiter and from the lagna; Mandi in the lagna with L1 debilitated [literal].
- v.24-29 Numbers of children (ten, nine, eight, seven, six) from named combinations [counts not usable].
- v.29-30 Jupiter in the 5th from Saturn and Saturn in the 5th from Jupiter, a malefic in the 5th: one child.
- v.30-31 A malefic in the 5th, or Saturn in the 5th from Jupiter: a child from another wife; three marriages.
- v.31-32 Malefic in the 5th, Saturn in the 5th from Jupiter, L1 in the 2nd, L5 with Mars: children lost as they are born, he himself long-lived [literal].
- Checkable core for the engine: (a) L5 in kendra/trine vs in 6/8/12; (b) L5 combust or debilitated; (c) malefics in the 5th from lagna AND from Jupiter; (d) Jupiter's aspect or company for L5; (e) the adoption pattern in v.9 and v.11.

## Ch. 17 — the 6th house, illness and wounds, 28 verses
- v.2 L6 in own sign, in the lagna or in the 8th: wounds on the body, in the part shown by the 6th sign.
- v.3-5 The same for relatives: the lord of the relative's house with that relative's significator and with L6, in 6 or 8, shows the wound; place by planet - Sun head, Moon face, Mars throat, Mercury navel, Jupiter nose, Venus eyes, Saturn feet, Rahu and Ketu abdomen.
- v.6 L1 in a sign of Mars or Mercury, aspected by Mercury: disease of the face or mouth.
- v.7-9 Mars or Mercury as L1 with the Moon, Rahu and Saturn: skin disease; Moon with Rahu in the lagna without L1: white patches; with Saturn dark, with Mars red.
- v.9-12 L6 and L8 in the lagna with the Sun: fever and swelling; with Mars: glands or weapon wounds; with Mercury: bile; with Jupiter: no disease; with Venus: through women; with Saturn: wind; with Rahu or Ketu: fear at the navel from low persons; with the Moon: from water and phlegm.
- v.13-14 Malefic in the 6th, L6 with a malefic, Saturn with Rahu: always ill.
- v.14-28 Illness or danger at stated ages from named combinations (fever at 6 and 12 with Mars in the 6th and L6 in the 8th; and so on). Ages not usable. v.26: L11 in the 6th and L6 in the 11th: loss of money through enemies. v.27: L5 in the 6th, L6 with Jupiter, L12 in the lagna: the son becomes an enemy.

## Ch. 18 — the 7th house and marriage, 42 verses
- v.1 L7 in own sign or exalted: full happiness from marriage.
- v.2 L7, when not in own sign or exalted, in 12, 6 or 8: a sickly spouse.
- v.3 Venus in the 7th: very passionate. Venus anywhere joined with a malefic: loss of the spouse [literal].
- v.4 L7 strong, joined or aspected by benefics: wealth, honour, happiness and good fortune.
- v.5 L7 debilitated, in an enemy's sign, combust or weak: a sickly spouse, and more than one marriage.
- v.6 L7 in a sign of Saturn or of Venus, aspected by a benefic - and especially when exalted: more than one marriage.
- v.7-9, 12-14 The planet in the 7th and the kind of partner or union [sexual and caste wording; recorded, not used].
- v.10-11 Malefics in the 12th and 7th, waning Moon in the 5th: ruled by the spouse and at odds with his own community.
- v.11-12 Saturn and Mars in the 7th, or as its lord: an unfaithful spouse [literal].
- v.14-15 L7 exalted, a benefic in the 7th, L1 strong and joined to the 7th: a spouse of good qualities, children and grandchildren.
- v.16 A malefic in the 7th or with L7: harm to the spouse, more so when weak.
- v.17 L7 weak in 6, 8 or 12, or debilitated: loss of the spouse [literal].
- v.18 Moon in the 7th, L7 in the 12th, Venus weak: no happiness from marriage.
- v.19 L7 debilitated in a malefic's sign with a malefic, the 7th in a neuter sign or navamsa: two marriages.
- v.20 Mars and Venus in the 7th, Saturn in the 7th, L1 in the 8th: three marriages.
- v.21 Venus in a dual sign with its sign-lord exalted, L7 strong: several marriages.
- v.22-34 Marriage at stated ages (5 to 33) from thirteen combinations of Venus, Saturn, L7, L1, L2, L8 and L11. The ages belong to another era and are not usable. What survives as pattern: Venus in a kendra from the lagna or from the Moon; Saturn in the 7th from Venus (later); exchange or mutual placement of L2 and L11; L7 in a benefic's sign.
- v.35-39 Loss of the spouse at stated ages [literal, not used]. v.37 is a snake-bite verse.
- v.40-41 Moon in the 7th from Venus, Mercury in the 7th from the Moon, L8 in the 5th: three marriages at stated ages.
- v.42 Mars in the 6th, Rahu in the 7th, Saturn in the 8th: the spouse does not live [literal].
- Checkable core for the engine: (a) L7 dignity - own or exalted vs debilitated, enemy sign, combust; (b) L7 in 6/8/12 without dignity; (c) benefic vs malefic in the 7th or with L7; (d) Venus with a malefic; (e) plural-marriage flags: v.5, v.6, v.19, v.20, v.21, v.40.
- Project note: v.6 says an exalted L7 in a sign of Saturn or Venus points to more than one marriage, while v.1 gives full happiness for an exalted L7. Both are in the text; the catalogue's recorded conflict is real and is Parashara's own.

## Ch. 19 — the 8th house and length of life, 15 verses
- v.1 L8 in a kendra gives long life.
- v.2 L8 with malefics in the 8th gives short life; so too L1 there.
- v.3 Saturn and L10 are also to be weighed for length of life.
- v.4 L6 in the 6th or 12th, L12 in the 6th or 12th, or these in the lagna or 8th: long life.
- v.5 L1 and L8 in own sign, own navamsa or a friend's sign: long life.
- v.6 L1, L8, L10 and Saturn in kendras, trines or the 11th: long life.
- v.7 Of many such combinations the strongest decides.
- v.8-13 Short-life combinations: L8 in a kendra with L1 weak; L8 debilitated with malefics in the 8th and L1 weak; and infant-death combinations [literal, not used].
- v.14 L1 exalted, Moon in the 11th, Jupiter in the 8th: long life.
- v.15 L1 very strong, aspected by benefics in kendras: long life with wealth and all good qualities.

- v.26 Venus in deep exaltation with L9, Saturn in the 3rd: lord of much fortune.

- v.31 Saturn in the 9th with the Moon, L1 debilitated: living on alms.
- v.32 More is to be judged from the positions of L1 and L9.
