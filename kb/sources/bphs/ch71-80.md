# BPHS chapters 71–80 — total Ashtakavarga, rays, the Sudarshana wheel, great-person yogas, elements, qualities, lost birth data, renunciation, women's charts (read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

## Ch. 71 — Length of life from Ashtakavarga, 4 verses
- v.1-4 A span of days or years for each count of points, summed over the planets and halved. **Not used** (guardrail).

## Ch. 72 — The combined Ashtakavarga (Samudaya), 31 verses
- v.1-2 A wheel of twelve from the lagna; add the points of all the tables sign by sign.
- **v.3-4 A sign with more than 30 points gives good; 25 to 30, middling; fewer, hardship. Use the best signs for auspicious undertakings and avoid the poor ones.**
- v.5 A planet in a best sign is good; elsewhere it gives bad.
- **v.6-8 House by house from the lagna: more points - good for that house; fewer - loss; middling - middling. If the 11th has more than the 10th there is gain; if the 12th has less than the 11th, [expense stays below gain]. One whose lagna has many points has comforts and means; the opposite, want.**
- v.9-10 Divide the houses into three blocks, as with dashas; the block held by malefics is the hard one, by benefics the good one, mixed mixed; the three blocks are childhood, the middle years and age.
- v.11-28 For the month whose sign has 7, 8, 9 ... up to 30 points: a named danger and a named gift to avert it (7 and 8 mortal danger, 9 snakes, 10 weapons, 12 water, 15 from authority, 17 illness, 18 quarrel, 19 living away, 20 loss of judgment, 22 trouble to relatives, 26 loss of money, 29 anxiety; 30 "full gain of grain"). [Danger wordings not used; kept only as "a sign with very few points marks a hard month when the Sun is there".]
- v.29 More than 30: money, children, comfort; more than 40: merit and fortune grow.
- **v.30-31 "What is clear by Ashtakavarga is clear for all undertakings. Transit is to be looked into only as long as the Ashtakavarga is not to hand; once the Ashtakavarga is to hand, transit [by itself] is fruitless."**

## Ch. 73 — Rays of the planets, 23 verses
- **v.1-3 At exaltation the Sun has 10 rays, Moon 9, Mars 5, Mercury 5, Jupiter 7, Venus 8, Saturn 5; at debilitation none; between, in proportion: the planet's distance from its debilitation point (the short way round, in signs) x its rays / 6.**
- v.3-6 "A further refinement said by others": multiply by 3 in the exaltation sign, 2 in moolatrikona, 3/2 in own sign, 4/3 in a great friend's, 6/5 in a friend's; halve in an enemy's, 2/5 in a great enemy's; unchanged in a neutral's.
- v.6-7 Planets set in the Sun's rays lose their rays - except Saturn and Venus.
- v.8-18 The total: 1-5 poor even if of a high family; up to 10 poor, carrying loads, without family or house; 11 few children, little money; 12 little money, [unfavourable character words]; 13 [the same]; 14 upright, supports the family, has money and learning; 15 all learning and qualities, first in the family, wealthy; beyond that heads of clans with many dependants, famous, happy; 21 supports fifty people; 22 generous and kind; 23 happy and gentle; up to 30 wealthy, strong, dear to rulers; up to 40 a chief supporting a hundred to a thousand; up to 50 a ruler; more, the highest ruler.
- **v.19-20 "According to the standing of the family": of the same rays a warrior's son becomes sovereign, a merchant's a ruler, a worker's wealthy, a priest's a performer of sacrifices.**
- v.21 A planet moving toward exaltation gives its rays fully; moving toward debilitation, less.
- v.22-23 "Without knowing the rays one cannot rightly know the result."

Honest note printed with it: by construction a random chart averages about 24, so the scale only says something well above 30 or below 18.

## Ch. 74 — The Sudarshana wheel, 28 verses
- v.4-7 Three rings on one centre, twelve spokes: inner ring the houses from the lagna, middle from the Moon, outer from the Sun, each with the planets. The first house is lagna, Moon and Sun together; the others follow.
- v.8-9 The Sun in the first house is good, elsewhere it acts as a malefic; a malefic in its exaltation sign does not give bad.
- v.10 A house joined or aspected by its lord and by benefics grows; joined or aspected by malefics it loses.
- v.11-14 An occupied house: result by the planets in it; an empty house: by the planets aspecting it; benefics good, malefics bad, mixed by the majority; a house with neither: by its lord.
- v.15-16 Even a benefic with more malefic divisions gives bad, and a malefic with more benefic divisions gives good; the divisions of own sign and exaltation are good, of an enemy's sign, a harsh planet's and debilitation bad.
- **v.18-20 Maitreya: if results come from this wheel, why did all the sages read from the lagna alone? Answer: when the Sun and the Moon are in signs different from the lagna and from each other, read from the wheel; when two or all three are in one sign, read from the lagna.**
- **v.21-26 Yearly results: each house in turn rules a year (then months, then two and a half days) - twelve-year rounds through life. Taking the year's house as a lagna: benefics in its kendras, trines and 8th give good; a house holding Rahu is harmed; one with many malefics is ruined; benefics should not be in its 12th or 6th; malefics in its 3rd, 6th and 11th give good.**
- **v.27-28 Judge the year and month from this wheel and from the Ashtakavarga together: when both agree the result is full; when they differ, by the stronger.**

## Ch. 75 — The five great-person yogas, 22 verses
- **v.1-2 Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn - strong, in own sign or exaltation, in a kendra - give Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Shasha.** [No count from the Moon is given; "strong" is a stated condition.]
- v.3-7 Ruchaka: long face, great energy, strength, fine brows, dark hair, fond of battle, ruddy-dark, destroyer of rivals, a leader of men, respectful to the learned; marks on the hand; [regions ruled, a span of years and manner of death - not used].
- v.8-12 Bhadra: lion-like, broad chest, an elephant's gait, long arms, wise, square build, knows yoga, sattvic, fine feet and beard, knows the sciences, independent in all work, able to please his own people, his wealth enjoyed by friends; [span not used].
- v.13-16 Hamsa: a swan's voice, fair, fine face, high nose, phlegmatic, honey-coloured eyes, red nails, intelligent, full cheeks, round head, fine feet; fond of water-sports; [span not used].
- v.17-19 Malavya: even lips, slim waist, moon-like lustre, fragrant, neither short nor very tall, even clean teeth, an elephant's voice, long arms; [region and span not used].
- v.20-22 Shasha: small teeth and face, brave, not short, lean belly, slim middle, fine shanks, clever, knows others' weak points, at home in forests, hills and forts, a commander, restless, interested in metals, [attached to women], having others' wealth; [span not used].
- Engine check: as coded (kendra from the lagna, own sign or exaltation, with the strength note).

## Ch. 76 — The five elements, 18 verses
- v.2-5 Lords: fire Mars, earth Mercury, ether Jupiter, water Venus, air Saturn; the Sun fire, the Moon water. The strongest planet gives the temperament; several strong ones a mixed temperament. In its own dasha each planet brings out the "lustre" of its element.
- v.6-10 Fire: hungry, restless, brave, lean, clever, eats much, sharp, fair, proud. Earth: fragrant, fond of comforts, steady happiness, strong, forgiving, a lion's voice. Ether: knows words and meanings, good at policy, bold, learned, open-mouthed, very tall. Water: lustrous, bears burdens, sweet-spoken, a ruler, many friends, gentle, learned. Air: generous, quick to anger, fair, fond of wandering, a ruler, hard to overcome, lean.
- v.11-15 The lustre in the dasha: fire - golden glow, bright eyes, success in all work, victory, gain; earth - pleasant fragrance, glossy nails and teeth, gain of merit, money and comfort; ether - clarity, skill in speech, pleasure in hearing good words; water - softness and health, enjoyment of tastes; air - dullness, confusion, dejection, illness, grief.
- v.16-17 This when Mars and the rest are strong; when weak, the reverse; debilitated or in an enemy's sign, the opposite; "from weak planets the results come in dreams and thoughts".
- **v.18 "For those whose birth is unknown, the running dasha is to be known from the results being experienced."** [Parashara's own warrant for checking a chart against lived events.]

## Ch. 77 — The three qualities, 22 verses
- v.1-4 Born when a sattva planet is rising [or strongest]: sattvic and wise; a rajas planet: rajasic; a tamas planet: tamasic; equal: mixed. Four kinds: best, middle, low, neutral.
- v.5-8 Sattva: calm, self-control, austerity, purity, forgiveness, straightness, no greed, truthfulness. Rajas: valour, vigour, steadiness, skill, not fleeing in battle, protection of the good. Tamas: greed, untruth, dullness, laziness, skill in serving. Mixed: skill in farming, trade and herding; speaks both truth and untruth.
- v.9-10 Know the type by these marks and employ the person accordingly; a quality counts as dominant when it exceeds the other two.
- **v.11-13 "Between master and servant, and between bride and groom, affection is steady only when they are alike in qualities." A pairing of unequal types works when the one relied upon is the higher. From matching qualities comes happiness; otherwise loss.**
- v.14 Seed, field, time of birth and company cause the grade of a person, each stronger than the one before.
- v.15 The quality in the person matches the time of birth; examine it before speaking of results.
- v.16-22 Time creates, sustains and withdraws; its four limbs and the four kinds of beings. [Cosmology.]
- Project note: v.11-13 is a matching rule (likeness of temperament) that the eight-fold point system does not capture; noted for `kb/matching`.

## Ch. 78 — Lost birth data, 16 verses
- v.4-9 When the year, half-year, season, month, lunar day or star is unknown, each is found from the chart of the moment of the question: Jupiter's sign from the twelfth-part of the question lagna; the half-year from the half of the lagna; the season from the lord of the lagna's drekkana; the month from the half of the drekkana; the lunar day in proportion; then the birth time.
- v.10-12 Which twelve-year round of Jupiter: add twelves according to the person's apparent age.
- v.13-16 The time of birth from the Sun's degree and its daily motion.
- **Not used**: the project never invents a birth time from a question chart; uncertain times are handled by the sensitivity table and discriminating questions.

## Ch. 79 — Renunciation yogas, 15 verses
- v.2-3 Four or more strong planets in one place: renunciation of the order of the strongest - Sun: ascetic; Moon: skull-bearer; Mars: red-robed; Mercury: single-staff; Jupiter: mendicant monk; Venus: wheel-bearer; Saturn: naked ascetic.
- v.4-5 If those planets are combust: a follower of that order, not initiated. Combust and weak with a strong Sun: the Sun's order.
- v.6 The lord of the Moon's sign, unaspected by others, aspecting Saturn: renunciation by the stronger of the two.
- v.7 The lord of the Moon's sign weak and aspected by Saturn alone: Saturn's order.
- v.8 The Moon in a drekkana of Saturn and in a navamsa of Saturn or Mars, aspected by Saturn: Saturn's order.
- v.9-10 Planetary war among them; a renunciation-giver defeated by another: the vow taken is given up.
- v.11-13 Several equal givers: all their orders are taken in turn, each in its planet's dasha.
- v.14 Jupiter in the 9th, with the Moon, Jupiter and lagna aspected by Saturn; if a raja yoga is present, a founder of a sacred order.
- v.15 Saturn in the 9th aspected by no planet; if a raja yoga is present, a ruler who takes initiation.
- Engine check: v.2-3, 7-8, 14-15 coded as read earlier; no change.

## Ch. 80 — Women's charts, 55 verses
- **v.2-4 "For women the results are to be known as for men." Particular to them: the body from the lagna, childbirth from the 5th, the husband's fortune from the 7th, [widowhood from the 8th]. Whatever cannot apply to a woman is to be said of her husband.**
- v.5-7 Lagna and Moon in even signs: of a womanly nature, with the qualities of a good girl, well-behaved; aspected by benefics: good-looking and healthy. In odd signs: mannish in form; aspected or joined by malefics: ill-tempered and without good qualities. Mixed: mixed. The stronger of lagna and Moon decides.
- v.8-16 A table of character by the thirtieth-part (trimshamsha) of the stronger of lagna and Moon, for each sign-lord (35 entries, from "of many virtues", "skilled in arts", "learned", "devoted", "a queen" to several labels about chastity and morals). **Not used in any reading** - the project does not pass moral labels on a person; the table's existence is recorded here and no entry is quoted.
- v.17 7th house empty and without benefic aspect: the husband is a poor sort of man; 7th in a movable sign: he lives away from home; [Mercury and Saturn there - wording not used].
- v.18-19 [Sun, Mars, Saturn and malefics in the 7th - separation and widowhood wordings, **not used**; kept as: malefics in the 7th without benefic help strain the marriage; benefics there protect it; mixed planets: a second marriage.]
- v.20-21 [Wordings about other attachments - not used.]
- **v.22-25 The husband by the sign and navamsa on the 7th: of Mars - fond of women, quick to anger; of Mercury - learned, skilled in arts, intelligent; of Jupiter - all good qualities, self-controlled; of Venus - fortunate, handsome, liked by women; of Saturn - older, slow; of the Sun (Leo) - very soft [or: harsh - the word can be divided either way], very hard-working; of the Moon (Cancer) - handsome, passionate, gentle. Sign and navamsa differing: mixed, by the stronger.**
- v.26-33 Each planet in the 8th of a woman's chart: eight verses of hardship and character labels. [**Not used as worded**; kept as: planets in the 8th mark hardship in the areas of the husband's fortune and her own comfort.]
- v.34 [Childlessness - not used.] v.35 A benefic navamsa on the 7th: fortunate, dear to her husband. [The first half is a medical wording - not used.]
- v.36-37 Lagna in a sign of Mercury with the Moon and Venus: every comfort in her father's house. Moon, Mercury and Venus in the lagna: many comforts and qualities; Jupiter there: wealth, children, happiness.
- v.38-42 [Childlessness, loss of children, harm to both families - **not used**.]
- v.43-45 "Poison-maiden" birth: (Ashlesha, Krittika or Shatabhisha) + (Sunday, Saturday or Tuesday) + a Bhadra lunar day (2nd, 7th, 12th); or a malefic with two benefics [?] in the lagna and two malefics in an enemy's sign. [Results not used.]
- **v.46 It is cancelled without doubt by a benefic 7th lord, or by a benefic in the 7th from the lagna or from the Moon.**
- **v.47 Mars in the lagna, 12th, 4th, 7th or 8th, without the aspect or company of a benefic** [the consequence is worded as the husband's death - never stated; this is the verse behind "Manglik"]. **The 2nd house is not in the verse.**
- **v.48-49 The same combination in a man's chart works against the wife; when two such people marry each other, the combination is broken for certain.**
- v.50-51 [Not used.]
- v.52 Mars, Mercury, Jupiter and Venus strong, lagna in an even sign: skilled in many sciences, a speaker on the highest knowledge.
- v.53 A malefic in the 7th and any planet in the 9th: she takes the renunciation shown by that malefic.
- v.54-55 [Death before or with the husband - **not used**.]
