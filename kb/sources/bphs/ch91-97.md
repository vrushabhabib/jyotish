# BPHS chapters 91–97 — rites for eclipse, gandanta and other births; the closing chapter (read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

Source: sanskritdocuments.org `par9197.itx` (Ahto Jarve's transliteration, update 1 Nov 2010). My own translation; the Sanskrit is not reproduced. This file replaces the partial notes on ch. 91 and 92 in `kb/sources/bphs-verse-readings.md`.

## Ch. 91 — Birth during an eclipse, 14 verses
- v.1 For those born at the time of an eclipse of the Sun or Moon [illness, hardship and want are stated; the danger wording is not used].
- v.2-4 Images: the lord of the star in which the eclipse falls, in gold; the Sun in gold for a solar eclipse, the Moon in silver for a lunar one; Rahu in lead.
- v.5-13 Red offerings to the Sun, white to the Moon, dark to Rahu; their mantras; fire-sticks of arka, palasha and durva; bathing of the child; feeding.
- v.14 "Having done the rite for an eclipse birth as prescribed, the person overcomes every obstacle and gains good fortune."
- Engine check: the eclipse-birth test (Sun or Moon within the eclipse limit of a node at new or full moon) stands; reported as "a birth for which Parashara prescribes a rite".

## Ch. 92 — Birth at a junction (gandanta), 11 verses
- v.1 Three kinds: of lunar days, of stars, of the lagna. [The harm wording for birth, travel and marriage is not used.]
- **v.2 Lunar-day junction: between a Purna day (5th, 10th, 15th) and the Nanda day that follows (6th, 11th, 1st) - four nadis, before and after.**
- **v.3 Star junction: Revati-Ashwini, Ashlesha-Magha, Jyeshtha-Mula - likewise four nadis.**
- **v.4 Lagna junction: Pisces-Aries, Cancer-Leo, Scorpio-Sagittarius - one ghati, before and after.**
- **v.5 Among these "Abhukta Mula" is the gravest: at the Jyeshtha-Mula junction, five ghatis [of Jyeshtha] and eight [of Mula].**
- v.6-11 The father sees the child after the rite, on the day the birth-impurity ends or another good day. Gifts: a bull for the lunar-day junction, a cow for the star junction, gold for the lagna junction. Born in the first part: bathing rite for the father too; in the second: for the mother. Images of the lunar day and star; fire offering; feeding.
- Engine check: the widths coded on 12 Nov (two ghatis each side of the star junction as "inside", four as "near"; half a ghati each side for the lagna; Abhukta Mula 5 and 8 ghatis) are the two possible readings of "four nadis, before and after" and "one ghati, before and after"; both are reported and labelled. The lunar-day junction of v.2 is **not coded** (to-do, part of the birth-time conditions line).

## Ch. 93 — Abhukta Mula, 20 verses
- v.1 Because the lords of Jyeshtha and Mula (Indra and the Rakshasa) are great enemies, their junction is the gravest.
- v.2 [A wording about setting the child aside or the father not seeing it for eight years - not used; recorded only as the reason families ask for the rite.]
- v.4 The rite on the twelfth day from birth, or when the birth star returns, or on a good day, or in the eighth year.
- v.5-20 A pavilion east or north of the house; an image of the star's deity (Nirriti) in gold; Indra as the over-deity and Water as the counter-deity; a pot with a hundred herbs; 1,008 or 108 offerings; the Tryambaka mantra; bathing of the parents and child; a milch cow to the priest; looking into ghee with a prayer.

## Ch. 94 — Jyeshtha and other junctions, 13 verses
- v.1-6 The Jyeshtha junction: the same pavilion and rite as for Mula, with Indra as the deity, Fire as over-deity and the Rakshasa as counter-deity.
- v.7-9 If means are lacking a gift of cows is enough: three for a Mula, Jyeshtha, Ashlesha or Magha junction; two for Revati or Ashwini; one for any other junction or bad yoga; or their price.
- v.10-13 [Four statements that a girl or boy born in Jyeshtha, the last quarter of Vishakha, the last three quarters of Ashlesha or the first three of Mula harms a named in-law - **not used**; "if that relative does not exist there is no fault" (v.13).] A rite at the time of marriage is prescribed.
- Project note: these verses are the source of the nakshatra objections raised in matching. The project does not state them; if a family raises one, the answer is v.13 and the prescribed rite.

## Ch. 95 — A child born after three of the other sex, 9 verses
- v.1-2 A daughter after three sons or a son after three daughters [a danger to both families is stated - not used].
- v.3-9 The rite: images of Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and Indra in gold on pots over heaps of grain; four Rudra hymns; 1,008, 108 or 28 offerings; bathing of the family; gifts; feeding the poor.

## Ch. 96 — Abnormal births, 10 verses
- v.1-6 Births much before or after term, with missing or extra limbs, and the like; conception or delivery at fifteen or sixteen "is not for good". [Harm wordings not used.] v.7 Animal births in certain months.
- v.8-10 The rite as in ch. 95. "One who does the rite when such a thing has happened gains all he wishes and lives long and happy."
- **Not used** in readings.

## Ch. 97 — Conclusion, 25 verses
- v.1-2 The line of teaching: Brahma to Narada, Narada to Shaunaka and others, and so to Parashara, who has told it to Maitreya as he knew it.
- v.2-4 It is not to be given to the envious, to one who delights in blaming others, to the dull, the ill-mannered or the unknown; it is to be given to one of good character, devoted, truthful, intelligent, modest and of known family.
- **v.5 "He who knows the measure of time and the positions of the planets and stars understands this science rightly - no other."**
- **v.6 "One who knows the true meaning of the science, is truthful and has mastered the senses - when he states a good or bad result, his word comes true."**
- v.7 Those who study or hear it grow in life, strength, means and good name.
- v.8-9 Thus the science told by Parashara to Maitreya, in many chapters, for the good of the world.
- v.10-24 The text's own list of contents, in order: creation; incarnations; qualities of planets and signs; special lagnas; the divisions and their use; sign aspects; early dangers and their cancellation; judgement of houses; results of the houses; results of the house lords; the non-luminous points; exact planetary aspects; exact strengths; good and bad shares (ishta-kashta); pada and upapada; argala; the significators; karakamsha; yoga-givers; nabhasa and other yogas; Moon's yogas; Sun's yogas; raja yogas; yogas of royal association; special wealth yogas; poverty yogas; length of life; death-inflicting planets; planetary states; the dashas and their results; sub-, sub-sub-, prana and sukshma periods; ashtakavarga of the Sun and the rest; trine reduction; one-lord reduction; the pinda; results of ashtakavarga; life-span by ashtakavarga; the combined ashtakavarga; rays; Sudarshana; marks of great persons; the elements; the three qualities; birth data of those whose birth is unknown; renunciation; women's charts; marks of the limbs; curses of a former birth with the means to children; unlucky lagnas, stars and lunar days at birth with their rites; abnormal births with their rites.
- v.25 "Thus a hundred subjects are set in this best of birth-texts."
