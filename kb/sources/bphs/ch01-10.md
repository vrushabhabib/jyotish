# BPHS chapters 1–10 — creation, the planets, the signs, special lagnas, the sixteen divisions, sign aspects, early-life dangers and their cancellation (read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

Source: sanskritdocuments.org `par0110.itx` (Ahto Jarve's transliteration; proofread by Ahto Jarve, Ginda Lass, Abhisyanta Tejaswi; update 22 Dec 2023). My own translation; the Sanskrit is not reproduced.

## Ch. 1 — The order of creation, 24 verses
- v.1-4 Maitreya asks Parashara: of the three branches of the science (hora, calculation, samhita) hora is the best; teach it, and how the world arose and how those in the sky are connected with those on earth.
- v.5-8 Parashara will teach it as heard from Brahma. It is to be given to the calm, the devoted, the truthful and the believing; not to another's pupil, an unbeliever or a deceiver.
- v.9-24 Cosmology: the one unmanifest Vishnu with three powers; from them Sankarshana, Pradyumna, Aniruddha; the three kinds of ego; gods, senses and elements. In all beings there are two portions, the individual and the supreme; in the planets from the Sun onward the supreme portion predominates. [No rule for chart reading.]

## Ch. 2 — The incarnations, 13 verses
- v.3 "The Lord in the form of the planets gives living beings the fruit of their actions."
- v.5-7 Incarnations from the planets: Rama from the Sun, Krishna from the Moon, Narasimha from Mars, Buddha from Mercury, Vamana from Jupiter, Parashurama from Venus, Kurma from Saturn, Varaha from Rahu, Matsya from Ketu.
- v.11-13 Past and future can be known only through this science; it must be studied; one who condemns it without knowing it suffers. [No rule for chart reading.]

## Ch. 3 — Nature and form of the planets, 74 verses
- v.2-5 Lights in the sky: those fixed are stars; those that move through the stars are planets. The zodiac is 27 parts from Ashwini and 12 equal parts from Aries.
- v.6 The rising sign is the lagna; results are judged from it and from the joining and separating of planets.
- **v.8 "Planets are to be made exact by whatever method brings calculation and observation into agreement"; place and time are to be exact too.** [The text itself authorises modern ephemerides.]
- v.10 The nine: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu.
- **v.11 Malefics: Sun, Saturn, Mars, the waning Moon, Rahu, Ketu. The rest are benefic. Mercury joined with a malefic is malefic.**
- v.12-13 Sun the self of all, Moon the mind, Mars strength, Mercury the giver of speech, Jupiter knowledge and happiness, Venus potency, Saturn sorrow.
- v.14-15 Sun and Moon the rulers, Mars the commander, Mercury the prince, Jupiter and Venus the ministers, Saturn the servant, Rahu and Ketu the army.
- v.16-17 Colours: Sun dark red, Moon fair, Mars red and not tall, Mercury dark green like durva grass, Jupiter fair, Venus dusky, Saturn dark.
- v.18 Deities: Fire, Water, Skanda, Vishnu, Indra, Shachi, Brahma for Sun to Saturn.
- v.19 Mercury and Saturn neuter; Moon and Venus female; Sun, Mars, Jupiter male.
- v.20 Elements: Mars fire, Mercury earth, Jupiter ether, Venus water, Saturn air.
- v.21 Classes: Jupiter and Venus priestly; Mars and Sun warrior; Moon and Mercury merchant; Saturn worker.
- v.22 Qualities: Jupiter, Sun, Moon sattva; Mercury and Venus rajas; Saturn and Mars tamas.
- v.23-30 Descriptions of each planet's body and temperament (Sun honey-coloured eyes, square build, bilious, intelligent, sparse hair; Moon windy and phlegmatic, round, sweet-spoken, fickle; Mars cruel, red-eyed, fickle, generous, bilious, angry, slim waist; Mercury fine body, speech with double meaning, fond of jokes, all three humours; Jupiter large body, tawny hair and eyes, phlegmatic, intelligent, learned in all sciences; Venus happy, handsome, fine eyes, a poet, phlegmatic and windy, curly hair; Saturn lean and tall, tawny eyes, windy, large teeth, lazy, lame, coarse hair; Rahu smoky, dark-bodied, forest-dwelling, fearsome, windy, intelligent; Ketu like Rahu).
- v.31 Body tissues: bone, blood, marrow, skin, fat, semen, sinew for Sun to Saturn.
- v.32 Places: temple, water-place, fire-place, playground, treasury, bedroom, refuse heap.
- v.33 Time spans: half-year, moment, day, season, month, fortnight, year for Sun to Saturn.
- v.34 Tastes: pungent, salt, bitter, mixed, sweet, sour, astringent.
- v.35 Directional strength: Mercury and Jupiter in the east, Sun and Mars in the south, Saturn in the west, Venus and Moon in the north.
- v.36 Strong at night: Moon, Mars, Saturn. Mercury always. The rest by day.
- v.37 Malefics strong in the dark half, benefics in the bright half; benefics in the northern course, the others in the southern.
- v.38 Lords of the year, month, day and hour are strong in rising order. Natural strength rises in the order Saturn, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Sun.
- v.39-47 Trees, the nodes' class and dwelling, metals and gems, cloths, seasons (Venus spring, Mars and Sun summer, Moon rains, Mercury autumn, Jupiter early winter, Saturn late winter; Rahu eight months, Ketu three), and the mineral / plant / living classes.
- **v.49-50 Exaltation: Sun Aries 10°, Moon Taurus 3°, Mars Capricorn 28°, Mercury Virgo 15°, Jupiter Cancer 5°, Venus Pisces 27°, Saturn Libra 20°. Debilitation the seventh sign at the same degree.**
- **v.51-54 Moolatrikona: Sun - Leo to 20°, the rest own sign. Moon - Taurus 3° exaltation, the whole remainder moolatrikona. Mars - Aries to 12°, the rest own. Mercury - Virgo to 15° exaltation, the next 5° moolatrikona, the rest own. Jupiter - Sagittarius to 10°. Venus - Libra to 15°. Saturn - Aquarius to 20°.**
- **v.55 Natural friendship: counted from a planet's moolatrikona sign, the lords of the 2nd, 12th, 5th, 9th, 8th and 4th and the lord of its exaltation sign are friends; the others enemies; a planet that falls on both sides is neutral.**
- v.56 Temporary friendship: planets in the 10th, 4th, 11th, 3rd, 2nd and 12th from each other are friends for the time; elsewhere enemies.
- v.57-58 Combined: friend + friend = great friend; friend + neutral = friend; enemy + neutral = enemy; friend + enemy = neutral; enemy + enemy = great enemy.
- **v.59-60 Share of the good result a planet gives: exalted - full; moolatrikona - three quarters; own sign - half; friend's sign - a quarter; neutral sign - an eighth; debilitated, combust or in an enemy's sign - nothing. The bad result runs the opposite way.**
- v.61-65 The five non-luminous points: Dhuma = Sun + 4 signs 13°20'; Vyatipata = 12 signs - Dhuma; Parivesha = Vyatipata + 6 signs; Indrachapa = 12 signs - Parivesha; Upaketu = Indrachapa + 16°40' (one sign more gives back the Sun). All malefic; with the Sun, Moon or lagna they harm lineage, life and understanding [as worded].
- v.66-70 Gulika and its companions: the day is divided into eight parts, ruled in order from the lord of the weekday, the eighth part lordless; the part of Saturn is Gulika. The night likewise, starting from the fifth planet from the weekday lord. The Sun's part is Kala, Mars's Mrityu, Jupiter's Yamaghantaka, Mercury's Ardhaprahara. The sign rising at Gulika's time, worked for the place, is "Gulika". [The verse does not say whether the start or the end of the part is taken.]
- v.71-74 Pranapada: worked from the time since sunrise and the Sun's sign type; in the 2nd, 4th, 10th or 11th from the lagna the birth is good, elsewhere not.

Movable, fixed, dual in turn; harsh and gentle, male and female alternately. Humours: bile, wind, mixed, phlegm in turn from Aries.

## Ch. 5 — Special lagnas, 24 verses
- v.2-3 Bhava lagna: moves one sign every 5 ghatis (2 hours) from sunrise; add the result to the Sun's position at sunrise.
- v.4-5 Hora lagna: one sign every 2½ ghatis (1 hour) from sunrise; added to the Sun at sunrise.
- v.6-8 Ghati lagna: one sign every ghati (24 minutes); added to the Sun at sunrise.
- v.9 A chart is drawn from each, with the planets where they are.
- v.10-15 Varnada: for an odd lagna count from Aries forward to the lagna, for an even lagna from Pisces backward; the same for the hora lagna; if both are odd or both even add the two counts, otherwise take the difference; that number counted from Aries (or backward from Pisces) is the Varnada. Its dasha starts from the stronger of the lagna and hora lagna.
- v.16-21 Its use for length of life of self and relatives [**not used**].
- v.22-24 Varnada is of signs, not planets; sub-periods by twelve.
- Engine check: none of these three lagnas is computed. Hora lagna and ghati lagna are simple (sunrise + elapsed time) and are on the to-do list; their main uses in the text are raja-yoga confirmation (ch. 39) and longevity (not used).

- v.34-41 The sixty names of the sixtieth-parts for odd signs (reversed in even signs); "planets in benefic sixtieth-parts give good results, those in harsh ones destroy".
- v.42-51 Names for a planet in its own divisions in several charts: two = Parijata, three = Uttama, four = Gopura, five = Simhasana, six = Paravata, seven = Devaloka, eight = Brahmaloka, nine = Airavata ("Shakra's mount"), ten = Shridhama (ten-division scheme); separate name lists for the six-, seven- and sixteen-division schemes.
- v.52-53 Divisions of a planet's exaltation, moolatrikona and own sign, and of the lords of kendras from its arudha, are good; divisions that come from combust, defeated, debilitated or weak planets, or planets in the "sleeping" states, destroy yogas.

## Ch. 7 — Use of the divisions; strength by twenty points; house groups, 43 verses
- **v.1-8 What each division is read for: D1 the body; D2 wealth; D3 happiness through brothers and sisters; D4 fortune; D7 children and grandchildren; D9 spouses; D10 "the great result"; D12 parents; D16 comfort and discomfort, vehicles; D20 worship; D24 learning; D27 strength and weakness; D30 misfortunes; D40 good and bad; D45 and D60 everything.** A house whose lord of the sixtieth-part is harsh is destroyed; where the lord of the sixteenth-part is benefic there is growth - "the word of Garga and others".
- v.9-10 Judge the houses and the planets' places by the twenty-point strength (Vimshopaka); by it the ripening of results is seen.
- v.11-16 Notes on grading within a division (full in own sign and exaltation, nil in the seventh from it, proportional between).
- v.17-23 Weights. Six divisions: D1 6, D2 2, D3 4, D9 5, D12 2, D30 1. Seven: D1 5, D2 2, D3 3, D7 2½, D9 4½, D12 2, D30 1. Ten: D1 3, D60 5, the other eight 1½ each. Sixteen: D2 1, D30 1, D3 1, D16 2, D9 3, D1 3½, D60 4, the other nine ½ each.
- v.24-25 Score in each division: own 20, great friend's 18, friend's 15, neutral 10, enemy's 7, great enemy's 5; multiplied by the weight and divided by 20.
- v.26-27 Below 5 no result; above 5 small; above 10 middling; above 15 full.
- v.28-32 A second measure: full when the planet is opposite the Sun, nil when with the Sun, proportional between. Both measures together grade the period: full-full "very full", weak-weak "very weak", and so on, for as long as its dasha lasts.
- v.33-37 Kendras 1, 4, 7, 10; panapharas 2, 5, 8, 11; apoklimas 3, 6, 9, 12; trines 5 and 9 from the lagna; the 6th, 8th, 12th are the bad houses; 4th and 8th are "chaturasra"; **upachayas are 3, 6, 10, 11**. Names of the twelve houses.
- **v.39-42 The same matters are also read from the significator: the father from the 9th from the lagna and the 9th from the Sun; matters of the 10th and 11th also from the 10th and 11th from the Sun; matters of the 4th, 1st, 2nd, 11th and 9th also from those houses from the Moon; the 3rd from Mars; the 6th from Mercury; the 5th from Jupiter; the 7th from Venus; the 8th and 12th from Saturn.**
- v.43 What is judged from a house is judged from its lord as well.

## Ch. 8 — Aspects of signs, 9 verses
- v.2-5 A movable sign aspects the fixed signs except the one next to it; a fixed sign aspects the movable signs except the one next to it; a dual sign aspects the other dual signs. Planets in the signs aspect in the same way.
- v.6-9 The diagram that shows it (Aries-Taurus east, Cancer-Leo north, Libra-Scorpio west, Capricorn-Aquarius south, duals at the corners).

## Ch. 9 — Dangers in early life, 45 verses
- **v.2 "For twenty-four years from birth there is danger from the birth chart; until then do not reckon the length of life."**
- v.3-45 Combinations for the death of an infant, of the mother, of the father. **None of this chapter is used** (guardrail: no death statements; no reading is given on infants). Recorded only as: v.43 "the Sun is the father and the Moon the mother of all beings"; malefics joined with or flanking the Sun, or in the 6th, 8th or 4th from it without benefics, show hardship to the father; the same from the Moon for the mother; "judge hardship or worse by weighing strength and weakness" (v.44-45).

## Ch. 10 — Cancellation of those dangers, 9 verses
- **v.2 Even one of Mercury, Jupiter or Venus in a kendra from the lagna removes all the danger, "as the Sun removes darkness".**
- **v.3 A strong Jupiter alone in the lagna removes the whole heap of dangers.**
- **v.4 The lagna lord alone, strong and in a kendra, removes all of it.**
- v.5 A night birth in the bright half, or a day birth in the dark half, with the lagna aspected by benefics.
- v.6 The Sun in the 12th for Libra lagna [a long-life statement; not used].
- v.7 Jupiter joined with Mars, or Mars aspected by Jupiter: removes the danger and is good for the mother.
- v.8 A malefic in the 4th or 10th between benefics, with benefics in kendras and trines: good for the father.
- v.9 Malefics hemmed between benefics, with benefics in kendras and trines: the danger goes at once, and the bad result of that house does not come.
- Project note: v.2-4 and v.9 are general protections and are usable in a reading in their positive form (a benefic in a kendra, a strong lagna lord in a kendra, a malefic enclosed by benefics). They are already among the "weakened by / protected by" checks; the verse numbers are now attached.
