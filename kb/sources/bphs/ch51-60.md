# BPHS chapters 51–60 — sub-periods: how they are worked out and what each of the 81 gives (read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

Source: sanskritdocuments.org `par5160.itx` (Ahto Jarve's transliteration, update 23 Oct 2026). My own translation; the Sanskrit is not reproduced. About 700 verses. Chapters 52–60 repeat one template for every pair, so the template is stated once and each pair then lists only what is particular to it, with its verse range. Remedies (recitations and gifts) close almost every pair; they are noted once here and not repeated. Death wordings are not carried over (guardrail); medical wordings are marked [medical] and not used.

## Ch. 51 — Working out the sub-periods, 16 verses
- v.1 Sub-period = dasha years x the sub-lord's own years / the total years of the system. The same again for the sub-sub-period and below.
- v.2 **The first sub-period belongs to the dasha lord itself; the others follow in the system's order.** The same order below.
- v.3-4 (For dashas of equal shares) nine equal parts; first the dasha lord's, then of the planets in its kendras and so on, by strength.
- v.5 A sign's dasha is divided into twelve sign sub-periods.
- v.6-9 Their order: start from the stronger of the dasha sign and its 7th; forward if it is odd, backward if even. Special order: for a movable sign the signs run one after another; for a fixed sign every sixth; for a dual sign the kendras, then those from the 5th, then from the 9th.
- v.10-12 "Ripening" (paka) and "enjoyment" (bhoga) signs: in the first round they are the same sign; as far as the ripening sign has moved from the first, so far the enjoyment sign is counted from it. Used in Chara, Sthira and Trikona dashas. Malefics in both: bodily and mental pain.
- v.13-16 Sub-periods in the Pinda-type dashas: the dasha lord takes a full share, a planet with it a half, in its trine a third, in its 4th/8th a quarter, in its 7th a seventh; shares are scaled to the dasha.
- Engine check: v.1-2 are exactly how `engine/dashas.py` builds Vimshottari sub-periods and sub-sub-periods. The Chara sub-period order in `engine/dashas_extra.py` follows K.N. Rao (starts from the next sign, direction by the dasha sign); v.6-9 start from the stronger of the sign and its 7th - a difference between Parashara's text and the modern school, recorded and left as it is because sub-periods of Chara are not used for prediction here.

## The template of chapters 52–60
For sub-lord X inside the dasha of Y the text gives, in this order:
1. **X well placed from the lagna** (kendra, trine or the 11th; exalted, own sign, own or exalted navamsa; with the lagna lord or the lords of the 9th and 10th; with benefics): the good results.
2. **X badly placed from the lagna** (6th, 8th or 12th; debilitated, combust, in an enemy's sign; with malefics): the bad results.
3. **X counted from the dasha lord Y** ("from the lord of the period"): in a kendra, trine, the 3rd or 11th - and for most pairs the 2nd - good; in the 6th, 8th or 12th from Y, or weak - bad.
4. Often a split of the sub-period into start, middle and end.
5. X as lord of the 2nd or 7th (or placed there): bodily trouble [maraka wording - never used to predict], followed by the remedy.

Rule 3 is in almost every pair. **Correction to the KB:** `kb/dashas/dasha-systems.md` 1.2 listed the 2/12 position between dasha lord and sub-lord as adverse on both sides. Parashara lists the sub-lord in the **2nd** from the dasha lord with the good places; only the 12th (with the 6th and 8th) is adverse. Corrected, and the worksheet now prints the sub-lord's house from the dasha lord beside every sub-period.

## What these ten chapters establish
1. **The sub-lord is judged three ways, always:** its house and dignity from the lagna, its company (lagna lord, lords of the 9th and 10th, a yoga-giver, or malefics), and its house from the dasha lord. The third test is present in nearly every pair.
2. **From the dasha lord:** kendra, trine, 3rd, 11th good; 2nd good in most pairs; 6th, 8th, 12th bad. (KB corrected - the 2nd was wrongly listed as adverse.)
3. **Some sub-lords are given bad results first, whatever the placement, and good only as an exception:** Ketu in the Sun's, Rahu's and Saturn's dashas; Rahu in Saturn's dasha; Saturn in Ketu's dasha; the Sun in Venus's dasha. Rahu's sub-period in the Sun's and the Moon's dashas starts badly even when well placed (first two months in the Sun's).
4. **Signs where a node does well are named:** Rahu in Cancer, Scorpio, Virgo, Sagittarius (55.1); Rahu in Cancer, Aquarius, Virgo, Taurus (58.47); Rahu in Aries, Virgo, Cancer, Taurus, Pisces, Sagittarius, Leo (57.68). The three lists differ; Cancer and Virgo are in all three.
5. **Marriage is named in these sub-periods:** Sun-Moon, Sun-Jupiter, Sun-Saturn, Sun-Mercury, Moon-Jupiter, Moon-Mercury, Mars-Moon, Rahu-Saturn, Rahu-Mercury, Rahu-Venus, Jupiter-Saturn (gain of a spouse), Jupiter-Mars, Mercury-Moon (spouse gained), Mercury-Jupiter, Ketu-Mercury, and Saturn-Venus (spouse and children) - always under the "well placed" condition. **A new house** is named in Moon-Venus, Rahu-Venus, Jupiter-Jupiter, Saturn-Mars, Mercury-Moon, Venus-Venus. **Return to one's own country** in Rahu-Jupiter, Rahu-Saturn and Venus-Rahu. These are word-level statements of the text, to be tested against the logged cases before any weight is put on them.
6. **Start / middle / end splits** are given for about a third of the pairs and are recorded in the tables above.
7. Transit as the trigger is named once only (57.27-28: Jupiter's and Saturn's transits in Saturn-Venus).
8. The text has internal anomalies (54.30-32 gives bad results for a good position; 57.13 gives good results under the bad-placement heading). They are recorded as they stand, not smoothed.
