## Ch. 25 — the non-luminous points in the houses, 87 verses
Results of Dhuma, Pata (Vyatipata), Paridhi, Chapa (Indrachapa), Shikhi (Upaketu), Gulika and Pranapada in each of the twelve houses. Broadly: these points harm the house they occupy except in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th, where most give strength; Gulika is the most consistently harmful (lagna, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 12th) and good in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th. v.86-87: the results of the visible planets are to be told from their position, exact aspect and strength. The engine does not compute these points; recorded for completeness.

## Ch. 27 — exact strength (Shadbala and Bhava bala), 40 verses
This is the root text for `engine/shadbala.py`. Values in sixtieths (virupas).
- v.1-2 **Exaltation strength:** planet minus its debilitation point (subtract from 360 if over 180), in degrees, divided by 3.
- v.2-3 **Dignity in the seven divisions:** own trine-sign 45, own sign 30, great friend 20, friend 15, neutral 10, enemy 4, great enemy 2.
  - **Differs from the engine.** The engine uses 45, 30, 22.5, 15, 7.5, 3.75, 1.875, which is B.V. Raman's scale and reproduces both his worked example and AstroSage. Parashara's own figures are the ones in this verse. The engine keeps Raman's as the default because it is the one validated against references, and offers Parashara's as an option; totals differ by a few virupas per planet.
- v.4 The seven divisions: sign, hora, drekkana, seventh, ninth, twelfth and thirtieth parts.
- v.5 Odd-even: Venus and Moon in an even sign and navamsa, the others in odd: a quarter (15). Kendra, next, falling houses: 60, 30, 15.
- v.6 Drekkana: first, middle, last third give 15 to male, neuter and female planets respectively.
- v.7-8 **Directional strength:** subtract the 4th cusp from Sun and Mars, the 7th from Jupiter and Mercury, the lagna from Saturn, the 10th from Venus and Moon; reduce to under 180; divide by 3.
- v.8-9 **Day-night strength:** from the distance of birth from midnight; Moon, Mars, Saturn strong at midnight, the others at noon; Mercury always 60.
- v.10-11 **Fortnight strength:** Moon minus Sun (under 180), divided by 3, for the benefics - Moon, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter; 60 minus that for the malefics.
  - **Correction to our own note.** On 12 Nov the engine began doubling the Moon's own fortnight strength "per Raman and BPHS". This verse does not double it. The doubling is Raman's, and is needed to reproduce his example; it is not in this text. The default goes back to no doubling (Parashara, and what AstroSage prints), with Raman's doubling as a named option.
- v.12 Thirds of the day: Mercury, Sun, Saturn; thirds of the night: Moon, Venus, Mars; Jupiter always. 60 each.
- v.13 Lord of the year 15, of the month 30, of the weekday 45, of the hour 60.
- v.13-14 Natural strength: sevenths of 60, rising in the order Saturn, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Sun.
- v.15-17 Solstice strength from the declination (by three sine-segments 45, 33, 12): north adds for Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Venus; south adds for Saturn and Moon; Mercury always adds; divide by 3.
- v.18 The Sun's motional strength equals its solstice strength; the Moon's equals its fortnight strength.
- v.19 Aspect strength: take away a quarter of the malefic aspect, add a quarter of the benefic aspect, and add the aspect of Mercury and Jupiter in full.
- v.20 Planetary war: the difference of the two strengths is added to the winner and taken from the loser.
- v.21-23 Eight kinds of motion and their strength: retrograde 60, again-retrograde 30, stationary 15, mean 30, slow 15, slower 7.5, fast 45, very fast 30.
- v.24-25 Motional strength of the five planets from the angle between the apex of the fast orbit and the half-sum of mean and true longitude (under 180), divided by 3.
- v.26-31 House strength: direction by the type of sign on the house (human signs measured from the 7th, quadruped from the 4th, insect and water from the lagna or the 10th), divided by 3; plus benefic and minus malefic aspect by quarters, Mercury's and Jupiter's in full; plus the strength of the lord; a house holding Mercury or Jupiter gains one unit, one holding Saturn, Mars or the Sun loses one; day, twilight and night births add a quarter to head-rising, both-rising and back-rising signs.
- v.32-33 Required totals: Sun 390, Moon 360, Mars 300, Mercury 420, Jupiter 390, Venus 330, Saturn 300.
- v.34-36 Required component minima for Jupiter, Mercury and Sun: position 165, direction 35, motion 50, time 112, solstice 30; for Venus and Moon: 133, 50, 30, 100, 40; for Mars and Saturn: 96, 30, 40, 67, 20.
- v.37-38 Among several planets forming a yoga, the strongest is the one that delivers it.
- v.39-40 Who may be trusted to predict: one skilled in calculation and in grammar, who knows logic, place and time, is self-controlled and has studied the text.
- Confirmed by this chapter: the drekkana order (male, neuter, female), the hour-lord rule, natural strengths, the required totals, Sun's and Moon's motional strength, and that Mercury's and Jupiter's aspects are added in full.

## Ch. 28 — Ishta and Kashta (good and bad portion), 20 verses
- v.2-6 "Exaltation rays" and "motion rays" are derived from the exaltation and motional strengths; the good portion (ishta) is the root of their product, the bad portion (kashta) the root of the product of their complements.
- v.7-9 Good portion by dignity in a sign: exalted 60, own trine-sign 45, own sign 30, great friend 22, friend 15, neutral 8, enemy 4, great enemy 2, debilitated 0; the complement is the bad portion; half of these in the other divisions.
- v.10-17 How the good and bad portions are applied to direction, time, aspect and house results.
- v.18-20 The same method for Ashtakavarga and for a house lying across two signs.

## Ch. 29 — Arudha pada, 37 verses
- v.2-3 Count from the lagna to its lord; the same count again from the lord gives the pada of the lagna. So for every house. The lagna's pada is the chief one.
- v.4-5 **The house itself and its 7th cannot be the pada.** When the count lands on the house itself, the pada is the 10th from it; when it lands on the 7th, the pada is the 4th from it. Examples in the text: lord in the 4th - the pada is the 4th; lord in the 7th - the pada is the 10th.
  - Confirms the engine's exception rule exactly.
- v.6 A planet's arudha: count from the planet to its sign and the same again.
- v.7 For planets with two signs and signs with two lords, use the stronger.
- v.8-12 11th from the pada occupied or aspected by planets: wealthy and happy; by benefics through fair means, malefics through unfair; all planets aspecting it: a ruler or like one; 11th aspected and 12th not: gain without obstacles.
- v.13-15 Many aspects and argalas on the 11th from the pada, benefic or exalted, raise fortune step by step.
- v.16-21 12th from the pada joined or aspected: much expense - benefics on good causes, malefics on bad. With Venus, Sun and Rahu: expense through rulers (more so with the Moon's aspect); Mercury: among kin, or through quarrels if a malefic aspects; Jupiter: through taxes; Saturn with Mars: through brothers.
- v.22 The same planets in the 11th give gain through those same sources.
- v.23-24 Rahu or Ketu in the 7th from the pada: stomach trouble; Ketu there with a malefic: bold, grey early.
- v.25 Jupiter, Venus and Moon - all, two or one - in the 7th from the pada: wealthy.
- v.26 An exalted planet, benefic or malefic, in the 7th from the pada: wealthy and well reputed.
- v.27 The same combinations apply to the 2nd from the pada.
- v.28 Mercury, Jupiter or Venus exalted and strong in the 2nd from the pada: wealth.
- v.29 The same combinations are read from the Karakamsa.
- v.30 Mercury in the 2nd from the pada: lord of a whole country; Jupiter: all-knowing; Venus: poet and speaker.
- v.31-32 The 7th house's pada in a kendra or trine from the lagna's pada, or either with a strong planet: wealthy and well known; the 7th's pada in the 6th, 8th or 12th from it: without wealth.
- v.33 A strong planet in the pada, its 7th, a kendra, a trine or a growing house: happiness between husband and wife.
- v.34 **The 7th's pada in a kendra or trine from the lagna's pada: friendship between the two; in the 6th, 8th or 12th: enmity.**
- v.35 The same test between the lagna's pada and the pada of the 5th and other houses shows friendship or enmity, gain or loss, with children and the rest.
- v.36 Lagna's pada and 7th's pada in mutual kendras, or in the 3rd and 11th, or in trines: a ruler.
- v.37 So from the lagna's pada and the 2nd's pada and others, looking at both places.
- Checkable additions for the engine: the 11th and 12th from the arudha lagna; the position of A7 from AL (kendra/trine vs 6/8/12) as the classical harmony-of-couple test.
