# Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra — full reading from the Sanskrit

Source: the Sanskrit e-text at sanskritdocuments.org (ITRANS files par0110 … par9197, transliterated by Ahto Jarve, proofread by Ahto Jarve with Ginda Lass and Abhisyanta Tejaswi for ch. 1-10; file dates between 2010 and Oct 2026; "prepared by volunteers … for personal study and research"). The text was opened directly in a browser and read in full; the translations in this folder are this project's own. The Sanskrit itself is not reproduced here, in keeping with the site's request.

Conventions
- One line per verse or verse-group: `v.N` then the rule in plain English. "L7" = lord of the 7th house; houses are counted from the lagna unless stated.
- [literal: …] marks harsh classical wording (death, widowhood, childlessness, character slurs). It is recorded for completeness and is never passed on literally in a reading.
- [?] marks a word or line whose reading is uncertain; the verse should be checked before a rule is built on it.
- Ages given by the text for marriage, children, illness or a parent's death belong to another era and are recorded only as evidence of which combination the text links to early, middle or late timing.

## What is deliberately not used
Length of life and death-inflicting periods (ch. 43-44, 71, parts of 46, 70); infant-death combinations (ch. 9); body marks and moles (ch. 81-82); character labels on women (80.8-16, 26-33); statements of widowhood, childlessness, a child's sex or number of children; reconstruction of a birth time from a question chart (ch. 78); every wording that a birth harms a named relative (ch. 85-96). They are recorded so that the reading of the text is complete and so that nobody mistakes their absence from a reading for ignorance of them.
