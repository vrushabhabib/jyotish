- v.83 "Thus the results of the star dashas with their sub-divisions, told in brief."
- What the three grids show: at these fine levels Parashara gives the result **by the natural character of the planet only** - Jupiter, Venus, Mercury and the Moon good, the Sun, Mars, Saturn and the nodes bad - with few exceptions (Jupiter's line treats Saturn kindly at the pratyantar level; Mars's sub-period is bad under almost every planet at the pratyantar level; Ketu's is bad under all nine). Many verses of ch. 62 and 63 are word-for-word the same. These levels last days to weeks; at an uncertain birth time they cannot be dated. **Project rule:** the grids are a reference, not a basis for dated statements.

## Ch. 64 — Sub-periods of the Kalachakra dasha, 58 verses
- v.2-55 For a birth in the navamsa of each sign, the result of each sign-lord's sub-period in its own navamsa-period. Pattern: Mars, Saturn and the Sun bring fever, wounds, quarrels, travel away, fear from authority; Mercury, Venus, the Moon and Jupiter bring learning, clothes, spouse and children, wealth, honour.
- v.56 The same holds for the reverse-order stars.
- v.57 "This result was told long ago as made by right and wrong action; all of it is surely met by living beings."
- **v.58 "The sub-period of a friend is good, even of a malefic; and that of an enemy is bad, even of a benefic."** [A general rule stated at the close: friendship between dasha lord and sub-lord overrides natural character.]

## Ch. 65 — Results by the navamsa in the Kalachakra dasha, 32 verses
- v.1-31 The 12 x 9 table of ch. 49 again (result of each sign-period for a birth in each sign's navamsa), with small differences of wording from ch. 49 (for example Virgo in an Aries navamsa: "comfort through women" here, "loss to women" there). Reference only.
- v.32 In a harsh planet's period do the propitiation; then good comes in that period.

## Ch. 66 — Ashtakavarga, 72 verses
- v.1-6 Maitreya: the results told so far are mixed by many opinions and by the mixing of planetary motions; people of the present age cannot say "it is exactly so"; give a method a small mind can follow for happiness, sorrow and the length of life from the planets' positions at a given time. Parashara agrees to give it.
- v.7-9 General rule restated: a house gives good when aspected or joined by benefics and by planets in exaltation or own sign - not by combust, debilitated or enemy-sign planets; aspected or joined by malefics so placed, the reverse.
- **v.10-12 "To settle the length of life and the course of life with certainty, neither Vasishtha nor Brihaspati is able - how much less men, and in the Kali age especially." Astrology is of two parts, general and particular; the general has been told, now the part that gives certainty.**
- v.13-15 As results are read from the lagna and from the Moon, so they are to be read from every planet; hence the eight tables, of the seven planets and the lagna, marked with "karana" (dot, unfavourable) and "sthana" (line, favourable).
- v.16-42 The dot-places of each of the seven planets, house by house, first as counts and then by naming who gives the dot. v.43-60 the line-places the same way. v.61-68 the lagna's own table.
- v.69 A dot is unfavourable, a line favourable.
- v.70-72 The chart: 10 vertical and 14 horizontal lines; the eight givers along the side, the twelve houses along the top.

## Ch. 67 — Reduction by trines, 5 verses
- v.2-3 The four trine groups (Aries-Leo-Sagittarius; Taurus-Virgo-Capricorn; Gemini-Libra-Aquarius; Cancer-Scorpio-Pisces).
- v.4 In each group subtract the smallest figure from all three. If one of the three is zero, make no reduction.
- v.5 If all three are equal, remove all. Then do the one-lord reduction.

## Ch. 68 — Reduction for signs with one lord, 6 verses
- v.2 Applies to the two signs of one planet when both have a figure. Both without planets and unequal: reduce the larger to the smaller.
- v.3 Both occupied by planets: no reduction. One occupied with the smaller figure and the other empty with the larger: make the empty one equal to the smaller.
- v.4 The occupied one has the larger figure: remove the whole of the empty one.
- v.5 Both empty and equal: remove both. One occupied and one empty, equal: remove the empty one.
- v.6 Cancer and Leo stand alone, each its own.

## Ch. 69 — The pinda, 4 verses
- v.1-2 Multiply the reduced figure of each sign by the sign's multiplier, and for a sign holding a planet by the planet's multiplier as well; the sum is the pinda.
- v.2-3 Sign multipliers: Taurus and Leo 10; Gemini and Scorpio 8; Libra and Aries 7; Capricorn and Virgo 5; the rest their own number (Cancer 4, Sagittarius 9, Aquarius 11, Pisces 12).
- v.4 Planet multipliers: Jupiter 10, Mars 8, Venus 7, Mercury 5, the others (Sun, Moon, Saturn) 5.

## Ch. 70 — Results from Ashtakavarga, 44 verses
- v.1-5 What each table is read for: **Sun** - self, nature, strength, father; **Moon** - mind, understanding, cheerfulness, mother; **Mars** - brothers, courage, qualities, land; **Mercury** - trade, livelihood, friends; **Jupiter** - bodily nourishment, learning, children, wealth; **Venus** - marriage, enjoyments, vehicles, association with women; **Saturn** - length of life, means of living, sorrow, fear, loss.
- v.5-6 Timing method: pinda x the points of the house in question, divided by 27; when Saturn transits the star of the remainder (or its trines), that house suffers. (Divided by 12: the sign.)
- v.7-18 The father from the 9th from the Sun, by that method [bereavement timing - **not used**]. v.15-18 comparisons with the father's chart: born with the 3rd sign from the father's birth sign rising - lives on the father's wealth; with the 10th from it - has the father's qualities; its lord in the lagna - excels the father.
- **v.19-20 The sign with no points in the Sun's table: in that solar month avoid marriage, vows and other auspicious rites. Where the points are many, do auspicious work in that month even if the Sun and Jupiter are otherwise unhelpful.**
- v.21 Likewise the Moon: on days when the Moon is in a sign with many blanks in its table, avoid auspicious work.
- v.22-23 The 4th from the Moon: mother, house, village. [Mother's timing - not used.]
- v.24-27 Mars's table: brothers, courage, steadiness; the 3rd from Mars is the brothers' house. After the trine reduction, where the figure is large: gain of land, happiness of spouse and brothers; where the figures are spent: loss when Mars passes there.
- v.28-29 The 4th from Mercury: family, maternal uncle, friends; Mercury passing a sign with many points: their happiness.
- v.30-33 The 5th from Jupiter: knowledge, right conduct, children; many points: happiness through children. [v.31-32 give a count of children from the points - **not used**.]
- v.34-36 Venus's table: from the direction and region of the signs with the most points come wealth, spouse and land. **The 7th from Venus is where gaining a spouse is judged; the spouse comes from the direction and region of that sign and its trines.**
- v.37-42 The 8th from Saturn and totals from lagna to Saturn as ages of illness [length-of-life material - **not used**].
- **v.43-44 The sign with no points in Saturn's own table: nothing good while Saturn passes through it. The sign with many points: good results are certain at that time.**
- Engine check: v.43-44 is the rule the worksheet already applies in section 13 (transit with the planet's own points). v.19-21 (months and days to avoid or prefer, from the Sun's and Moon's tables) and v.34-36 (direction of the spouse from the 7th from Venus) are **not in the worksheet yet** - added to the to-do list. The reductions of ch. 67-69 are not coded; their main use in ch. 70 is bereavement and longevity timing, which the project does not predict.
