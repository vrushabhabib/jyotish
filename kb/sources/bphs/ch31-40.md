# BPHS chapters 31–40 — argala, significators, karakamsa, functional nature, yogas (read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

## Ch. 31 — Argala (intervention), 18 verses
- v.2 Argala is what makes the result of a house firm and the result of a planet steady.
- v.3 A planet in the 4th, 2nd or 11th from a house or planet forms its argala. Planets in the 10th, 12th and 3rd from it, respectively, obstruct these.
- v.4 Obstructing planets that are weaker or fewer do not count.
- v.4-5 Three or more malefics in the 3rd form an argala of their own (the "reverse" argala).
- v.6 The 5th is also an argala place; the 9th obstructs it. For Rahu and Ketu the reckoning is reversed.
- v.7 One planet: weak argala; two: middling; more than two: best.
- v.8 Argala is of two kinds, from a sign and from a planet; unobstructed it gives good results, obstructed none.
- v.9 The result comes in the period of the sign or planet that holds the argala.
- v.11 Unobstructed argala on the arudha, the lagna or the 7th: very famous and fortunate.
- v.12 A planet, malefic or benefic, in a good argala that also aspects the lagna strengthens it.
- v.13-16 Argala on the 2nd: wealth and grain; 3rd: happiness from siblings; 4th: house, cattle, relatives; 5th: children and intelligence; 6th: fear of enemies; 7th: wealth and married happiness; 8th: hardship; 9th: rise of fortune; 10th: honour from rulers; 11th: gains; 12th: excess of expense.
- v.17 Benefic argala gives many comforts, malefic argala middling, mixed still good.
- v.18 Argala on the lagna, 5th and 9th together: a ruler, fortunate.

## Ch. 32 — Significators (karakas), 37 verses
- v.1-2 The movable significators are seven, Sun to Saturn, or eight with Rahu. "When two planets are equal in degrees, consider up to Rahu." Some hold seven, some eight.
- v.3-4 The planet with the most degrees is the Atma-karaka; ties are settled by minutes, then seconds. The one with the fewest is the last.
- v.5 Rahu's degrees are counted backward: subtract from 30.
- v.6-12 The Atma-karaka is the chief, like a king; the others act only in accord with it. If it is adverse the others cannot give their good; if favourable they cannot give their bad.
- v.13-15 **Order, by falling degrees:** Atma (self), Amatya (minister), Bhratri (sibling), Matri (mother), Pitri (father), Putra (child), Jnati (kin), Dara (spouse).
- v.16 "Others say the mother's significator is also the child's."
  - **Effect on the engine.** With seven planets the text's own scheme is: self, minister, sibling, mother-and-child, father, kin, spouse. The engine's seven-karaka list (the one AstroSage prints) calls the fifth planet "child" and has no father. By this verse the fifth planet is the father's significator and the child shares the mother's. The engine now carries both labelings and uses Parashara's for the child.
- v.16-17 If two planets are equal in degrees, the later significator is dropped and the fixed significator is used for it.
- v.18-21 **Fixed significators:** father - the stronger of Sun and Venus; mother - the stronger of Moon and Mars; Mars - sister, wife's brother, younger brother, mother; Mercury - maternal relatives; Jupiter - paternal grandfather; Venus - husband; Saturn - sons; and a further group for wife, parents, in-laws and maternal grandfather [the planet for this last group is not named in the line as printed].
- v.22-24 **By house from a planet:** father from the 9th from the Sun, mother from the 4th from the Moon, brother from the 3rd from Mars, maternal uncle from the 6th from Mercury, child from the 5th from Jupiter, spouse from the 7th from Venus, death from the 8th from Saturn.
- v.25-30 Yoga-karakas: planets in own, exalted or friendly signs that are in kendras from each other, the one in the 10th most of all. With such planets even one born in a humble family prospers; results follow the family's standing.
- v.31-34 **House significators:** 1 Sun, 2 Jupiter, 3 Mars, 4 Moon, 5 Jupiter, 6 Mars, 7 Venus, 8 Saturn, 9 Jupiter, 10 Mercury, 11 Jupiter, 12 Saturn.
- v.35-37 The 11th, 3rd, 8th, 6th, 2nd and 12th are called cruel houses; connection with them harms a house. Kendras and trines are auspicious; by connection with them even a bad planet turns good.
- Project note: v.22-24 are the classical basis for reading a relative "from the significator" as well as from the lagna. v.18: for the father Parashara names the Sun **or Venus**, whichever is stronger - the three sources that seemed to conflict in the family file are all in these verses.

## Ch. 34 — Functional nature of the planets by house lordship, 46 verses
General rules
- v.2 Benefics that rule kendras do not give good; malefics that rule kendras do not give bad; lords of trines give good.
- v.3-4 The lagna, being both kendra and trine, is specially good. 5th and 9th are houses of wealth, 7th and 10th of happiness. Lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th give bad results.
- v.5 Lords of the 12th, 2nd and 8th give results according to their company and their other house.
- v.6 The 8th lord is not good, being 12th from the 9th; if he also owns the 3rd, 7th or 11th he is bad; if he also owns a trine he gives good.
- v.7 Where these rules compete the stronger prevails. **Sun and Moon do not carry the blemish of ruling the 8th.**
- v.8-9 Natural grading: Jupiter and Venus benefic, Moon middling, Mercury neutral; Sun, Saturn, Mars malefic. In rising order of strength: full Moon, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus among benefics; waning Moon, Sun, Saturn, Mars among malefics.
- v.10 The blemish of ruling a kendra, for benefics, rises in the order Moon, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus.
- v.11-12 **Raja yoga:** lords of a kendra and a trine in each other's signs, together in one sign, one in the other's sign, or in full mutual aspect.
- v.13 One planet ruling both a kendra and a trine, placed in a kendra or trine, is a special yoga-karaka.
- v.14 A malefic becomes good by ruling a kendra only if it also rules a trine.
- v.15 If the kendra and trine lords themselves also rule bad houses, their mere connection gives no yoga.
- v.16-17 Rahu and Ketu give the results of the lords they join and the houses they occupy; in a kendra with a trine lord, or in a trine with a kendra lord (joined or aspected), they become yoga-karakas.

## Ch. 35 — the 32 Nabhasa (sky-pattern) yogas, 50 verses
Formation (v.7-17), seven planets only
- Three "refuge" yogas: all in movable signs - Rajju; fixed - Musala; dual - Nala.
- Two "petal" yogas: benefics in three kendras - Mala; malefics in three kendras - Sarpa.
- Twenty "shape" yogas: all in two adjacent kendras - Gada; in lagna and 7th - Shakata; in 10th and 4th - Vihaga; in lagna, 5th and 9th - Shringataka; in the three trines of any other house - Hala; benefics in lagna and 7th with malefics in 10th and 4th - Vajra; the reverse - Yava; all in the four kendras, mixed - Kamala; all outside the kendras - Vapi; in the four signs from the lagna - Yupa; from the 4th - Shara; from the 7th - Shakti; from the 10th - Danda; in seven signs from the lagna - Nauka; from the 4th - Kuta; from the 7th - Chatra; from the 10th - Chapa; in six alternate signs from the lagna - Chakra; in six alternate signs from the 2nd - Samudra. Ardha-chandra is named in the list but its formation verse is not in the text as printed.
- Seven "number" yogas, only when none of the above applies: all in one sign - Gola; two - Yuga; three - Shula; four - Kedara; five - Pasha; six - Dama; seven - Vina.
Results (v.18-49), in brief: Rajju - fond of travel, good-looking, harsh; Musala - honour, knowledge, wealth, steady; Nala - uneven limbs, saves money, clever; Mala - always comfortable, vehicles and good food; Sarpa - crooked, cruel, poor, unhappy; Gada - always busy earning, sacrifices, music, gold; Shakata - sickly, lives by carts, poor, no friends; Vihaga - wanderer, messenger, quarrelsome; Shringataka - fond of dispute, enduring in battle, happy, good spouse; Hala - eats much, poor, farmer, anxious; Vajra - happy at the start and end of life, brave; Yava - observes vows, happy in mid-life, steady; Kamala - wealth and virtue, long life, wide fame; Vapi - skilled at saving, steady wealth, sons; Yupa - self-knowledge, sacrifices, vows; Shara - maker of arrows, jailer, hunter, cruel; Shakti - poor, unsuccessful, lazy, long-lived, skilled in battle; Danda - loses sons and wife, poor, servile; Nauka - lives by water, well known, greedy; Kuta - liar, jailer, poor, lives in hill forts; Chatra - shelters his own people, kind, dear to rulers, happy at the start and end; Chapa - liar, keeper of secrets, thief, happy in mid-life; Ardha-chandra - commander, handsome, strong, jewels; Chakra - a ruler at whose feet others bow; Samudra - gems and wealth, enjoyments, steady fortune; Vina - music and dance, happy, wealthy, a leader; Dama - helps people, just, wealthy, learned; Pasha - liable to bondage, capable, talkative, many servants; Kedara - useful to many, farmer, truthful, happy; Shula - sharp, lazy, poor, violent, famed in battle; Yuga - heretic, poor, outcast; Gola - strong but poor, unlearned, always unhappy.
- v.50 These yogas give their results throughout every dasha.

Vapi in the text is simply "all outside the kendras"; the engine uses the stricter classical reading (all in the houses next to the kendras, or all in the falling houses). ## Ch. 37 — Yogas of the Moon, 13 verses
- v.1 Moon in a kendra, the next house, or a falling house from the Sun: wealth, intelligence and skill are low, middling, high.
- v.2-4 Moon in its own or a great friend's navamsa, by day aspected by Jupiter or by night by Venus: wealth and happiness. The reverse, unaspected by them: little or no wealth.
- v.5 **Adhi yoga:** benefics in the 8th, 6th and 7th from the Moon: ruler, minister or commander by their strength.
- v.6 All benefics in the growing houses (3, 6, 10, 11) from the Moon: very wealthy; two: middling; one: little.
- v.7-10 A planet other than the Sun in the 2nd from the Moon - **Sunapha**; in the 12th - **Anapha**; in both - **Durudhara**.
- v.11-12 **Kemadruma:** no planet other than the Sun with the Moon or in the 2nd or 12th from it - "or" no planet other than the Moon in a kendra from the lagna. Result: despised, without learning, poor.
  - Note: the second clause is usually read as a condition that must also hold (so a planet in a kendra from the lagna cancels the yoga). The verse as printed joins the two with "or"; the engine keeps the usual reading and treats a planet in a kendra as a cancellation.
- v.13 A Moon yoga in particular overrides the results of other yogas.
