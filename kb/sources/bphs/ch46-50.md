## Ch. 46 — The kinds of dasha, 210 verses

### The list (v.3-11)
"Dashas are of many kinds; **among them Vimshottari is held the chief**" (v.3). Named after it: Ashtottari, Shodashottari, Dwadashottari, Panchottari, Shatabdika (100), Chaturashiti (84), Dwisaptati (72), Shashtihayani (60), Shattrimshat (36) - all star-based (v.3-5). Then Kala, Chakra, Kalachakra ("honoured among all dashas", v.6), Chara, Sthira, Kendradi, Karaka, Brahma-graha, Manduka, Shula, Yogardha, Drig, Trikona, Rashi, Panchasvara, Yogini, Paindi, Amshi, Naisargika, Ashtakavarga, Sandhya, Pachaka, Tara. v.11 [?]: the others "are not accepted by all".

### Vimshottari (v.12-16)
- v.12 Counted from Krittika, three rounds; lords in order Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Ketu, Venus.
- v.13 Count from Krittika to the birth star, divide by 9; the remainder gives the first lord.
- v.14 "The full life was stated earlier as 120 years; therefore in Kali the Vimshottari dasha is the chief."
- v.15 Years 6, 10, 7, 18, 16, 19, 17, 7, 20.
- v.16 Balance: dasha years x elapsed part of the star / whole span of the star = expired; the rest remains.
- Engine check: identical to `engine/dashas.py`.

### Kala and Chakra dashas (v.43-51)
- v.44-49 Kala: five ghatis before and after sunrise and sunset are twilight (20 ghatis in all); 20 ghatis of day are "full", 20 of night "dull"; the part elapsed in the birth segment, scaled, is multiplied by 1 to 9 for the Sun ... Ketu.
- v.50-51 Chakra: begins from the lagna sign for a night birth, from the sign holding the lagna lord for a day birth, from the 2nd house sign for a twilight birth; **ten years a sign**, in order.

### Chara dasha (v.155-167)
- v.155 For each sign from the lagna to the 12th: **the count from the sign to its lord is the number of years**. [The verse states the count plainly; whether the starting sign is counted in (and one taken off, as the Jaimini tradition does) is not said here. [?]]
- v.156 The twelve signs make four groups of three from Aries. In an odd group (Aries-Gemini, Libra-Sagittarius) count forward; in an even group (Cancer-Virgo, Capricorn-Pisces) count backward.
- v.157 Scorpio has two lords, Ketu and Mars; Aquarius has Saturn and Rahu.
- v.158-159 Both lords in their own sign: 12 years. One in the sign and one elsewhere: take the one elsewhere.
- v.160-163 Both elsewhere: take the stronger. Stronger = the one with a planet beside it over the one alone; if both have company, the one with more planets; if equal, by the strength of the sign - "movable, fixed, dual, strong in that order"; if still equal, the one giving more years.
- v.164 One of them exalted and the other elsewhere: take the exalted one.
- v.165 **Add one year when the lord is exalted; take one off when it is debilitated.**
- v.167 The order of the dashas starts from the lagna sign and runs forward or backward **by the group of the sign in the 9th house**.
- Engine check: forward/backward count groups (v.156), the order by the 9th house (v.167 - it reproduces K.N. Rao's list of direct lagnas exactly, checked for all twelve), the two-lord rules of v.157-160 and the "more years" last step all match `engine/dashas_extra.py`. Two differences found: (1) the sign-strength step of v.163 was missing - **added**; (2) the engine followed K.N. Rao and AstroSage in not applying the one-year exaltation/debilitation change of v.165 - now available as `bphs_adjust=True`, default unchanged so that the AstroSage cross-check still holds.

### Sthira and the sign dashas that follow (v.168-190)
- v.168-169 Sthira: movable signs 7 years, fixed 8, dual 9; starts from the sign holding the "Brahma" planet.
- v.171-173 Brahma: the strongest of the lords of the 6th, 8th and 12th (taken from the stronger of the lagna and the 7th) that sits in an odd sign behind it [?]; or the 8th lord from the atmakaraka, or a planet in the 8th. If Saturn or a node qualifies, the 6th planet from it is taken. Several candidates: the one with most degrees; then the stronger.
- v.174-176 Yogardha: the mean of the Chara and Sthira years, starting from the stronger of the lagna and the 7th. Kendradi: from the stronger of lagna and 7th, signs in kendras first, then the next, by strength; also taken from the atmakaraka; years as in Chara.
- v.177-178 Karaka dasha: starts from the atmakaraka and follows the karakas in order; years = the count from the lagna to the karaka.
- v.179-180 Manduka (also called Trikuta): from the stronger of lagna and 7th, forward in an odd sign and backward in an even; years as in Sthira.
- v.181-182 Shula: "by some, for judging the end"; from the stronger of the 2nd and 8th [reckoned from lagna and 7th]; years as in Sthira. **Not used** (guardrail).
- v.183-184 Trikona: from the strongest of the lagna and its trines, run like Chara; years counted to the lord.
- v.185-187 Drig: the 9th and the signs it aspects, then the 10th and the signs it aspects, then the 11th likewise; years as in Sthira.
- v.188-190 Rashi dasha in the star: the Moon's elapsed part of the star x 12 / the star's span gives signs, added to the lagna; from there forward in an odd sign and backward in an even.

### Remaining kinds (v.191-209)
- v.191-194 Panchasvara: the five vowels with the consonants under them in rows; the vowel over the first letter of the name starts; five dashas of 12 years.
- v.195-200 **Yogini**, "told by Shiva": Mangala, Pingala, Dhanya, Bhramari, Bhadrika, Ulka, Siddha, Sankata; from them arose Moon, Sun, Jupiter, Mars, Mercury, Saturn, Venus, Rahu. Birth star number + 3, divided by 8; remainder 1 = Mangala. Years 1 to 8. Balance from the star as in Vimshottari.
- v.201 Paindi, Amshi, Naisargika: the same as the length-of-life methods of those names.
- v.202-203 Ashtakavarga dasha: first the strongest of lagna, Sun and Moon, then the planets in its kendras and so on by strength; results by ashtakavarga strength.
- v.204-206 Sandhya: a twelfth of the full life to each house from the lagna; Pachaka subdivides it.
- v.207-209 Tara: "by some"; the Vimshottari lords with the nine star-names (Janma, Sampat, Vipat, Kshema, Pratyari, Sadhaka, Vadha, Maitra, Parama-maitra), started from the strongest planet in a kendra; only for a chart with a planet in a kendra.
- Engine check (Yogini): names, lords, years and the +3 / divide-by-8 rule are identical to `engine/dashas_extra.py`.

## Ch. 47 — Results of the dashas, 89 verses
- v.2 Results are of two kinds, general and special: by the planet's nature and by where it sits.
- v.3-4 **By the planet's strength; and timing inside the dasha by its drekkana: a planet in the first third of its sign gives its result at the start of the dasha, in the middle third in the middle, in the last third at the end - reversed when the planet is retrograde.**
- v.5-6 At the start of a dasha, the dasha lord in the lagna with benefic aspect, exalted, in own or a friend's sign: good. In the 6th, 8th or 12th, debilitated, combust or in an enemy's sign: bad.
- General pattern for every planet: strong = in exaltation, own sign, moolatrikona, in a kendra, trine or the 11th, with the lords of the 9th and 10th. Weak = debilitated, in the 6th, 8th or 12th, with malefics or the lords of bad houses.
- v.7-15 Sun. Strong: wealth, great honour from the ruler, vehicles; with the 5th lord children, with the 2nd lord riches, with the 4th lord vehicles. Weak: anger of the ruler, fines, living away, fever, loss of name, quarrels with relatives, worry on the father's side. A benefic's aspect gives some comfort in the middle.
- v.17-26 Moon. Strong and full: fortune, money and grain, good events at home, vehicles, success of efforts, white clothes, children, cattle; in the 2nd exalted or own, much wealth and learning. Waning or debilitated: loss of money; weak with malefics: dullness, worry, trouble through servants and to the mother. In the 6th, 8th or 12th: displeasure of the ruler, mental pain. Strong in a bad house: gain at times.
- v.28-33 Mars. Strong: land, money, honour, a place abroad, happiness of brothers; in a kendra or the 3rd, money through courage and victory over rivals - good at the start, hardship at the end. Weak in a bad house with malefics: unwelcome.
- v.35-36 **Rahu is exalted in Taurus and Ketu in Scorpio; their moolatrikonas are Gemini and Sagittarius; their own signs Aquarius and Scorpio - "Virgo and Pisces according to some".**
- v.36-43 Rahu. Well placed (kendra, trine, 11th or 3rd, a benefic's sign, with benefics or a yoga-giver): comfort, vehicles, children, a new house, honour from a foreign ruler. In the 8th or 12th, with malefics or a maraka, debilitated: loss of place, worry, trouble to spouse and children, poor food. Start: bodily trouble and loss; middle: comfort and gain at home; end: hardship.
- v.45-51 Jupiter. Strong: authority, fame, worship, spouse and children, vehicles, sacred study, wishes granted. Debilitated, combust, with malefics, in the 8th or 12th: loss of place, worry, trouble to children, loss of cattle, pilgrimage. Hardship at the start, comfort in the middle and end.
- v.53-60 Saturn. In exaltation, own or a friend's sign, moolatrikona, the 9th, the 3rd or 11th: honour, good name, money, command, a happy home. In the 6th, 8th or 12th, debilitated or combust: danger from poison and weapons, loss of place, separation from parents, trouble to spouse and children, confinement. With benefics or a yoga-giver, in a kendra, trine or the 11th, or **in Pisces or Sagittarius**: authority and vigour.
- v.62-70 Mercury. Strong: money, good name, more knowledge, favour of rulers, health, gain from trade. With or aspected by malefics: displeasure of rulers, quarrels with relatives, going abroad, serving others. In the 6th, 8th or 12th: loss of gains, wind disorders, anaemia [medical wording not used], fear of rulers, thieves, fire, loss of land and cattle. Start: money and learning; middle: honour; end: sorrow.
- v.72-77 Ketu. In a kendra, trine or the 11th, a benefic's sign, exalted: favour of the ruler, headship of a village or region, vehicles, children, travel abroad that ends well; **in the 3rd, 6th or 11th Ketu's dasha gives comfort**. Start: rise; middle: fear; end: far travel. In the 2nd, 8th or 12th with malefic aspect: confinement, loss of relatives and place, illness.
- v.79-87 Venus. Exalted, own sign, kendra, trine: a crowning, vehicles, clothes, jewels, fine food, music, weddings, children, recovery of lost wealth. In the 6th, 8th or 12th or debilitated: enmity with one's own people, trouble to the spouse, loss in business, loss of cattle. As lord of the 9th or 10th in the lagna or 4th: headship of a region, temples and tanks built, charity.
- v.88-89 In its own sub-period these results come; in other sub-periods according to strengths. As lord of the 2nd or 7th: bodily trouble [maraka - not used to predict]; remedies named.

## Ch. 50 — Results of the Chara and other sign dashas, 97 verses
- v.2-3 Judge each house-sign by the strength of its lord: full result when strong, middling when middling, the reverse when weak.
- v.4 **Malefics in the 8th or the trines from the dasha sign: that dasha gives sorrow.**
- v.5 **Malefics in the 3rd and 6th from it: victory; benefics there: defeat.**

A malefic's sign holding a malefic: sorrow throughout. A benefic's sign with both: hardship first, comfort after.

Rahu in the obstruction place, 12th, 6th or 8th: great fear, confinement on a journey, trouble from rulers and rivals.
- v.24-28 Sun, Mars, Rahu or Saturn in the sub-period sign: a fall, anger of rulers. A debilitated planet in it or its trine: danger. An exalted planet in it or its trine: comfort, headship of a town, children, money, command. The dasha lord aspected by Jupiter in a benefic's sign: money, good events, children.
- v.29-32 Signs hostile to each planet: Sun - the signs of Venus and Saturn; Moon - Scorpio, Libra, Aquarius; Mars - Aquarius, Pisces, Gemini, Libra, Virgo; Mercury - Cancer, Pisces, Scorpio, Aquarius; Jupiter - Taurus, Libra, Gemini, Virgo; Venus - Leo, Scorpio, Cancer, Sagittarius; Saturn - Aries, Leo, Sagittarius, Scorpio, Cancer.
- v.33-35 Planets that give raja yoga, and planets in the 2nd, 3rd and 4th from a sign, make its dasha good. **Even a malefic between two benefics gives good.** A sign with benefics in its trines: good.
- v.36-41 Judge the start and the end of a dasha by the sign it starts from and ends in: a friendly benefic's sign at both ends is good. A dasha starting in a malefic's sign with a weak lord, or where a debilitated planet sits in it or its trine, or whose lord is debilitated: reversal of fortune, loss of money, illness.
- v.42 Rahu's own signs are the four from Aquarius, Ketu's the four from Scorpio; a dasha starting there is good.

Jupiter, Venus or a benefic lord in the sign: every prosperity. Exalted planets or a benefic lord in the 4th: vehicles, land, cattle (Moon there: grain; full Moon: treasure; Venus: music; Jupiter: a palanquin of gold).
- v.73-83 Grades of a planet's dasha by its dignity: at deep exaltation with great strength - "complete", gives authority and lasting wealth; in exaltation - "full", much prosperity; between exaltation and debilitation, moving down - "descending"; in a friend's sign or exaltation division - "middling", gives money; moving up from debilitation to exaltation - "ascending"; in debilitation or an enemy's sign and division - "lowest", fear and sorrow; at deep debilitation and weak - "empty", gives loss.
- v.84-87 A link of the 9th lord and Jupiter by conjunction, aspect or kendra raises the fortune even in other planets' periods. Planets in their own kendras, panapharas and apoklimas give full, half and a quarter.
- v.88-89 **Planets in head-rising signs give their results at the start of their dasha, in both-ways-rising signs in the middle, in back-rising signs at the end.** A natural or temporary friend as sub-period lord gives good; the reverse gives hardship.
- v.90-97 Sub-periods by sign: divide the dasha among the twelve signs starting from the sign the dasha lord occupies; the sub-period of a sign that is a friend's or own sign gives authority and wealth; of a bad house, an enemy's sign, with a debilitated or harsh planet - quarrels and illness. Weigh by the many or few points of that sign in the planet's own ashtakavarga. Divide again for the sub-sub-periods.
