## Ch. 43 — Length of life, 78 verses
Recorded for completeness. **Never used to state a length of life in a reading.**
- v.4-5 Planets, by their positions and strength, are the givers of years. Three computed systems follow.
- v.6-8 **Pinda:** years at exaltation - Sun 19, Moon 25, Mars 15, Mercury 12, Jupiter 15, Venus 21, Saturn 20; half at debilitation; in proportion between.
- v.9-13 Reductions: combust - half (not Venus or Saturn); in an enemy's sign - a third (not when retrograde); by position in the visible half from the 12th back to the 7th - whole, half, third, quarter, fifth, sixth for malefics and half of that for benefics; a malefic in the lagna reduces further.
- v.14-15 The lagna's own contribution, by its sign and degrees or by its navamsa.
- v.16-17 **Natural:** Moon 1, Mars 2, Mercury 9, Venus 20, Jupiter 18, Sun 20, Saturn 50.
- v.18-22 **Amsa (navamsa-based):** years from the navamsa position; tripled if exalted or in own sign, doubled in own navamsa or drekkana or vargottama.
- v.24-29 Maximum life-spans of various beings.
- v.30-32 Which system: lagna strongest - Amsa; Sun strongest - Pinda; Moon strongest - Natural; two equal - the mean of the two; three - the mean of three.
- v.33-40 **The three-pair method:** (1) L1 and L8, (2) Saturn and Moon, (3) lagna and hora lagna. Both in movable signs, or one fixed and one dual - long. One movable and one fixed, or both dual - middle. One movable and one dual, or both fixed - short. What two or three pairs agree on is taken; if all three differ, the lagna-hora pair; but if the Moon is in the lagna or 7th, the Saturn-Moon pair.
- v.41-46 Year bands: long 120, 108 or 96 (three, two or one pair); middle 80, 72 or 64; short 32, 36 or 40; adjusted in proportion to the degrees.
- v.47-50 Raising and lowering by a band: Saturn among the deciding planets lowers a band (not when in own sign or exalted, or when only malefics join or aspect); Jupiter in the lagna or 7th with only benefic influence raises one.
- v.52-54 Seven grades of life: infant danger 8, danger by yoga 20, short 32, middle 64, long 120, "divine" 1000, unmeasured.
- v.60-78 Combinations for full, long, middle and short life: benefics in kendras and L1 with benefics or aspected by Jupiter; L1 in a kendra with Jupiter and Venus; three planets exalted with L1 and L8 and no malefic in the 8th; malefics in 3, 6, 11 with benefics in kendras and trines and L1 strong; benefics in 6, 7, 8 with malefics in 3 and 11; the stronger of L1 and L8 in a kendra (long), next house (middle), falling house (short); L1 a friend of the Sun (long), neutral (middle), enemy (short); L3 with Mars and L8 with Saturn combust or with malefics (short); L1 in 6, 8 or 12 with a malefic and no benefic help; malefics in the kendras without benefic aspect and L1 weak; malefics in the 12th and 2nd without benefic help.

## Ch. 44 — Death-inflicting (maraka) planets, 46 verses
Recorded for completeness. **Never used to predict a death.**
- v.2-3 The 3rd and 8th are the two houses of life; the houses 12th from them - the 7th and the 2nd - are maraka houses; the 2nd is the stronger.
- v.3-4 Their lords, malefics placed in them, and malefics joined with those lords are all marakas; death is possible in their periods.
- v.5 First settle whether the life is short, middle or long; only then look for the maraka.
- v.6-7 Failing these: planets connected with the 12th lord; sometimes the periods of benefics, of the 8th lord, or of malefics alone.
- v.8 A maraka does not act in the sub-period of a benefic even if connected with it; it does act in a malefic's sub-period even without connection.
- v.9 **Saturn, when a malefic for the chart and connected with a maraka planet, overrides all the others.**
- v.10-12 Short life to 32 [the figure is printed as 22 in one line], middle to 64, long beyond, highest above 100. **Up to the age of twenty the length of life cannot be known.**
- v.13-14 Deaths in childhood come from the parents' faults or from the child's own infant-danger yogas; reckon a life only after that.
- v.15-18 Other markers: the vipat, pratyari and vadha stars for short, middle and long lives; the lords of the 22nd drekkana and of the 23rd star; the lords of the 2nd and 12th from the Moon - malefics among these kill, benefics give illness.
- v.19 Death is possible in the period of the 6th lord and in the sub-periods of the 6th, 8th and 12th lords.
- v.20-21 When several marakas are strong, their periods give illness and hardship; the strongest is the chief one.
- v.22-24 Rahu or Ketu in the lagna, 7th, 8th or 12th, or 7th from a maraka lord or with one, is a maraka in its own periods. For Capricorn and Scorpio lagnas Rahu is so. Rahu in the 6th, 8th or 12th gives hardship in its period unless joined or aspected by a benefic.
- v.25-34 The manner and place of death from the planets in the 3rd house; v.35-37 from the 8th; v.38-40 what becomes of the body, from the drekkana; v.41-45 the world one came from and goes to.
- Project note: v.8 is a classical statement of the same principle as our Rule 1 - a period lord delivers through sub-periods that are connected to it and agree with it in nature.

## Ch. 45 — States (avasthas) of the planets, 155 verses
- v.3-4 **Age states:** in an odd sign the six-degree steps are infant, child, youth, old, dead; in an even sign the reverse. Results: a quarter, half, full, a little, none.
- v.5-6 **Waking states:** own sign or exaltation - awake, full result; friend's or neutral sign - dreaming, middling; enemy's sign or debilitation - asleep, none.
- v.7-9 **Nine states:** exalted - blazing; own sign - at ease; great friend's sign - glad; friend's - calm; neutral - poor; enemy's - distressed; with a malefic - crippled; in a malefic's sign - wicked; with the Sun - angry.
- v.10 A house gives results according to the state of the planet in it.
- v.11-17 **Six moods:** ashamed - in the 5th with Rahu or Ketu, or with the Sun, Saturn or Mars; proud - exalted or in its trine-sign; hungry - in an enemy's sign, with or aspected by an enemy, or with Saturn; thirsty - in a watery sign, aspected by an enemy, without benefic aspect; glad - in a friend's sign, with or aspected by a friend, or with Jupiter; agitated - with the Sun and aspected by malefics and an enemy.
- v.18 A hungry or agitated planet destroys the house it sits in.
- v.19-20 Mixed states give mixed results; weak planets lose, strong ones give in full.
- v.21 A planet in the 10th that is ashamed, thirsty, hungry or agitated: a life of hardship.
- v.22 An ashamed planet in the 5th: loss of children, one remains [literal].
- v.23 An agitated or thirsty planet in the 7th: the spouse does not live [literal].
- v.24-29 Results of each mood: proud - new houses, gardens, rule, skill in arts, steady gain; glad - a large home, clothes, land, happiness from the spouse, enemies overcome, learning grows; ashamed - aversion to good, illness of children, pointless travel; agitated - poverty, bad judgment, loss of money, trouble to the feet, a ruler's anger; hungry - grief, weakness, quarrels with enemies, blocked money, clouded mind; thirsty - illness through women, authority in bad work, loss of money through slander by one's own people.
- v.30-39 **Twelve activity states** (lying down, sitting, eyes-and-hands, shining, going, coming, in assembly, arriving, eating, wishing to dance, eager, sleeping), found by a formula from the planet's star, its number, its navamsa, the birth star, the hour and the lagna; and three sub-states (seeing, moving, not moving) from the first sound of the name.
- v.40-146 The result of each of the twelve activity states for each of the nine planets.
- v.147-155 General rules: benefics "lying down" in a house always help it; malefics "eating" in a house ruin it; a malefic "sleeping" in the 7th is good unless a malefic aspects it; a malefic "sleeping" or "lying down" in the 5th is good; in the 8th it gives an untimely end unless helped by benefics; a malefic "lying down" or "eating" in the 10th brings many troubles; the Moon in the 10th "eager" or "shining" is a raja yoga.
