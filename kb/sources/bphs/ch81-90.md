# BPHS chapters 81–90 — body marks, moles, curses of a former birth, propitiation of planets, births that need a rite (read in full from the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026)

Source: sanskritdocuments.org `par8190.itx` (Ahto Jarve's transliteration, update 1 Nov 2010). My own translation; the Sanskrit is not reproduced. This file replaces the partial notes on ch. 83, 86 and 87 in `kb/sources/bphs-verse-readings.md`.

## Ch. 81 — Marks of the limbs (of women), 85 verses
- v.2 "As Shiva told Parvati." v.3-84 The sole, toes, nails, heel, shanks, knees, thighs, hips, navel, belly, chest, shoulders, arms, palms and their lines, fingers, back, neck, chin, cheeks, mouth, lips, teeth, tongue, palate, smile, nose, eyes, lashes, brows, ears, forehead, head and hair, each with a "fortunate" and an "unfortunate" form and a label attached (wealth, rank, widowhood, character, number of children).
- v.85 "The same is to be considered for men."
- **Not used.** This is physiognomy, not chart reading; it attaches fortune and moral labels to a person's body. Nothing from this chapter enters the knowledge base or any reading.

## Ch. 82 — Moles and hair-whorls, 15 verses
- v.2 Marks on the left of a woman's body and the right of a man's are fortunate. v.3-14 Results by the place of a mole, mark or whorl.
- **Not used**, for the same reason.

## Ch. 83 — Curses of a former birth shown as want of children, 111 verses
Guardrail: the project never tells a person they will have no children. These combinations are recorded as the text has them; the engine raises two of the groups as internal flags only, to prompt care in the children section and the classical remedy, never as a statement.

- v.2-6 Maitreya: by what fault is one without a son, how is it known from the chart, and what is the means of getting one? Parashara answers as Shiva answered Parvati.
- v.7 Jupiter, the lagna lord, the 7th lord and the 5th lord all without strength.
- v.8 Sun, Mars, Rahu and Saturn strong in the 5th while the significators of children are weak.

- v.109-110 Remedy by the planet causing the fault: Mercury or Venus - worship of Shiva; Jupiter or the Moon - mantra, yantra and medicine; Rahu - the gift of a daughter in marriage; the Sun - praise of Hari; Ketu - gift of a cow; Mars or Saturn - recitation to Rudra.
- v.111 For the removal of all such faults: hearing the Harivamsha with devotion.
- Engine check: `sarpa_shapa` covers v.9-12 and `pitru_dosha` v.20-23 and 27, as corrected on 12 Nov. v.13-16 and v.24-30 are not coded; the other six groups are not coded. This is left as it is on purpose: the flags never become statements, and widening them would only raise more silent flags. What matters for a reading is recorded: **Parashara's "father's curse" is a narrow set of eleven combinations, every one tied to the 5th house - not "Sun with Rahu or Saturn", the popular test that fires in 61% of random charts.**

## Ch. 84 — Propitiation of the planets, 27 verses
- v.2-3 The happiness and unhappiness of all beings depend on the planets; one who wants peace, prosperity, rain, long life or nourishment should worship them.
- v.4-5 Images: Sun copper, Moon crystal, Mars red sandalwood, Mercury and Jupiter gold, Venus silver, Saturn iron, Rahu lead, Ketu bell-metal; or drawn in their colours on cloth, in their directions.
- v.6-14 The form of each (Sun on a lotus with a chariot of seven horses; Moon white with ten horses; Mars red, four-armed, on a ram; Mercury yellow, on a lion; Jupiter yellow and Venus white, four-armed with staff, rosary and water-pot; Saturn sapphire-blue on a vulture with bow and arrows; Rahu fierce-faced, dark blue, on a lion; Ketu smoke-coloured on a vulture). All crowned.
- v.15-16 Flowers and cloth of the planet's colour, fragrance, lamp, incense; whatever substance and food is dear to each.
- v.17-18 The Vedic mantra of each.
- **v.19-20 Recitation counts: Sun 7,000; Moon 11,000; Mars 10,000; Mercury 9,000; Jupiter 19,000; Venus 16,000; Saturn 23,000; Rahu 18,000; Ketu 17,000.**
- v.21-22 Fire-sticks: arka, palasha, khadira, apamarga, pipal, udumbara, shami, durva, kusha; offered with honey, ghee, curd or milk; 108 or 28 of each.
- v.23-24 Food given: jaggery rice, milk-rice, sacrificial rice, milk with sixty-day rice, curd rice, ghee rice, ground [sesame], [meat], mixed rice; or according to one's means.
- v.25 Gifts: a cow, a conch, a bull, gold, cloth, a horse, a black cow, iron, a goat.
- **v.26 "Whichever planet is badly placed for a person at any time, that one he should worship with care."** v.27 The rise and fall of people depend on the planets.
- KB check: `kb/remedies/remedies.md` table compared cell by cell; one correction (Mars 10,000, not 11,000) and Rahu's material is lead. Items the project does not relay (animal gifts, meat) stay marked as such.

## Ch. 85 — Births that are inauspicious for other reasons, 4 verses
- v.1-4 Even with a good lagna and good planets a birth is called troubled when it falls on: the new moon; the 14th of the dark half; Vishti (Bhadra) karana; the birth star of a brother or sister or of a parent; the Sun's entry into a sign; an eclipse of the Sun or Moon; Vyatipata and the like bad yogas; any of the three kinds of gandanta; Yamaghanta; a lost lunar day; a "burnt" combination; a birth after three children of the other sex; an abnormal delivery. "By the rite there is welfare."

## Ch. 86 — Birth on the new moon, 9 verses
- v.1 [Hardship to the parents is stated.] v.2-9 The rite: a pot with five kinds of leaves and five gems; images of the Sun and Moon in gold, silver or copper; fire offerings 108 or 28 times; bathing of the couple and child; gifts.

## Ch. 87 — Birth on the 14th of the dark half, 13 verses
- v.1-3 The lunar day is divided into six parts: a birth in the first is good; the other five are each assigned a relative or possession that is harmed [wordings not used]. v.4-13 The rite to Shiva.

## Ch. 88 — Birth in Bhadra, on a lost lunar day, or in a bad yoga, 5 verses
- v.1-5 On a good day shown by the astrologer: worship of the deities and planets, bathing of Shiva's image, a ghee lamp, circling the sacred fig, 108 offerings with Vishnu's mantra, feeding.

## Ch. 89 — Birth on the same star as a brother or a parent, 7 verses
- v.1 [A danger to one of the two is stated - not used.] v.2-7 The rite to the deity of that star, on a day with good strength of Moon and star, avoiding the empty lunar days and Vishti.

## Ch. 90 — Birth at the Sun's entry into a sign, 18 verses
- v.1 The seven names of the solar ingress by weekday from Sunday: Ghora, Dhvankshi, Mahodari, Manda, Mandakini, Mishra, Rakshasi.
- v.2 [Want and sorrow are stated for one born then; "by the rite comes happiness".] v.3-18 The nine-planet sacrifice, pots on heaps of grain, images of the ingress with the Sun and Moon, recitation of the Mrityunjaya mantra 1,008, 108 or 28 times.

## How chapters 85–90 are used
The engine reports gandanta, eclipse births and (now) the conditions of ch. 85 as **"a birth for which Parashara prescribes a rite"**, with the rite's name, and never with the harm wording. The worksheet already covers gandanta and eclipse; new moon, dark 14th, Vishti, solar ingress and Vyatipata are on the to-do list as one "birth-time conditions" line.
