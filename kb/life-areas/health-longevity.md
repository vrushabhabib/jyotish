# Health and Longevity — KB rules

Tradition: Parashari base (BPHS), Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses. Houses counted from Lagna unless stated.

## FIRM NOTE — read before using anything below

- These rules describe **tendencies** recorded in old texts. They are **never diagnoses**. The agent must not name a disease as a prediction, must not advise on treatment, and must tell the person to see a doctor for any health concern.
- **Never predict death** — not the native's, not a relative's. Maraka and longevity material is kept here only so the agent understands the texts and can recognise "a period that needs care". Output wording must stay at "a period to look after health / be careful", never dates of death, never "short life".
- The classical longevity ages and causes of death below are reference data. Do not show them to a user as findings about a person.
- Trimsamsa verses about women (BPHS ch. 80) are moral character judgments from another era. Do not output them about any real person.

## Project calibration (from our verified charts)

1. **Named-illness predictions are low reliability.** A predicted illness (acidity/chest trouble from Moon–Ketu in Cancer) was wrong. Do not name illnesses from a sign + planet pairing.
2. **Injury location followed the HOUSE that Mars and Saturn both afflict, not the sign Mars sits in.** When checking injury-prone body areas, first find the house receiving the influence (occupation or aspect) of both Mars and Saturn and use the house → body part map (table B below), before looking at Mars's sign.
3. Ketu in the 3rd with Saturn and Mars aspecting the 3rd = no younger siblings (confirmed twice) — see family-members.md.
4. "Sun in 10th" described the native's own authority, not the father — see family-members.md.

### H1. The three-pair method (BPHS-43 v.33–40; PVR §14.4)

Pairs: (1) Lagna lord and 8th lord; (2) Moon and Saturn; (3) Lagna and Hora Lagna. For each pair look at the modality of the two signs occupied.

| Signs of the pair | Class |
|---|---|
| Both movable; OR one fixed + one dual | Long |
| One movable + one fixed; OR both dual | Medium |
| One movable + one dual; OR both fixed | Short |

## Reliability notes

**Widely agreed classical material:** sign → body part sequence; planet → humour and disease classes (Phaladeepika 14); "read the 6th, 8th, 12th, the 6th lord and its companions" (HLT-04); the need for multiple confirmations (HLT-05); balarishta and its cancellations (BPHS 9–10); the three-pair longevity method (BPHS 43, repeated in Jaimini and PVR); maraka definition (BPHS 44).

**Classical but inconsistent:** year boundaries of the three longevity classes (four different sets above); arishta age limit (BPHS says 24; Phaladeepika 12 for balarishta and 20 for yogarishta); Rahu/Ketu as marakas (verse gloss truncated); 7- vs 8-karaka for Maheswara.

**Modern or weakly sourced:** every D30-as-disease rule (HLT-30), all D6 use (HLT-33), 64th navamsa (HLT-67), surgery rules (HLT-76), all KP-style accident rules (HLT-74, 75, 78), Saturn/Rahu–Moon psychology (HLT-87, 88), dosha assignments for Rahu and Ketu.

**Project evidence:**
- A named illness predicted from sign + planet (Moon–Ketu in Cancer → acidity/chest) was wrong. Named-illness output is disabled; use body-region and "period needing care" language only.
- Injury site followed the house jointly afflicted by Mars and Saturn (HLT-71), not Mars's sign. This is our best-supported health rule so far (small sample).

**Empirical tests:**
- Narlikar et al. 2009 (*Current Science* 96(5), 641–643): 27 Indian astrologers, 40 charts each of bright vs mentally handicapped teenagers; mean 17.25 correct against 20 expected by chance; an institutional team scored 102/200. Read at TEST.
- Dudley 1995 (cited at TEST): accidental death from Vedic charts — 11 hits vs 10 expected by chance in 20 road-death cases.
- No positive controlled result for any health or longevity rule was found. Treat everything here as a hypothesis.
