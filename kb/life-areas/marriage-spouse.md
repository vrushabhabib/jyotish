# Marriage and Spouse — Knowledge Base (Parashari base, Lahiri, whole-sign)

> **Corrections from the Sanskrit (12 Nov 2026) - these override the rules below where they differ.** See `kb/sources/bphs-verse-readings.md`, chapter 80.
> - BPHS 80.19 is about planets in the **7th** house of a woman's chart (malefics harm, benefics protect, mixed = remarriage), not the 8th. The "benefics in the 8th are protective" reading and the conflict built on it are withdrawn.
> - Older or dull husband (BPHS 80.24): the **7th house in Saturn's sign or navamsa**. "Saturn with the 7th lord" is not the classical rule.
> - Mangal dosha (BPHS 80.47-49): Mars in 1, 4, 7, 8 or 12 without a benefic's aspect or conjunction; the same holds for a man's chart; **broken when both partners have it**. These three points are Parashara's own.
> - Visha-kanya cancellation (80.46): 7th lord or a benefic in the 7th from the lagna or the Moon.


Scope: promise/denial/delay, spouse description, plurality/separation/widowhood, timing, Mangal dosha.
Jaimini and KP are noted where they add a checkable test. All rules are paraphrased, not quoted.

### Timing and description rules confirmed in the Sanskrit of Phaladeepika ch. 10 (read 12 Nov 2026, `kb/sources/phaladeepika/ch10-12.md`)
- **PD10-a** — The 5th and 7th from the lagna and from the Moon, joined or aspected by their lords or benefics: children and spouse both do well (10.1).
- **PD10-b** — A benefic in the 7th gives happiness unless it is the lord of the 6th, 8th or 12th; even a malefic in its own sign in the 7th does good (10.6).
- **PD10-c** — Direction the spouse comes from: the signs of the planet in the 7th, the 7th lord and Venus (10.12). Parashara adds the 7th from Venus (BPHS 70.35-36).
- **PD10-d** — Timing by dasha: a planet in the 7th, one aspecting the 7th, the 7th lord (10.13); the stronger of the lords of the sign and navamsa holding the 7th lord, Venus and the Moon (10.14).
- **PD10-e** — Timing by transit: Venus or the 7th lord in a trine of the sign or navamsa of the lagna lord (10.12); the lagna lord in the 7th sign (10.13); Jupiter in a trine of the sign or navamsa holding the 7th lord (10.14).
- In a woman's chart Mantreshvara reads the husband and her good fortune from the 7th and children from the 9th (11.1); Parashara reads children from the 5th (BPHS 80.3). Check both and name the text.

## How to read this file

- Each rule has an ID, a machine-checkable condition, the stated result, the source, and a note.
- "Read at" means the URL was actually fetched during this research run (Nov 2026). Verse numbers follow the edition at that URL; BPHS chapter and verse numbers differ between the Santhanam and G.C. Sharma printings, so treat verse numbers as locators for that URL only.
- `[UNSOURCED]` = "unsourced – common practice": widely repeated, but not traced to a primary text in this run.
- `[MEMORY]` = attribution given from general knowledge of the named book; the book itself was not readable online in this run. Verify before treating as a citation.
- Classical results are worded for a male native with a wife. Unless a rule says otherwise, mirror it for a female native (BPHS 80.2-4 instructs exactly this).
- Historic outcome wording ("wife dies", "widow") must be read as "loss, separation or serious trouble through the spouse". BPHS ch. 30 itself uses a word meaning loss, not death.

## Project calibration (from our own 3 verified charts)

These are in-house observations. They outrank any single classical rule when the two disagree, and they should be extended as more charts are verified.

- CAL-1. A sub-period (antardasha) produced a relationship only when its lord had a real link to the 7th house, the 7th lord, or the karaka (Venus/Jupiter). A sub-period lord with no such link did not deliver. Implication: require an explicit link test before naming any period (see MAR-T01, MAR-T02).
- CAL-2. "Saturn with the 7th lord = older spouse" FAILED once: the husband was 4 years younger. Implication: every age-gap rule (SP-20 to SP-22) is low confidence and must never be stated as fact.
- CAL-3. A sub-period lord conjunct the 7th lord delivered marriage even though it was also the career (10th) lord. Implication: conjunction with the 7th lord is a sufficient link; a planet's other lordships do not block the marriage result. This agrees with TVH's "planet associated with the 7th lord in D1 or D9" and with PD-10 v.13.
- CAL-4. Descriptions of another person (looks, nature, profession of the spouse) are the least reliable output. Implication: present section B as tendencies, with low confidence, and never as identification.

Suggested confidence tiers for output: timing with dasha + transit agreement = medium; promise/denial = medium-low; spouse description = low; number of marriages and widowhood = very low and ethically sensitive (do not volunteer).

## A. Promise, denial and delay of marriage

### A1. General method for any house (applies to the 7th)

- **MAR-01** — Condition: 7th house occupied or aspected by benefics, by its own lord, or by lords of good houses, AND free of malefic occupation/aspect, AND 7th lord not debilitated, combust or in an enemy sign. Result: the house gives its full good result (marriage and happiness from it). Source: PD-15 v.1. Note: this is the generic bhava test; it is the backbone of "promise".
- **MAR-02** — Condition: 7th lord in the 8th from the 7th or otherwise in 6/8/12 from lagna, OR combust/debilitated/in enemy sign, AND no benefic aspect or conjunction. Result: the 7th house is damaged; a benefic aspect restores it. Source: PD-15 v.3, v.5. Note: PD-15 v.9 softens this: a strong lord in a dusthana does only slight harm.
- **MAR-03** — Condition: 7th house, 7th lord and Venus all weak AND hemmed between malefics or joined by malefics. Result: destruction of the house matters; certainty rises with each extra condition met. Source: PD-15 v.6. Note: implement as a score, not a boolean.
- **MAR-04** — Condition: treat the 7th house as a lagna and judge its own 12 houses; also treat Venus (karaka) as lagna and judge from there. Result: second and third opinions on marriage. Source: PD-15 v.20-21. Note: PVR (sec. 7.3.9 and 8.3) uses the 7th from Venus, not Venus itself, for marriage.
- **MAR-05** — Condition: 7th lord in own sign or exaltation. Result: full happiness through marriage. Source: BPHS-18 v.2.
- **MAR-06** — Condition: strong 7th lord joined or aspected by a benefic. Result: wealth, honour and happiness through marriage. Source: BPHS-18 v.5.
- **MAR-07** — Condition: 5th and 7th houses, counted from lagna or Moon, occupied or aspected by the 9th lord, benefics, or their own lords. Result: both houses (children and spouse) prosper. Source: PD-10 v.1. Note: gives explicit licence to repeat the test from the Moon.
- **MAR-08** — Condition: 7th house is an even sign AND 7th lord and Venus are in even signs AND 5th and 7th lords are strong and not combust. Result: the native gets wife and children. Source: PD-10 v.9.
- **MAR-09** — Condition: lords of the 2nd, 7th and 12th in kendra/trikona and aspected by Jupiter; OR benefics in the 2nd, 7th and 11th counted from the 7th lord. Result: happy spouse with children. Source: PD-10 v.10. Note: the houses 2, 7, 11 here anticipate the KP 2-7-11 group.

### A2. Denial or lack of marital happiness

- **MAR-10** — Condition: Moon in the 7th AND 7th lord in the 12th AND Venus weak. Result: no marital happiness. Source: BPHS-18 v.18. Note: often quoted online as "no marriage"; the text read says no happiness.
- **MAR-11** — Condition: Venus and Moon in opposition to Mars and Saturn (the two pairs 7th from each other). Result: wifeless or childless. Source: PD-10 v.5.
- **MAR-12** — Condition: Moon with Saturn in the 7th. Result: for a man, without wife or without children; for a woman, remarriage. Source: PD-10 v.8. Note: a common modern reading is simply "delay"; the text is harsher.
- **MAR-13** — Condition (female chart): Saturn [in the 7th, per context] aspected by malefics. Result: remains unmarried into old age. Source: BPHS-80 v.18. Note: confirmed in Brihat Jataka 24.8 (read in the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026): Saturn in the 7th under malefic aspect - she grows old unmarried.
- **MAR-14** — Condition: Upapada (UL) joined or aspected by malefics or in a malefic's sign, with no benefic aspect or conjunction. Result: happiness from spouse does not fully arise; renunciation or wifelessness is possible. A benefic aspect or conjunction cancels this. Source: BPHS-30 v.4-5.
- **MAR-15** — Condition: malefic in the 7th AND any planet in the 9th (female chart). Result: renunciation. Source: BPHS-80 v.53.
- **MAR-16** — Condition (KP): sub lord of the 7th cusp signifies mainly houses 1, 6, 10 (12th from 2, 7, 11) and does not signify 2/7/11. Result: obstruction, delay, or denial within the dasha judged. Source: KP-AJ. Note: KP-JH gives the negating group as 6, 10, 12 with 1 and 8 as additions. The two KP secondary sources disagree on the exact list; no KP Reader was readable in this run. KP needs Placidus cusps and KP ayanamsa, so it cannot be computed from a Lahiri whole-sign chart.

### A3. Delay

- **MAR-17** — Condition: Saturn occupies or aspects the 7th house, or is with the 7th lord or Venus. Result: delay (later than peers), not denial. Source: KP-AJ and TVH state it as practice; no classical verse traced. `[UNSOURCED]` Note: see CAL-2; Saturn's link did not give an older spouse in one of our charts, so keep "delay" and "older spouse" as separate, weak claims.
- **MAR-18** — Condition: 7th lord in the 8th, or Saturn in the 7th/8th, while a marriage-capable dasha runs. Result: the dasha may pass without marriage; the event shifts to the next capable period. Source: TVH (author's example). `[UNSOURCED]`
- **MAR-19** — Condition: UL afflicted by Saturn, Mars, Rahu or Ketu. Result: delay, friction or broken engagements before marriage, not denial. Source: JH-SP. Note: softer than BPHS-30 v.4-5 (MAR-14).
- **MAR-20** — Condition: Venus in the 5th AND Rahu in the 5th or 9th. Result: marriage at 31 or 33 (a late age by the text's standard). Source: BPHS-18 v.33. Note: the only BPHS age rule that points clearly to delay; see section D4 for the full list.

## B. Spouse description (LOW confidence — see CAL-4)

Order of evidence used by most authors: 7th house and occupants, 7th lord, karaka, then D9 7th, then Jaimini factors (Darakaraka, Upapada, A7). Use agreement between at least three of these before saying anything.

### B1. 7th house, 7th lord and occupants

- **SP-01** — Condition: 7th lord exalted AND lagna lord strong AND a benefic in the 7th. Result: virtuous spouse who extends the family line. Source: BPHS-18 v.14-15.
- **SP-02** — Condition: 7th lord in 6/8/12 and not in own sign or exaltation. Result: sickly spouse. Source: BPHS-18 v.3.
- **SP-03** — Condition: 7th lord debilitated, combust or in an enemy sign. Result: sickly spouse and more than one marriage. Source: BPHS-18 v.5.
- **SP-04** — Condition: malefics in the 12th and 7th AND waning Moon in the 4th. Result: spouse is controlling and hostile to the native's family. Source: BPHS-18 v.10-13.
- **SP-05** — Condition: planet occupying the 7th. Result: Saturn = sick or weak spouse; Mars, Jupiter, Venus described through body build (BPHS wording is physical and dated). Source: BPHS-18 v.8-9. Note: modern rewordings (JH-SP): Venus = attractive and refined; Saturn = mature, sometimes older, with delay; Jupiter = wise and principled; Mars = invokes Mangal dosha.
- **SP-06** — Condition: 7th lord benefic and strong. Result: good-natured spouse and good children. A malefic that owns the 7th still benefits the spouse. Benefics in the 7th are good unless they own 6/8/12. Source: PD-10 v.6.
- **SP-07** — Condition: Saturn and Mars in Cancer falling in the 7th. Result: chaste, fortunate, good-looking wife. Source: PD-10 v.3. Note: a striking exception to the usual "malefics in the 7th" reading.
- **SP-08** — Condition: UL lord or Venus exalted / debilitated. Result: spouse from a good family / the opposite. Source: BPHS-30 v.14.
- **SP-09** — Condition: benefic connected with UL or the 2nd from UL. Result: good-looking, virtuous spouse. Source: BPHS-30 v.15.

### B2. Female chart: husband from the sign and navamsa of the 7th

- **SP-10** — Condition (female chart): sign on the 7th or the navamsa rising on the 7th belongs to — Mars: husband fond of other women and short-tempered; Mercury: learned and skilled; Jupiter: virtuous and self-controlled; Venus: handsome and liked by women; Saturn: old and foolish; Sun: very gentle and hard-working; Moon (Cancer): charming, passionate, gentle. Mixed sign/navamsa: weigh by strength. Source: BPHS-80 v.22-25. Note: the same list is in Brihat Jataka 24.11-12 (read in the Sanskrit, 12 Nov 2026 - `kb/sources/brihat-jataka/`); both texts say the Sun's husband is "very gentle", not harsh. "Saturn = old husband" is another age rule to treat as weak (CAL-2).
- **SP-11** — Condition (female chart): 7th house, by sign or navamsa, owned by a benefic. Result: husband bright, learned, well-known, wealthy. Otherwise: husband with defects, a gambler or deceitful, loss of wealth, and separation. Source: PD-11 v.3.
- **SP-12** — Condition (female chart): lagna and Moon both in even signs and aspected by benefics. Result: good husband, sons, prosperity, good character. Both in odd signs with malefic aspect or conjunction: masculine bearing, hard to govern, poverty. Source: PD-11 v.2; BPHS-80 v.5-6. Note: BPHS-80 v.8-16 then refines by the trimsamsa of the stronger of lagna/Moon; that is character of the woman, not of the spouse.
- **SP-13** — Condition (female chart): which houses to read. Husband and marital welfare = 7th; mangalya (duration of married state) = 8th; children = 9th (Phaladeepika) or 5th (BPHS); chastity = 4th; counted from the stronger of lagna or Moon. Source: PD-11 v.1; BPHS-80 v.3. Note: CONFLICT — Phaladeepika gives children to the 9th in a female chart, BPHS gives the 5th. Check both.

### B3. Karaka

- **SP-14** — Condition: karaka for wife is Venus; for husband, Jupiter. Source: Venus as spouse karaka in PD-15 v.16-17; Jupiter for husband is stated as practice (TVH) and is standard teaching. Note: PVR sec. 7.3.9 uses the 7th from Venus in D9 for marriage matters.
- **SP-15** — Condition: Venus in the 7th. Result: strong sexual desire. Venus joined to a malefic in any house: loss of wife. Source: BPHS-18 v.4. Note: this is the classical seed of the folk rule "karako bhava nashaya" for Venus in the 7th; the verse itself only says excess desire. **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** 'Venus in the 7th harms marriage' rests only on Jataka Parijata 2.52 (a 15th-century compilation; its companion verse 2.72 says Venus in the 6th instead). Minority view; never stated alone.
- **SP-16** — Condition: malefics in the 4th, 8th or 12th from Venus, OR Venus between two malefics, OR Venus joined or aspected by malefics. Result: loss of wife. Source: PD-10 v.1.

### B4. Jaimini factors: Darakaraka, Karakamsa, Upapada, A7

- **SP-17** — Condition: Darakaraka (DK) = the chara karaka with the lowest degrees within its sign. Result: DK signifies the spouse. Source: JS 1.1.18 (planet below the Gnatikaraka); PVR sec. 8.1 and table 13. Note: CONFLICT — PVR uses 8 chara karakas (Rahu included, Ketu excluded; Rahu's degree counted from the end of the sign); the 7-karaka scheme (Sun to Saturn) is used by other Jaimini teachers, including K.N. Rao `[MEMORY]`. The DK can differ between schemes. Compute and store both; flag when they differ. **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** the lowest-degree planet as the spouse's significator is Parashara's own (BPHS 32.13-16, read in the Sanskrit); the Jaimini sutra number is from a secondary page and was not read.
- **SP-18** — Condition: DK planet's nature. Result: Sun = authority and discipline; Moon = caring, emotional; Mars = energetic, assertive; Mercury = intelligent, talkative; Jupiter = wise, ethical; Venus = refined, relationship-minded; Saturn = structured, patient, "often older or more mature"; Rahu (8-karaka only) = unconventional or foreign background. Source: JH-SP (modern author, no classical verse given). `[UNSOURCED]` in classics. Note: PVR sec. 8.3 says to read DK itself, not the 7th from DK.
- **SP-19** — Condition: planets in the 7th from Karakamsa (the navamsa sign of the Atmakaraka). Result: Jupiter and Moon = handsome, loving spouse; Rahu = connection with a widowed person; Saturn = older spouse, or sickly, or ascetic by nature; Mars = spouse with a bodily defect; Sun = spouse sheltered by family, free of defects; Mercury = spouse skilled in arts and music. Source: JS 1.2.57-62 (numbering as in the translation read). Note: "Saturn = older" appears here in a sutra, which is the best classical basis for the age rule; still treat as weak (CAL-2). **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** confirmed as BPHS 33.47-48 (read in the Sanskrit): Moon and Jupiter - very good-looking; Venus - passionate; Mercury - skilled in arts; Sun - kept within her own family; Saturn - older, ascetic or sickly; Rahu - a widow [the last is not passed on].
- **SP-20** — Condition: Upapada (UL) = arudha pada of the 12th house. Compute: count from the 12th house to its lord, then the same distance from the lord. Exception used by PVR: if the result falls in the 1st or 7th from the 12th house itself, take the 10th from the result. Result: UL shows the marriage and the spouse. Source: PVR sec. 9.2 and 9.4; BPHS-30 v.2. Note: CONFLICT — some teachers do not apply the arudha exception `[MEMORY]`; store both values when they differ. Rath (RATH-PAL) warns against computing an "upapada" from the navamsa lagna.
- **SP-21** — Condition: UL in a benefic's sign or joined/aspected by benefics. Result: full happiness from spouse and children. Source: BPHS-30 v.3.
- **SP-22** — Condition: Darapada (A7) = arudha of the 7th house. Result: shows the kind of people one relates to and how relationships look from outside, not the spouse proper. Source: PVR sec. 9.4. Note: the popular Rath-school rule "A7 in kendra/trikona from Arudha lagna = harmony and attraction; in 6/8/12 = friction" could not be read at a primary URL in this run `[MEMORY]`.
- **SP-23** — Condition: D1 and D9 disagree on the spouse. Result: prefer D9 for marriage-specific statements. Source: JH-SP (modern). `[UNSOURCED]` in classics. Note: PVR table 11 simply assigns marriage and spouse to D9.
- **SP-24** — Condition: D9 7th house occupants and D9 7th lord. Result: add second and third layers of spouse character; D9 lagna changes about every 12-13 minutes so birth time must be good. Source: JH-SP; TVH. `[UNSOURCED]` in classics as a planet-by-planet table.

### B5. Direction, distance, birth sign of spouse

- **SP-25** — Condition: take the strongest of (a) a planet in the 7th, (b) the 7th lord, (c) Venus. Result: the direction belonging to the sign it occupies shows the direction or region the spouse comes from. Source: PD-10 v.12. Note: sign directions are the standard set (Aries/Leo/Sagittarius east, Taurus/Virgo/Capricorn south, Gemini/Libra/Aquarius west, Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces north) `[MEMORY]`. Some astrologers use planetary directions instead; conflict unresolved.
- **SP-26** — Condition: candidate signs for the spouse's Moon sign = signs trine to the sign or navamsa of the lagna lord or 7th lord; exaltation or debilitation sign of the lagna lord or 7th lord; the sign with most bindus in the native's Moon ashtakavarga. Result: spouse's janma rashi is likely among these. Source: PD-10 v.11. Note: this yields up to about 9 of 12 signs, so it has almost no discriminating power. Useful only as a weak consistency check.
- **SP-27** — Condition: 7th lord, UL or DK in the 9th or 12th. Result: spouse from far away or foreign; 7th lord in the 12th specifically = significant geographic distance. Source: JH-SP. `[UNSOURCED]` in classics. Note: the common extension "movable sign = far, fixed = near/same town, dual = in between" is `[UNSOURCED]`.
- **SP-28** — Condition: Rahu with or aspecting the 7th house, 7th lord or Venus; or Rahu as DK. Result: spouse from another community, culture or country. Source: JH-SP for Rahu as DK; rest `[UNSOURCED]`. Note: JS 1.2.58 gives Rahu in the 7th from Karakamsa as link to a widowed person, which is the nearest sutra.

### B6. Age gap (VERY LOW confidence — CAL-2)

- **SP-29** — Condition: Saturn in the 7th from Karakamsa. Result: older spouse (or sickly, or ascetic). Source: JS 1.2.59. **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** this is BPHS 33.47 (read in the Sanskrit) - one of only two classical statements on an older spouse, the other being the 7th house in Saturn's sign or navamsa (BPHS 80.24; Brihat Jataka 24.11). The sutra number is from a secondary page.
- **SP-30** — Condition (female chart): 7th house in Saturn's sign or navamsa. Result: old husband. Source: BPHS-80 v.24.
- **SP-31** — Condition: Saturn in the 7th or with the 7th lord; Saturn or Rahu as DK. Result: older or more mature spouse; Rahu = substantial age difference. Source: JH-SP. `[UNSOURCED]` in classics for the 7th-lord conjunction form. Note: this exact form FAILED in our chart (husband 4 years younger). The opposite folk rule, "Mercury on the 7th = younger-looking or younger spouse", is `[UNSOURCED]`. Output wording should be "maturity of temperament", never a number of years. **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** no verse found in any text read; 'Saturn with the 7th lord' failed our own test (CAL-2). Superseded by SP-29 and the rule in section 0. Do not use.

### B7. Love versus arranged; profession; family

- **SP-32** — Condition: link (conjunction, mutual aspect or exchange) between 5th and 7th houses or lords; 7th lord in the 5th; DK in the 5th; UL linked to the 5th. Result: marriage by own choice. UL linked to the 2nd or 4th: family-introduced. Source: JH-SP. `[UNSOURCED]` in classics. Note: PD-10 v.2 reads 7th lord in the 5th as harm to wife or lack of sons, so the classical and modern readings of the same placement conflict.
- **SP-33** — Condition (KP): 5th cusp sub lord also signifies 7 or 11 and links with the 7th. Result: leaning to personal choice; otherwise family-arranged. Source: KP-AJ, which adds that KP writers do not agree on a formula.

Ethical note: these are the most fear-inducing and least testable rules. The agent should not volunteer widowhood or death statements, and should restate them as "strain on the bond" when asked directly.

### C1. More than one marriage

- **PL-01** — Condition: 7th lord debilitated or in a malefic's sign with a malefic, AND the 7th house or its navamsa falls in a sign of a "eunuch" planet (Mercury or Saturn). Result: two wives. Source: BPHS-18 v.19.

## D. Timing of marriage

Working order: (1) confirm promise; (2) find the Vimshottari period whose lords pass the link test; (3) confirm with double transit; (4) cross-check with D9 and with a Jaimini sign dasha; (5) narrow with Jupiter trine rules.

### D1. Vimshottari

- **MAR-T01** — Condition: dasha or antardasha lord is (a) in the 7th, (b) aspecting the 7th, or (c) the 7th lord. Result: marriage may happen. Source: PD-10 v.13. Note: this is the classical form of CAL-1.
- **MAR-T02** — Condition: dasha/antardasha lord is conjunct or otherwise associated with the 7th lord in D1 or D9; or is Venus (men) / Jupiter (women); or is linked to the 2nd house. Result: marriage-capable period. Source: TVH; OJ-151. Note: confirmed once in-house (CAL-3): conjunction with the 7th lord delivered even though the planet was also 10th lord.
- **MAR-T03** — Condition: compare (a) the dispositors of the 7th lord in rashi and navamsa with (b) Venus and Moon; take the strongest. In that planet's dasha, when Jupiter transits a sign trine to the rashi or navamsa sign of the 7th lord. Result: marriage. Source: PD-10 v.14.
- **MAR-T04** — Condition: dasha lord is placed in the D9 7th, or is D9 7th lord, or joins/aspects Venus in D9. Result: period more likely to deliver; strongest when the same planet is also linked to the D1 7th. Source: TVH. `[UNSOURCED]` in classics. Note: the widely taught additions "dasha of the D9 lagna lord" and "dasha of Rahu (gives like Venus/marriage)" are `[UNSOURCED]`.
- **MAR-T05** — Condition: Jupiter mahadasha or antardasha. Result: marriage can occur because Jupiter is the general giver of auspicious events. Source: DM-SUN. `[UNSOURCED]` in classics.
- **MAR-T06** — Condition (KP): mahadasha, bhukti and antara lords are all significators of 2, 7, 11, with their sub lords not signifying the negating houses; ruling planets at the moment of judgment repeat the same significators. Result: marriage in that period; one level only = weak. Source: KP-JH; KP-AJ. Note: significator strength order: planets in the star of occupants, occupants, planets in the star of the house lord, the house lord.

### D2. Transit

- **MAR-T07** (double transit) — Condition: within the same span (commonly 6-12 months), transiting Jupiter AND transiting Saturn each aspect or occupy the 7th house or the sign of the 7th lord; many versions also accept the lagna/lagna lord and the 2nd. Check from lagna and from Moon. Must coincide with a capable dasha (MAR-T01/02). Result: marriage in that window. Source: attributed to K.N. Rao throughout modern literature (his books "Planets and Children" and the Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan group research "Timing of Marriage" are the usual citations) `[MEMORY]`; the wording read in this run is from OJ-151 and TVH, neither of which cites him. Note: OJ-151 adds: Jupiter alone gives proposals that do not close; Saturn's aspect is needed for commitment. CONFLICT on what must be hit — 7th house only, or 7th and 7th lord, or lagna/lagna lord as well. The more targets allowed, the more often the rule is "true" by chance: Saturn aspects 3 signs plus its own (4 of 12) and Jupiter 4 of 12, so a single target is hit by both about 1 time in 9 at random, and with four targets most years qualify. Record how many targets were hit. **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** not in Parashara, Varahamihira, Mantreshvara or Jataka Parijata as read; a modern rule. The classical transit triggers are in section 0 (Phaladeepika 10.12-14, 16.31-35). Both kinds tested at chance on our six dated events, so every window is tentative.
- **MAR-T08** — Condition: transiting Venus or 7th lord in a sign trine to the rashi or navamsa sign of the lagna lord. Result: time of marriage (month level). Source: PD-10 v.12.
- **MAR-T09** — Condition: transiting lagna lord enters the 7th house sign. Result: marriage (month level). Source: PD-10 v.13.
- **MAR-T10** — Condition: add longitudes of lagna lord and 7th lord (mod 360); transiting Jupiter (or Venus) over that point or its trines. A second point: longitude of the janma-nakshatra lord + 7th lord. Result: marriage window. Source: TVH, which calls the first a vivaha saham. Note: PVR sec. 28.8.2 also uses Jupiter's transit over or aspect of the natal vivaha saham, but PVR's saham is the Tajaka one (Venus − Saturn + lagna) `[MEMORY]`. The two "vivaha sahams" are different formulas. CONFLICT.
- **MAR-T11** — Condition: transiting Sun enters the navamsa sign of natal Jupiter (birth in uttarayana) or of natal Venus (birth in dakshinayana), or their trines, while aspected by transiting or natal Jupiter. Result: month of marriage. Source: DM-SUN (attributes to "classical works", unnamed).
- **MAR-T12** — Condition: transiting Jupiter in 2, 5, 7, 9 or 11 from natal Moon. Result: generally supportive year for auspicious events. Source: Outlook India column (https://www.outlookindia.com/astrology/astro-picks/favorable-time-periods-for-marriage-based-on-transits) gives 1, 2, 5, 7, 11; the traditional gochara set is 2, 5, 7, 9, 11 `[MEMORY]`. CONFLICT on the house list.

### D3. Jaimini and divisional dashas

- **MAR-T13** — Condition (Chara dasha): the running sign dasha (or sub-period sign) contains the DK, or the DK is in the 7th from it, or the DK aspects it or its 7th by sign aspect. Result: marriage period; used to confirm Vimshottari. Source: TVH (example: DK in the 7th from the dasha sign). Note: the fuller K.N. Result: marriage period. Source: PVR sec. 30-31 (D9 is "the right chart for marriage"; Narayana dasha of D9 preferred). The UL-specific trigger list is `[MEMORY]` (Rath school).
- **MAR-T15** — Condition (annual chart): Ithasala (applying aspect) between year lagna lord and 7th lord, or with Venus or the lord of the vivaha saham. Result: marriage in that year. Source: PVR sec. 28 (p. 387).

### D4. Age estimation (classical, child-marriage era)

- **MAR-T16** — BPHS age table. Condition → age: 7th lord in a benefic's house with Venus exalted/own sign → 5 or 9; Sun in 7th with its dispositor joined to Venus → 7 or 11; Venus in 2nd with 7th lord in 11th → 10 or 16; Venus in a kendra with lagna lord in Capricorn/Aquarius → 11; Venus in a kendra with Saturn 7th from Venus → 12 or 19; Venus 7th from Moon with Saturn 7th from Venus → 18; 2nd lord in 11th with lagna lord in 10th → 15; exchange of 2nd and 11th lords → 13; Venus 7th from the 8th (i.e. in the 2nd) with its dispositor joined to Mars → 22 or 27; 7th lord in 12th with lagna lord in the 7th navamsa → 23 or 26; 8th lord in 7th with Venus in navamsa lagna → 25 or 33; Venus in 5th with Rahu in 5th or 9th → 31 or 33; Venus in lagna with 7th lord in 7th → 27 or 30. Saturn-Venus opposition appears in two rules as a mild delayer relative to the others.
- **MAR-T17** — Condition: Saturn's influence on the 7th/7th lord/Venus → after 28-30; Rahu/Ketu on the 1-7 axis → sudden or unconventional timing; no malefic influence and a capable dasha in the early twenties → early. ## Reliability notes

### Widely agreed in the classical texts read

- The generic bhava method (MAR-01 to MAR-04): house, lord and karaka, each checked for strength and affliction, repeated from the Moon. Phaladeepika ch. 15 and BPHS agree.
- 7th lord strong and well aspected = good marriage; 7th lord in 6/8/12, debilitated, combust or with malefics = trouble (BPHS-18 v.2-5, 16-17; PD-10 v.4, 15).
- Malefics on the 7th and on the 2nd (male) or 8th (female) harm the bond; benefics there protect (PD-10 v.7; BPHS-80 v.19).
- Dasha of a planet in, aspecting or owning the 7th gives marriage (PD-10 v.13). This is the single best-supported timing rule and matches our CAL-1 and CAL-3.
- Upapada and its 2nd house as a marriage indicator is genuinely in BPHS (ch. 30), not a modern invention; but the reading of individual verses (PL-17) is unsettled.
- Benefic aspect as the universal cancellation (BPHS-80 v.46-47; BPHS-30 v.5; PD-15 v.5).

### Classical but not usable literally

- Counts of wives (PL-01 to PL-09), ages of marriage (MAR-T16), and dated deaths of the wife (PL-26). They assume polygamy and child marriage.
- Stri jataka results that grade a woman's chastity or blame her chart for her husband's death (BPHS-80, PD-11). Record for completeness; do not output.

### Modern teaching or internet lore (no classical verse found in this run)

- Double transit of Jupiter and Saturn (MAR-T07): a 20th-century research rule attributed to K.N. Rao, not a classical verse. Its strength is that it is checkable; its weakness is the loose target list.
- Planet-by-planet Darakaraka personality tables (SP-18), love versus arranged rules (SP-32), foreign spouse rules (SP-27, SP-28), distance by sign modality, spouse profession (SP-34).
- A7/Arudha lagna harmony rules and UL timing triggers (Rath school, taught in courses; primary pages not read).
- Most Mangal dosha cancellations beyond KD-C01 to C03. The long lists (AV-KD has 21 items) are unattributed and disagree with each other. Only "no benefic influence" and "matching charts" have a BPHS basis.
- "Dosha ends at 28", "all three reference points cancel".

### Base rates and empirical tests found

- Mangal dosha base rate: a simulation of 100,000 random charts (1970-2008 births, Lahiri) found Mars in 1/2/4/7/8/12 from lagna in 50.1% of charts, from Moon in 49.8%, and from lagna OR Moon in 75.0%. Adding Venus pushes it higher. Without the 2nd house the lagna figure is about 42% (my arithmetic from that page's per-house table). A "dosha" present in half to three-quarters of people cannot by itself predict an outcome that affects a small minority. Source: AM-PREV. The page measured placement only, not outcomes.
- ACVA-KD: all three reference points afflicted together occurs in about 1/8 (12.5%) of charts by chance.
- No published outcome study of Mangal dosha (divorce or spouse mortality in Manglik vs non-Manglik marriages) was found in this run. The Wikipedia entry (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangala_Dosha) classes it as a superstition and cites sociological sources only.
- KP second-marriage rules: astrogle.com reports 74%, 79% and 93% agreement for three rules on 100 charts of people with two marriages (KP-AG). No control group, so the false-positive rate is unknown and the figures are not evidence of predictive power.
- General tests of Indian astrology: the 2008 Pune double-blind test designed by Jayant Narlikar with Sudhakar Kunte, Narendra Dabholkar and Prakash Ghatpande — astrologers judging charts of mentally disabled vs bright students scored at chance; a later re-analysis of 338 + 338 charts against traditional affliction rules found no rule that separated the groups. Source read: https://www.skeptic.org.uk/?p=41058 (the original paper is Narlikar et al., Current Science, 2009 `[MEMORY]`). Not about marriage, but it is the best-designed test of Jyotish chart-judging in general.
- Western sun-sign compatibility: Helgertz and Scott, "The validity of astrological predictions on marriage and divorce", Genus 76:34 (2020), Swedish register data, about 66,000 marriages and 46,000 unions followed for divorce; no consistent effect. Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41118-020-00103-5 . Tests sun signs only, not Vedic rules.
- In-house: see Project calibration. Three charts is too few to estimate hit rates; log every prediction and outcome so that rules can be scored.
