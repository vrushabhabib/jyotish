Tradition: Parashari base (BPHS), Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses. Jaimini and KP are noted only where they add something.
All houses are counted whole-sign from the reference named (Lagna unless stated). "Aspect" = Parashari graha drishti unless stated.

## 1. Order of analysis (agent procedure)

1. 10th house, its occupants, its lord (sign, house, dignity, conjunctions) from Lagna — CAR-01..12.
2. Repeat from Moon and Sun; take the strongest reference — CAR-13..15.
3. Karmajiva: navamsa lord of the 10th lord — CAR-16..18 and the planet table.
4. D10: lagna, 10th, D1 10th lord's place in D10, kendras, 6th vs 7th — CAR-20..27.
5. Amatyakaraka and A10 — CAR-28..33.
6. Service / business / partnership decision — CAR-34..40.
7. Foreign link — CAR-41..44.
8. Timing: dasha first, then Jupiter–Saturn transit — CAR-45..52.

## 5. Dasamsa (D10)

Calculation: each sign is cut into ten 3° parts; in odd signs count from the sign itself, in even signs from the 9th from it (PVR §6.2.10). BPHS-7 v.3 says the Dasamsa shows "great results" (mahat phala).
D10 needs an accurate birth time: the D10 lagna changes every ~12 minutes of clock time (3° of lagna). If birth time is uncertain by more than ~5 minutes, down-weight every D10 rule.

## 7. Arudha of the 10th (A10, Rajya pada)

Calculation (pada of any house): count from the house to the sign of its lord, then the same count again from the lord. Exception as given by PVR §9.2: if the result is the house itself or the 7th from it, take the 10th from the result. BPHS-29 v.4–5 as read words the exception differently (lord in the 4th → 4th is the pada; lord in the 7th → 10th is the pada). Engines differ; record which rule is used.

## Reliability notes

**Widely agreed classical rules (high confidence that the tradition says this):**
- 10th house and lord strength (CAR-01..12, L1..L12), kendra–trikona raja yoga (CAR-53..55), karmajiva navamsa-lord rule and planet lists (CAR-16, planet table), AmK rules (CAR-28..31). All are in BPHS, Brihat Jataka or Phaladeepika.
- "Results come in the dasha of the yoga planets" (CAR-45, CAR-60).

**Modern practice, not classical verse:**
- Job vs business from 6th vs 7th (CAR-34, 35), every D10 house-meaning rule (CAR-20..27) — BPHS gives only one phrase about the Dasamsa ("great results"). D10 house reading is 20th-century practice.
- Double transit (CAR-46..48) — 20th-century research rule; attribution itself is disputed between the sources I read.
- All KP rules (CAR-37..39) — KP is a 20th-century system with its own houses and ayanamsa; the pages I read cite no KP Reader by volume.
- Foreign-career rules other than CAR-41; element table; Rahu/Ketu professions.

**Known weaknesses:**
- Classical profession lists describe pre-industrial trades; the modern mapping is interpretation.
- BPHS fixed ages (CAR-51) and "becomes a king" outcomes must be scaled to context.
- The enjoylearningsanskrit glosses look machine-assisted; a few verses are garbled (noted in the family and health files). Verify key verses against Santhanam's printed edition before quoting.

**Empirical tests:** I found no controlled test of any career rule. General tests of Indian astrology (Narlikar et al., *Current Science* 96(5), 2009: 27 astrologers, 40 charts each, average 17.25 hits vs 20 expected by chance — read at https://thoughtnaction.antisuperstition.org/an-indian-test-of-indian-astrology/) found no better-than-chance performance. Treat every rule here as a hypothesis to be scored against the project's verified charts.
