# Family Members: Parents, Siblings, In-laws — KB rules

Tradition: Parashari base (BPHS), Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses. Houses counted from Lagna unless stated.

## 0. Verified rules - these win over anything below that differs
*(Moved to the top on 12 Nov 2026; read in the Sanskrit.)*

### Significators of the parents by day and night (Phaladeepika 2.25, Sanskrit read 12 Nov 2026)
For a day birth the Sun stands for the father and Venus for the mother; for a night birth Saturn stands for the father and the Moon for the mother. The other pair then stands for the father's brother and the mother's sister. Parashara's general statement is "the Sun is the father and the Moon the mother of all" (BPHS 9.43) and the 9th house is the father's (BPHS 7.39; Phaladeepika 1.14). Use the house first, then the significator that fits the time of birth.

## Project calibration (from our verified charts) — apply before any rule below

1. **Ketu in the 3rd + Saturn AND Mars both aspecting the 3rd = no younger siblings**, even with an own-sign Moon in the 3rd. Confirmed twice. This overrides BPHS ch. 14 v.12–13 (Moon in the 3rd gives siblings).
2. **Injury location followed the HOUSE that Mars and Saturn both afflict**, not Mars's sign (see health-longevity.md HLT-71). Apply the same logic to a relative's derived house only with caution.
3. **"Sun in the 10th" described the native's own authority, not the father.** For the father, check the 9th lord and the father karakas (Sun; 9th from Sun; Pitrukaraka) first. Do not read a 10th-house planet as a description of the father unless those agree.
4. **Named-illness predictions are low reliability** (a predicted illness from Moon–Ketu in Cancer was wrong). Do not name illnesses for relatives either.

Ethics: the texts give ages for a parent's death. These are reference data only. **Never predict death of any family member**; at most say "a period when a parent may need care or when distance/separation is possible".

## 5. Derived houses (bhavat bhavam)

Phaladeepika states the method for karakas (FAM-03); applying it to houses from the Lagna is standard practice. ## Reliability notes

**Widely agreed classical rules:** 4th/Moon = mother; 9th/Sun = father (with the 10th as a secondary father house); 3rd/Mars = younger siblings; 11th = elder siblings; "house + lord + karaka must all agree" (PD-16 v.25); counting houses from the karaka (BPHS-32 v.22–23, PD-16 v.21–24); D3 for siblings and D12 for parents (BPHS-7).

**Classical but unreliable in practice:**
- Sibling counts (FAM-54, 57): the BPHS verses are textually corrupt and assume very large families.
- Ages for a parent's death (FAM-20..25, 30): single-factor verses with fixed years; no test supports them. Never output.
- Sex of siblings from planet gender (FAM-50, 51): our own two verified charts contradict FAM-51.

**Source conflicts:**
- Father's house: JSD says South India/Parashara = 9th, North India = 10th — the reverse of the regional description in the project brief. BPHS itself uses the 9th for father but ties "paternal happiness" to the 10th lord. Resolution adopted: 9th primary, 10th secondary (FAM-09, 11).
- Father karaka: BPHS-32 v.18 says the stronger of Sun and Venus; v.20 lists Venus alone; Phaladeepika uses the Sun. Use the Sun, note Venus as a Jaimini-style alternate.
- Drekkana scheme: Parashari vs Jaimini parivritti vs Somanatha give different D3 charts.
- 7- vs 8-karaka scheme changes Bhratru/Matru/Pitru karakas.

**Modern or weakly sourced:** all D12 reading rules beyond "9th = father, 4th = mother" (FAM-77..79, vendor page); D3 placement rules (FAM-69); in-law relationship rules (FAM-87); sibling-birth timing (FAM-90). The derived-house table is logically sound but its predictive value is untested.

**Project evidence (small sample):**
- FAM-56 (Ketu in 3rd + Saturn and Mars aspecting = no younger siblings): confirmed in two verified charts, including one where the classical Moon-in-3rd rule predicted siblings.
- "Sun in 10th = father" failed; it described the native's own authority (FAM-11).

**Empirical tests:** none found for any family rule. Tempora's pages on the 3rd house and D12 (https://www.tempora.ltd/findings/3rd-house, https://www.tempora.ltd/findings/dwadasamsa-d12-chart) state openly that they report no calibration for these rules. Narlikar et al. 2009 (general test, astrologers at chance) is the only controlled Indian study read (https://thoughtnaction.antisuperstition.org/an-indian-test-of-indian-astrology/).
