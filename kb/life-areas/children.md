# Children — Knowledge Base (Parashari base, Lahiri, whole-sign)

> **Correction from the Sanskrit (12 Nov 2026).** BPHS 80.3: in a woman's chart childbirth is read from the **5th** house. The serpent-curse and father's-curse verses (83.9-13, 83.20-27) were re-read; the narrower conditions are in `kb/sources/bphs-verse-readings.md` and override the wording below.


Scope: promise, delay, denial, adoption, medical assistance, Saptamsa (D7), Beeja and Kshetra sphuta, Santana tithi, number and sex of children, timing, male versus female chart, classical remedies.

## 0. Verified rules - these win over anything below that differs

### Rules confirmed in the Sanskrit of Phaladeepika ch. 12 (read 12 Nov 2026, `kb/sources/phaladeepika/ch10-12.md`)
- **PD12-a** — 5th lord and Jupiter well placed from the lagna and from the Moon; 5th joined or aspected by lords of good houses and benefics; lagna lord and 5th lord joined, in mutual aspect or exchanged (12.1).
- **PD12-b** — A malefic in its own sign in the 5th gives children (12.3). Scorpio, Taurus, Virgo and Leo on the 5th: fewer and later.
- **PD12-c** — Fertility points: Jupiter + Moon + Mars in an even sign and navamsa (woman); Sun + Venus + Jupiter in an odd sign and navamsa (man) (12.14).
- **PD12-d** — Timing by dasha: lagna lord, 7th lord, 5th lord, Jupiter, a planet in or aspecting the 5th (12.25, 27-28).
- **PD12-e** — Timing by transit: Jupiter in the sign (or navamsa sign) of the 5th lord, or in a trine of the sign or navamsa of natal Jupiter or the 5th lord (12.25, 29); the lagna lord joining the 5th lord or reaching the 5th sign (12.26).
- Mantreshvara's "others say" note on Jupiter in the 5th (15.26) is a minority view; it is not stated by itself.
- Never stated to a client: sex or number of children, or that there will be none (12.2, 4-11, 13 are recorded and not used).

- **JP13-a** Children are gained when the 5th, its lord or Jupiter is joined or aspected by a benefic; when the lagna lord is in the 5th with a strong 5th lord and Jupiter; when a strong Jupiter in the 5th is aspected by the lagna lord (Jataka Parijata 13.16-19, read in the Sanskrit 12 Nov 2026). Judge also from the 5th from Jupiter (13.9).
- **JP13-b** Timing (13.48-50): Jupiter transiting a trine of the sign of (5th lord + lagna lord); the periods of the lords of the 5th from Jupiter, Moon and lagna and of the 9th; of planets joining or aspecting them. Tentative like every timing rule.
- **JP13-c** In a woman's chart Jataka Parijata 16.5-6 gives children from the 9th and adds that some say the 5th - so read both, as already ruled.
- The same text's cautions about Jupiter in the 5th (13.30-31) are tied to four signs only and are never stated to a person.

## How to read this file

- Each rule has an ID, a machine-checkable condition, the stated result, the source, and a note.
- "Read at" URLs were fetched during this research run (Nov 2026). Verse numbers follow the edition at that URL.
- `[UNSOURCED]` = "unsourced – common practice". `[MEMORY]` = attribution from general knowledge of the named book or school; not readable online in this run.
- Classical texts say "son" (putra). Read as "child" unless the rule is about sex.
- Two charts are involved in every birth. Every text read that addresses it says to judge both partners. A single chart can at most describe that person's side.
- Sensitive area: fertility, pregnancy loss and sex of a child. The agent must not predict the sex of an unborn child (illegal to determine prenatally in India under the PCPNDT Act, and the rules have no demonstrated accuracy), must not state childlessness as fact, and should point medical questions to a doctor.

## A. Promise of children (5th house, 5th lord, Jupiter)

- **CH-01** — Condition: 5th lord in a kendra or trikona. Result: full happiness from children. 5th lord in 6/8/12: childlessness. Source: BPHS-16 v.2. Note: PD-15 v.9 softens the second half: a strong lord in a dusthana does only slight harm.
- **CH-02** — Condition: Jupiter, Moon and 5th lord well placed; OR 5th house aspected by a benefic or by the lord of a good house; OR lagna lord and 5th lord conjunct, in mutual aspect, or in exchange. Result: children assured. Source: PD-12 v.1.
- **CH-03** — Condition: 5th house aspected by strong Mercury, Jupiter and Venus AND 5th lord strong. Result: many children. Source: BPHS-16 v.12.
- **CH-04** — Condition: 5th lord exalted, or in the 2nd, 3rd, 5th or 9th from lagna, AND joined or aspected by Jupiter. Result: good fortune through children. Source: BPHS-16 v.16.
- **CH-05** — Condition: 5th and 7th from lagna or Moon occupied or aspected by the 9th lord, benefics or their own lords. Result: both spouse and children prosper. Source: PD-10 v.1. Note: licence to repeat every 5th-house test from the Moon.
- **CH-06** — Condition: judge from four points — 5th from lagna, 5th from Moon, 5th from Jupiter, and the navamsa dispositor of the 5th lord. Result: agreement of these raises confidence. Source: BVB-D7; PD-15 v.21 (treat the karaka as a lagna). Note: Phaladeepika v.26 of ch. 15 lists "Jupiter in the 5th" among karaka-in-own-house placements that distress the house; this is the classical root of "karako bhava nashaya" and conflicts with the popular view that Jupiter in the 5th is ideal. BPHS-16 v.18 gives Jupiter in the 5th a child, but late (age 32-33).
- **CH-07** — Condition: a malefic who owns the 5th sits in the 5th → gives children; any malefic in the 5th → many sons; a benefic in the 5th in own sign or exaltation → loss of children. Source: PD-12 v.3 as translated at the URL read. Note: counter-intuitive and probably elliptical in translation; BPHS-16 v.30 says a malefic in the 5th gives only one son. CONFLICT. Do not use without a second edition.
- **CH-08** — Condition: lords of lagna, 5th, 7th and 9th, plus Jupiter and Venus, strong; fruitful signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) on the 5th or holding the 5th lord. Result: supports conception. Barren signs (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Virgo) on the 5th/11th lords or lagna with malefic aspect: delay or denial. Source: BVB-D7. Note: CONFLICT on barren signs — Phaladeepika's "childless signs" are Scorpio, Virgo and Leo as the 5th (PD-12 v.3), and PD-11 v.4 lists Scorpio, Virgo, Taurus, Leo for the Moon in a woman's 5th. Scorpio is "fruitful" in one list and "childless" in the other.

## B. Delay, difficulty, denial

- **CH-09** — Condition: 5th lord combust, afflicted or weak. Result: no child, or the child does not survive. Source: BPHS-16 v.3.
- **CH-10** — Condition: 5th lord in the 6th AND lagna lord conjunct Mars. Result: first child lost; no further children. Source: BPHS-16 v.4.
- **CH-11** — Condition: 5th lord debilitated in 6/8/12 AND Ketu and Mercury in the 5th; OR 5th lord debilitated and not aspecting the 5th AND Saturn and Mercury in the 5th. Result: "kaka-vandhya" — only one child. Source: BPHS-16 v.5-6. Note: the page's gloss wavers between "barren" and "one child"; the traditional sense is one child only.
- **CH-12** — Condition: 9th lord in lagna AND 5th lord debilitated AND Ketu and Mercury in the 5th; OR 5th lord in 6/8/12, debilitated or in enemy sign, with affliction in the 5th. Result: child only with great difficulty. Source: BPHS-16 v.7-8.
- **CH-13** — Condition: 5th house, Jupiter and Moon afflicted by malefics with no benefic aspect; OR 5th hemmed by malefics, with the relevant lords in 6/8/12. Result: no children. Source: PD-12 v.2.
- **CH-14** — Condition: 5th sign is Scorpio, Virgo or Leo. Result: few children and after a long wait. Source: PD-12 v.3.
- **CH-15** — Condition (late child after effort, three variants): (a) Sun in the 5th in a childless sign, Saturn in the 8th, Mars in lagna; (b) Saturn in lagna, Jupiter in the 8th, Mars in the 12th, with a childless 5th sign; (c) Moon in the 11th, a malefic in the 5th from Jupiter, many planets in lagna. Result: a child late in life after great effort. Source: PD-12 v.4.
- **CH-16** — Condition (family ends, four variants): malefic in 4th + Venus in 7th + Moon in 10th; malefics in 12th, 8th, 5th and 1st; Venus and Mercury in 7th + Jupiter in 5th + malefics in 4th; Moon in 5th + malefics in 8th, 12th and 1st. Result: no continuation of the line. Source: PD-12 v.6.
- **CH-17** — Condition: malefic in lagna with lagna lord in the 5th; 5th lord in the 3rd; Moon in the 5th in an odd sign or odd navamsa aspected by the Sun. Result: trouble through children or none. Source: PD-12 v.7.
- **CH-18** — Condition: 5th lord debilitated, in enemy sign, combust, or with a lord of 6/8/12; or 5th lord in the 5th with no benefic aspect. Result: loss of children. Source: PD-12 v.9, v.19. Note: the second clause ("5th lord in own house without benefic aspect = loss") conflicts with CH-07 and with the general rule that a lord in its own house strengthens it.
- **CH-19** — Condition: a malefic in the 5th, or Saturn in the 5th from Jupiter. Result: only one child. Source: BPHS-16 v.30.
- **CH-20** — Condition: lagna lord in the 2nd AND 5th lord conjunct Mars. Result: children do not survive though the native is long-lived. Source: BPHS-16 v.32.
- **CH-21** — Condition: Venus and Moon opposite Mars and Saturn. Result: no wife or no children. Moon with Saturn in the 7th (male): the same. Source: PD-10 v.5, v.8.
- **CH-22** — Condition: Rahu in the 5th AND 5th lord with a malefic AND Jupiter debilitated; or a malefic 5th from Jupiter and also 5th from lagna. Result: loss of a child (text gives ages 32-40 of the native). Source: BPHS-16 v.21-22.
- **CH-23** — Condition: Saturn aspecting or occupying its own sign in the 5th. Result: children, but late. Rahu in the 5th can give many children; Rahu conjunct or aspecting the 5th lord is an affliction. Source: PP-BK (author's practice). `[UNSOURCED]` in classics.
- **CH-24** (curses, BPHS) — Condition: the planet afflicting the 5th house/lord/Jupiter names the karmic cause. Rahu on the 5th or 5th lord = serpent curse; Sun weak in the 5th with 9th-house links = father's curse; Moon weak with 4th lord afflicted = mother's; Mars in the 5th with 3rd-lord links = brother's; Mercury with Jupiter, Mars and Rahu on the 5th and Saturn in lagna = maternal uncle's; Rahu in Jupiter's sign with Jupiter, Mars, Saturn on the 5th = Brahmin's; 7th lord or Venus in the 5th with 2nd/5th/7th lords in 8/12 = wife's; Saturn and Sun in the 5th with 5th lord in the 12th = departed spirit's. Result: childlessness until the remedy is done. Source: BPHS-83 v.9-105. Note: Phaladeepika gives a parallel scheme by planet (PD-12 v.19-22): Sun = offence to Shiva/ancestors; Moon = hurting mother or a married woman; Mars = village deity or kin; Mercury = Vishnu's displeasure; Jupiter = harm to family priest or fruit tree; Venus = flowering tree, women, cows; Saturn = peepal tree, Yama, spirits; Rahu = serpent; Ketu = Brahmin; Gulika = departed spirit. The two texts agree on Rahu = serpent and Sun = ancestors; other attributions differ.

## C. Adoption, other-born children, medical assistance

- **CH-25** — Condition: 5th house in a sign of Mercury or Saturn AND joined or aspected by Saturn and Mandi (Gulika). Result: adopted child. Source: BPHS-16 v.9; PD-12 v.8 (same, with "Mandi or Saturn"). The two texts agree.
- **CH-26** — Condition: 5th lord weak AND no link to the lagna lord or the 7th lord. Result: adoption. Source: PD-12 v.8.
- **CH-27** — Condition: six planets in the 5th AND 5th lord in the 12th AND lagna lord and Moon strong. Result: adopted child. Source: BPHS-16 v.11.
- **CH-28** — Condition: Mars and Saturn in the 9th from Upapada [reference point as in BPHS-30 v.23-27]. Result: childless, or an adopted child or stepchild. Source: BPHS-30 v.27; relayed with the same meaning in SHIV-D7 and TPA-UL (Jaimini sutra 1.4.27-28 by their numbering).
- **CH-29** — Condition: 5th lord in a movable sign AND Moon with Rahu AND Saturn in the 5th; OR Moon in the 8th from lagna, Jupiter 8th from Moon, with malefic influence. Result: a child born of another (not the native's own). Source: BPHS-16 v.14-15. Note: a modern reading could include donor conception; that reading is not in any text.
- **CH-30** — Condition: Beeja sphuta (male) or Kshetra sphuta (female) falls in the unfavourable parity in sign and navamsa (see section E). Result: conception only "after great effort"; modern authors read this as medical assistance needed. Source: PD-12 v.14 for "great effort"; BVB-D7 and PP-BK for the medical reading. Note: there is no classical rule for IVF or surgery; any such rule is a modern extension. `[UNSOURCED]` examples in circulation: Mars or Ketu linked to the 5th = surgical/caesarean birth; Rahu = technology-assisted conception.
- **CH-31** — Condition: Sun alone in the 5th in Cancer (also Mars or Venus so placed). Result: children by a second spouse. Source: PD-12 v.5. Also BPHS-16 v.31: malefic in the 5th or Saturn 5th from Jupiter = a child through another wife.

## D. Saptamsa (D7)

- **D7-01** (construction) — Condition: divide each sign into 7 parts of 4°17'08.57". In an odd sign count the parts from the sign itself; in an even sign from the 7th sign from it. Source: BPHS ch. 6 v.10-11 as cited in BVB-D7; PVR sec. 6.2.7 (p. 54). Agreed by all sources read.
- **D7-02** — Condition: D7 lagna and its lord free of malefic occupation or aspect. Result: ease in having children and happiness from them. Affliction: difficulty or worry. Source: BVB-D7. Note: OJ-112 reads D7 lagna as overall happiness and legacy from children; SHIV-D7 as the native's attitude to children.
- **D7-03** — Condition: where the D1 5th lord sits in D7 (good house and dignity vs 6/8/12 or debilitation); Jupiter's place in D7 (1, 5, 9 or own sign = strong). Result: confirms or weakens the D1 promise; 5th lord in D7 6/8/12 = delay or complication. Source: JH-D7; OJ-112 (weak Jupiter in D7 = delay). `[UNSOURCED]` in classics.
- **D7-04** — Condition: D7 lagna odd with benefic occupation/aspect → happiness from sons; even → from daughters. Source: BVB-D7. Note: BPHS-30 v.28 gives a different parity statement (planet in an odd sign in the relevant house = many children/daughters by the gloss read; even = few). The glosses are unstable; low confidence.
- **D7-05** (counting, direct) — Condition: D7 lagna in an odd sign: first child = 5th house, second = 7th, third = 9th, then 11th, each the 3rd from the previous. Source: OJ-112 (5th, 7th, 9th, no parity condition); SHIV-D7 (odd lagna: first from the 5th, then every third); JH-2C ("by threes"); TPA-UL.
- **D7-06** (counting, reverse) — Condition: D7 lagna in an even sign: count backwards — first child = 9th house, second = 7th, third = 5th, then 3rd, 1st (called manduka gati, frog-leap). Source: TPA-UL (states the 9th → 7th → 5th → 3rd → 1st sequence and calls it "complex and debated"); the odd/even assignment is the Rath/PVR-school teaching `[MEMORY]` — the PVR textbook portions fetched in this run (roughly pp. 1-99 and pp. 385-431) did not contain it. Note: CONFLICT — (a) many teachers ignore parity and always use 5, 7, 9; (b) one "bhavat bhavam" tradition gives the second child to the 9th (5th from the 5th) in D1 (JH-2C); (c) miscarriages and terminations are counted as children by some and skipped by others. The agent should state which convention it used.
- **D7-07** — Condition: for each child's house in D7, read the house, its lord and occupants. Result: describes that child and events with that child. Male planets (Sun, Mars, Jupiter) influencing the house suggest a son; female planets (Moon, Venus) a daughter; Mercury and Saturn are neutral/"eunuch" and follow the sign. Source: PVR sec. 3.2.7 (p. 30: example of the house of the first child influenced by Jupiter, Mars, Mercury → son; Moon, Mercury → daughter). Note: sex rules are unreliable; see section F.
- **D7-08** (Jaimini, from Upapada/D7 lagna) — Condition: planets in the 9th [from the reference point: Upapada per BPHS-30 v.23-25; D7 lagna per SHIV-D7/TPA-UL]: Saturn, Moon(?) or Mercury → childlessness; Sun, Jupiter or Rahu → many children; Moon alone → one child; mixed → a child after delay; Mars and Saturn → adoption. Source: BPHS-30 v.24-27; SHIV-D7 citing Jaimini sutras 1.4.24-28. Note: CONFLICT — BPHS-30 as glossed lists Saturn, Moon, Mercury as denying and then Moon alone as giving one son; SHIV-D7 and TPA-UL list Mercury, Saturn and Venus as denying. The Venus-versus-Moon difference is a known variant reading. Reference point also differs (Upapada vs D7 lagna).
- **D7-09** — Condition: Upapada in Leo with Moon → few children; Upapada in Virgo → daughters. Source: BPHS-30 v.29.
- **D7-10** — Condition: dasha of a planet that has argala on the D7 lagna. Result: childbirth. Source: SHIV-D7. PVR (example 125, pp. 405-406) times a birth with Narayana dasha of D7, using the 5th lord, the 11th house and the putra saham.
- **D7-11** — Condition: read the saptamsa also as the chart of desires and attachments; its 7th behaves like the 1st of the navamsa. Source: RATH-PAL. Note: interpretive frame only.

## E. Beeja sphuta, Kshetra sphuta, Santana tithi

- **BK-01** (Beeja sphuta, male chart) — Formula: (Sun + Venus + Jupiter longitudes) mod 360°. Take its sign and its navamsa. Result: odd sign AND odd navamsa = strong virility; one odd and one even = mixed, children after effort; both even = weak (modern authors: seek medical advice). Source: PD-12 v.14 (formula and the odd/odd and mixed cases); PP-BK and BVB-D7 (both-even case and medical reading). Note: the verse read does not spell out the both-even case; it is an inference used by all modern authors.
- **BK-02** (Kshetra sphuta, female chart) — Formula: (Moon + Mars + Jupiter longitudes) mod 360°. Result: even sign AND even navamsa = strong fertility; mixed = children after great effort; both odd = weak. Source: PD-12 v.14; PP-BK; BVB-D7.
- **BK-03** — Condition: additional checks used by modern authors: the sphuta's sign lord and navamsa lord strong and unafflicted; sphuta not in 6/8/12 from lagna; no Saturn/Rahu/Ketu on it; transit Jupiter over the sphuta or its trines as a conception window. Source: `[UNSOURCED]` common practice (not in the Phaladeepika verse read).
- **BK-04** (Santana tithi) — Formula: Phaladeepika's Sanskrit, as reported at the URL, is (5 × Moon) − Sun; the English gloss there and both modern sources compute 5 × (Moon − Sun). Then mod 360°, divide by 12° to get a tithi 1-30 (1-15 bright half, 16-30 dark half). Result: a good tithi in the bright half = child without much effort; dark half = difficulty or denial, weighed by tithi strength. Source: PD-12 v.15; PP-BK (spreadsheet form: 5 × (Moon − Sun), mod 360, /12); BVB-D7. Note: CONFLICT on the formula. The two forms differ by 4 × Sun and give different tithis. Compute both and flag disagreement; most software uses 5 × (Moon − Sun) `[MEMORY]`.
- **BK-05** — Condition: Santana tithi is Amavasya (30), a chhidra tithi (4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 14), or the half-tithi is Vishti karana or a fixed karana. Result: no children unless remedied. Source: PD-12 v.15; PP-BK.
- **BK-06** (tithi-wise remedies) — Condition → remedy: Vishti or fixed karana → worship Krishna with Purusha Sukta; 4th → worship the serpent king; 6th → Skanda/Subrahmanya; 8th → a vow of hearing sacred texts (Shravana vrata); 9th → hear the Ramayana; 12th → feeding/charity of food; 14th → worship Rudra; new/full moon (15th) → rites for ancestors; dark half from the 10th onward in three parts → serpent king, Skanda, Vishnu in turn. Source: PD-12 v.16-18.
- **BK-07** — Condition: precedence. Result: 5th-house affliction, Jupiter, and running dasha/transit outweigh the sphutas; both partners must be judged together. Source: PP-BK. Note: the same author states his nine sample charts do not validate the method.

## F. Number and sex of children (all LOW reliability)

- **NS-01** — Condition: count planets in the 5th and planets joined to the 5th lord; each in a friendly navamsa counts, those in debilitated or enemy navamsa are losses; repeat from Jupiter's sign. Result: number of children. Source: PD-12 v.13 (translation garbled at the URL read).
- **NS-02** — Condition: number of navamsas elapsed by the 5th lord (or by Jupiter) in its sign = number of children, reduced for malefic aspects. Source: `[UNSOURCED]` common practice (attributed online to Jataka Parijata / Sarvartha Chintamani; not read). **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** not found in any text read. In any case the project never states a number of children.
- **NS-03** — BPHS fixed counts: Jupiter at deep exaltation with karaka and benefics, 2nd lord with Rahu → ten sons; 9th lord in the 9th, Jupiter in the 5th, 5th lord strong → nine; 2nd lord in the 10th, Saturn in the 9th, 5th lord in the 5th → eight; 2nd lord and 5th lord in the 5th → seven, with twins; Jupiter and Saturn mutually 5th/9th → six, three surviving; malefic in the 5th or Saturn 5th from Jupiter → one. Source: BPHS-16 v.25-30. Note: literal counts belong to another era; use only as "more" versus "fewer". **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** recorded as the text has it; never stated to a person (no count of children is ever given).
- **NS-04** — Condition: 5th house (sign or navamsa) or 5th lord in a male sign/navamsa, or joined/aspected by male planets → sons; female sign/navamsa or female planets → daughters. Source: PD-12 v.11.
- **NS-05** — Condition: 5th lord, Jupiter, Mars and Sun all in male navamsas. Result: many children. Source: PD-12 v.10.
- **NS-06** — Condition: 5th lord conjunct the Moon or in the Moon's drekkana. Result: daughter(s). Source: BPHS-16 v.13.
- **NS-07** — Condition: 5th sign Cancer with Saturn in it → many children; Moon or Mercury there → few; Jupiter in the Moon's sign as the 5th → many daughters. Source: PD-12 v.5.
- **NS-08** — Condition: sign parity in the child's house in D7, or of the 9th from Upapada. Result: sex or count. Source: BVB-D7; BPHS-30 v.28; TPA-UL (9th in an odd sign = many, even = few). Note: three sources give three different meanings to the same parity test.
- **NS-09** (reliability) — With two outcomes, any sex rule is right about half the time by chance, and the texts give several rules that often disagree for the same chart (sign parity, navamsa parity, planet sex, drekkana, Moon). No source read reports a test. JH-D7 states outright that classical sex rules do not hold up statistically and declines to give them. The agent should refuse sex prediction and give number-of-children only as "the chart leans to few / several", never a count.

## G. Timing of childbirth

- **CT-01** — Condition: dasha or antardasha of the lagna lord, 7th lord, 5th lord, Jupiter, a planet in the 5th, or a planet aspecting the 5th. Result: birth of a child. Source: PD-12 v.25. Note: this is the core dasha rule. By analogy with our marriage calibration (a sub-period delivers only with a real link), require the antardasha lord to be one of these or conjunct the 5th lord.
- **CT-02** — Condition: within such a dasha, transiting Jupiter in the sign or navamsa sign occupied by the 5th lord (v.25), or in a sign trine to the rashi or navamsa sign of the 5th lord (v.29). Result: year of birth. Source: PD-12 v.25, v.29.
- **CT-03** — Condition: of the four planets — 5th lord, Jupiter, and the dispositors (rashi and navamsa) of each — take the strongest. Result: birth in its dasha or antardasha. Source: PD-12 v.28.
- **CT-04** — Condition: sum the longitudes of lagna lord, 7th lord and 5th lord; the nakshatra of that point gives a dasha lord; birth in that planet's mahadasha, in the antardasha of the planet in, aspecting or owning the 5th. Source: PD-12 v.27 (method only partly legible in the translation read; the "sum of longitudes" reading is the usual one `[MEMORY]`).
- **CT-05** — Condition: transiting lagna lord conjunct the natal 5th lord, or in its own or exaltation sign, or in the 5th house, or in the sign of the 5th lord. Result: month of conception or birth. Source: PD-12 v.26.
- **CT-06** — Condition: sum the longitudes of the Moon's nakshatra lord and of the 5th lord from it [reading uncertain]; Jupiter transiting that sign or its trines. Result: birth. Source: PD-12 v.30. Note: unclear in translation; low priority.
- **CT-07** (double transit) — Condition: transiting Jupiter AND Saturn both aspect or occupy the 5th house or the sign of the 5th lord (many versions accept the 9th as well), from lagna and from Moon, during a capable dasha (CT-01). Result: childbirth window, usually read as the year before or of the birth. Source: K.N. Rao, "Planets and Children" `[MEMORY]` — no page stating this rule for children could be read in this run; JH-2C mentions Jupiter and Saturn only informally. Treat as `[UNSOURCED]` until checked against the book. Note: same chance-rate caveat as for marriage: with 5th, 5th lord and 9th all allowed, from both lagna and Moon, a large share of years qualify. **Checked 12 Nov 2026:** not in the texts read; modern. The classical triggers are in section 0 (Phaladeepika 12.25-29).
- **CT-08** (second and later children) — Condition: second child from the 7th (3rd from the 5th), third from the 9th; so the 7th lord's and then the 9th lord's periods apply. Result: timing of later children. Source: JH-2C; OJ-112. Note: one tradition gives the second child to the 9th (5th from the 5th). CONFLICT, same as D7-06.
- **CT-09** — Condition (conception windows): man — Sun and Venus strong and transiting own signs/navamsas in an upachaya sign; woman — Mars and Moon so placed. Result: time when conception can occur. Source: PD-12 v.12. Note: Phaladeepika v.31-33 adds rules from the conception (adhana/nisheka) chart: Sun in the 3rd sign from nisheka lagna or trine to it; Moon's dwadasamsa at conception fixes the Moon sign at delivery. These need the conception time and are impractical.
- **CT-10** — BPHS age rules: Jupiter in the 5th with its dispositor [5th lord] joined to Venus → child at 32-33; 5th lord in a kendra with Jupiter → 30 or 36; Jupiter in the 9th from lagna with Venus 9th from Jupiter, or lagna lord with Venus → 40. Source: BPHS-16 v.18-20. Note: all are late ages; these are delay signatures, not literal ages.

- **CT-12** (KP) — Condition: sub lord of the 5th cusp signifies 2, 5, 11 → children promised; signifies 1, 4, 10 (12th from each) → denied or delayed; birth in the joint period of significators of 2, 5, 11. Source: JH-D7 mentions only that KP gives the 5th cusp sub lord primary weight; the house groups are standard KP teaching `[MEMORY]`. Not computable from a whole-sign Lahiri chart.

## H. Male versus female chart

- **MF-01** — Condition: house for children. Male chart: 5th. Female chart: 9th according to Phaladeepika (PD-11 v.1) and to the Jaimini-school sources (SHIV-D7, TPA-UL); 5th according to BPHS (BPHS-80 v.3). Note: CONFLICT. Compute both in a woman's chart and report agreement.

- **MF-04** — Condition (female chart): Moon in Scorpio, Virgo, Taurus or Leo falling in the 5th. Result: few children. Source: PD-11 v.4.

- **MF-06** — Condition (female chart): Moon and Venus in lagna with Saturn and Mars, 5th aspected by a malefic; or lagna in a sign of Saturn or Mars holding Moon and Venus with malefic aspect; or Sun and Moon in the 8th in own signs. - **MF-08** — Condition (female chart): benefics in the trines → children, happiness, wealth; weak malefics in the trines → barrenness or early loss. Lagna and Moon in even signs with benefic aspect → good sons. Source: PD-11 v.2, v.5.

## Reliability notes

### Widely agreed in the classical texts read

- The three-factor test: 5th house, 5th lord and Jupiter, repeated from the Moon, with benefic aspect as protection and 6/8/12 placement, debilitation, combustion or malefic conjunction as damage (BPHS-16 v.2-3; PD-12 v.1-2, 9, 19; PD-15).
- The adoption yoga: 5th in a sign of Mercury or Saturn with Saturn and Mandi. BPHS-16 v.9 and PD-12 v.8 agree almost word for word, which is rare.
- Mars + Saturn as an adoption signature in the Jaimini/Upapada scheme (BPHS-30 v.27).
- Rahu on the 5th = serpent curse with naga worship as remedy (BPHS-83 and PD-12 v.22 agree).
- Timing: dasha of 5th lord, Jupiter, lagna lord, 7th lord, occupant or aspector of the 5th, with Jupiter transiting the 5th lord's sign or trines (PD-12 v.25-29). This is the best-supported timing method and is purely classical.
- D7 construction (odd from the sign, even from the 7th).
- Beeja = Sun + Venus + Jupiter, Kshetra = Moon + Mars + Jupiter, with odd/even parity (Phaladeepika; all modern sources agree on the formulas).

### Classical but conflicting between texts

- Children in a woman's chart: 9th (Phaladeepika, Jaimini school) versus 5th (BPHS).
- Barren/childless signs: Scorpio is fruitful in the modern list and childless in Phaladeepika.
- Santana tithi formula: (5 × Moon) − Sun versus 5 × (Moon − Sun).
- Which planets deny children in the Jaimini rule: Saturn/Moon/Mercury versus Saturn/Mercury/Venus; and the reference point (Upapada versus D7 lagna).
- A malefic in the 5th: many sons (PD-12 v.3) versus one son (BPHS-16 v.30); 5th lord in the 5th: good (general rule) versus loss without benefic aspect (PD-12 v.9).
- Jupiter in the 5th: karaka-in-house harm versus the popular "best placement". Settled 12 Nov 2026: Parashara does not state the harm; Mantreshvara reports it as what "others say" (15.26); the only verses that assert it are Jataka Parijata 2.52 and 2.72, a late compilation. Treat Jupiter in the 5th by its dignity and aspects like any planet.

### Modern teaching or internet lore

- D7 child-by-child counting with direct/reverse order by lagna parity (D7-05, D7-06): taught in the Sanjay Rath / P.V.R. Narasimha Rao school and repeated widely, but not located in BPHS, Phaladeepika or the textbook pages read. Treat as a school convention.
- Double transit for childbirth (CT-07): attributed to K.N. Rao; not classical.
- "Medical assistance / IVF" readings of the sphutas, Rahu = technology, Mars/Ketu = surgery.
- D7 planet tables (D7-03), Sun/Moon as extra karakas.
- All literal counts of children.

### Empirical tests found

- No published statistical test of any Jyotish childbirth rule (5th-house yogas, D7, Beeja/Kshetra sphuta, Santana tithi) was found in this run.
- Beeja/Kshetra: the only data seen is nine anonymised charts on planetarypositions.com, whose author says outcomes were not verified and the method is not validated by them (PP-BK).
- Base-rate check for the sphutas: sign parity and navamsa parity are each about 50/50 and nearly independent, so about 25% of men get "both odd" (good), 50% "mixed", 25% "both even" (bad); the same split applies to women. Real-world infertility affects roughly one couple in six to seven (WHO estimate, about 17.5% lifetime prevalence `[MEMORY]`), so "mixed or bad" (75%) hugely over-flags. The sphutas cannot be used as a screening test.
- Sex of child: no test found; chance alone gives 50% (NS-09). JH-D7 explicitly declines sex prediction on accuracy and ethical grounds.
- General tests of Indian astrology (Narlikar, Kunte, Dabholkar, Ghatpande, Pune 2008; later re-analysis of 676 charts) found chart-judging at chance level for a different question (intellectual disability). Source read: https://www.skeptic.org.uk/?p=41058 . It is the nearest rigorous test and argues for humility across all domains.
- In-house: no child-timing cases verified yet. Log each prediction (dasha lords, link type, transit targets hit) with the actual outcome so CT-01, CT-02 and CT-07 can be scored the way the marriage rules were.
