# Upaya (remedies) — what is prescribed for what

Principle: **strengthen the functional benefic, pacify the malefic.** A gemstone *strengthens* — never give a gem for a malefic planet. Mantra/daan/fasting *pacify* — safe for any planet. Always prescribe by **Lagna lordship**, never by Sun sign or Moon sign alone.

## Gemstones (ratna) — by planet, ONLY if the planet is a functional benefic for the lagna (lord of 1/5/9, yogakaraka, or 4/7/10 lord that is a natural benefic and not also a dusthana lord)

Lagna-wise safe gems (typical): Aries: coral, ruby, yellow sapphire · Taurus: diamond, emerald, blue sapphire · Gemini: emerald, diamond · Cancer: pearl, coral, yellow sapphire · Leo: ruby, coral, yellow sapphire · Virgo: emerald, diamond · Libra: diamond, blue sapphire, emerald · Scorpio: coral, yellow sapphire, pearl · Sagittarius: yellow sapphire, ruby, coral · Capricorn: blue sapphire, diamond, emerald · Aquarius: blue sapphire, diamond, emerald · Pisces: yellow sapphire, pearl, coral.
**Never** blue sapphire, hessonite, cat's eye without a trial period (3 days under the pillow / on the arm). Diamond and emerald for Aries/Scorpio lagna, ruby for Libra/Taurus etc. are contraindicated.

## Mantras (japa; count 108× daily or the classical totals)
Sun 7,000 · Moon 11,000 · Mars 10,000 · Mercury 9,000 (some 17,000) · Jupiter 19,000 · Venus 16,000 · Saturn 23,000 · Rahu 18,000 · Ketu 17,000 — Kaliyuga multiplies ×4 in some traditions.
Beej: Sun *Om Hraam Hreem Hraum Sah Suryaya Namah* · Moon *Om Shraam Shreem Shraum Sah Chandraya Namah* · Mars *Om Kraam Kreem Kraum Sah Bhaumaya Namah* · Mercury *Om Braam Breem Braum Sah Budhaya Namah* · Jupiter *Om Graam Greem Graum Sah Gurave Namah* · Venus *Om Draam Dreem Draum Sah Shukraya Namah* · Saturn *Om Praam Preem Praum Sah Shanaishcharaya Namah* · Rahu *Om Bhraam Bhreem Bhraum Sah Rahave Namah* · Ketu *Om Sraam Sreem Sraum Sah Ketave Namah*.
Deity mantras: Sun → Aditya Hridayam, Gayatri; Moon → Shiva; Mars → Hanuman Chalisa, Kartikeya; Mercury → Vishnu Sahasranama; Jupiter → Guru stotra, Dakshinamurthy; Venus → Lakshmi, Durga; Saturn → Hanuman, Shani stotra (Dasharatha-krita); Rahu → Durga, Bhairava; Ketu → Ganesha.

## Daan (charity) — on the planet's day
Sun: wheat, jaggery, copper, red cloth · Moon: rice, milk, silver, white cloth · Mars: red lentils (masoor), coral, red cloth · Mercury: green moong, emerald-green cloth, books · Jupiter: chana dal, turmeric, yellow cloth, gold, teaching · Venus: curd, sugar, white cloth, perfume, silver · Saturn: black sesame, black urad, mustard oil, iron, black cloth, to labourers/disabled · Rahu: coconut, black/blue cloth, to lepers/outcasts · Ketu: multi-coloured blanket, dog feeding, banana.

## Fasting (vrat) — Sun: Sunday (salt-free) · Moon: Monday · Mars: Tuesday · Mercury: Wednesday · Jupiter: Thursday (yellow food) · Venus: Friday · Saturn: Saturday · Rahu/Ketu: Saturday/Tuesday.

## Yantra, Homa, Shanti
Navagraha shanti homa for multiple afflictions; Kaal Sarp puja (Trimbakeshwar/Ujjain tradition); Mangal shanti (Mangalnath, Ujjain; kumbh vivah for Manglik brides in some regions); Pitru shanti at Gaya/Haridwar; Rudrabhishek for Saturn/Moon; Maha Mrityunjaya japa for 8th/maraka periods; Sudarshana homa for enemies (6th).

## Behavioural / Lal Kitab-style (popular North Indian)
Feed crows (Saturn/Pitru), cows (Venus/Jupiter), dogs (Ketu), ants (Mercury); flowing water offerings for Rahu/Moon; keep silver (Moon) or copper (Sun) on the body; respect the karaka person (father for Sun, mother for Moon, teachers for Jupiter, spouse for Venus, workers for Saturn) — treated as the most effective "remedy" in practice.

## What the agent must say
Remedies are prescribed as *support*, with the caveat that gemstone advice requires the chart's functional-benefic check and a trial; the agent should not present remedies as guarantees.
