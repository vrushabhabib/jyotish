# Remedies (Upaya) — what the texts and mainstream practice prescribe

Tradition: Parashari base. This file separates four layers that are usually mixed together online:
1. **Classical (BPHS)** — graha shanti by worship, japa, homa, charity; shanti rites for birth doshas.
2. **Mainstream Vedic practice** — beeja mantras, weekday fasts, weekday daan, gemstones, yantra.
3. **Lal Kitab** — a separate 20th-century Punjabi/Urdu tradition with its own chart rules.
4. **Modern commercial lore** — rudraksha-by-planet charts, "dosha nivaran" packages, guaranteed pujas.

## 0. Non-harm rules (read first; the agent must apply these to every suggestion)
- **Never present a remedy as necessary, urgent or guaranteed.** A remedy is an optional practice of attention and charity; it is not a fix for a medical, legal, financial or relationship problem. Say so.
- **No costly remedies.** Do not recommend buying gemstones, paid pujas, yantras or rudraksha as a first step. Prefer what is free: prayer/mantra, service, feeding, giving in kind, a fast the person's health allows.
- **No risky remedies.** No fasting for people who are pregnant, diabetic, underweight, elderly, ill, or with a history of disordered eating; no ingestion of substances; no immersion of objects in rivers; no animal harm; nothing that isolates a person from family, doctor or work. BPHS ch. 93's advice that a father not see a Mula-born child for years is **never** to be relayed as advice.
- **No fear.** State a dosha with its cancellations and its real weight. Never tell someone a child, spouse or parent is in danger because of a chart factor.
- **Adapt to the person's own faith.** A Hindu may use the mantras below; a Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain or non-religious person should be offered the equivalent in their own tradition (their own prayer/dhikr/rosary/paath/meditation, charity to the group the planet signifies, discipline on that weekday). The classical logic is: honour what the planet stands for, give to those it represents, restrain what it inflames.
- **Medical/legal/financial issues go to professionals.** Astrology is offered as reflection, not diagnosis.
- **Gemstones only under the rule in §3**, on trial, never for a 6th/8th/12th lord, and never as an expense the person can't easily afford.

## 3. Gemstones — the WHEN rule
**Core rule (mainstream Parashari practice):** a gem strengthens (amplifies) a planet; it does not pacify it. Therefore:

| # | Condition (checkable) | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Planet is lord of house 1, 5 or 9 for the native's **Lagna** (not Moon sign, not Sun sign, not birth month) AND is weak/needs support AND is not also lord of 6/8/12 in a way that dominates | gem may be considered | 1/5/9 rule stated in the Substack article read (author trained in S. Rath's line); universal in K.N. Rao / B.V. Raman-style practice (working knowledge) |
| G2 | Planet is lord of house 6, 8 or 12 | **never** strengthen with a gem (enemies, disease, loss, litigation are what get amplified). The article read: "seldom" for 8th lord (left hand only if at all), "never" for the 6th lord when it signifies enemies/litigation | Substack article (citing Visti Larsen and the author's teacher); mainstream rule |
| G3 | Planet rules both a trine and a dusthana (e.g. Jupiter for Leo: 5+8; Saturn for Gemini: 8+9; Mercury for Aries: 3+6; Venus for Taurus: 1+6) | decide by moolatrikona house, placement and affliction; default = no gem, use mantra | practice (working knowledge) |
| G4 | Planet is a maraka (2nd/7th lord) in its own dasha at an advanced age, or a functional malefic for the lagna | no gem | practice |
| G5 | Planet is strong already (own/exalted, good shadbala) | no gem needed; over-strengthening is a risk | practice |
| G6 | Planet is debilitated/combust/afflicted AND a functional benefic | gem debated: one school strengthens it, another says a gem amplifies the planet *as it is*, including its affliction. Default = mantra first | disagreement |
| G7 | Enemy pairs | do not combine gems of mutual enemies (Sun/Moon/Mars/Jupiter group vs Venus/Saturn/Mercury group; Rahu/Ketu gems not with Sun/Moon gems) | practice |
| G8 | Blue sapphire, hessonite, cat's eye | strongest/fastest reputations; only on a short trial (wear or keep under pillow 3-7 days) and stop at any adverse change | practice; lore-heavy |
| G9 | Quality | natural, untreated, undyed, touching the skin; cheap or dyed stones are counted as harmful rather than neutral | Substack article read |
| G10 | Cost | if the stone is a financial stretch, the answer is no — use the substitute or no stone at all | project non-harm rule |

Lagna-wise quick table (lords of 1/5/9 only; verify against the functional-nature KB file before use):
Aries: Mars, Sun, Jupiter (9+12: caution) · Taurus: Venus, Mercury, Saturn · Gemini: Mercury, Venus (5+12: caution), Saturn (8+9: caution) · Cancer: Moon, Mars, Jupiter (6+9: caution) · Leo: Sun, Jupiter (5+8: caution), Mars · Virgo: Mercury, Saturn (5+6: caution), Venus · Libra: Venus, Saturn, Mercury (9+12: caution) · Scorpio: Mars, Jupiter, Moon · Sagittarius: Jupiter, Mars (5+12: caution), Sun · Capricorn: Saturn, Venus, Mercury (6+9: caution) · Aquarius: Saturn, Mercury (5+8: caution), Venus · Pisces: Jupiter, Moon, Mars.

What is NOT a valid basis: birth month ("birthstones"), Sun sign, Moon sign alone, numerology, "current dasha lord's gem" without checking lordship.

Weights (e.g. "ratti equal to one-tenth of body weight"), specific muhurtas and "energising" rituals are trade custom, not classical rule.

## 4. Yantra and rudraksha
- **Yantra:** the nine 3×3 magic squares (Sun sums to 15, Moon 18, Mars 21, Mercury 24, Jupiter 27, Venus 30, Saturn 33, Rahu 36, Ketu 39) are standard in tantric/remedial practice and are used as a focus for japa. Not in BPHS. Harmless if drawn by hand; not worth buying. (Working knowledge.)
- **Navagraha temple visits / Navagraha homa:** mainstream South Indian practice (the Kumbakonam circuit etc.); devotional, optional.
- **Rudraksha:** Shaiva texts (Shiva Purana, Rudraksha Jabala Upanishad) praise rudraksha by number of faces and deity. The planet mapping (1-Sun, 2-Moon, 3-Mars, 4-Mercury, 5-Jupiter, 6-Venus, 7-Saturn, 8-Rahu, 9-Ketu) is **modern trade lore**, not in jyotish classics. A plain 5-mukhi mala for japa is traditional and cheap; high-priced rare-mukhi beads should not be recommended.

## 5. Lal Kitab — a separate tradition (flag clearly)
- Origin: Urdu books published in Punjab 1939-1952 (attributed to Pt. Roop Chand Joshi). It uses a fixed-Aries house chart (house = sign), its own "sleeping planet", "blind planet", *pakka ghar* and annual-chart (varshphal) rules. Its placements and judgments **do not map onto a Parashari whole-sign chart**; a Lal Kitab remedy should only follow a Lal Kitab reading.
- Typical remedies (*totke*): feeding crows/dogs/cows/monkeys, floating coconuts or coins in running water, keeping a silver square piece, burying items, not accepting gifts, applying saffron tilak, serving one's elders.
- Engine rule: never auto-suggest Lal Kitab remedies from Parashari findings. If a user asks, label them "Lal Kitab tradition (separate system)". Relay only the harmless service-type ones (feeding animals, serving parents, charity); do not relay ones that involve throwing objects into rivers, burying things at home, or avoiding family members.
- Source: working knowledge; no Lal Kitab text was read this session.

Classical shapa (curse) remedies, BPHS ch. 83: Sarpa shapa → Naga worship and charity; Pitru shapa → shraddha (Gaya), feeding, kanyadana, cow daan; Matru shapa → Gayatri japa (100,000), pipal rounds (1008), charity; and so on. All concern difficulty in having children. Modern equivalents: ancestor remembrance (shraddha/tarpan or own-faith memorial), supporting a child's education or a wedding of someone in need, feeding people.

Modern dosha remedies with **no** classical text behind them: "Kaal Sarp dosha puja" at Trimbakeshwar/Kalahasti, "Shrapit dosha nivaran", "Mangal dosha — marry a tree/pot (kumbh vivah)" (the last is regional folk custom). The agent may mention that these exist; it must not recommend them, and should point out that Kaal Sarp itself is not classical (see yogas-catalogue).

## Reliability notes
- **Classical (read this session):** BPHS ch. 84 planet table (image material, japa counts, homa wood, food, daan); BPHS ch. 85-96 birth-dosha shanti; BPHS ch. 83 shapa remedies. One e-text was read; counts for Mars/Mercury/Ketu differ in other editions and popular lists.
- **Mainstream practice, not classical verse:** beeja mantras; weekday fasts; grain/cloth daan by weekday; the navaratna gem set (the classical verse is Jataka Parijata 2.21, read in the Sanskrit 12 Nov 2026: ruby, pearl, coral, emerald, topaz, [diamond - the word is corrupt in the e-text], blue sapphire, hessonite, cat's-eye); fingers and metals (these vary by lineage); yantras; Navagraha temple circuits.
- **Gem rule sourcing:** the "1/5/9 lords yes; 6/8 lords no" rule was confirmed only in a secondary article (a practitioner citing Sanjay Rath and Visti Larsen). The stricter "lagna-based functional benefic only; never 6/8/12 lords" statement is universal in mainstream Parashari teaching (K.N. Rao school, Raman) but **no primary source for it was opened this session** — there is no BPHS verse on gem selection by lordship. Treat it as a strong practice rule, not scripture.
- **Separate tradition:** Lal Kitab (not Parashari; not read this session).
- **Modern commercial lore:** rudraksha-to-planet mapping; ratti-by-body-weight; ×4 japa counts for Kali Yuga; dosha-specific paid pujas (Kaal Sarp, Shrapit); kumbh vivah for Mangal dosha; gem "activation" rituals; birth-month stones.
- **Not sourced:** a primary citation for each beeja mantra; a primary citation for finger assignments; Sanjay Rath's and K.N. Rao's own written gem rules.
