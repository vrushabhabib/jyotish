# Match protocol (added 20 Oct 2026)

Lesson: Vrushab x Pooja Kalburgi was scored ~24/36 and called "a real match" without checking whether Pooja fits what Vrushab's own chart says about his wife. His chart's strongest, life-verified marriage signature (7L Moon with Rahu at 2 deg, Venus karaka in 12th, Rahu aspecting the 7th, D9 Moon in Sagittarius; every past relationship was with someone from another background/country) was never tested. Ashtakoota is Moon-to-Moon temperament only; it says nothing about whether a candidate is the partner either chart describes.

Order for every match, before any score:
1. From EACH chart, write the partner description (7th house, 7L, karaka, DK, 7th from Moon, D9 7th, A7/UL) and the strongest verified pattern from the person's past relationships.
2. Test the candidate against it with facts: same or different community, region, country, language, religion; where the couple would live; age gap; profession. Ask the user for missing facts; never guess caste/community.
3. If a chart's repeated signature (e.g. Rahu on 7L = outside one's circle/country) is unmet by the candidate, say so first and grade the match down, whatever the Ashtakoota.
4. Only then: Ashtakoota with doshas and cancellations, Manglik, inter-chart contacts (only those not shared by everyone born the same weeks), and timing overlap.
5. Verdict = promise fit + score + timing, in that order of weight.
