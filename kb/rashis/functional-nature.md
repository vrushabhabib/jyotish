# Functional benefics & malefics by Lagna (BPHS ch. 34) — the single most important table for reading a chart

Rule: trikona lords (1,5,9) benefic; 3,6,11 lords malefic; 2,8,12 lords neutral (take on nature of their other sign / associations); kendra lords (4,7,10) — natural benefics owning them get blemished, natural malefics improve. Planet owning a trikona AND a kendra = **Yogakaraka**. Lord of 2/7 = **maraka**.

**Use**: a "good" planet by natural nature in a "bad" functional role gives bad results in its dasha (e.g. Jupiter for Taurus/Libra/Capricorn/Aquarius lagnas). The agent must say which applies. Nodes: Rahu/Ketu act as the lord of the sign they occupy and the planets they conjoin.
