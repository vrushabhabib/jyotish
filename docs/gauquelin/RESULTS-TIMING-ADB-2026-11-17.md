# The timing test — result (17 Nov 2026)

Rules and collection protocol were fixed in PRE-STATED-TIMING-ADB.md before the first page was
opened. Sample: the first 400 alphabetical entries of Astro-Databank's marriages category, taken
mechanically; 360 parsed; 169 with recorded times (Rodden AA/A) and month-or-better dated events.
377 events (304 day-dated), 226 of them marriage-area from 137 people. Stored anonymously - birth
moment, coordinates, rating, event type and date only; no names were collected. Each real chart was
pooled against 1,000 wrong-birth control charts with the engine's pre-registered machinery
(engine.control.pool), unchanged. Bar: pooled share of controls at or above the real score < 0.001.

## The four sealed rules

| Rule | Marriage set (226 events) | All areas (377 events) |
|---|---|---|
| T1 sub-period link (ad_link) | 103 vs controls 116.8 - share 0.96 | 197 vs 206.4 - share 0.82 |
| T2 link-or-movable | 110 vs 127.1 - share 0.98 | 207 vs 222.4 - share 0.94 |
| T3 main-period link | 110 vs 114.0 - share 0.68 | 204 vs 203.4 - share 0.51 |
| T4 third-level link (day-dated) | 99 vs 97.5 - share 0.45 | 166 vs 164.2 - share 0.45 |

Share = fraction of 1,000 control-chart sets scoring at least as well as the real charts. 0.5 is
pure chance. Every sealed rule sits at chance or a shade below it (within noise).

## Verdict
The project's one live hint - the sub-period link, which showed a faint edge on five known people -
shows nothing on 137 strangers with recorded birth times and documented marriage dates. The faint
edge was luck. Under the sealed protocol this closes the question: no part of a total reading now
has tested support. Readings from this engine state the calculation, state the tradition with its
labels, and promise nothing.

## Exploratory note, outside the sealed set
Of the ~24 registry rules the pool reports, one (link_lagna_tie, pre-stated 13 Nov) came out at
share 0.021 on the all-areas set. One rule at ~0.02 among 24 is what chance produces; it fails the
sealed bar and is NOT a result. It is the only candidate that may deserve one pre-registered
confirmation on a fresh sample (entries 400-800 of the same category walk); if that is run, this
note is its pre-registration.
