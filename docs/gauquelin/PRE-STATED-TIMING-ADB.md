# The timing test on Astro-Databank — rules fixed before any data is collected (17 Nov 2026)

This is the decisive test: the one part of a total reading still open. Fixed here BEFORE the first
ADB page is opened, so collection cannot shape the rules.

## Data to collect (built-in browser, when the desktop is connected)
- People with Rodden rating AA or A only (recorded birth times).
- From each page: birth date, time, place, rating, and every dated life event with at least month+year
  (marriage, divorce, child's birth, major career start or end, relocation). Death is collected as a
  record-keeping field only and never tested or spoken, per project rule.
- Collection is mechanical: every qualifying entry in the category walked is taken, none skipped for
  how its chart looks. The collector logs the page list first, then extracts, so the sample is fixed
  before any chart is cast. Target: 300+ people, 1,000+ dated events.

## The rules under test (exactly the ones already in the engine, unchanged)
1. T1 sub-period link (our ad_link): the event falls in a sub-period whose lord is strongly linked
   (occupies / owns / joined with / karaka) to the event's life area. The project's one live hint.
2. T2 link-or-movable (link_or_mov), as coded.
3. T3 main-period link (md_link) - already demoted on our data; given its last chance here.
4. T4 third-level link (pd_link) on day-dated events only.

## Scoring, fixed now
- Each person's real chart against 200 wrong-birth controls (same method as engine.control).
- Headline number: real hits vs control mean, share of controls scoring as well; pooled across all
  people. Bar: the pooled share of controls at or above the real score must be under 1 in 1,000.
- Marriage-area events are the primary set (most reliably dated in biographies); other areas secondary.
- No rule may be added, dropped or reworded after collection begins. A null closes the question:
  no part of a total reading will then have tested support, and readings say so.
