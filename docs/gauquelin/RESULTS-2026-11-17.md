# Gauquelin profession test — first run, 17 Nov 2026

Design and rules were fixed and pushed in PRE-STATED-RULES.md before any chart was cast (commit history proves the order).

751 date-matched pairs (sportsman vs scientist born within 730 days of each other; the era overlap
caps the pair count). Each rule had to say which of the two is the sportsman. Pre-stated bar for a
result: accuracy >= 53% AND p < 0.001.

Descriptive: Mars in the 12th/9th whole-sign house — sportsmen 16.2%, scientists 18.9%, base 16.7%.

## Verdict
Every pre-stated rule scored at chance on 751 pairs. The one nominal p<0.05 points the wrong way and
is what eight simultaneous tests produce by luck. At this sample size a real 55% edge would have been
unmissable (p < 10^-6). These classical career rules, as we coded them, do not separate elite
sportsmen from scientists.

## Honest limits
- Whole-sign houses approximate Gauquelin's diurnal sectors; this is not an exact replication of his
  claim, only of its whole-sign shadow.
- Register times are rounded, blurring the lagna; this costs power but cannot create a false null of
  this flatness at n=751.
- The classical-category mapping (athlete→Mars) is an interpretation; the texts predate sport as a
  profession. A null here binds these rules on this contrast, nothing more.
