# Charts - fresh start, 13 Nov 2026

This folder was emptied on 13 Nov 2026 at the owner's instruction: every reading is redone from scratch on the rebuilt system, from birth data given afresh.

- A chart file is created here only from birth data supplied in the new round. Its `computed` block is written by `python3 -m engine.store`; nothing is typed by hand.
- **Do not open `data/archive/` when preparing a reading.** It holds the chart files, readings, forecasts and the ledger of the earlier round (Sep-Nov 2026), made with earlier calculations and earlier rules. They are kept only so that old and new can be compared when the owner asks for that.
- What carries over from the earlier round is only what each person **said about their own life** (their answers). Those are in the project memory, one file per person, under the heading "Answers given". They may be used as facts and to check a chart; the conclusions the earlier readings drew from them may not.
- `data/ledger.jsonl` starts empty. The earlier track record (24 fully right of 39 past questions) belongs to the earlier system and is not quoted as this system's record.
