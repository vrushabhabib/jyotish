# Life-events form - to be filled BEFORE the person sees anything from their chart

Why: a rule can only be tested on dates the chart did not suggest. Please fill this from your own memory or papers.

**Birth:** date ____  time ____ (from: hospital record / family memory / guess; could be wrong by ___ minutes)  place ____  sex ____

**10 to 15 events.** For each: what happened, the date as exactly as you know it, whether it was good or bad FOR YOU, and how sure the date is.

| # | Area (pick one) | What happened (one line) | Date (YYYY-MM-DD, or YYYY-MM, or year) | Good / bad / neither | Date from a document? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | | |

Areas: career (job start, loss, promotion, business opened or closed) - money (large gain or loss) - marriage (met, engaged, married, separated) - home/mother (moved house, bought property or vehicle, mother's major event) - siblings (birth or marriage of a brother or sister) - father/fortune - children (birth) - foreign/loss (moved abroad, heavy loss) - self/health (only the date of a hospital stay or accident; no diagnosis needed).

Please do NOT write: details of illness, anything about anyone's death, or anything you would not want stored. Dates are kept in a private file, never in the project's shared files; only counts are saved.

After filling: save as JSON with `python3 -m engine.intake new NAME` (it asks the questions) or hand the table over as it is.
